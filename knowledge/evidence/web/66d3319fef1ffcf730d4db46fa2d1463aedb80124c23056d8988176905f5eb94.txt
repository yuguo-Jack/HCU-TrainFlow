The NVIDIA CUDA Deep Neural Network library (cuDNN) provides optimized operations for training and inference on NVIDIA GPUs. Use attention, matrix multiplication, convolution, and multi-operation fusion through your framework or the cuDNN Python and C++ APIs.
Integrations
Use cuDNN through the tools in your AI stack, or build directly on its graph APIs. These paths show how selected frameworks and libraries reach cuDNN. Support depends on the operation, selected backend, library versions, and GPU.

Operations
cuDNN supports individual operations and flexible fusion patterns. Describe a computation as an
operation graph
so cuDNN can select an execution plan for your GPU.
Attention
Scaled dot-product attention for transformer training and inference.
Matrix multiplication
Matrix products and supported fusion patterns.
Convolution
Convolution and cross-correlation for vision and other workloads.
Normalization
Batch, layer, instance, and RMS normalization.
Mixture of experts
Grouped matrix multiplication for expert workloads.
Pointwise and reduction
Arithmetic, activations, comparisons, and reductions.
cuDNN also supports softmax and
