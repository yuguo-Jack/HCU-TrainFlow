<a href="#"><img alt="P1" src="https://greptile-static-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/badges/p1.svg?v=9" align="top"></a> **Cleanup hangs after test failures**

The `finally` block calls `barrier()` even when a test fails on just one rank. Passing ranks can enter the next test's collective instead, so their work no longer matches the failed rank's barrier. This can hang until NCCL times out, delay the original failure, and prevent `destroy_process_group()` from running.

Keep the barrier on the success path. Put guarded destruction in `finally`, without calling `world_group()` during cleanup.

```suggestion
        try:
            if args.compile:
                run_compile_parallel_tests()
            else:
                run_parallel_tests()
            torch.distributed.barrier(world_group())
            torch.cuda.synchronize()
        finally:
            if torch.distributed.is_initialized():
                torch.distributed.destroy_process_group()
```
