# test(gated_attention_block): assert the structure of the norm-weight gradient below 512 reduction rows -- the stage-exact fp32 bound on the norm + RoPE backward and the bf16 bound on its dQ / dK input, the random-walk statistic printed where it is not a bound

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1530) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

**Serves the bf16 gated-attention-block backward tests' `dW_q_norm` / `dW_k_norm` assertion at SHORT reductions: below 512 reduction rows the tests assert the STRUCTURE of the deviation -- the norm + RoPE backward stage exact to its fp32 bound over the block's own inputs, its dQ / dK input inside the bf16 block's bound -- instead of a random-walk statistic that was calibrated on reductions of 512 rows or more; the statistic is printed there. Test-only: 2 files, +172 / -3; no kernel, engine, `Capabilities` or binding change; no tolerance changed (every bound used already exists in the module).**

## What

- `test/python/gated_attention_block/cutedsl/test_block_backward.py`: `_assert_dw_norm_structured` (the structured form), `_dw_norm_own_sum` (the fp64 sum over the block's OWN bf16 dQ / dK slot, the slab's bf16 pre-norm band, the saved fp32 rstd and the bf16 cos / sin tables), `_rope_adjoint_abs` (an elementwise upper bound of the RoPE adjoint), `_dw_norm_rows` (the reduction length, read off the oracle), the dispatcher `_assert_dw_norm` and `_DW_NORM_STRUCTURED_BELOW_ROWS = 512`; `_check_all_grads` calls the dispatcher; `_fp64_oracle` and `_fp64_oracle_from_record` append the per-token quantities the form reads (`dq_post / dk_post`, `q_pre / k_pre`, `rstd_q / rstd_k`).
- `test/python/gated_attention_block/cutedsl/test_block_thd_backward.py`: the packed oracle packs those per-token quantities back row by row beside `dh`; `_check_all_grads_packed` calls the dispatcher.
- At 512 rows or more the dispatcher calls `_assert_dw_norm_close` exactly as before: every dense accept cell and every packed cell of 256 or more tokens keeps its current assertion byte for byte.
- The dispatcher reads the row count off the oracle's appended keys, so the record-seeded dense test -- whose result carries only `grads` and `oracle` (2048 rows) -- takes the calibrated form; below the threshold a run without the block's own fields (`blk`, `geom`, `saved`, `ws`, `inp`) is refused by name instead of dying on an attribute error.

## Why: a statistic asserted as a bound at a 5-token packing

`dW_q_norm` / `dW_k_norm` are held to `2^-5 x mass + 1e-2 x |ref|` (`_DW_NORM_NOISE`, `_DW_NORM_RTOL`), with `mass = sqrt(sum of the squared row terms)`: a random-walk statistic, calibrated on 11 norm cells at S >= 256 whose reductions sum 512 rows or more (`|diff| / mass` 0.0156-0.0195, a 1.6x margin). At the packing `(5, 0, 0)` -- five tokens followed by two empty sequences -- the `dW_k_norm` reduction sums **10 rows** (5 tokens x 2 kv heads). On the dataset a 212-SM Rubin part draws (torch's Philox lays the draws out by grid size, which follows the SM count, so one `manual_seed` is a different tensor on 204 and 212 SMs) the statistic reads **1.141** of that bound at one of 256 columns, and `test_thd_zero_length_sequence_bwd[trailing_empties_T5-unfused]` / `[trailing_empties_T5-fuse_gate_bwd]` are red in the sm107 CI job while a 204-SM part passes them.

Decomposed along the block's chain (`got - ref = (got - S) + A + B + AB`, with `S` the fp64 sum over the block's own inputs, `A` the SDPA stage's bf16 dK deviation propagated, `B` the forward record's slab / rstd rounding propagated), the miss is the statistic, not a kernel defect:

- `got - S`: the norm + RoPE backward kernel and its reduce deviate from the fp64 sum over their OWN inputs by **2e-7 of the mass** -- 0.000 of the fp32-tight stage bound `1e-4 mass + 1e-3 |S|` that `test_stage_localisation_via_autograd_grad` already pins, a bound 300x finer than the random-walk one;
- the bf16 dK slot itself sits at **0.60** of the bf16 block's bound against the oracle's post-norm gradient, **0 of 10 rows outside** -- the SDPA stage's own tolerance, met;
- `A` is the whole deviation (1.063 of the bound at the miss column): ten bf16-level row terms whose signs do not average out (four same-sign rows add to 1.46 of the bound against two opposite ones). Over nine datasets the 10-row statistic exceeds its bound on **4** (1.14 / 1.35 / 1.48 / 1.58, one or two columns each), the 40-row `dW_q_norm` on 1 (1.05); on one dataset it sits at 0.017-0.026 of the mass from 26 rows up and at 0.019 at 512 rows -- the calibration regime. The max over 256 columns of a sum of a few bf16-level terms is not the quantity the coefficient was calibrated as.

A defect would look nothing like this: one dropped row term at the miss column is 0.57-0.69 of the mass = **18-22x** the noise coefficient; a wrong scale is ~|ref|.

## The form below the threshold

Each part of the deviation is held to the bound of the stage that produces it; nothing here is a new tolerance:

1. the norm + RoPE backward kernel + reduce against the fp64 sum over its OWN inputs: `|got - S| <= 1e-4 mass_S + 1e-3 |S|` (measured 2e-7..5e-7 of the mass at the 5-token cell; 0.001 of the bound at the 256-row dense cell);
2. the dQ / dK slot against the oracle's post-norm gradient under the bf16 block's bound form (`_assert_grad_close`, the tolerance every other bf16 band of the block is held to; measured 0.35-0.74, 0 rows outside on every dataset);
3. the propagated cap on the whole deviation: `|got - ref| <= bound_1 + sum_rows |RoPE^T|(bound_2)[r] |x_hat_ref[r]| + sum_rows |g_ref[r]| |x_hat_blk - x_hat_ref|[r]` -- (1), (2) and the forward record's measured slab / rstd rounding, each propagated row by row;
4. `cos >= _COS_MIN`; the random-walk statistic is PRINTED with its worst cell and RETURNED (the suites' `worst` tables keep their meaning).

**Condition (3) is a consistency check, not a third detector -- the effective end-to-end assertion below the threshold is (1) + (2).** By the identity above the cap is implied by (1) and (2) up to the second-order cross term `AB` (measured 1.2e-3 of the noise bound), and it is LOOSE: per column it is 1.35-28x the random-walk bound at the 10-row `dW_k_norm` (median 4.9x, 28.0x at the miss column), 3-67x (median 12-13x) at the 40-row `dW_q_norm` and 44.8x median / 74.7x max at the 256-row dense cell -- a sum of absolute bounds grows with the rows, the random-walk bound with their square root; the form prints the ratio. So below the threshold the deviation from the fp64 oracle is held through the SDPA stage's per-element bf16 bound on the dQ / dK slot (2) and the fp32-tight bound on the norm + RoPE backward's own arithmetic (1) -- and not through any bound on `|got - ref|` itself (the statistic is printed). What that trade buys and costs, measured: a 1 % scale error reads **9.58x** the fp32 bound of (1) at 10 rows and **9.66x** at 256 rows while the random-walk statistic reads 0.983 and 0.736 -- INSIDE its bound at both lengths -- so on the stage's own arithmetic the structured form is strictly finer than the statistic; on the propagated SDPA-stage deviation it is strictly coarser (any deviation inside the per-element bf16 bound passes, however its row terms add up). A random-walk propagation of bound_2 would restore a statistic of the same shape and fail the same four of nine datasets for the same reason; it is not proposed.

**What it catches:** any arithmetic defect of the norm + RoPE backward or the reduce -- a dropped token, a wrong rstd or scale, a wrong RoPE adjoint, a stale slot read -- at (1); an SDPA-stage dQ / dK deviation beyond the bf16 block's bound on ANY element at (2); a propagated miss no stage explains at (3). **What it does not catch** (by design, and neither does the form before this change): a dQ / dK deviation INSIDE the bf16 block's bound -- that is the tolerance the whole block is held to; a forward-record (slab / rstd) deviation beyond the measured term -- the forward suite's record pins own it; and below the threshold the random-walk statistic itself is not asserted (printed; above the threshold asserted exactly as before).
