# sdpa fwd sm100/sm107: each softmax warpgroup waits its own mb_o_empty slot (cga2 split-KV / THD hang)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1541) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Summary

Fixes a GPU hang of the SM100-line FROST SDPA forward prefill kernels at `TILE_CGA_M=2`: both softmax warpgroups waited `mb_o_empty[0]` at the top of every tile, and slot 0's producer chain has no back-edge through warpgroup 1, so the TMA-STG warp could complete a second phase of that barrier before warpgroup 1 performed its parity wait. The wait then aliases and the CTA deadlocks (correct numerics on every tile that did complete, 100 % GPU utilization until killed). Each warpgroup now waits its own slot, `mb_o_empty[sub_tile_id]`, whose producer chain runs through that warpgroup's own stats publish.

Fixes #1532
Fixes #1525

## Root cause

`mb_o_empty[qs]` is a single-phase barrier TMA-STG arrives once per tile per O sub-tile after the correction's `o_full[qs]`. A parity wait on it is correct only while the producer is at most one phase ahead of the waiter. Slot 0's chain is

```
MMA bmm2_done[0] -> correction qs=0 epilogue (waits stat_full[0] = softmax WG0's publish) -> o_full[0] -> TMA-STG -> o_empty[0]
```

which passes through WG0 and the correction but never through WG1. Whenever WG1 is delayed across a tile boundary for longer than that chain takes, TMA-STG completes `o_empty[0]` twice before WG1's wait; WG1's parity wait aliases and now needs the *next* completion, which needs the next tile's `o_full[0]`, which needs the correction to finish *this* tile's `qs=1` epilogue, which needs WG1's stats. An empty (q-tile, split) unit makes the chain shortest (no MMA and no softmax work: a few barrier hops and the partial store), the hint-less spin wait form and GPU time-slicing supply the delay, and the number of empty hand-outs supplies the odds.

Captured with a host-visible heartbeat trace (an instrumented copy of the kernel writing per-warp-role stage codes into pinned host memory, dumped while hung) on a cc 10.3 part: of 152 resident CTAs, 151 had finished the kernel (correction at its exit arrive, TMA-LDG in its drain, MMA at the TMEM dealloc wait); the one stuck CTA was on an empty unit with WG1 at the top-of-tile `o_empty[0]` wait, the correction at `stat_full[1]`, and TMA-STG already past `o_full[0]` (i.e. one `o_empty[0]` completion ahead of WG1). Every role agreed on the tile index.

The static barrier table is balanced (every init count equals its issuing lanes, every ring is otherwise one-phase bounded) — this is a missing *back-edge* for one consumer, not a miscount.

On a cc 10.0 part neither issue reproduces (1,001 launches of the #1532 configurations; 350,000 replays of the #1525 case, idle and under GPU time-slicing from a second process), so #1525 is covered by the mechanism (the same kernel body with the same slot-0 wait, replaying empty and one-tile units under xdist GPU sharing), not by an independent reproduction.

## Scope

The same one-line change in the eleven two-warpgroup forward kernels: `sm100/prefill_d128_{f16,fp8,mxfp8}`, `sm100/prefill_d192_d128_{fp8,mxfp8}`, `sm107/prefill_d128_{f16,fp8,mxfp8}`, `sm107/prefill_d192_d128_{f16,fp8,mxfp8}` (the sm100 d192x128 fp8 kernel performs the wait through a local helper and was added in the review follow-up commit). `sm100/decode_d128_f16` has one softmax warpgroup (its own publish is slot 0's back-edge) and is unchanged. No `Capabilities` or knob change (Rule S2 exempt).

## Test

`test/python/sdpa/frost/test_sdpa_fwd_o_empty_backedge.py` (L0): a no-GPU, spelling-agnostic source pin that none of the eleven two-warpgroup kernels waits `mb_o_empty[0]` at the top of the tile, and the smallest deterministically hanging shape run for 20 synchronised executes in a child process under a wall budget (a regression fails the test instead of wedging the suite), with an fp32 reference check.

Validation on the worktree build: the new module plus the smoke tier of the sm100 forward suites (f16, fp8, mxfp8, paged, prepared THD, split-KV) on a cc 10.0 part and of the sm107 forward suites on a cc 10.7 part — results in the first comment.
