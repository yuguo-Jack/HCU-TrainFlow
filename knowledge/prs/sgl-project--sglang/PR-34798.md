## Motivation
There has been extensive discussion around making HiCache work better for small host-memory setups: https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/28614, https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/20535, https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/16909, https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/14056. Out of this, we've converged on the following design: use the L2 host-memory layer as a buffer. This decouples L2 from the complex tree management and the frequent, pressure-driven evictions it would otherwise incur, while still capturing the full benefit of IO latency hiding and maximizing cache hit rate through the larger L3 layer.
Will update design doc and benchmark number later, open this PR for preview and early testing.
