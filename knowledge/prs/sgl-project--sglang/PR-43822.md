# [AMD] MoE sort patch: fuse the stage-1 MXFP8 quant at hidden 5120 and sort up to 385 experts

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43822) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

On MI35x, `moe_sorting_small` replaces aiter's MoE sorting at small batches. It can also emit the stage-1 MXFP8 activation quant in the same launch, which saves aiter's separate `fused_mx_quant_moe_sort` launch. Two gates kept DeepSeek-V4.1 off both paths:

- **The quant gate required `hidden % 2048 == 0`.** DeepSeek-V4.1's hidden size is 5120, so every MoE layer still ran aiter's stage-1 quant as its own launch.
- **The distributed sort only ran up to 256 experts.** With `--enforce-shared-experts-fusion`, V4.1's target MoE has E=385 (384 routed + shared). Rows 10-16 at top-7 therefore went to aiter's sort plus quant, two launches.

At TP4 batch 1-2, a decode cycle runs about 43 MoE layers. Each launch costs about 2-4 us under graph replay.

## Modifications

All changes are in `python/sglang/kernels/ops/moe/moe_sorting_small.py`.

- **Stage-1 quant at 1024- or 2048-column steps.** The quant is now emitted whenever the hidden size is a multiple of 1024: 5120 (V4.1) and 7168 (V3) gain it, and 2048-multiples keep their existing step. Smaller steps stay on aiter; they were never checked bitwise or timed, and they would unroll many quant iterations per pair.
- **Distributed sort up to E=385, up to 112 routing pairs.** The limit is the named constant `_DISTRIBUTED_MAX_PAIRS_UP_TO_385_EXPERTS`. Past 112 pairs the distributed sort + quant loses to aiter at E=385 (it breaks even near 126 and is slower from 140).

## Accuracy Tests

`test/registered/amd/test_moe_sorting_small.py` (gfx950): 7 passed. Each case guards one regression:

- **Sort parity at E=385, 16 rows (112 pairs, block 32/64).** This is the first time the distributed sort runs with more than 256 experts.
- **Quant bytes and e8m0 scales at hidden 5120**, checked bitwise against aiter's `fused_dynamic_mxfp8_quant_moe_sort`, for 9 rows (compact sort) and 16 rows (distributed sort).
- **The 16/17-row cutoff (112/119 pairs).** Fails if the E≤385 clause is dropped or widened.
- **End to end, `fused_moe` A8W4 at hidden 5120 / inter 640.**
  - Covers 385/7 at 1 and 16 rows, routed through the HIP split-K router GEMV and gate, plus the DSpark draft's 129/4 at 1 row.
  - The stage-1 GEMM output must be bitwise equal to stock aiter's.
  - The test also fails if the sort-time quant was skipped; restoring the old `% 2048` gate makes it fail.
  - The full MoE output is checked within 2% of its max only, because stage 2 accumulates top-k rows with bf16 atomics.

End to end, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash TP4 with DSpark and real acceptance, together with #43821, on main 166b4ae7fc (unit tests re-run after rebasing on f006fb49ec): correct answers (391 / Paris / 42), 0 NaN / error lines, real accept length 2.54 against about 2.55 on main.
