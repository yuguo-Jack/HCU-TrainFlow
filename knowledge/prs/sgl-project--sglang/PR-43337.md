# [diffusion] perf: run MiniMax-H3's QK-norm + RoPE two rows per warp, bit-identical

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43337) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

MiniMax-H3 normalizes and rotates q/k with `fused_qknorm_rope_warp`. With head_dim 128 the kernel gives each warp one (token, head) row, so a lane moves only 8 bytes per row. The kernel is issue-bound, not bandwidth-bound: ncu shows 0.69 IPC per scheduler with 48% of stalls on L1TEX, and it reaches 2.5 TB/s on H200. It runs on every block: in place on the sequential Ulysses path, and once per head group and destination on the pipelined path (#43164).

## Modifications

- `fused_qknorm_rope_half_warp`: the same kernel with two rows per warp. Each row gets 16 lanes, and each lane moves 16 B of q/k and 16 B of the cos/sin cache.
- **Bit-identical by construction.** Each lane covers two lanes of the warp kernel. It keeps their two partial sums, accumulated in the same order, and replays that kernel's xor butterfly:
  - offsets 16..2 become 8..1, applied to each partial;
  - the final xor-1 step becomes `part0 + part1`.

  The per-element norm and RoPE expressions are unchanged.
- It is chosen at compile time for head_dim 128, NeoX, the norm rounded before RoPE, a half-width cache, and CUDA. Every other instantiation keeps the warp kernel.
- `_jit_qknorm_rope_module(half_warp=False)` keeps the warp kernel for the new test.

## Accuracy Tests

- `test_qknorm_rope_out_of_place.py::test_two_rows_per_warp_match_one_row_per_warp` (new) compares the two kernels' bytes. It covers:
  - in-place and out-of-place;
  - BF16 and FP16 activations, with BF16, FP16 and FP32 caches;
  - rope_dim 32, 96 and 128;
  - int32 and int64 positions;
  - an odd row count, so the last warp gets one row.

  The file is now registered on `1-gpu-large` as well as `4-gpu-b200`, so both Hopper and Blackwell check the claim.
- `test_rope.py` passes all 1264 cases on H200, including the `torch.equal` checks against the split QK-norm + `sgl_kernel` RoPE baseline at H3's shape.
- Old and new outputs are byte-identical at H3's per-rank shapes (24826 / 18871 / 9436 tokens, in place and per exchange block).
- End to end on 4×H200, the MP4 md5 is unchanged in every arm: Ref2VA at Ulysses 2 (four runs), and T2VA at Ulysses 2 and 4.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Per call on H200 (the same GPU for every column), 56 + 56 heads in place, or one exchange block on the pipelined path:

| tokens | path | before | after |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 24826 | in place, 56 + 56 heads | 567.5 µs (2.5 TB/s) | 430.5 µs (3.3 TB/s), −24% |
| 24826 | exchange block, 4 + 4 heads | 51.9 µs | 40.5 µs, −22% |
| 18871 | in place | 430.0 µs | 329.1 µs, −23% |
| 18871 | exchange block, 4 + 4 heads | 40.5 µs | 32.5 µs, −20% |
| 9436 | in place | 218.6 µs | 170.0 µs, −22% |
| 9436 | exchange block, 2 + 2 heads | 15.0 µs | 13.0 µs, −13% |

Two rows per warp with scalar cos/sin loads already gave 440.6 / 45.4 µs at 24826 tokens. The 16 B cache loads add the rest.

End to end, client latency, 4×H200, 50 steps, default quality. These runs sit on top of #43164 and #43327, and the arms differ only by this PR:

| Case | before | after | e2e | denoise stage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Ref2VA 5 s, Ulysses 2 (ABBA) | 218.71 / 218.97 s | 218.47 / 218.70 s | −0.12% | 212.49 → 212.24 s |
| T2VA 5 s, Ulysses 2 | 140.76 s | 140.30 s | −0.33% | 137.01 → 136.55 s |
| T2VA 5 s, Ulysses 4 | 71.83 s | 71.68 s | −0.20% | 70.03 → 69.86 s |

On the pipelined path the kernel runs once per head group and destination, interleaved with attention. The H200s sit at their 700 W cap during denoise, so part of the freed time comes back as a lower clock.
