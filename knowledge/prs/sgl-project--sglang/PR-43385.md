# [diffusion] fix: keep a LoRA unmerged under merge_mode auto when merging would round its update away

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43385) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

`--lora-merge-mode auto`, the default, merges an adapter into regular BF16 weights. Distilled adapters carry updates far smaller than a BF16 ulp of the weights they modify, so the merge rounds most of the update away. The MiniMax-H3 Turbo LoRA (`larryvrh/MiniMax-H3-Turbo-Lora`) is one: a merge drops 77–97% of the update in typical layers, and 75% over the whole transformer.

Nothing fails or warns when this happens. The output just comes out softer than the adapter's reference, which applies the LoRA at runtime for exactly this reason. So the default silently degrades these adapters.

## Modifications

- `BaseLayerWithLoRA.merge_rounding_norms()` estimates, over an even sample of rows, how much of the active update a merge into the weight dtype would round away. It uses the same slicing and scaling as the merge, and writes nothing.
- Under `auto`, `set_lora` with one adapter now does the following:
  - attaches the adapter unmerged and measures the share of the update a merge would lose;
  - if that share is above 50% (`AUTO_MERGE_MAX_ROUNDING_LOSS`), i.e. the merge would destroy most of the update, keeps the adapter dynamic and logs why;
  - otherwise merges as before.
- The result is cached per (module, paths, strengths, alphas), so repeated `set_lora` calls do not measure again.
- **Why 50%.** The threshold has to separate adapters a merge destroys from adapters it only trims. CI's style LoRAs lose 14% (`Cseti/Wan-LoRA-Arcane-Jinx-v1`) and 31% (`reverentelusarca/elusarca-anime-style-lora-z-image-turbo`) in a merge. Earlier cuts at 10% and 30% made them dynamic, and the dynamic path then cost Wan2.1 1.3B +52% and Z-Image +44% per step, failing both cases' latency checks. At 50% they merge exactly as before, while the distilled H3 Turbo adapter (75%) stays dynamic.
- Explicit `merge` and `dynamic` are unchanged, and so are FSDP-sharded and quantized weights, which `auto` already keeps dynamic.
- The `--lora-merge-mode` help and the MiniMax-H3 cookbook describe the new rule.

## Accuracy Tests

- `test_lora_auto_merge_rounding.py` (new, CPU) checks:
  - the estimate against the rounding of an actual merge;
  - that an update below a BF16 ulp is mostly lost and one above it survives;
  - that FP32 weights report nothing;
  - that the pipeline decision measures once and follows the threshold.

  The existing LoRA unit tests still pass (27 passed).
- End to end on 2×H200, MiniMax-H3 T2VA at Ulysses 2 with the Larry Turbo v4 adapter, `num_inference_steps: 9`, same seed:

  | `--lora-merge-mode` | log | MP4 md5 | latency |
  |---|---|---|---:|
  | `auto` (this PR) | "stays unmerged on transformer: merging it into the base weights would round away 75% of its update" | `95a418705f7b` | 27.60 s |
  | `dynamic` | — | `95a418705f7b` | 27.59 s |
  | `merge` | — | `342c3151970f` | 25.78 s |

  `auto` now gives the dynamic output byte for byte. Side by side, the merged video is visibly softer: the cats blur and the scene loses detail that the dynamic run keeps.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Keeping the Turbo LoRA dynamic costs 1.8 s on the 9-point schedule (27.6 against 25.8 s), which is the price of getting the adapter's full effect. A faster dynamic path, for example adding the LoRA delta inside the second GEMM, can follow separately. Adapters whose updates survive a merge, which covers the usual style LoRAs, still merge as before.
