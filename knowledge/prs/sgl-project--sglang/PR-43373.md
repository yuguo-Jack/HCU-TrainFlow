# [diffusion] perf: pipeline MiniMax-H3's Ulysses exchange against cuDNN SDPA on Blackwell

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43373) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

Stacked on #43361 (#43164 is merged). Merge #43361 first; this PR's own change is the cuDNN gate in `minimax_h3.py` and one cookbook line.

## Motivation

On Blackwell, MiniMax-H3's dense attention runs on cuDNN SDPA through `DynamicCudnnSDPAImpl`. The pipelined Ulysses exchange from #43164 only accepted the FlashAttention backend, so B200/B300 kept the sequential exchange and got none of it.

## Modifications

- The pipeline also accepts `AttentionBackendEnum.DYNAMIC_CUDNN_SDPA`. Attention still runs through the backend's `forward_varlen`, one head group at a time.
- The skip-softmax check only exists on the FlashAttention impl, so it is now optional.
- Cookbook: the configuration note says the pipeline runs against FlashAttention on Hopper and cuDNN SDPA on Blackwell.

## Accuracy Tests

- **Per head group, cuDNN SDPA gives the same bytes as those heads inside the full call.** Checked with `torch.equal` on 4×B300 at H3's per-rank attention shapes:
  - 49652 tokens × 28 heads in groups of 4 (Ref2VA, Ulysses 2);
  - 37742 tokens × 28 heads in groups of 4 (T2VA, Ulysses 2);
  - 37744 tokens × 14 heads in groups of 2 (T2VA, Ulysses 4).

  It holds on both torch 2.14.1 with cuDNN 9.24 and torch 2.13 with cuDNN 9.20.
- End to end on 4×B300, the MP4 md5 is the same for the sequential exchange and for 7, 4 and 2 groups, in every cell.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Client end-to-end latency, 4×B300, 50 steps, default quality, one run per arm. The base is this stack (#43164 + #43361) with #43327 and #43337, and `SGLANG_DIFFUSION_ULYSSES_PIPELINE_GROUPS` selects the sequential exchange (0) or a group count:

| Case | sequential | 7 groups (default) | 4 groups | 2 groups |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| T2VA 5 s, Ulysses 2 | 65.83 s | 63.24 s (−3.9%) | 63.40 s (−3.7%) | 63.48 s (−3.6%) |
| T2VA 5 s, Ulysses 4 | 34.20 s | 32.75 s (−4.3%) | — | 32.90 s (−3.8%) |
| Ref2VA 5 s, Ulysses 2 | 102.68 s | 99.24 s (−3.4%) | 98.70 s (−3.9%) | 98.89 s (−3.7%) |

- Blackwell gains more than Hopper (2–3%) because its exchange is a larger share of a faster step.
- Splitting heads costs cuDNN some efficiency, which FlashAttention 3 does not pay. On B300, 7 group calls over the Ref2VA shape take 3.5% longer than one call over all heads, and over the Ulysses 4 shape (2 heads per call) 5.4% longer. That is why 4 groups edge out 7 for Ref2VA in this single-run pass.
- A paired ABBA of 7 against 4 groups on B300 puts 4 ahead by only 0.1–0.2%, within run-to-run noise: Ref2VA 98.70 → 98.51 s, T2VA 63.74 → 63.68 s. So the automatic count stays at 7 for both backends.
