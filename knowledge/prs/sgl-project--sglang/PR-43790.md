# [DSpark] Share draft params, logits dtype, confidence and the accept tail between graph and eager

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43790) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

- The graph tail sizes its step-logits buffer on its first (warmup) call from the dtype the base logits actually have, instead of guessing it from the lm_head weight.
- One `resolve_draft_params` resolves temperatures, the greedy mask and step temperatures for both the graph-tail staging (into its static buffers through `out=`) and the eager draft.
- Confidence is computed through one model method, `compute_confidence` (on `DSparkConfidenceHead.predict`), and the proposal returns `confidence` and `confidence_raw` on both paths. The raw-logit stash on the head and the planner's copy of the confidence code are removed.
- One `build_accept_outs` runs the accept tail (simulated length, simulated bonus, the three TP syncs, finalize, out tokens) for both the in-graph verify epilogue and the eager accept; the in-graph greedy accept goes through `AcceptGreedy` like the eager one.
- Tests: confidence `predict`; accept tail parity between the graph and eager paths, ragged and simulated cases.

Behavior changes:
- STS collection and the confidence probe now also cover steps whose proposal ran in the draft graph (they were skipped).
- The dense confidence invariant is reported as `dspark.model.confidence` (was `dspark.planner.confidence`).
- A dense draft whose lm_head is quantized with a float-typed weight (e.g. ModelOpt FP8) now builds its graph tail; it used to fail when sizing the step-logits buffer.

## Verification

- Old vs new draft outputs (tokens, step logits, greedy mask, temperatures, confidence, raw confidence, noise offset) bit-identical over 96 cases, CPU and CUDA (vanilla, gated, RNN markov heads; with and without a confidence head; `sample_from_anchor`; muP; greedy and sampling).
- In-graph and eager accept: kernel order, TP sync sites and values, and outputs byte-identical to main over 20 cases on GB300.
- GB300: the DSpark draft, markov sample, kernel parity, block verification, stacked context KV parity, accept tail, greedy-draft accept and simulated bonus tests pass (49 passed).
