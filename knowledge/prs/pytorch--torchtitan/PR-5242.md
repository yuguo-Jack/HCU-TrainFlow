# [rl] DAPO-Math grader: read thin-space digit groups, compare words as strings (1/2)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan/pull/5242) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

**Stacked on** the two approved DAPO-Math grader PRs ("score in grader processes with a hard timeout" and "box the gold answer before parsing"), carried on the base branch `felipemello1/dapo-math/trunk`. Once they merge, this stack retargets to main with no other change.

**TLDR:** The DAPO-Math grader scores correct answers like `10\,002` and `Indonesian` (gold `indonesian`) 0. It also accepts any reordering of a word gold (`DAECB` for `EDACB`), which no run has hit yet. This PR fixes both. On 36,784 real responses, 14 scores change, all wrong 0s that become 1.

**Problem**
1. **Thin spaces.** Models often group digits with a LaTeX thin space: `10\,002`.
2. **Math-Verify adds the groups.** It reads `10\,002` like the mixed number `2 \frac{1}{2}`: 10 + 002 = 12, so `12` scores 1 and the right answer 0.
3. **Words.** Math-Verify reads a bare word as a product of letters: `tea` is t*e*a, so `eat` scores 1.
4. **Orders.** INTELLECT-3-RL gold `EDACB` (a finishing order) accepts `DAECB`, a wrong order printed in the problem, written with or without spaces.
5. **Case.** `I` reads as the imaginary unit and `e` as Euler's number, so `Indonesian` does not match `indonesian`.
6. **Cost.** In a Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-Base run on INTELLECT-3-RL math, 12 of the 1,154 reward-0 completions were correct: 11 thin-space answers and 1 `Indonesian`. No reordered word scored 1 there, so the anagram fix is prevention.

**Solution**
1. `_THIN_SPACE_IN_NUMBER` drops a `\,` between a digit and exactly 3 digits, in the answer and the gold, before parsing.
2. If the boxed answer and the gold are each one word of 3+ letters, `score_math_response` compares them as lowercase strings and skips Math-Verify. Spacing between the answer's letters (`D A E C B`, `D\,A\,E\,C\,B`) is dropped first. An answer whose letters reorder a word gold scores 0 whatever wraps them (`(DAECB)`, `\text{DAECB}`, `D\cdot A\cdot E\cdot C\cdot B`), since Math-Verify would read the gold as a product.
3. Unchanged: one- and two-letter answers (`ab`, a segment `CD`), `\text{}` words (Math-Verify already reads `\text{Indonesian}` as one lowercase symbol), and everything else.

**Results**
```text
answers scored 1, old -> new grader (math-verify 0.9.0, 10 s grader timeout)
Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-Base run, INTELLECT-3-RL    4,579 rollouts    3,425 -> 3,437   +11 thin space, +1 Indonesian
MathArena AIME/HMMT/Apex outputs           19,974 answers   14,885 -> 14,887   333\,400, 2\,077\,992\,225 (MathArena: correct)
DAPO-Math runs (4B and 30B)                12,231 rollouts   4,507 -> 4,507
each gold graded against itself             9,789 golds      unchanged
```
No score goes 1 -> 0, and the old grader reproduces the logged reward on all 4,579 run rollouts.

On the 201 letter-only golds, a correctly ordered answer never goes 1 -> 0, in any case, spaced or not. Reordered letters of a 3+ letter gold go 1 -> 0: 220 written together, 113 spaced. So `CBA` for the triangle gold `ABC`, and a multi-select `ADC` for `ACD`, now score 0.

You may ask:
- Why not a Math-Verify option? `parse` already turns on every `NormalizationConfig` flag; `10\,002` still reads as 12 and `eat` as t*e*a.
- Why 3 letters? 29 of INTELLECT-3-RL's 31 two-letter golds are segments or products (`AB`, `pq`), where `ba` should equal `ab`. Its kept 3+ letter golds are words, names, orders, and one triangle name (`ABC`).
- Why only `\,`? In 35,826 boxed real answers, `\,` separates digit groups 38 times and `{,}` (already parsed) 10 times. `\;` and `\ ` never do; a plain space does once, in a wrong answer.
- Why the gold too? No gold has a thin space today; this keeps a future one from reading as 12.

NOTE: not run: GPU or end-to-end RL; the change is grader-only.

**Follow up**
