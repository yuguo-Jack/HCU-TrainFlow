# Rename activation checkpointing policies to activation remat configs

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan/pull/5102) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

Stack from [ghstack](https://github.com/ezyang/ghstack/tree/0.18.0) (oldest at bottom):
* __->__ #5102
* #5101

The policies are plain configs applied with one function, since the policy
objects held nothing beyond their config:

* `ActivationRematConfig` (formerly `RegionAC.Config`) saves regions matching
  `save_regions` except those matching `recompute_regions`.
* `FullActivationRematConfig` (formerly `FullAC.Config`) and
  `SelectiveActivationRematConfig` (formerly `SelectiveAC.Config`, the trainer
  default) are fixed presets of it.
* `apply_activation_remat(model, config)` replaces
  `config.build(dump_folder=...).apply(model)`.

No behavior change; call sites, recipes, tests and docs are renamed.
