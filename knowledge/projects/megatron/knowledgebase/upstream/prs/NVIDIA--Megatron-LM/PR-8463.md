# What does this PR do?

Fixes two MoE bugs in the sequence-chunked MLP path (`mlp_chunks_for_training` / `mlp_chunks_for_prefill`) in `TransformerLayer._run_mlp`, which `TransformerLayer` and `HyperConnectionTransformerLayer` share:

- **EP deadlock from unequal MoE call counts.** `num_chunks` was capped at the local sequence length, and `torch.chunk` can return fewer chunks than requested (10 tokens into 8 gives 5 chunks of 2). Every MoE call runs EP all-to-alls, so a rank with a shorter local sequence (e.g. a 1-token padding micro-batch used to even out micro-batch counts across DP ranks) made fewer calls than its EP peers and the group hung. MoE layers now always make exactly `num_chunks` calls (`tensor_split`), padding a shorter sequence with masked dummy tokens whose outputs are dropped. A mask is built when the caller passed none, so the router leaves the dummies out of its aux-loss, z-loss and expert-bias counts. Dense MLPs keep the old cap.
- **Routing replay (`moe_enable_routing_replay`) with chunking.** `RouterReplay` holds one top-k tensor per micro-batch (`[s * b, topk]`), and `get_replay_topk` gathers the call's scores (`[chunk_tokens, num_experts]`) with it, so replay failed on the first chunk ("Size does not match at dimension 0"). Recompute popped one micro-batch entry per chunk, and recording kept only the last chunk's indices. The new `ChunkedRouterReplay` gives each chunk its slice of the micro-batch's indices, passed to the chunk's call as `MoELayer.forward(replay_topk_idx=...)`, and records the concatenated indices (static buffers included) after the last chunk. As an argument, the slice is also what a recompute of that call replays: with selective `"moe"` recompute, backward reruns each chunk's checkpointed MoE call on its own, not the chunk loop, in whatever order autograd picks. Full-layer recompute reruns the loop, which splits the micro-batch's `replay_backward_list` entry again.
- **Quantile balancing with a short local sequence** now raises `NotImplementedError` before any MoE call: the dummy tokens need a padding mask, which quantile balancing does not support yet (base ran this case with fewer chunks). Whether to make quantile balancing honor the mask instead is left to its maintainers.

The chunk loop moves into `TransformerLayer._run_mlp_in_chunks`; the non-chunked and recompute paths are untouched.
