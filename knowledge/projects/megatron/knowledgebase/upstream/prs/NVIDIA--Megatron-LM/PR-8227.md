# [DSv4.1] V4.1 training builder and packed pipeline plans

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8227) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

Select the V4.1 builder from training arguments, prepare typed PP plans from ContextParallelBatch, preserve logical packed lengths and account for actual indexer producers. Include a runnable smoke recipe and explicit unsupported-mode validation.

Depends on #8226 and its prerequisites. Training-specific change: https://github.com/FDecaYed/Megatron-LM/commit/3d2dddc44b07789d3ad6ae286d705d630206999d. The final merge target is main after the dependencies land.

Validation on H100: CLI/config/builder selection, SBHD and packed THD, CP2, PP2/VPP2 with P2P overlap, full recompute with TE fused wgrad, 16K single-document CP2 sparse-loss training, and 8-GPU DP2/EP2/PP2/CP2 checkpoint save/resume all passed. Final latest-main regression: 27 tests passed plus combined 8-GPU train/eval. Small proxy models, not released-model convergence or production performance.

Refreshed stack validation includes 16K single-document CP2/r1 sparse-loss training and evaluation. In a controlled BF16 4K small-model H100 recipe, step time improved from about 280 ms to 229–230 ms (source implementation: 265–267 ms). This is a proxy measurement, not released-model or MXFP8/SM100 qualification.
