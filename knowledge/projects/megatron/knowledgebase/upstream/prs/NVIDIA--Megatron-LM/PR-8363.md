# What does this PR do?

Fix plain-text SFT tokenizer round trips when the underlying Hugging Face tokenizer inserts BOS through its post-processor. Plain strings default to `add_special_tokens=False`, and `ids_to_text` accepts `remove_special_tokens` so `detokenize(..., skip_special_tokens=True)` takes effect. Explicit special-token insertion and conversation tokenization retain their behavior.

The regression fixture builds a small local tokenizer whose post-processor inserts BOS even though `tokenizer_config.json` sets `add_bos_token=false`. It covers plain-text round trips, explicit BOS insertion, both decode settings, and conversation tokenization without downloading model assets.
