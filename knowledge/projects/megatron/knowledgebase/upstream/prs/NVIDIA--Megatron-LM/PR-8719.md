# Add opt-in Liger RMSNorm through ops/norm

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8719) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

- [ ] I, the PR author, have personally reviewed every line of this PR.

## What does this PR do?

Add opt-in Liger RMSNorm through the new `ops/norm` layout.

Stacked on #8656; this is the RMSNorm-only successor to the corresponding part of #5438. It does not depend on the separate cross-entropy contribution.

`--transformer-impl local --normalization RMSNorm --use-liger` selects the adapter through `LocalSpecProvider`. Standard GPT block construction includes the decoder final norm, MTP norms and enabled Q/K norms. Other provider slots and cross entropy stay unchanged. Missing selected dependencies fail through `ops._backends.require`; unselected paths do not import Liger.

The adapter retains checkpoint keys, zero-centered gamma and sequence-parallel gradient marking. Kitchen, TE/inference norm providers and unsupported custom model builders are not silently replaced.

CI: the H100 ops, models and determinism-kernel buckets install `liger-kernel==0.8.5` with `--no-deps` in latest/dev runs on dev images. The shared export check fails before tests on a broken installation. The image's `IMAGE_TYPE` marker excludes LTS even when GitHub labels its recipe environment `dev`; runtime installation remains optional.

## Performance (Liger v0.8.5)

Measured on H100 80GB with **exact release `v0.8.5` (`1ab779f`)**, PyTorch 2.13.0.2+cu130, Triton 3.7.1 and TE 2.20.2.0+cu130. Times are median synchronized wall latency; lower is better.

**RMSNorm, forward + backward:** BF16, non-unit weights, nonconstant upstream gradients. 10 warmups, 3 x 50 samples. Single GPU with reused inputs (hot-cache measurement).

| Tokens | Hidden | Torch (ms) | Liger (ms) | Speedup vs Torch | TE (ms) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 128 | 4096 | 0.133 | 0.353 | 0.38x | 0.312 |
| 2048 | 4096 | 0.131 | 0.351 | 0.37x | 0.307 |
| 8192 | 4096 | 0.255 | 0.365 | 0.70x | 0.313 |
| 8192 | 8192 | 0.503 | 0.426 | 1.18x | 0.413 |

Liger beats Torch at the largest listed shape but regresses at the smaller shapes; TE is faster at every listed shape. This is not a generally faster default.

**Full-model ablation:** 32-layer Llama2-7B shape, H4096, FFN11008, vocab32K, BF16, batch1, TP8; only this PR's operation changes. Three independent process launches/backend, 5 warmups +20 steps/sequence, forward + backward without optimizer or CUDA graphs. Table latencies are medians of the three process medians; the last column shows paired trial ranges (positive = slower).

| Sequence | Native model (ms) | Liger-only model (ms) | Paired latency change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 512 | 83.544 | 100.464 | +14.58% to +21.03% |
| 2048 | 84.253 | 99.744 | +17.01% to +21.75% |
| 4096 | 155.667 | 155.245 | -0.27% to -0.25% |

Short/medium-sequence eager training regresses; the long-sequence difference is small.

**Numerics:** the separate full-gradient diagnostic has BF16 relative-L2 differences of 2.92-5.37% versus native and approximately 1.31e-05 in FP32. These are not convergence-equivalence claims.
