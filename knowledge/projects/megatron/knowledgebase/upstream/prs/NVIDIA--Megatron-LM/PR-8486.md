[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8486) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

Record module-and-phase occurrences in the first training iteration and replay the captured order for later prefetch. Enable the training integration with --megatron-fsdp-record-iteration; forward-only evaluation gathers on demand without consuming the saved order. Combined 1F1B hooks supply their compute phase.

Depends on #8485 and must merge after that prerequisite. Until its upstream mirror is available, this draft targets main and includes the prerequisite changes; review the incremental diff linked below. The module-call schedule must remain fixed, and the flag requires MFSDP v2 with CUDA graphs disabled.
