# Use the model's process groups in post-training and Flextron code

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8760) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

Depends on #6303 (which contains #6293), #8017, #8049 and #8742. Please review the diff from their merge: [scoped diff](https://github.com/Connor-XY/Megatron-LM/compare/334a0a7c70...yx/pstate-f2-post-training-elastification).
- The new fallbacks use #6293's `warn_global_process_group_fallback` and #8049's `resolve_process_groups`, `require_process_groups` and `ProcessGroupFallbackWarning`.
- This PR rewrites the `model_provider` fallback that #6303 adds to `pretrain_hybrid_flex.py`.
- The new Flextron `get_batch` calls the `is_first_or_last_pipeline_stage(vp_stage, pp_group=..., vp_size=...)` signature from #8742.
- `test_hybrid_flex_router.py` imports #8017's `forbid_global_process_groups` for the router weight-sync tests.
- #8742 is based on main. Its merge here keeps its side of the `get_batch` lines in `pretrain_gpt.py` and `pretrain_hybrid.py` and of the `pretrain_vlm.py` imports, against #6293's. The merge commit lists the resolutions.
- The process-group allowlist records 172 reads on the base, and this branch leaves it unchanged.

#8754 adds `vp_size=` to the same `mtp_on_this_rank` call in `pretrain_hybrid_flex.py`. Whichever lands second keeps both lines.

Land this with or right after #8759. Training passes the run's collection to dataset providers that accept one only after #8759, so until then a stock Flextron run warns once, from `is_dataset_built_on_rank`, which its dataset provider passes to the dataset builder.

**Why.** `megatron/post_training` and `megatron/elastification` read the global `parallel_state` grid, although their callers already hold the model's `ProcessGroupCollection`:
- the ModelOpt loss sliced the sequence-parallel loss mask by the global TP rank and size, and all-reduced split distillation losses over the global TP group;
- `print_distributed_quant_summary` chose the ranks that print by the global DP, TP, EP and PP ranks;
- the Flextron loss reduced over the global TP group and gathered the per-budget report over the global DP group;
- the Mamba and grouped-MLP elasticity hooks sliced their masks by the global TP and expert-TP ranks;
- the Flextron router was named by the global pipeline rank, seeded its Gumbel noise and counted accumulation steps by the global DP rank and size, and built its linear layers on the global TP group;
- `pretrain_hybrid_flex.py` read, broadcast and sharded batches, drew budgets and chose the dataset-building ranks on the global grid.

A model built on other groups reduced its losses, sliced its masks and seeded its router over the wrong ranks.

**What.** One commit per package.
- Post-training:
  - `loss_func` passes the model's `tp` group to `_mask_loss` through a new `tp_group` argument;
  - `loss_func(model=None)` still works. `pretrain_gpt.loss_func` passes it when ModelOpt is on and the caller, such as `pretrain_vlm.py`, binds no model. The plain loss needs no group;
  - `print_distributed_quant_summary` reads the model's `dp_cp_gtp_remat`, `tp`, `ep` and `pp` groups.
- Flextron:
  - the loss uses the model's `tp` and `dp_gtp_remat` groups;
  - the Mamba hook uses the mixer's `tp_group`, and the grouped-MLP hook the module's expert-TP group;
  - `FlextronModelManager` builds the router on the model's collection and names it by the rank in its `pp` group. `FlextronRouter` takes a `pg_collection` for its seeds, accumulation steps, linear layers and sharded state dict;
  - the injected forward checks the last stage of the model's `pp` group;
  - `get_batch`, `get_grad_acc_based_random_choice`, `is_dataset_built_on_rank` and `train_valid_test_datasets_provider` take a `pg_collection`. `forward_step` passes the model's, and `get_batch` reads `tp`, `pp`, `cp` and `dynamic_cp` as `pretrain_hybrid.py` does;
  - `train_valid_test_datasets_provider` delegates to main's shared `build_gpt_datasets` and passes it `is_dataset_built_on_rank` bound to the collection. Without a collection, `is_dataset_built_on_rank` warns and falls back to the global groups when the dataset builder calls it.

A third commit makes `FlextronRouter._sync_router_weights` broadcast each tensor-parallel shard over the model's own groups. Before, it broadcast every router weight from global rank 0 over WORLD.
- The router's linear layers are sharded over `pg_collection.tp`. At TP 2, both TP ranks ended up with TP rank 0's shard. The two halves of each hidden layer got identical gradients, so they never diverged, and the router trained with half its hidden width. On a job where the model spans fewer ranks than WORLD, the broadcast mixed the routers of different models, or hung when the other ranks built no router. Strict mode cannot catch this, because no fallback is involved.
- The sync now broadcasts over `pg_collection.pp` from its first rank, then over `pg_collection.dp_cp` from its first rank. Every pipeline stage builds a router, and finalize all-reduces its gradients over PP, so the PP peers have to start equal. `dp_cp` is the replica group of dense weights, the same group DDP's `broadcast_params()` uses.
- It is not `dp_cp_gtp_remat`. With GTP on, the router's TE and local linear layers are also sharded over `gtp_remat` and record `dp_cp` as their replica group. A broadcast over `dp_cp_gtp_remat` would overwrite distinct GTP shards.
- Every rank of the model builds the same routers, one per model chunk, from the same config and full layer pattern. So all members of a rank's `pp` group and `dp_cp` group issue the same broadcasts in the same order, and no rank outside the model issues them. A group of size 1, or an axis set to `None`, is skipped by all its members, so `None` never becomes WORLD.
- `FlextronRouter` now requires `pp` and `dp_cp` in its collection, next to `tp` and `dp_gtp_remat`.

After this PR, each package has one direct `parallel_state` accessor call left: the warned TP fallback in its `_mask_loss`. The other fallbacks warn and then go through `resolve_process_groups` or `use_mpu_process_groups()`.

**Tests.**
- New `tests/unit_tests/post_training/test_post_training_process_groups.py`: the split and sequence-parallel distillation losses reduce over the given group, `loss_func` reduces over the model's group, the plain loss needs no group, and the quantization summary picks its printing ranks from the model's groups. Omitted groups warn and use the global ones.
- New `tests/unit_tests/elastification/test_elastification_process_groups.py`: the Flextron loss and report, the router, the injected forward, `get_batch`, the budget draw, the dataset predicate and provider, `forward_step`, and the Mamba and grouped-MLP masks use the given groups. Collections without a needed group are rejected, and omitted groups warn.
- The CPU tests replace the global accessors with stubs that fail. The GPU tests build strided TP and DP groups unlike the global ones.
- `test_hybrid_flex_router.py` and `test_flextron_grouped_mlp_elasticity_manager.py` pass their groups explicitly.
- `test_dataset_providers.py` accepts the hybrid-flex provider's trailing `pg_collection` parameter in main's signature check.
- Router sync, in `test_hybrid_flex_router.py`:
  - `test_router_weights_are_synced_per_tensor_parallel_shard[1|2]` fills every weight with its global rank, then runs the sync at TP2 x DP2 and TP2 x PP2. Each TP shard must come from the lowest of its PP x DP-CP replicas, and the 2 TP ranks must keep different shards.
  - `test_router_weights_sync_stays_inside_the_model_groups` makes each rank pair one model with 2 data-parallel replicas, unlike the global grid, and runs the sync with every global accessor raising. Ranks 2 and 3 must keep rank 2's weights.
  - `test_router_rejects_a_collection_without_its_sync_groups[pp|dp_cp]` checks the new requirement.
  - The sync tests use the local linear layers, so they build without Transformer Engine. The TE path runs the same sync code but has no test of its own.

Its golden values were recorded with the duplicated router shards, so its loss is expected to drift from them. It runs only in the nightly scope, so it needs a manual run with this PR, and a golden-value refresh if it drifts.

Before the sync, the branch ran its four elastification and post-training test files on 4 CPU ranks with Gloo: 46 passed, 15 skipped and 11 failed.

- The 11 failures are in the two existing files and come from that environment. 4 need CUDA. The 7 `TestFlextronRouter` tests fail on the environment's mocks (`StopIteration` from a mocked layer builder, and `str is not a Module subclass`).
- With the router-sync change reverted, all 5 new tests fail on ranks 2 and 3. On ranks 0 and 1, 4 fail. The sub-grid test passes there because the old source, global rank 0, happens to be the right one for that pair.
- On 2 ranks, the 3 sync tests skip and the 2 rejection tests pass.

The process-group checker reports 172 grandfathered reads on the base and on this branch.

**Compatibility.** On the standard grid every group is the same object as before, so losses, masks, seeds, batches and checkpoints do not change.
- At TP 1 on the global grid, the router gets the same weights as before, since every rank already held rank 0's values. At TP > 1, each TP rank now keeps its own initialized shard, so Flextron router numerics change from the first step. The nightly Flextron goldens likely need a refresh.
- The router syncs over `dp_cp` rather than `dp_cp_gtp_remat`, for the reason given above. Do reviewers agree with that choice?
- New `pg_collection` and `tp_group` arguments default to `None`. That keeps the global groups and emits a `ProcessGroupFallbackWarning` (deprecated in 0.21, removed in 0.23). `pretrain_hybrid_flex.model_provider`, which already fell back silently, now warns too.
- `print_distributed_quant_summary` runs whenever a quantized checkpoint is loaded. For a model without a collection that sets its groups, such as `MimoModel`, it warns and prints by the global ranks instead of failing.
- These Flextron-only paths raise `ValueError` where main used the global groups or passed `None`, which `torch.distributed` treats as WORLD:
  - the KD report and `FlextronRouter`, for a collection without `dp_gtp_remat` or another group they need. `FlextronRouter` now also needs `pp` and `dp_cp`;
  - `get_batch` and `is_dataset_built_on_rank`, for `None` groups.

  `get_model()` always builds a full collection, so stock runs are not affected.
- Known gaps, all outside stock runs:
  - an explicit `dp_gtp_remat=None` counts as one rank in the router and the budget draw, while the KD report raises for it;
  - an explicit `dp_cp_gtp_remat=None` makes every data-parallel replica print the quantization summary;
  - a model without a `pg_collection` attribute fails in the Flextron loss, the router setup and the injected forward. Every in-repo model on these paths is a `GPTModel` or `HybridModel`, which always has one.
- The `megatron/post_training` commit needs a review from the post-training code owners.
- Needs `determinism-exempt` only while the PRs below this one are unlanded. CI diffs against main, and the base carries registered kernel files. This PR's own diff passes the coverage check.
