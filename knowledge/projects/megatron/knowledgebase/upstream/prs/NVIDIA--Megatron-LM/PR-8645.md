# perf(moe): fused batch-invariant squared ReLU and MXFP8 quantization

## Original PR description

# What does this PR do?

Fuses the batch-invariant MoE expert activation with its MXFP8 quantization into one kernel. The data and scales it produces are bitwise those of the two kernels it replaces.

## Details

Under batch-invariant mode with MXFP8 experts, `mcore_fused_moe` ran two kernels between FC1 and FC2:
1. `squared_relu_with_probs`: square and routing-probability multiply in FP32, one BF16 rounding, as in training's fused weighted squared ReLU.
2. `mxfp8_quantize_rows` on the first `n_used` rows.

The non-batch-invariant path already fuses its activation with the quantization, but it applies no routing probability there.

`squared_relu_with_probs_quantize_mxfp8` runs the activation's statements for each live row, rounds to BF16 exactly where the unfused path stores its output, and runs `_mxfp8_quant_swizzle_row`'s statements on those values: group max, power-of-two scale, FP8 cast, and the swizzled scale store. Alignment-padding rows (permutation map -1) quantize a zero row, which matches the unfused path quantizing the zeros the activation writes there. Rows at or beyond `n_used` are not touched, and their scales stay zero. The clamped (`activation_func_tanh_clamp_scale`) variant is supported. `mcore_fused_moe` uses the kernel for squared ReLU when the experts are MXFP8 and `disable_fused_quant_kernels` is not set.

## Performance

GB200, Nemotron-3-Nano engine decode (MXFP8 experts, TP2 with SP, EP4, CUDA graphs), batch-invariant mode: each decode step is 0.07 to 0.13 ms faster at 1 to 32 requests and 1k to 8k context, against steps of 5.7 to 7.7 ms.

## Tests

- `tests/unit_tests/inference/test_moe_permute.py::test_batch_invariant_squared_relu_quantize_matches_unfused` compares fused and unfused output bitwise, both data and swizzled scales, over:
  - 1 to 300 rows, with `n_used` below the row count;
  - alignment-padding rows;
  - magnitudes over 2^-60 to 2^60 per 32-element group;
  - signed zeros;
  - with and without the tanh clamp.
  It also compares the fused output with quantizing training's `weighted_squared_relu` output.
- `tests/unit_tests/determinism/kernels/test_inference_kernels.py::test_batch_invariant_squared_relu_quantize_replays` replays the kernel bit-exactly and checks it against the two-kernel path at 16,384 rows.
- On GB200: `test_moe_permute.py`, `test_inference_kernels.py`, `test_manifest.py`, `test_vllm_fused_moe_batch_invariant.py` and `test_batch_invariant_live_rows.py`, 1,223 passed, 1 xpassed and 5 skipped (FlashInfer NVFP4 unavailable); `test_moe_dispatching_and_routing.py` on 4 GPUs, 60 passed and 9 skipped per rank; `tests/unit_tests/inference/test_hybrid_moe.py` on 4 GPUs, 130 passed and 1 skipped per rank.
- Inference engine (Nemotron-3-Nano, MXFP8, TP2/SP, EP4, batch-invariant mode): log-probabilities are bitwise those of the two-kernel path, 0 of 5,592 prompt, 0 of 2,048 generated and 0 of 3,820 rescored tokens differ.
- black, isort, pylint and ruff with the `tools/autoformat.sh` settings report no changes on the changed files.
