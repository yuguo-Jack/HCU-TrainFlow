# [SSM] Bump FLA to 0.6.0 so the recurrent KDA and GDN2 kernels stop reading past g

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8861) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

- [ ] I, the PR author, have personally reviewed every line of this PR.

# What does this PR do?

Bumps flash-linear-attention (FLA) from 0.5.1 to 0.6.0. In 0.5.1, `fused_recurrent_kda` and `fused_recurrent_gdn2` read past the end of the gate `g` when the head dim is not a power of two, and 0.6.0 fixes this. The bump also ports the GDP chunkwise context-parallel backend to the CP kernels FLA 0.6.0 renamed.

**Bug.** Both kernels tile the head dim K to `BK = next_power_of_2(K)`. They read each (token, head) row of the per-channel gate `g` with stride K and no mask (FLA 0.5.1 `fla/ops/kda/fused_recurrent.py:158` and `fla/ops/gdn2/fused_recurrent.py:177`); the `q`, `k` and `dt_bias` loads are masked.
- For K = 96, 160, 192, ..., every row reads `BK - K` lanes past its end. For the last row, those lanes lie past the end of `g`.
- Garbage there lands in padded state rows. A NaN pattern propagates (`0 * exp(NaN)`) and turns that head's whole output NaN. This holds with the gate formed in the kernel and with a precomputed log decay, which is how Megatron's GDN2 passes it.
- Megatron runs these kernels on packed single-token sequences: the context-free MTP draft step of #8710 calls `fused_recurrent_kda` for KDA and `fused_recurrent_gdn2` for GDN2. KDA and GDN2 accept such head dims (`validate_kda` only requires K > 0, and FLA's chunked kernels take K <= 256).
- fla-core 0.6.0 masks both loads (`mask=mask_k, other=0`, `kda/fused_recurrent.py:160` and `gdn2/fused_recurrent.py:180`). 0.5.2 does not.

**Fix.**
- `pyproject.toml`: `flash-linear-attention==0.6.0`. It pins `fla-core==0.6.0`. Its new `transformers>=5.6.2,<6` requirement is met by the lock's 5.19.0.
- API check. For every FLA symbol Megatron imports (GDN, GDN2, KDA, GDP, conv, l2norm, the CP context), I compared the 0.5.1 and 0.6.0 signatures. Changes are additive (new trailing keyword arguments with defaults), and Megatron passes everything by keyword. 0.6.0 moved `fla.modules.l2norm`, `fla.modules.fused_norm_gate` and `fla.modules.convolution` into new packages and keeps the old paths as aliases, so Megatron's imports still resolve. Two exceptions:
  - `chunk_local_cumsum` dropped `head_first`, which Megatron does not pass.
  - FLA 0.6.0 split `fla.ops.cp.chunk_delta_h` into `chunk_fwd` / `chunk_bwd` / `chunk_merge` and renamed the three CP Triton kernels that `megatron/core/ssm/context_parallel/gdp.py` launches directly. With the bump alone, the FLA GDP CP backend would fail to import (`HAVE_FLA_GDP_CP = False`), and every GDP chunkwise CP config would raise at construction.
- `gdp.py` now launches `chunk_cp_fwd_kernel_h`, `chunk_cp_bwd_kernel_dh` and `chunk_cp_fwd_bwd_kernel_merge`, with the arguments renamed:
  - `BK1` becomes `BK` and `BLOCK_SIZE` becomes `BV`.
  - In the merge kernel, `pre_or_post_num_ranks` becomes `num_ranks`, `INTRACARD_MODE` becomes `IS_INTRACARD` and `NUM_SEQ_ENTRIES` becomes `N`, and the new `h_seq_idx` is passed as `None`.
  - The kernels compute the same `[h_local | M]` summaries with the same grids.
  - Under the Triton interpreter (CPU), I ran the three launches with FLA 0.5.1 and the old code, and with FLA 0.6.0 and the port: BLH and varlen fragments, K = 64, 96 and 128, forward and backward merges. The outputs were bitwise identical. That holds only under the interpreter, which ignores `input_precision`; GPU numerics change, see below.
- The GDP chunkwise CP `ImportError` now names the requirement, flash-linear-attention >= 0.6.0. The port drops 0.5.1.

**Tests.** On H100 in the CI image (`mcore_ci_dev:main`, 2026-11-02), with this PR's `uv.lock` change applied in the container (flash-linear-attention and fla-core 0.6.0), the regression tests below pass (49 cases). They also pass under compute-sanitizer memcheck, with the caching allocator off, with no sanitizer errors. On `main` (FLA 0.5.1), 28 of the 49 fail. The tests that separate the bug are the over-read cases of `test_fla_fused_recurrent.py`: all 16 at K = 96 and 192 fail on `main`, and the 8 K = 64 cases pass. The 12 CP merge cases of `test_gdp_cp_kernels.py` also fail on `main`, but only by a small margin: relative errors of 1.25e-3 to 1.37e-3 against the 1e-3 tolerance, at K = 64 as well. That miss follows from 0.5.1 running the merge in TF32 (see below), so the merge test does not separate the bug from 0.6.0's numerics change. The CP summary cases and `test_interleave_last_update` pass on `main`. The neighbor tests `test_kda.py` and `test_gated_delta_net2.py` pass on 4 H100s (60 cases; 38 cases, 16 skipped). On GB200 in the CI image (`mcore_ci_dev:main`, aarch64, 2026-11-02), with the same change, `test_gated_delta_net.py::test_parallel_gated_delta_net_correctness` passes on 4 GPUs (40 cases). The regression tests did not run on GB200.
- `tests/unit_tests/ssm/test_fla_fused_recurrent.py` (new, 1 GPU): runs one step of `fused_recurrent_kda` (with and without in-kernel l2norm and `lower_bound`) and of `fused_recurrent_gdn2` (gate formed in the kernel, and a precomputed FP32 log decay as Megatron's GDN2 passes it) from a zero state, with `g` as a contiguous view at the start of a NaN-filled buffer, at K = 64, 96 and 192. Each output is checked against the gate-independent one-step reference: `beta * v * (q.k) / sqrt(K)` for KDA, `w * v * (q.k) / sqrt(K)` for GDN2. On CPU (`TRITON_INTERPRET=1`), FLA 0.5.1 fails all 16 non-power-of-two cases, with the last (token, head) row NaN, and passes the 8 K = 64 cases. FLA 0.6.0 passes all 24.
- `tests/unit_tests/determinism/kernels/test_gdp_cp_kernels.py` (new, 1 GPU, registered for `ssm_gdp_kernels` and `ssm_gated_delta_product`, also marked for GB200 CI). For the three CP launches `gdp.py` makes (BLH and varlen fragments, K = 64, 96 and 128):
  - Each launch replays bit-exactly under side-stream contention.
  - Each half of each summary is checked against FLA's chunk scans, which do not go through these launches. Forward: `h_local` is the final state of `chunk_gated_delta_rule_fwd_h` from a zero state, and `M @ h_in` its final state from a random `h_in` with zero values. Backward: `dh_local` is the initial-state gradient of `chunk_gated_delta_rule_bwd_dhu` with no final-state gradient, and `dM @ dh_out` its gradient from `dh_out` alone, with zero `do` and `dv`. The varlen checks use the fragments `gdp.py` summarizes: the pack's last sequence forward, its first backward.
  - The merge is checked against the same composition in FP64: ranks 0, 1, 2 forward, and 2, 1, 0 backward.
  - With FLA installed, a failed import of `gdp.py` is an error, not a skip, so an FLA that renames the CP kernels again cannot turn the file green. `test_gdp_chunkwise_context_parallel.py::test_interleave_last_update` now skips only without FLA, for the same reason.
  - On CPU (FP32, since the interpreter mishandles BF16 dots; FLA's chunk scans needed a one-line interpreter-only change to a loop index cast), all 24 cases pass, at relative errors near 1e-7. Deliberately mis-mapping `BV`/`BK`, the head counts, `MULTI_SEQS`, the backward fragment or the merge rank gives a relative error of 0.65 or more in at least one check. The tolerance is 2e-2 for the summaries: a torch model of the chunk scan's BF16 rounding puts it at 0.5 to 0.75% here. The merge tolerance is 1e-3. With FLA 0.5.1, every case errors at the `gdp.py` import.
- `tools/check_kernel_determinism_coverage.py --base-ref origin/main` passes, and so does `test_manifest.py`.

## What else changes with FLA 0.6.0 (not changed here)

**Backward kernel dispatch moves on GB200.** FLA routes two backward kernels to TileLang through its backend dispatch.
- FLA 0.5.1 enables both TileLang backends on every GPU whenever `tilelang` imports. `BaseBackend.default_enable` is `True`, and the "Hopper only" docstring of the common backend is not implemented. The backends serve the gated `chunk_bwd_dqkwg` when q/k and v have the same head count and `h` has q's dtype, and KDA's `chunk_kda_bwd_wy_dqkg_fused` when the head counts match. Megatron expands q/k to the value heads before calling FLA, so these checks pass in both versions. The dev image has `tilelang==0.1.12` (through mamba-ssm) and nvcc.
- FLA 0.6.0 enables the common backend (`chunk_bwd_dqkwg`) by default only on Hopper with Triton >= 3.4 (`CommonTileLangBackend.default_enable`), and every TileLang backend now also needs a usable nvcc. KDA's backend still applies on every GPU; it newly rejects `use_graph=True`, which Megatron does not pass.
- Net effect: nothing moves on H100. On GB200, `chunk_bwd_dqkwg` moves from TileLang to FLA's autotuned Triton `chunk_bwd_kernel_dqkwg`. It is part of the backward of GDN's FLA backend (`chunk_gated_delta_rule`), of FLA's GDP (`chunk_gated_delta_product`), and of `gdp.py`'s chunkwise CP. KDA's backward uses `chunk_kda_bwd_wy_dqkg_fused` and GDN2's its own fused Triton kernel, so neither moves. GB200 GDN and GDP training numerics, and their goldens, can move. `test_gated_delta_net.py::test_parallel_gated_delta_net_correctness`, which CI schedules on GB200 only, passes on GB200 with FLA 0.6.0 (see Tests). How far the goldens move was not measured.
- `FLA_TILELANG=0` turns TileLang off. On Hopper, the gated Triton `chunk_bwd_dqkwg` it falls back to works only with Triton >= 3.7.1 (the dev image has 3.8.0). With Triton 3.4 to 3.7.0, FLA 0.6.0 raises `RuntimeError` there (`fla/ops/common/chunk_o.py:728`); 0.5.1 raised for any Triton >= 3.4.

**Deterministic mode (#8883, `fix/kda-fla-deterministic`).** That PR adds `fla` to the autotune policy's default modules, so `deterministic_mode` pins FLA's Triton autotuners.
- TileLang kernels never reach Triton's autotuner. They compile one configuration per shape and GPU and use no atomics, so they need no pinning, and that PR's tests replay through them.
- With this bump, GB200's `chunk_bwd_dqkwg` is a Triton autotuner. In deterministic mode it is reached by GDP's backward: GDP trains on FLA whatever `deterministic_mode` says, while GDN and GDN2 require their torch backend there. With #8883 it is pinned like GDP's other FLA autotuners; without it, it is timed, as those already are on `main`.
- FLA 0.6.0 moved its norm kernels to `fla.modules.norm`. #8883 matches kernels by function name and finds both versions' TileLang backends, so its tests hold on either version. The two PRs merge cleanly in either order.

**Context-parallel numerics change on GPU.** In 0.6.0, the CP summary kernels compose the state transform M in TF32x3 (`cp/chunk_fwd.py:286`, `cp/chunk_bwd.py:277`) and accumulate `h` with a fused `tl.dot(a, b, acc)`. The CP merge also runs in TF32x3, and `build_cp_context` always uses the TF32x3 affine chain (`use_tf32x3_affine_chain=False` now raises). In 0.5.1, these FP32 dots ran in Triton's default TF32. So on H100 and GB200, the chunkwise CP summaries of GDP, GDN and KDA differ from 0.5.1. They are more accurate, but not bitwise equal, and CP loss goldens can move.

**Other kernel changes.** 0.6.0 also uses int64 program ids and adds a dispatch wrapper around `fused_recurrent_kda`, among other changes; they apply to training as well.

**Lock and images.** `uv.lock` is not regenerated in this PR. `uv lock` outside the CI container fails while building the git sources (`mamba-ssm` needs setuptools/torch without build isolation, and `torch-memory-saver` needs `TMS_CUDA_MAJOR`). Regenerated in the CI container, the lock must change only:
- `flash-linear-attention` 0.5.1 -> 0.6.0 and `fla-core` 0.5.1 -> 0.6.0 (both `py3-none-any` wheels; `fla-core` still depends only on `einops`, which is locked);
- the megatron-core `requires-dist` entry for the `ssm` extra, to `flash-linear-attention==0.6.0`.

`transformers` stays at 5.19.0, and `tilelang` 0.1.12 and the torch/triton overrides stay. `Dockerfile.ci.lts` installs the `ssm` extra from the same lock, so the LTS image moves to FLA 0.6.0 too. It cannot be held at 0.5.1 without losing GDP chunkwise CP, which now needs 0.6.0. `test_kda.py` and `test_gated_delta_net2.py` have not been run on the LTS image. FLA 0.6.0 caps `transformers` below 6 in every image with the `ssm` extra, so a later transformers 6 upgrade waits for FLA to lift the cap. #8860 (`fix/fa4-quack-lock`) also regenerates `uv.lock`; whichever of the two lands second re-locks in the CI container.
