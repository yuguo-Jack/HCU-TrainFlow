This assertion is too broad as it rejects inter-document masking with CP > 1 across all training entrypoints. This is not supported by the deprecated `GPTModel`, but the [`HybridModel` path](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/blob/b8a9fa86bb8c79ae97ff9d989e621f892eacc21a/megatron/core/context_parallel/utils.py#L245-L289) already implements per-document padding and partitioning, with [batch tests](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/blob/b8a9fa86bb8c79ae97ff9d989e621f892eacc21a/tests/unit_tests/data/test_get_batch.py#L541-L595) covering CP=2/4. Can you please scope the restriction to just the unsupported `GPTModel` path?
