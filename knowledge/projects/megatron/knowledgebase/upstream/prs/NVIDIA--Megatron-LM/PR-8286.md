# Honor per-module quantization override in the fused GroupedMLP path

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8286) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

The TE op-fuser builds its quantizers from the ambient autocast recipe, so a --te-precision-config-file override that keeps the experts quantized but with a different recipe (e.g. MXFP8 MTP experts under a global NVFP4 recipe) was ignored by the fused kernels: forward ran under NVFP4 while the module and its GTP gather were MXFP8, and backward then failed in the NVFP4 amax lookup.

Enter the module's own quantization autocast around the fused call, exactly as TEGroupedLinear.forward does on the unfused path. fc1 and fc2 must carry the same override since fusion spans both. A bf16 override still disables fusion.

Requires the matching Transformer Engine change that quantizes GTP-gathered BF16 weights inside the fused grouped MLP.
