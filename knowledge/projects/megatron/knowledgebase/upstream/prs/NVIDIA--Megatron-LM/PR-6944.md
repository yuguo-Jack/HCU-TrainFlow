## Changes

- Rebase the approved integration snapshots onto the fixed dev base below,
  inheriting merged DSA-backward and MTP-head FLOPs fixes instead of duplicating them.
- Add native 256-GPU GB300 4K/128K/1M MoK recipes with shared repeated MTP7.
- Release forward-sharing checkpoint snapshots after their single replay, as now
  in [#6472](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6472)'s first commit:
  repeated-MTP shared tensors that are also checkpoint outputs otherwise form a
  reference cycle that only the garbage collector frees.
- Add opt-in `--dsa-indexer-reuse-checkpoint-topk` (default off): under activation
  recompute, the replay of a top-k DSA layer computes the sparse indexer loss from
  the top-k indices and scores selected by its first call instead of rerunning
  the dense indexer and top-k.
- Record current focused/PP4 validation separately from the historical full-model
  performance, independent 128K stage runs and NSys evidence.

## Metric scope and qualification limits

- **Logged/model TFLOPS:** logical model arithmetic on valid tokens/lengths,
  including detached MTP heads; FMA=2. No precision weighting or recompute charge.
- **Execution-adjusted TFLOPS:** valid-pair defining GEMMs and accounted replay/
  recompute, plus the declared norm proxies. Excludes padding/tile inflation,
  router GEMMs, other elementwise/communication/optimizer work; not instruction
  counting or peak utilization. Unlogged MoK replay gives P4 an interval, not
  an exact point. Use step time/tokens for speedup, not inflated replay TFLOPS.
- **Nonfinite-performance policy:** NaN/Inf loss or gradient-norm observations
  alone may be retained as numerical metadata, explicitly not numerical
  correctness or convergence evidence. They cannot waive finite positive timing,
  complete native steps/microbatches, no skipped optimizer work, legal sparse
  capacity, topology, source/backend or genuine capture/replay checks. A shortened
  top-k workload, dropped routes, overflow retry/backend fallback, CUDA/NCCL
  error or other structural failure invalidates the advertised performance cell;
  a separately measured fallback must be labeled separately. No gradients,
  scores, native computations or source guards are sanitized to obtain a result.
- Allocator maxima include warmup/capture. Device-used is sampled on all 256
  ranks after steps 1/2/32, not continuously; retain per-rank/PP-stage evidence.
- Full-iteration graphs apply to fixed realized packing, effective shape and
  microbatch counts. Enabled dynamic CP does not establish arbitrary changing
  CP/shape/microbatch graph support.
- Standalone rows and the split-stage comparison are not ABBA evidence.
  No extra P4 repeat or drift bookend was added within the split-stage comparison;
  no unmeasured drift qualification is claimed. Each failed eager stage completed
  its bounded fresh repeat and remains unqualified. Every allocation is labeled and
  no cross-allocation ratio is claimed as a controlled causal speedup.
- Resolved commands, source/image/dependency identities, native YAML checksums,
  topology and raw logs remain in the experiment evidence.
