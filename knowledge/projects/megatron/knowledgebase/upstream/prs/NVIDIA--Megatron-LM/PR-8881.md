# [MoE][RL][BatchInvariant] Partition the batch-invariant MoE combine by the rollout layout and reject layouts training cannot reproduce

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8881) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

- [ ] I, the PR author, have personally reviewed every line of this PR.

# What does this PR do?

Under batch-invariant RL, training's MoE combine now reproduces the combine of the models that run the rollouts, whatever rollout layout the run uses. Rollout layouts whose reductions training cannot reproduce bit for bit are rejected when the arguments are validated, instead of silently diverging.

**Bug.** Under batch-invariant mode, the AllToAll dispatcher's unpermute (`moe/batch_invariant.py::unpermute`) adds a token's expert outputs on a fixed tree. Each EP rank rounds the sum over its contiguous shard of experts to one fp32 partial, then the partials are added across ranks. Where the partials round depends on the EP size that sets the partition.
- **Inference-optimized rollouts** combine physically on that tree: each rank sums its experts in fp64 and rounds once to fp32 (`unpermute_tokens_in_expert_order`), then the partials are added either as an fp32 rank-order chain (`"ordered"`) or exactly (`"multimem"`). The partition is the rollout EP.
- **Training's mirror** read its partition from `parallel_state.get_expert_model_parallel_world_size()`, which is the training EP. RL can run rollouts on a separate model at another EP size: `--rl-inference-expert-model-parallel-size`, or the `ep=` of disaggregated `--inference-shards`. Training's combine then rounds at different points from the rollout's.
- **Example:** a token whose experts 0, 1, 3, 4 carry 1, 2^-8, 2^-24, 2^-24 combines to 1.0078125 at EP 1 and to 1.0 at EP 2, 4 or 8 under `"ordered"`. On H100 a repro measured that witness, and 5 of 512 random tokens differing, between training at EP 1 and a rollout at EP 8.

Other rollout layouts break parity in ways no partition can fix, and nothing rejected them:
- **A different TP or expert-TP size** splits each row-parallel GEMM's K dimension differently, so the partials themselves differ. This holds for dense models too.
- **Inference-optimized rollouts with expert TP > 1** add the expert-TP partials and the EP partials in one rank-order sum, since #4743 made that group ETP x EP. Training reduces expert TP first and then combines.

## Fix

- `moe/batch_invariant.py::unpermute` takes `ep_size`, the EP size of the combine it reproduces, as a required keyword argument. It no longer reads `parallel_state`, following the process-group guidance for `megatron/core`. `moe_utils.unpermute` passes `batch_invariant_ep_size` through and requires it in batch-invariant mode. The AllToAll dispatcher passes `TransformerConfig.batch_invariant_moe_combine_ep_size`, or its own EP group's size when that is unset.
- `RLConfig.batch_invariant_moe_combine_ep_size` derives the partition from the rollout layout. The rollout models are the disaggregated shards from `--inference-shards`; else the separate inference model, using its `--rl-inference-*` sizes and training's where unset; else the training model itself. Under batch-invariant RL with MoE:
  - TE or local rollout models combine with the same unpermute as training, on the partition in their config, which they copy from training's. The field is training's EP, so any rollout EP matches, disaggregated pools included. This is the layout main already reproduced through `parallel_state`.
  - Inference-optimized rollout models combine physically, so the field is their EP size.
- `validate_args` sets `args.batch_invariant_moe_combine_ep_size` from it. The training config is therefore built with the field, and so is every config copied from it: rollout models, disaggregated shards, `get_config_for_layer` and the MTP copy. `TransformerConfig.__post_init__` checks that the field divides `num_moe_experts` and is set only for batch-invariant MoE. The field is internal and, like other derived fields, is excluded from the CLI. Building the field into the config replaces the first version's write after the model was built, which missed per-layer configs and disaggregated shards.
- The same derivation rejects, under batch-invariant RL, each layout that cannot reproduce training, naming the reason:
  - a rollout TP other than training's, for any model;
  - a rollout expert TP other than training's. A shard's `expt_tp` defaults to its `tp`, and the training ETP defaults to the TP;
  - inference-optimized MoE with expert TP > 1;
  - inference-optimized disaggregated pools at different EP sizes, since training can reproduce only one physical partition. TE and local pools at different EP sizes remain supported, as above.

Different TP sizes cannot be made to match by any reduction order. The companion TP PR #8868 makes the TP reductions independent of a token's position at a fixed TP size; it does not make different TP sizes agree. That is why this PR rejects them.

Interactions:
- The PR merges cleanly with origin/main and with #8868 in either order. Together they cover the TP reduce-scatter (#8868) and the EP combine (this PR), so batch-invariant rollouts at a supported layout reproduce training's reductions.
- It also merges cleanly with #8866, d1, d5 and n12.

## Tests

- `tests/unit_tests/transformer/moe/test_moe_batch_invariant_unpermute.py` (1 GPU):
  - `test_alltoall_combine_reproduces_rollout_ep[ep][collective]`, for EP in {1, 2, 4, 8} under both collectives. The real `MoEAlltoAllTokenDispatcher` runs on an EP group of 1, with the field set when the config is built, as `validate_args` does. Its `combine_postprocess` must equal the inference combine at that EP size bit for bit. The reference runs each shard through the real inference per-rank kernel and then does the collective's cross-rank sum. A witness table asserts that the reference is sensitive to the partition. On `main` the test is expected to fail for EP 2, 4 and 8 under `"ordered"`, and for EP 2 and 4 under `"multimem"`: with one expert per shard the multimem sum is exact, so EP 8 matches EP 1 there. The GPU run below could not check this per-EP split on `main` (see there).
  - `test_alltoall_combine_defaults_to_own_ep`: when the field is unset, the partition follows the layer's EP.
  - The existing tests pass `ep_size` instead of overriding the global EP size.
- `tests/unit_tests/training/config/test_rl_config.py` (CPU):
  - `test_batch_invariant_moe_combine_ep_size` covers the field per layout: TE rollouts on a separate model and on disaggregated pools at EP {4, 4} and {1, 4} all get training's EP, and inference-optimized rollouts get their EP. The disaggregated TE case would have used the shard EP before.
  - `test_batch_invariant_rl_rejects_rollouts_that_cannot_reproduce_training` covers each rejection.
  - `test_validate_args_builds_the_combine_ep_into_the_training_config` checks that `validate_args` derives the field, `core_transformer_config_from_args` builds it into the config, and a deep copy keeps it.
  - These fail on `main` and on the first version of this PR.
- `tests/unit_tests/transformer/test_transformer_config.py::test_batch_invariant_moe_combine_ep_size_is_validated_at_construction` (CPU).
- Updated for the explicit argument: `tests/unit_tests/rl/test_rl_batch_invariant.py` and `tests/unit_tests/determinism/kernels/test_moe_kernels.py::test_batch_invariant_unpermute_replays`. The kernel itself is unchanged and its determinism test is updated, so the gate passes without a label.

On H100 in the CI image (`mcore_ci_dev:main`, 2026-11-02), the regression tests pass (55 cases). On `main` all 55 fail, but not by assertion: `main` lacks the API they call (`unpermute`'s `ep_size` argument and the `batch_invariant_moe_combine_ep_size` field of `TransformerConfig` and `RLConfig`), so they stop with a `TypeError` or `AttributeError` and do not show the bug there. The step 3 GPU repro of this finding (R04-F4) did, on `main` on H100: training's combine at EP 1 differed from the rollout engine's at EP 8 (the witness token 1.0078125 against 1.0, and 5 of 512 random tokens). The neighbor tests (`rl/test_rl_batch_invariant.py`, `test_moe_kernels.py::test_batch_invariant_unpermute_replays`, `test_moe_batch_invariant.py`) pass (17 cases).
