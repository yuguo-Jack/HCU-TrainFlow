- sylvesterkaczmarek · COMMENTED · 2026-09-04T10:19:57Z · [source](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/7083#pullrequestreview-5111760109)

One concurrency concern: `_DRAIN_CALLBACK_SCHEDULED` is process-global while the pending queues are shared across chain/group keys. If two backwards overlap, the second schedules no callback, and the first backward's callback can drain the second backward's in-flight weights and signal grad-ready early. Could the drain state be scoped to the active backward rather than the process?
