## Summary
- Keep dispatcher rows for padded tokens, zero their routing probabilities, and exclude them from load-balancing counters.
- Reuse the regular MoE checkpoint path for selective recompute in the modality MoE layer.
- Skip expert-bias collectives when the update rate is zero.
