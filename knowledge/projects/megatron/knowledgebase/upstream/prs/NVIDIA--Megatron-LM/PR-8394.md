# Integrate MFSDP v2 Muon with MCore training

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8394) · state: open · merged: False

## What does this PR do?

Enable MCore training with Muon and Adam under MFSDP v2, using the standalone `FsdpMuon` merged in #8313. Existing MCore selection rules send eligible matrices to Muon and embeddings, output projections, non-matrix parameters, and explicit exclusions to Adam by default.

- Construct separate dense and expert Muon optimizers on their respective data-parallel meshes, preserving FSDP parameter metadata.
- Keep optimizer parameter ownership disjoint while computing one gradient-clipping norm across the chain.
- Save and restore Muon momentum and Adam moments through a combined FQN-keyed FSDP checkpoint. Extend fused-parameter checkpoint transforms to handle momentum and v2 DTensors, and initialize momentum for loading without taking an optimizer step.

Enable with `--optimizer muon --use-megatron-fsdp --megatron-fsdp-version 2 --data-parallel-sharding-strategy optim_grads_params --muon-no-split-qkv --ckpt-format fsdp_dtensor`. FP32/BF16 and dense/expert parameters are supported with one-dimensional DP meshes. Tensor parallelism, hybrid DP meshes, split-QKV updates, and precision-aware Adam are unsupported. MFSDP handles optimizer sharding; omit `--use-distributed-optimizer`.

## Validation

Validated on two RTX A6000 GPUs with Emerging Optimizers 0.3.0:

- FP32/BF16 loss curves and joint gradient norms against unsharded MCore Muon+Adam, plus exact checkpoint continuation through SwiGLU transforms, including optimizer states.
- Expert-parallel Muon/Adam reference comparisons and separate dense/expert mesh assignments.
- Existing Adam checkpoint, FSDP clipping, chained-optimizer, and FSDP checkpoint-transform tests.
- Real BF16 `pretrain_gpt.py` smoke run: save after two steps, restart in a fresh process, train two more steps, and save again.
- Black, isort, Pylint, Ruff, and kernel determinism coverage checks passed. Mypy still reports errors, including missing dependency typing and optimizer factory/type-union errors.

The final rebase adds two upstream commits concerning GDN inference and review automation; GPU validation preceded that rebase.
