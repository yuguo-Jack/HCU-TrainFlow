# Pack whole requests into CUDA-graph-gated chunked prefill steps

# What does this PR do?
With --inference-cuda-graph-all-prefills, chunked prefill snapped every newly admitted request to the largest captured-graph boundary within its own remaining length. A request that fits in the step but does not land on a boundary was therefore split, and since a partial admit ends the scheduling loop, every step carried one finishing remainder plus one new partial request. On arc_challenge (~1.7k-token prompts, max_tokens=4096) each engine ran 2-prefill, 2048-token steps while hundreds of requests waited.

Admit the whole remainder when it fits and the batch still matches a captured graph (padding up to the graph's token count), and split only a request that does not fit, at the largest boundary within budget, so steps pack to the largest graph. The decision moves into _cg_chunked_prefill_chunk_size, with unit tests including a scheduler-loop simulation of the old and new packing.

On an 8-node (TP4/EP32) arc_challenge eval, steps went from [2048 tokens] 2 prefills to [4096 tokens] 3-4 prefills, and eval wall time dropped 16% (226 s to 189 s).
