# What does this PR do ?
<!-- Add a one line overview of what this PR aims to accomplish. -->
I ran into an issue where gradients kept growing during the backward pass after enabling recompute. 
After debugging, I traced the root cause to `DeepepManager.token_probs` not being properly released. This caused the corresponding leaf tensor `hidden_states` (with `retain_grad()` applied) and its `hidden_states.grad` to remain unfreed. 
The issue was resolved by assigning `None` to `self.token_probs` after the `combine` step.
