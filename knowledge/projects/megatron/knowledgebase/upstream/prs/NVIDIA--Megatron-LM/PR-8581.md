# Add fp4_dense_mxfp8: MXFP8 non-expert linears in FP4 models

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8581) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

fp4_dense_mxfp8 runs the non-expert linear layers of an FP4 model in MXFP8 (E4M3) while the routed experts keep the FP4 recipe; an explicit quant_recipe entry still wins. fp4_dense_mxfp8_param keeps those layers' primary weights in MXFP8, all-gathered through the grad buffer (reuse_grad_buf_for_mxfp8_param_ag, now accepted with fp4_param_gather). In that mixed mode only buffers holding MXFP8 params reuse the grad buffer; NVFP4 expert buffers keep their packed param buffer, get the regular post-all-gather processing, and are re-quantized from their main params by _copy_main_params_to_param_buffer.
