# Fix NaNs in QK clipping with mixed-sign logits

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/7517) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Why

QK clipping scales Q and K when an attention head exceeds the configured positive logit threshold. A different head can have a negative maximum logit in the same step. Computing `threshold / maximum` and clamping only its upper bound leaves a negative scale for that head; applying the fractional clipping powers then puts NaNs into Q and K weights and their full FP32 master weights.

This is the numerical case identified in #7382, whose broader hybrid-layer and distributed-optimizer changes were retained downstream. The same denominator defect is present in both standard attention and MLA.

## What changed

- Compute the scale as `threshold / maximum.clamp_min(threshold)` in both attention implementations. Every head at or below the threshold receives scale one; an over-threshold head keeps the existing ratio.
- Preserve standard attention's query-group reduction and Q/K scaling, and MLA's separate non-RoPE and RoPE scaling. V weights remain unchanged.
- Extend the existing mixed-logit regressions across MHA, GQA, direct-Q MLA, and Q-LoRA MLA. The cases include negative maxima, signed zero, the threshold boundary, and full FP32 master weights.

The production change remains within the two clipping implementations and adds no allocation, configuration option, or alternate clipping path.
