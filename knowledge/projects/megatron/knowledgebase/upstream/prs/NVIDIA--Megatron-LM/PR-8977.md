# [Test][Inference] Independent oracles for kernels outside inference: attention, MLA and RoPE call sites

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8977) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

- [ ] I, the PR author, have personally reviewed every line of this PR.

# What does this PR do?

Adds independent oracle tests for the attention, MLA and RoPE kernel call sites that dynamic and static inference reach outside `megatron/core/inference`: paged FlashAttention (FA2/FA3/FA4), FlashMLA decode, static `flash_decode`, `apply_rotary_pos_emb_with_cos_sin`, the dynamic MLA path and the FP8 inference padding wrapper. Each module checks the call site against a reference that does not share the kernel's algorithm. Tests only.

**Stacked on the oracle harness PR #8966 (`test/inference-oracle-harness`)**, which adds `tests/unit_tests/inference/oracles/oracle_utils.py` and its conftest. Review this PR's own commits (the tests, setup fixes after the first GPU runs, `29df89613`, cases added after mutation testing, `a7e0b1d44`, sanitizer cases, and `2dd1e3417`, MLA variants and production sizes). Retarget to `main` once the harness is merged.

## Why

Before this PR, these call sites had no independent numerical check:
- The default FA2 paged path, FlashMLA decode, static `flash_decode` and the RoPE helper were tested with mocked kernels or for shapes only.
- The dynamic MLA path had no numerical test of absorption or of the whole-pool up-projection.
- The FP8 padding wrapper was tested with mocks, or only for completing a run.

The only strong attention oracle was the batch-invariant end-to-end test against the training forward. It covers FA3/FA4 with 16-token pages, and no window, softcap or sink.

## Tests

All under `tests/unit_tests/inference/oracles/attention/`. Each module's docstring states the contract, the reference, the tolerance class, the variants and which cases depend on open PRs.

- **`attention_reference.py`** (shared, no tests):
  - **Paged pool.** Laid out as the dynamic context lays it out. Live pages are shuffled, and table entries past a request's pages point at a dummy page. Unused pages, the dummy page and key tails hold NaN. Value tails are zero, as the context guarantees. NaN guard pages sit on both sides.
  - **Gather.** A request's K/V come from its block-table row and length only.
  - **fp64 attention.** Bottom-right causal, window `(W, 0)`, softcap, sink as an extra denominator term, GQA.
  - **Trusted yardstick.** The same attention in the kernel dtype, in SDPA's math-backend order.
  - **RoPE.** fp64 rotation and a dtype yardstick.
- **`test_flash_attn_paged.py`**: `Attention.flash_decode_and_prefill` with FA2, FA3 and FA4, against fp64 attention over the KV gathered from the block table, at the scale derived from the configured head dim. Bound per token row.
  - Prefill/mixed and decode (`S` 1, 2, 4).
  - Multi-token decode whose packed query rows `S * H / Hk` (96/128/160/288: MQA, 32 heads, `S` 3/4/5/9) sit below, at and past the 128-row M tile and FA4's 256-row SM100 tile.
  - Page 256 for FA2, and 64/128/256 for FA3/FA4.
  - Lengths `1, P-1, P, P+1, 2P-1, 2P+1, 4097` in every batch, and a chunk that crosses a page boundary.
  - GQA 1/4/8, head dim 64/128, window `P-1/P/P+1`, softcap, learnable sink, zero-length padding requests. Factors combined pairwise.
  - FA4 with `max_seqlen_k` at the block-table capacity.
  - FA3/FA4 batch-invariant bitwise property.
  - FA2 with NaN value tails.
  - Read-only pool, guards included.
  - Pages past 2^31 pool elements (`large`).
  - Sanitizer cases.
  - FA2 refuses a page size it cannot read.
- **`test_flash_mla_decode.py`**: the FlashMLA branch against fp64 latent attention, at MLA's scale derived from the config.
  - Lengths around the 64-token block, and 4097.
  - `S` 1/2/4, heads 16-128, batch 1/2/#SMs/#SMs+1, sink.
  - NaN tails and dummy page.
  - CUDA-graph replay with changed lengths.
  - FlashMLA's natural-log LSE.
  - int32 offsets (`large`).
  - The SM90 kernel is probed in a subprocess, because a build without it exits the process.
- **`test_static_flash_decode.py`**: `Attention.flash_decode` against fp64 RoPE and attention.
  - Positions `0, 1, 127, 128, 129, 255, 256, 257, 511` in one batch.
  - GQA 1/4/8, NeoX/interleaved, rotary dim D, D/2 and none, softcap, sink, window 127/128/129.
  - The cache write: k' within the bound, v exact, every other element unchanged.
  - Static layer steps: `flash_decode` on/off x output gate x window x full/partial batch, plus MHA without RoPE. The tensor that reaches `linear_proj` is checked against fp64 attention over the RoPE'd history.
  - GPT and Hybrid flash-decode generation.
  - Production shapes: Qwen2.5-1.5B (12/2 heads of 128, batch 64, 4096-token cache) and Llama-3.1-8B (32/8 heads, batch 8, 8192-token cache).
- **`test_rope_with_cos_sin.py`**: the flash-attn rotary kernel and the torch fallback, against the fp64 pairwise rotation.
  - `d_rot` D, D/2, 16, both pairings, S up to 8193, bf16/fp16/fp32, strided input.
  - Pass-through channels exact.
- **`test_mla_dynamic_path.py`**:
  - **Scenario.** Prefill, absorbed decode, mixed and decode steps of a latent-caching MLA layer under a real `DynamicInferenceContext`. They are checked against an fp64 MLA reference built from the training path's own inputs, bounded by the bf16 training forward. DeepSeek-V3's head widths (128/64/128), random RMSNorm gains, and MLA's scale from the config; the training forward gets that scale explicitly.
  - **Padding rows.** Live rows do not change when the attention kernel leaves NaN in the padding rows, in bf16 and FP8 tensorwise.
  - **Unused pages.** Live rows do not change when unused pool pages hold 1e4 or NaN.
  - **Rejections.** DSA with the latent cache is rejected at config; a pinned FA2 is refused with a ValueError.
  - **YaRN.** DeepSeek-V3's scaling (`mscale_all_dim` 1) and Megatron's default (`mscale_all_dim` 0). The reference rotates RoPE itself and derives the scale and amplitude from the YaRN definition.
  - **TP 2.** Without and with sequence parallelism (TE spec), and with the inference-optimized layers and sequence parallelism, on 2 GPUs.
  - **Speculative decoding and MTP draft steps.** Verification steps with 2 and 3 query rows per request. With the MTP draft-KV plane, every draft depth after prefill, verification and mixed steps.
  - **Chunked prefill.** A 129-token prompt split at 63, 64 and 65 tokens.
- **`test_fp8_padding_wrapper.py`**: TELinear, TEColumnParallelLinear, TERowParallelLinear and TELayerNormColumnParallelLinear at TP 1/2/4 with sequence parallelism, against fp64 `x @ W^T`.
  - Trusted: TE's own unsharded linear.
  - At TP 1, bit-exact against the unwrapped module on the padded input.
  - S around the 16-row (MXFP8: 32-row) alignment.
  - Tensorwise and delayed scaling on H100, MXFP8 on GB200.
  - Production widths: Llama-3.1-8B's QKV, attention-output and FFN projections and DeepSeek-V3's KV down-projection, with 132 and 2044 tokens.

Tolerance: harness policy, factor 2 against the trusted implementation, no hand-picked atol. Exact for copies and metamorphic properties.

### Results

H100 (CI image, first run on the first commit): the 4-GPU FP8 wrapper entry passed 82/82. The 1-GPU entry passed 101/105; the 4 failures were test-setup errors that the second commit fixes:
- a model-parallel RNG that was never seeded;
- MLA pre-hooks that returned a value, which replaced TE attention's arguments;
- a padded-count check read before the step set the count.

Of the cases expected to fail on `main`, n2, n6, s7, d5, d7, #8878 and the strided-query case failed on the bug itself.

The second commit also adds `[batch=partial-gqa_without_rope]`. The earlier partial-batch case gave TE a dense RoPE'd key, so TE made k and v contiguous and the strided k/v slices never reached it. The new case asserts that TE receives a dense query and equal-stride k/v cache slices, then compares outputs.

On CPU (torch 2.14):
- The reference self-checks pass (5): gather order, and agreement with torch's fp64 SDPA and with the sink identity.
- The RoPE fallback cases fail on `main` (14), as the audit predicted. They pass with #7465's diff applied.
- The DSA config check fails on `main` and passes with #8570's config change.

A fake-kernel smoke check of the paged, FlashMLA and page-offset plumbing passed on a laptop GPU. That is not evidence; the oracles also rejected deliberately broken fakes (causal offset, dropped softcap, sink or window, wrong value slice).

### Follow-up `29df89613` (mutation testing)

The mutation run (`audit/step4/mutation/RESULTS.md`) left seven O8b survivors:
- **Scale taken from the module** (paged FA, MLA): the references now derive the scale from the configuration. The MLA training twin, which shares the layer's class, gets that scale as an explicit `softmax_scale`, so the trusted bound does not move with the derivation under test. The FlashMLA oracle derives it too.
- **A second RMSNorm of the cached latents** was invisible with unit gains: the gains are now random.
- **The query split order** was invisible with equal 64/64 widths (`torch.split` by sizes gives the same split): the MLA cases now use DeepSeek-V3's 128/64 widths.
- **FA4 told one query per request in multi-token decode**: on SM100, FA4 sizes its schedule from `max_seqlen_q * H / Hk`, and every case fit one 128-row tile either way. The new M-tile decode cases cross it. On SM90 FA4 schedules varlen tiles from `cu_seqlens_q` and reads `max_seqlen_q` only for the split heuristic, which `num_splits=1` bypasses: equivalent there.
- **FlashMLA scale through `get_softmax_scale`**: equivalent; it returns the MLA layer's `softmax_scale` first.

Not run on a GPU yet. The MLA scenario now runs FA prefill and the TE training forward at DeepSeek's 192/128 head dims.

### Follow-up `a7e0b1d44` (sanitizer cases)

### Follow-up `2dd1e3417` (MLA variants and production sizes)

The step 4 ratings left gaps in three kernels. This commit covers them.

- **MLA dynamic path.**
  - **Reference.** It now applies RoPE itself, from the training twin's `q` and `k_pe` before rotation. The cos/sin and the scale come from DeepSeek-V2/V3's YaRN definition: amplitude `m(s, mscale) / m(s, mscale_all_dim)`, scale `m(s, mscale_all_dim)^2 / sqrt(192)`.
  - **Twin.** The training twin's attention is rebuilt with that scale. MLA multiplies `mscale^2` into any configured `softmax_scale`, so a configured scale would still move with the layer. The twin's own RoPE is checked against the fp64 rotation.
  - **Checked on CPU.** The reference agrees with Megatron's YaRN embedding within 3.6e-5 in fp32.
  - **New cases.**
    - YaRN with `mscale_all_dim` 1 and 0.
    - TP 2 without SP, and TP 2 with SP for both the TE and the inference-optimized layers.
    - Speculative decoding with 1 and 2 tokens, with and without the MTP draft-KV plane. The draft layer's depths are checked against its own training forward. The commit pass's output, which the controller discards, is not checked.
    - Chunked-prefill splits at 63, 64 and 65 tokens.
  - **Collectives.** Comparisons are asserted after the last step, so one rank's failure cannot leave the other waiting in a collective.
- **Static `flash_decode`.**
  - Production shapes: Qwen2.5-1.5B at batch 64 with a 4096-token cache, and Llama-3.1-8B at batch 8 with an 8192-token cache.
  - The kernel-level scale now comes from the configured head dim.
- **FP8 padding wrapper.**
  - Production widths: up to 14336 input and 28672 output features.
  - Token counts of 132 and 2044, at TP 1/2/4.
  - Tensorwise on H100 and MXFP8 on GB200.

Not run on a GPU yet.

## Notes for reviewers

- **FA3 in CI.** The CI image has no FA3. Its paged cases therefore skip wherever it is missing, also on a run that targets the hardware: CI exports `MCORE_ORACLE_TARGET_SM=9`. FA2 and FA4, which the image ships, still fail such a run if they are missing.

- **Behaviour changes.** None; tests only.
- **Hardware markers.** FA3/FA4 and FlashMLA cases carry `requires_sm`. Blackwell cases also carry `launch_on_gb200`.
- **Missing libraries.** A missing FlashAttention generation skips, but fails on a run that sets `MCORE_ORACLE_TARGET_SM`.
- **FA3.** The CI image has no FA3, so the FA3 cases are deselected in our staging.
- **Suspected, not run.** FA4 with 64-token pages may read the block-table entry past a request's last page. For MHA that entry is the dummy page, which nothing zeroes. The FA4 `page=64` cases poison it with NaN.
- **Window under `no_mask`.** The static non-flash-decode decode step relies on TE honouring a window under `no_mask`.
- **Large cases.** The int32-offset cases need 17 GB (FA) and 9 GB (FlashMLA) and run only with `-m large`.
