# What does this PR do?
Megatron-FSDP backward-prefetch fixes: prefetch the row-wise weights that activation recompute re-gathers in backward.

**Recompute prefetch.** With a transpose (column-wise) weight buffer, as in MXFP8, a selectively recomputed module's forward re-run in backward needs the row-wise weights, which were released after the forward. Prefetch is disabled in that re-run, so the row-wise bucket was gathered on demand and the main stream stalled on every such gather. Buckets gathered this way are now recorded, and later backward gathers also gather the row-wise copy of recorded buckets in their prefetch window. This is a no-op without transpose buffers (the backward already gathered the same buffer) and is disabled with `fsdp_double_buffer`.
