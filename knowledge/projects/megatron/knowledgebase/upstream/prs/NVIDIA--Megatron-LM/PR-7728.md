## What does this PR do?

Honor independent MFSDP v2 sharding policies for dense and expert parameters. DeepSeek V3 MLPerf uses dense inner ZeRO-2, expert inner ZeRO-3, and outer ZeRO-1 for both. At 256 GPUs, EP64, and four optimizer instances, the dense mesh is `(4,64)` and the expert mesh is `(4,1)`.

Remove the adapter's dense ZeRO-3 restriction and build expert-specific placements for MoE models even at EP=1. Dense tests compare losses against a ZeRO-3 reference spanning all DP ranks, including gradient accumulation. MoE tests check placements and finite losses across repeated optimizer steps with independent dense/expert outer policies at EP=1/2. CUDA-graph tests cover full training and optimizer replay for hybrid ZeRO-2.

The DBuffer support from #7633 and CUDA-graph profiler fix (#7726) are now merged in `main`. This PR is independent of the quantized-wrapper change (#7727). Companion Bridge fix: https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Megatron-Bridge/pull/6200.
