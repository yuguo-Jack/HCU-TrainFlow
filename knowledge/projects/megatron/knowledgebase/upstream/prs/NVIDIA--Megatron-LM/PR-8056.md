# fix: fix delayed-wgrad gradient-reduction bug that breaks latent MoE under Megatron-FSDP

# What does this PR do?
`--delay-wgrad-compute` with Megatron-FSDP and `moe_latent_size` launches gradient reduce-scatters against weight gradients that do not exist yet, in two independent ways: hooks fired by the wrong schedule slot (causes NaN), and one hook racing its wgrad across CUDA streams (causes grad norm=inf). This PR makes delayed wgrad usable for latent MoE under Megatron-FSDP.
