# fix(inference): keep streamed partials aligned with the merged record after eviction

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/8150) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

**Problem.** With `SamplingParams(streaming=True)`, a request that the dynamic engine evicts under KV pressure, or recomputes on suspend, streams wrong partial frames after it resumes. The stream stalls, then skips tokens, so the concatenation of `ENGINE_REPLY_PARTIAL` deltas no longer equals the final reply (`record.merge()`). Log probs shift with the tokens.

**Root cause.** `_try_send_streaming_partials` keeps a per-request emitted-token offset (`_partial_emit_lengths`) over the whole stream, but slices it into `record[-1].generated_tokens`. `DynamicInferenceRequestRecord.checkpoint()` (eviction and suspend recompute) appends a new segment whose prompt absorbs the earlier generations and whose `generated_tokens` restart at 0. The surviving offset therefore indexes past the start of the new segment.

**Fix.** Index the stream in `record.merge()` coordinates. The total length and the token, log-prob and top-n slices span all checkpoint segments (`_slice_merged_segments`, with no merged copy), and prompt log probs come from the first segment, as in `merge()`. Also bound `new_top_n_logprobs` by `emit_end` like tokens and log probs. Previously, held-back top-n entries were sent early and then sent again.

**Tests.** `test_streaming_partials_continue_across_checkpoint` (CPU; two checkpoints; with and without stop-word holdback; `streaming_interval` 1 and 3) and `TestDynamicInferenceEngine::test_streaming_partials_match_merged_record_after_eviction` (GPU; tiny KV pool forces real evictions; streamed deltas must equal the final request). Both fail on main and pass with this change.
