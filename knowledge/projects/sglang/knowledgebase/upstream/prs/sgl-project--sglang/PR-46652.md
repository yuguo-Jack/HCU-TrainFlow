# [Perf] [AMD] Kimi-K3: fused MoE routing, sort, quant and epilogue

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/46652) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

At K3 decode batch sizes, MoE routing, sorting, activation quant and the epilogue were separate small launches, and launch-bound.

## Modifications

Triton kernels in `kernels/ops/moe/` (`k3_route_sort*`, `k3_moe_epi`, `moe_sorting_small`, `k3_moe_cfg`) replace them. They are wired into `moe_runner/aiter.py` and gated to K3 on gfx950.

## Accuracy Tests

- `test_moe_sorting_small.py`, `test_k3_sort_plan*.py`: 111 pass.

Full K3 stack on MI355X TP8 (DFlash, prod config) vs B300, McNemar:
- GPQA-D 89.4 vs 87.9 (p=0.55)
- AIME25 96.7 vs 93.3 (p=1.0)
- MATH500 92.2 vs 92.2 (p=1.0)
- MMLU-Pro 89.6 vs 90.0 (p=0.77)
- GSM8K 97.2%

## Speed Tests and Profiling

- Turning it off costs -7.6/-4.9/-8.9% on the agentic replay and -6.4/-3.4% at 90k prefix (mean -6.2%).

Speed numbers: throughput change from turning this feature off on the full stack, agentic replay at C=1/8/32 and 90k-prefix at C=1/8.
