# [Fix] hicache: Withhold only protected bytes from page-cache credit

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47557) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

The cgroup host-memory headroom used to size the HiCache host pool credits reclaimable page cache back against a cgroup's usage. Since #42788 it withholds that credit to respect `memory.min` protection, but it withholds all of it as soon as any descendant cgroup has a nonzero `memory.min`. A single small protected child, for example a `systemd-oomd.service` under `system.slice` with `memory.min` set, therefore cancels the entire page-cache credit, and the host pool is sized as if gigabytes of easily reclaimable cache could never be freed.

## Modifications

- `_has_protected_descendants` is replaced by `_protected_children_bytes`, which returns how many bytes reclaim at a cgroup must leave to its children: the sum over its **direct** children of `min(memory.min, memory.current)`, with `max` counting as the child's full usage. The kernel caps a child's effective protection at its usage, and a deeper cgroup's protection at its ancestors', so direct children bound it.
- The headroom subtracts only that amount from the page-cache credit (`max(0, page_cache - protected)`) instead of zeroing it.
- A child without `memory.min` (no memory controller enabled) contributes nothing. An unreadable or malformed value still withholds the whole credit, as before.

Unit tests in `test/registered/unit/mem_cache/test_host_memory.py`:
- the sibling-protection case now covers a `memory.min` above the child's usage, below it, `max`, and an unreadable value;
- a service under `system.slice` keeps its cache credit when only a small `systemd-oomd.service` child is protected, and a child's protection is capped at its usage;
- a deeper descendant's protection is capped by its ancestor.
