# [Perf] [AMD] kimi-k3: Stream the attention-residual bank read for prefill on gfx950

## Motivation

Kimi-K3's attention-residual (AttnRes) read on ROCm (`attn_res_hip._agg_kernel`: score the bank rows against the
current prefix, softmax, weighted sum, output RMSNorm, in one launch) keeps a row's whole next_pow2(bank rows) x
next_pow2(hidden) fp32 tile in registers. That is the basis of its speed at decode sizes, but on prefill-sized batches
whose bank tile pads to 8 rows it runs as one register-bound CTA per CU, where enough smaller CTAs per CU would stream
the bank at HBM bandwidth.

## Modifications

Gated on gfx950.

- AttnRes reads of at least 64 rows whose bank tile pads to 8 rows (5-8 bank rows) stream the bank two rows at a time
  with an online softmax (`attn_res_hip._agg_stream_kernel`) instead of holding it in registers. Same math as
  `_agg_kernel`; the running rescale rounds differently in the last ulp.
- Smaller batches, and banks of up to 4 rows (where the register tile still wins), keep `_agg_kernel`.

The thresholds are module constants (`STREAM_MIN_ROWS`, `STREAM_MIN_TILE_ROWS`); no new env var.

## Accuracy Tests

**Setup for every number below:** 8x MI350X (gfx950, 1000 W), Kimi-K3 (MXFP4 MoE, bf16 attention) at TP8 with EAGLE3
(topk 1, 3 steps, 4 draft tokens), FP8 KV cache, `SGLANG_USE_AITER=1`, aiter's K3 FlyDSL kernels
(`AITER_SITUV2_A8W4=1 AITER_FLYDSL_FORCE=1 SGLANG_AITER_K3_OPT=1`), CUDA graphs for bs 1/2/4/8/16, and an aiter bf16 GEMM table with K3 rows re-tuned on this pod with HBM-resident weights
(`AITER_CONFIG_GEMM_BF16`; not part of this PR, it belongs in aiter's model configs). EAGLE3 on K3 needs the
aux-capture commit of #43599, applied locally for these runs. Microbenchmarks rotate their weights through HBM.
Per-feature numbers were measured on the integration branch of this series as each piece landed (Oct 13-14,
earlier `main`). All branches of the series are on `main` 4eb606b2ae (2026-10-17); the kernel tests below ran on this
branch, and the end-to-end checkpoints on that `main` plus the queue up to each slot.

- GSM8K 400 with the streaming read: 0.985 / accept 3.62. End to end: see below.
- `test_attn_res_hip.py` (new): the streaming read against the register-tile kernel at 5 and 8 bank rows, with and
  without the residual add and the bank write: the output within one bf16 ulp, the written prefix and bank row bit
  for bit. On MI350X: 1 passed (4 subtests).
