## Motivation

Adds pool-level KV sharding support for DSA indexer caches.
- Shares page ownership, scratch mapping, and layer-ahead prefetch between latent KV and indexer caches.
- Supports token-based latent buffers and page-based indexer buffers while preserving packed FP8 keys and FP32 scales.
- Gathers cached prefixes into scratch and persists new tokens only on their owning rank.
- Adds 2-GPU and 4-GPU tests covering cache contents, page reuse, and asynchronous gathering.
DSA backend metadata, fused indexer stores, and model-runner integration remain follow-up work.
