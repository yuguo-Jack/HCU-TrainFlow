## Motivation

The cookbook's **Agentic Long-Context Deployment** sections (#42726, #43083, #43799, #43983, #44065, #44228, #44654, #44712, #45039, #45111, #45311, #45513, #45749, #46005, #46506, #46547, #46807, #46948, #47027) mirror SemiAnalysis InferenceX AgentX submissions as raw launch commands, each linked to the run that measured it. The configurations are mirrored from SemiAnalysis submissions. This PR:

1. **Syncs to InferenceX `main`** (`70a76017`). The MiniMax-M3.1-Flash FP8 draft-KV PRs [InferenceX #4600](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4600) (GB300) and [#4598](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4598) (B200), the conc-1 PR [#4509](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4509) (GB200) and the Qwen3.5 H200 HiCache PR [#4455](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4455) have merged. Their lanes now come from their dashboard runs instead of the PRs.
2. **Adds DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash updates** from three new in-progress InferenceX PRs:
   - B300 SGLang, with a new low-concurrency key ([InferenceX #4627](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4627)).
   - GB300 SGLang on the `20261029` nightly, with a megakernel TP4 c1 point ([InferenceX #4603](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4603)).
   - GB300 Dynamo+SGLang on the bonus-fixed nightlies, with prefill-HiCache disagg points ([InferenceX #4595](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4595), new head).
3. **Pins two PRs to measured runs.** [InferenceX #4556](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4556) (DeepSeek-V4 B200 disagg) and [InferenceX #4524](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4524) (MiniMax-M3.1-Flash B300) now have `/use` runs on their current heads and are shown as verified.
