## Motivation

The cookbook's **Agentic Long-Context Deployment** sections (#42726, #43083, #43799, #43983, #44065, #44228, #44654, #44712, #45039, #45111, #45311, #45513, #45749, #46005, #46506, #46547) mirror SemiAnalysis InferenceX AgentX submissions as raw launch commands, each linked to the run that measured it. The configurations are mirrored from SemiAnalysis submissions. This PR:

1. **Adds DeepSeek-V4 B200 Dynamo+SGLang disagg 1P1D DEP8 DSpark6 HiCache deployments** ([InferenceX #4556](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4556)) and **Muse Spark Open GB300 SGLang TP8·EP8 (2 nodes) and nightly-1019 TP4·EP4 deployments** ([InferenceX #4454](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4454)), both in progress.
2. **Updates MiniMax-M3.1-Flash**: B200 retune and TP2 ([InferenceX #4536](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4536)), B300 TP2 ([InferenceX #4524](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4524)), conc-1 low-latency points on B200 / B300 / GB200 ([InferenceX #4509](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4509)), and GB300 TP2 c20–c36 ([InferenceX #4525](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4525), merged).
3. **Keeps five merged Klaud-Cold image bumps that the InferenceX dashboard does not show** (see below), pinned to their `/use` runs.
4. **Syncs to InferenceX `main`** (`7ca692b9`) and overlays the InferenceX PRs in the table below.
