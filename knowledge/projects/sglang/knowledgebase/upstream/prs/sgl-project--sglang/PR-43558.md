# [ROCm][Perf] Qwen3.8-27B dense mono decode: persistent FlyDSL GDN and FFN launches (TP1 and TP2, MI355X/MI350X)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43558) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

> **Draft. Stacked on #43516, which includes #43481.** The diff below contains both PRs. Review only the last 15 commits (from "Add a FlyDSL dense FFN mono kernel for Qwen3.8-27B decode").

## Motivation
At small batch, each Qwen3.8-27B decode step runs about 21 small launches per GDN layer. On gfx950 that leaves weight streaming well below HBM bandwidth. This PR brings the mono-kernel approach from #43516 (Qwen3.5 MoE) to the dense 27B model:
- at TP1, at most two persistent launches per decoder layer;
- at TP2, one launch for each rank's share of each layer's GDN mixer and FFN, between the stock all-reduces.

## Modifications
- **K2** (`qwen3_5_mono_flydsl/dense_ffn.py`), all 64 layers. One persistent launch runs:
  - at TP1: out_proj / o_proj and the residual add, post_attention_layernorm, then the MXFP4 gate_up, SiLU-mul and down projections;
  - at TP2: the rank's half of the intermediate (gate_up, SiLU-mul, down), from the stock post_attention_layernorm's output, up to down's partial.
- **K1** (`qwen3_5_mono_flydsl/dense_gdn.py`), the 48 GDN layers. One persistent launch runs:
  - at TP1: everything from the layer input to K2's input (the residual add and input_layernorm, in_proj_qkvz and in_proj_ba, the conv update, the gated delta rule, the gated norm);
  - at TP2: the rank's half of the GDN heads, from the stock input_layernorm's output, with out_proj folded in, up to the rank's out_proj partial. Nothing crosses ranks inside the kernel.
- **Runtime** (`srt/layers/qwen3_8_dense_mono.py`) plus small hooks in `qwen3_5.py`.
  - **When it runs:** pure decode steps no wider than a kernel's row cap, on gfx950, at TP1 or TP2, with PP1 and no spec decode.
    - Every other step runs the stock layers, and attention layers keep the stock attention core.
    - At TP2 the stock layers run the step, and the kernels replace the rank's GDN mixer and FFN. The all-reduces, the norms, attention and its o_proj stay stock.
    - There is no fused all-reduce epilogue in this PR.
  - **Row caps by TP:** 4 rows for both K1 and K2 at TP1, and 8 rows for both at TP2.
    - `SGLANG_QWEN3_8_DENSE_MONO_K1_MAX_ROWS` / `_K2_MAX_ROWS` override them: up to 8 at TP1, 16 at TP2.
    - The chosen caps are logged once per rank.
    - A step wider than one kernel's cap runs that part stock.
    - The TP1 defaults come from an MI355X cap sweep. Set both overrides to 8 to take steps of up to 8 rows.
  - **Weights:** a (16,16)-shuffled copy of the MXFP4 weights, made before decode graph capture: 10.8 GiB at TP1 (8.9 GiB with K1 off). Prefill keeps the stock GEMMs on the checkpoint layout.
  - **Guards.** The path stays off and logs the cause in any of these cases:
    - an MXFP4 linear is not a plain Quark MXFP4 weight on the Triton `gemm_afp4wfp4` path;
    - a weight is already preshuffled (`is_shuffled` / `aiter_bpreshuffled`), so nothing is shuffled twice;
    - a weight was dequantized to bf16 (`AITER_FORCE_A8W4`);
    - GemmaRMSNorm is not on its ROCm Triton kernel (needs `SGLANG_USE_AITER=1`);
    - fewer than 256 CUs;
    - hierarchical cache or LMCache.
  - **Self-checks.** After the shuffle, K1 and K2 are compared with the layers' own stock modules by `torch.equal`, at widths 1, min(8, cap) and the cap.
    - K2 checks its output and residual (TP1), or this rank's down partial (TP2).
    - K1 checks its core, residual (TP1), conv state and SSM state with a pad row, and with the fold, the out_proj partial.
    - Any mismatch turns the path off with a warning. At TP2 the ranks agree: the path is off on all ranks if any rank is off.
- **Flags:**
  - `SGLANG_QWEN3_8_DENSE_MONO` (default off) turns the path on.
  - `SGLANG_QWEN3_8_DENSE_MONO_OPROJ` (default on): at TP1 it folds out_proj / o_proj into K2; at TP2 it folds each GDN layer's out_proj into K1.
  - `SGLANG_QWEN3_8_DENSE_MONO_K1` (default on) enables K1.
  - `SGLANG_QWEN3_8_DENSE_MONO_K2` (default on) runs K2 at TP2.
  - `SGLANG_QWEN3_8_DENSE_MONO_K1_MAX_ROWS` / `_K2_MAX_ROWS` (unset: the TP's default caps above).

### Exact by construction
Every GEMM in K2 reproduces aiter's `gemm_afp4wfp4` arithmetic, not only its result. For each width and TP:
- it takes the stock config's split-K count: gate_up 2 splits up to 8 rows, else 1; down 2 at TP1 up to 8 rows, else 1; the TP1 o_proj fold 4;
- it runs each split as one chain of scaled MFMAs over the K steps in order, from a zero accumulator;
- it adds the split partials in the split-K reduce's order (`tl.sum`'s pairwise tree);
- it rounds to bf16 once.

The MFMA shape (16x16x128 vs stock's 32x32x64) does not change the bits. The out_proj fold in K1 also uses the stock split at each width: 3 splits up to 8 rows, 1 at 9..16, as aiter's `gemm_afp4wfp4` configs choose.

`StockChainTest` builds rows whose exact sums need more than 24 bits: a large block and its negation next to small ones, across quarter and split boundaries. The same GEMMs summed in another K grouping differ from stock there, and K2 matches stock at every width.

## Accuracy
Both kernels are bit-exact with the stock decode path: Triton `gemm_afp4wfp4` with `dynamic_mxfp4_quant`, GemmaRMSNorm's ROCm Triton kernel, SiluAndMul, the Triton conv update, the packed gated delta rule kernel and RMSNormGated. They use the stock kernels' lane layouts, reduction orders and math lowerings on gfx950, including the FMAs that Triton contracts and the raw `v_sqrt` / `v_exp` / `v_log`.
- **Unit tests** (`test/registered/amd/test_qwen3_8_dense_mono.py`, AMD gfx950 CI, skipped elsewhere), on random MXFP4 weights:
  - widths 1–8 at TP1, and 1–16 on both ranks' slices at TP2;
  - K2: output and residual (TP1), down partial (TP2);
  - K1: core, residual, conv state and SSM state, on the first-layer path and with pad rows (slot -1), plus the folded out_proj partial at TP2;
  - `StockChainTest` and `StockSplitTest` (stock split counts and chains), `StockAccumulationTest` (stock GEMM == bf16(fp32(exact sum)) at K 5120 / 17408 / 8704), `RowCapsTest` (default caps by TP and overrides);
  - a norm reduced in a different order fails these tests: it changes an MXFP4 code on about 1 row in 640.
- **End to end** (GSM8K, 5-shot, greedy):
  - TP1: 0.940 with the flag on and 0.940 with it off, and all 200 generated texts are identical between on and off.
  - TP2 (cap 8): 0.935 on and off, and 200/200 greedy completions are identical to stock. With caps 16: 0.940 (stock 0.940). After the exact-by-construction K2: 0.940 at TP1 and 0.935 at TP2, unchanged.
  - Batched greedy texts at batch 16 cannot be compared directly. Stock is not self-deterministic there (115/128), because step widths depend on prefill admission and stock's GEMM partition changes between M 8 and 9. Every mono configuration agrees with every other at 123–128/128.
