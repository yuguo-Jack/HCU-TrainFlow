# [Perf] [NVIDIA] dsv4: Swap-AB sparse decode attention up to 24 rows with PDL and a narrow combine

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/44733) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

> (auto generated by Claude)

Split from #44626 by @YAMY1234 (the sparse decode attention part), without the `--enable-dsv41-low-latency-decode` switch.

## Modifications

- `can_use_swapab_attention` takes up to `SM100_MAX_ROWS = 24` rows (was 8). Past 8 rows, SM100 used FlashMLA on 64 zero-padded heads; swap-AB is faster up to 24 rows, and FlashMLA wins from 32.
- `swapab_attention` launches the split-KV kernel and the combine with PDL (`pdl=True` by default). `partial_gluon` waits before its first load and triggers its dependents after its last global load. The combine waits, then triggers right away, so `fused_rope_wo_a` streams its weights during the combine.
- Narrow combine (`narrow_combine=True` by default): one-warp programs over 128 columns when there is an extra key range.
- gfx950 is unchanged: `swapab_gluon_hip` passes `pdl=False, narrow_combine=False` and still dispatches at most 8 rows.

## Accuracy

B300: `test_swapab_attention.py` passes. Ad hoc cases at 1-24 rows (eager and CUDA graph, with and without PDL / narrow combine) match the fp64 reference at the bf16 rounding floor. PDL and the trigger position are bit-identical to the non-PDL path. At 9-24 rows, swap-AB is closer to the reference than FlashMLA (RMS error 1.00x vs 1.27-1.38x the bf16 floor).

## Speed

B300, one CUDA graph of `fused_q_norm_rope` -> attention -> `fused_rope_wo_a_bf16` (all with PDL), cold L2, us, median of 5 processes:

| Rows | main | this PR | Speedup |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 | 19.37 | 16.71 | 1.16x |
| 8 | 19.63 | 16.85 | 1.17x |
| 12 | 29.14 | 17.28 | 1.69x |
| 16 | 29.19 | 20.24 | 1.44x |
| 24 | 29.58 | 21.52 | 1.37x |

PDL alone accounts for 1.6-2.5 us of the chain at every row count. In the end-to-end decode of DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on GB300 (TP4, EP1, DSpark), sparse attention per step drops from 824 to 363 us at batch 2 (12 rows) and from 827 to 519 us at batch 4 (24 rows).
