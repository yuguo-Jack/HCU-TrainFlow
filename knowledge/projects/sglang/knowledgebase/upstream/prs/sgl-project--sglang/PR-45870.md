# [CI] [AMD] diffusion: Run more than one multimodal-gen matrix job at a time

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45870) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## What

Raise `max-parallel` on the two AMD multimodal-gen matrices in `pr-test-amd.yml` from 1 to 4 (`multimodal-gen-test-1-gpu-amd`) and 2 (`multimodal-gen-test-2-gpu-amd`) on mi300. Dispatches with any other `runner_arch` keep 1.

## Why

Each matrix is image × partition, and `max-parallel: 1` runs every entry strictly one after another. Since #35603 and #38632, the daily 17:30 schedule runs two images (rocm724 and rocm720), so the 1-GPU job runs 8 jobs in series and the 2-GPU job runs 4.

In the scheduled runs below, each job group's span equals the sum of its job times, so no time was spent waiting for runners:

| Run | 1-GPU multimodal | 2-GPU multimodal |
|---|---|---|
| [39467849653](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39467849653) (7.2.4 + 7.2.0) | 8 jobs, 6.1 h | 4 jobs, 9.1 h |
| [39295915056](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39295915056) (7.2.4 + 7.2.0) | 8 jobs, 6.3 h | 4 jobs, 10.5 h |
| [39421857090](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39421857090) (rocm10) | 4 jobs, 2.3 h | 2 jobs, 5.2 h |

These two groups are the last jobs to finish in those runs.

## Notes for the reviewer

- The limit dates from #13760 (December 2025): "Run one at a time to avoid eviction from resource exhaustion during AITER kernel JIT". It was dropped in #24614, restored by #31409 when PR jobs moved to MI300, and carried into the consolidated workflow by #38632. This PR keeps a throttle rather than removing it. 4 matches `stage-b-test-1-gpu-small-amd` on the same 1-GPU pool. `max-parallel` is also per run: multimodal jobs from different PR runs already share these pools at the same time.
- Expected effect on the 7.2 schedule is roughly 6 h to 2 h for the 1-GPU group, and about half for the 2-GPU group. More once #45861 stops partition 1 from running into its timeout.
- If the eviction problem is still real on MI300, it should show as pods lost mid-job in the validation run below and in the first scheduled runs; reverting is a one-line change.

## Test plan

- Dispatched both multimodal stages on rocm10 from this commit: [run 39833433672](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39833433672). All six jobs (4 × 1-GPU, 2 × 2-GPU) started together at 18:41 UTC and none was evicted. The 1-GPU group finished in 53 min, against 2.3 h in the comparable scheduled rocm10 run, and the 2-GPU group in 3.1 h, against 5.2 h, held up only by the LTX-2.5 timeout that #45861 removes. The two red jobs are pre-existing: 2-GPU partition 0 is that LTX-2.5 case, and 1-GPU partition 3 fails `test_cosmos_dreams.py::test_overlap_and_serial_vae_inputs_match` in its unit-test step, as it does on `main` ([run 39421857090](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39421857090)).
