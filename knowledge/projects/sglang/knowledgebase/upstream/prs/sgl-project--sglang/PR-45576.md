## Motivation



| RCCL | DDA blocks | Total tok/s | Median TTFT (s) | Median TPOT (ms) | Median ITL (ms) | P99 ITL (ms) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2.30.7 bundled, default | 256 | 7730 | 22.47 | 52.64 | 30.59 | 40.14 |
| 2.30.7 bundled, `MAXBLOCKS=64` | 64 | 8135 | 22.71 | 48.64 | 27.75 | 31.21 |
| 2.32.3 latest, default | 96 | 8247 | 21.36 | 49.04 | 28.67 | 36.78 |
| 2.32.3 latest, `MAXBLOCKS=64` | 64 | 8173 | 22.23 | 48.85 | 27.94 | 31.40 |

- The bundled 2.30.7 (`2.30.7-HEAD:7da4302`) uses a fixed 256 DDA fabric blocks. Capping at 64 gives +5.2% throughput, -9.3% median ITL and -22% p99 ITL.
- 2.32.3 (rocm-systems develop @ `4ca8623`) defaults to 96 blocks here. That alone gives the best throughput (+6.7%) and TTFT (-5.0%) and -6.3% median ITL, but p99 ITL improves by only 8.4%. The first run in the fresh container was discarded as JIT warm-up.
- On 2.32.3, `MAXBLOCKS=64` still trades about 4% TTFT for -15% p99 ITL and -2.5% median ITL; throughput is flat.
- At TP4, `MAXBLOCKS=64` makes no difference (all metrics within +/-0.4%).

**Background (4GPUs)**
We first found this regression with RCCL 2.30.7. At conc=64 (8192 in / 1024 out), median ITL went from 40.59 ms on stock 2.30.4 to 50.85 ms (+25%), although throughput improved by 6.8%. That 2.30.7 was built by us from rocm-systems @ `44704e4` against the ROCm 10 SDK in `rocm/sgl-dev:v0.5.19-rocm10-mi45x-20260913`. Root cause: `RCCL_DDA_FABRIC_MAXBLOCKS` defaults to 192, so the DDA fabric barrier issues 768 flag writes per all-reduce (96 on 2.30.4). Capping it at 64 brought median ITL to 38.63 ms (-4.8% vs stock) and throughput to +20.4% vs stock.
