## Motivation

Issue: #39700 and #42170. In prefill node, Decoder-swa-replay and BCG cannot be turned on together. This PR supports their compatibility.
<img width="1716" height="294" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f6d76e6d-2a45-4119-82c3-0149a63103bc" />


## Modifications

**Developed based on #40333**. To avoid introducing complexity, this PR let the **encoder be captured into BCG** and the **decoder remain eager** when BCG and --encoder-decoder-swa-bounded-replay are turned on together.
