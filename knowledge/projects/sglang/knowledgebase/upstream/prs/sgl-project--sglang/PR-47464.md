## Motivation

The cookbook's **Agentic Long-Context Deployment** sections (#42726, #43083, #43799, #43983, #44065, #44228, #44654, #44712, #45039, #45111, #45311, #45513, #45749, #46005, #46506, #46547, #46807, #46948, #47027, #47214, #47303) mirror SemiAnalysis InferenceX AgentX submissions as raw launch commands, each linked to the run that measured it. The configurations are mirrored from SemiAnalysis submissions. This PR:

1. **Syncs to InferenceX `main`** (`ea8b3439`). Three PRs have merged, and their lanes now come from their dashboard runs instead of the PRs:
   - DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash B300 ([InferenceX #4627](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4627), 2026-10-29): `dsv41flash-fp4-b300-sglang-agentic-dspark` and `-dspark-lowconc`, previously in progress.
   - MiniMax-M3.1-Flash B300 TP2 ([InferenceX #4524](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4524)), and the shortest-prefill-first TP2 points added on top of it ([InferenceX #4647](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4647), [run 40895671658](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/actions/runs/40895671658), append-only on 2026-10-29). Each point is credited to the run that measured it. #47303 had added the #4647 points to the data by hand; they are now generated by the sync.
2. **Adds Muse Spark Open updates** from two in-progress InferenceX PRs:
   - GB200 SGLang, retuned per concurrency ([InferenceX #4670](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4670)).
   - GB300 SGLang, with two new keys, `musespark-fp4-gb300-sglang-agentic-dflash-multinode` and `-dflash-nightly1019` ([InferenceX #4454](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4454), overlaid again after #47214).
3. **Keeps the DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash GB300 overlays** ([InferenceX #4603](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4603), and [InferenceX #4595](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4595) at its new head) and the Qwen3.5 GB300 overlay ([InferenceX #4333](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4333)).
