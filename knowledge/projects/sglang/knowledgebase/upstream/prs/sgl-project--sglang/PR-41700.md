# [Refactor] transformers-backend: Upgrade to 5.21.0, fix embedding support, and improve inference performance

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/41700) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Summary

Refactors the Transformers backend into focused modules, fixes embedding and model loading issues, and improves inference performance. This PR touches the transformers backend only and has no impact on native SGLang inference.

- Fix pooling, embedding projections, reranking heads, token-type IDs, and padding handling.
- Fix MoE routing, MLA loading, multimodal MLP fusion, and EAGLE3 target support.
- Use SGLang projection, normalization, and rotary kernels where compatible.
- Remove unnecessary tensor copies around attention and improve encoder backend selection.

## Performance

Measured on NVIDIA B200 GPUs with BF16, Transformers 5.15.0(follows PR to update to 5.17.0 https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/39012), and backend optimizations enabled. Values are median output tokens per second with higher is better. TP1 uses one GPU, and TP2 uses two.

### Qwen3-8B

Three fresh-server runs per configuration.

| Input tokens | Output tokens | Concurrency | Native TP1 | Transformers TP1 | Native TP2 | Transformers TP2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 128 | 128 | 1 | 274 | 248 | 361 | 323 |
| 128 | 128 | 32 | 7,236 | 6,669 | 9,564 | 8,493 |
| 1,024 | 128 | 1 | 266 | 241 | 358 | 317 |
| 1,024 | 128 | 32 | 4,258 | 3,955 | 6,224 | 5,749 |
| 8,192 | 128 | 8 | 675 | 628 | 1,055 | 977 |
| 128 | 1,024 | 8 | 2,109 | 1,954 | 2,841 | 2,636 |

The Transformers backend reaches **89 - 93% of native throughput** across these workloads. 

### DeepSeek-V2-Lite

TP1 with FlashInfer, two runs per configuration.

| Input tokens | Output tokens | Concurrency | Native | Transformers | Relative throughput |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,024 | 128 | 32 | 3,491 | 2,775 | 80% |
| 4,096 | 64 | 8 | 787 | 532 | 68% |

## Validation and scope

- 30 tests passed in the reported GPU regression suite.
- Dense generation compared against Hugging Face and native SGLang at TP1 and TP2.
- Embedding and reranking checks covered BGE, MiniLM, LaBSE, ModernBERT, and MS MARCO.
- Additional checks covered Qwen MoE, Qwen2.5-VL, EAGLE3, and synthetic LoRA adapters.
