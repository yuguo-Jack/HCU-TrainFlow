# [Perf] [AMD] Kimi-K3: DFlash prep/tail graphs and fused post-verify

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/46655) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

The DFlash draft prep and post-verify tail (draft KV store, accept/commit bookkeeping) ran eagerly as many small launches each step.

## Modifications

- The eager draft block moves out of `forward_batch_generation`.
- Prep and tail are captured as graphs.
- The post-verify kernels and the draft argmax pack are fused.

## Accuracy Tests

- `test_k3_dflash_post_fusions.py`, `test_k3_draft_argmax_pack.py`, `test_dflash_accept_bonus_buffers.py` and `test_dflash_worker_platform_hooks.py`: 11 pass.

Full K3 stack on MI355X TP8 (DFlash, prod config) vs B300, McNemar:
- GPQA-D 89.4 vs 87.9 (p=0.55)
- AIME25 96.7 vs 93.3 (p=1.0)
- MATH500 92.2 vs 92.2 (p=1.0)
- MMLU-Pro 89.6 vs 90.0 (p=0.77)
- GSM8K 97.2%

## Speed Tests and Profiling

- Turning it off costs +0.2/-10.3/-1.1% on the agentic replay and -3.4/-1.6% at 90k prefix (mean -3.2%).

Speed numbers: throughput change from turning this feature off on the full stack, agentic replay at C=1/8/32 and 90k-prefix at C=1/8.
