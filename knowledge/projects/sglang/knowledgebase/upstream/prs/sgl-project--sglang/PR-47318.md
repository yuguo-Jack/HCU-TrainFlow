# [Perf] dspark: Cut the DeepSeek-V4.1 draft and accept launches

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47318) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

Supersedes sgl-project/sglang#45663. Stacked on #47316: its two commits show here until it merges.

## Motivation

At TP4 and TP8, a DeepSeek-V4.1 DSpark draft runs its Markov head over a TP-sharded vocab, five to seven positions a block. Each position ran:
- an embedding lookup;
- the W2-shard GEMV;
- a partial launch;
- the candidates' all-gather;
- a finish launch;
- a TP broadcast of the tokens.

Around the Markov head:
- the draft parameters took four launches;
- the draft KV commit copied each stage's KV slice;
- the in-graph accept copied its six results into its static buffers.

On MI350X with the AMD monokernels, these launches are a visible share of a batch-1 step.

## Changes from #45663

- Rebased on main.
- sgl-project/sglang#44465 already keeps the draft logits on their shards on HIP, so #45663's "a batch that does not sample leaves the draft logits sharded" is dropped.
- The commit that changes behavior is now opt-in: the fused bias GEMV can draft differently on near-ties.
- The Markov fold is added, opt-in.

## Modifications

Same outputs as main, always on:
- **The draft sampling parameters in one launch** (`fill_draft_params`): the temperature clamp, the greedy mask and the step temperatures.
- **The draft KV commit takes strided slices on HIP.** HIP's KV store (`fused_k_norm_rope_flashmla`) reads an explicit row stride, as Blackwell's does.
- **The in-graph accept writes its static buffers.** The finalize kernel writes its three lengths there and copies `correct_len` and the bonus through. The out-token kernel fills its buffer and widens `correct_len` as it loads it. Seven launches a step go.

Opt-in, children of `SGLANG_ENABLE_AMD_MONO_RUNTIME_OPTIMIZATION`:
- **The Markov step's bias GEMV in the launch that adds it** (`SGLANG_OPT_DSV41_DSPARK_MARKOV_FUSED_BIAS`).
  - A CTA a 64-column tile reads its W2 rows once for a 16-row block, rounds the bias to BF16 as `F.linear` does, and adds it.
  - It sums in another order than hipBLASLt, so near ties can draft differently. The accept verifies the drafts either way.
- **A Markov step's tokens picked in the next step's launch** (`SGLANG_OPT_DSV41_DSPARK_MARKOV_FOLD`). With the fused GEMV, and the kept step logits on the shard:
  - each step's launch picks the previous step's tokens from its gathered candidates;
  - only the last step runs a finish launch;
  - every rank picks from the same gathered candidates, so the block's tokens are broadcast once instead of every step.

  The finish kernel now takes its output's stride. The sharded accept passes its own and returns identical results.
