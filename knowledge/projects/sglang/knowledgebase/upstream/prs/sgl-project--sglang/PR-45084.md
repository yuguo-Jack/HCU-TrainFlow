# [Feat] thinking-budget: Decouple thinking budget from --enable-strict-thinking

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45084) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

`--enable-strict-thinking` bundled two independent features: blocking model-specific excluded tokens (tool-call markers, EOS) during the thinking phase, and enforcing a thinking budget. Users who only need an exact thinking cap had to turn on strict thinking — and its excluded-token blocking — to get one.

## What changed

- A thinking budget now works with only `--reasoning-parser` and a token-filtering grammar backend (`xgrammar` is the default). `--enable-strict-thinking` keeps a single meaning — excluded-token blocking — and behavior with the flag on is unchanged.
- Budget sources: the `SGLANG_MAX_THINK_TOKENS` server-wide cap, per-request `max_thinking_tokens` on `/generate`, and `custom_params.thinking_budget` on chat completions. `ReasonerGrammarObject.enable_token_filter` is now derived from the filter callable plus the presence of excluded tokens or a budget, so a per-request budget applied after construction activates filtering.
- Budgets the runtime cannot enforce are rejected instead of silently ignored:
  - `/generate` `max_thinking_tokens` requires `--reasoning-parser` and a token-filtering grammar backend (previously `--enable-strict-thinking`).
  - A budgeted request whose resolved grammar cannot enforce it (no reasoning parser, a non-filtering backend, or a grammar that owns the full assistant turn such as `full_assistant_ebnf` or the canonical auto tool-call structural tag) is aborted with a clear error, on both cache hits and compiled futures.
  - `SGLANG_MAX_THINK_TOKENS` fails startup without `--reasoning-parser` / think-end token ids / a `ReasonerGrammarBackend`, with `--skip-tokenizer-init`, and under UNO speculative decoding; per-request budgets under UNO are rejected.
  - A `ThinkingBudgetLogitProcessor` (or subclass) on the request owns `custom_params.thinking_budget`; any other processor, an empty processor string, or one that fails to deserialize leaves the budget to the grammar path.
- `docs/advanced_features/thinking_budget.mdx` updated to the new contract.

## Tests

- New and updated unit tests: budget enforcement without the strict flag (env and per-request), startup fail-fast validation, rejection paths (UNO, non-enforceable grammars on both cache paths), processor ownership (empty string, unrelated processors, subclasses), and the pre-existing strict-thinking paths.
- `pytest test/registered/unit/constrained/ test/registered/unit/parser/test_reasoning_parser.py test/registered/unit/managers/test_tokenizer_manager_rid_cleanup.py test/registered/unit/spec/test_uno_tree_config.py`: **343 passed, 225 subtests passed** (CPU).
- `pre-commit` green on all changed files.
