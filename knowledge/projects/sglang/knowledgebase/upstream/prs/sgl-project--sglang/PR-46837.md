## Motivation

- Though DFlash now supports DPA mode, SGLang runs in eager mode, resulting launch overhead.
- Update DFlash draft & verify batch planning to capture graphs and replay them in both active and idle ranks.

## Modifications

1. Allow draft workers to initialize CUDA Graph when DPA is enabled. This lets `batch.can_run_decode_cuda_graph=True`.
2. Make DFlash draft workers produce their drafts **locally** to avoid cross-DP collective collision.
3. Allow idle target worker to replay its graph.
4. Align idle target worker's custom masks.
