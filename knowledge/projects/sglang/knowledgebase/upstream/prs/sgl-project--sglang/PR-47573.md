# [Doc] minimax-m3: GB300 MXFP8 recipe MoE runner deep_gemm -> flashinfer_trtllm (conditional on the next FlashInfer release)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47573) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

Sibling recipe change to #47187, MoE side. On GB300 (TP4, MXFP8 recipe), FlashInfer's fused MXFP8 MoE (`trtllm_fp8_block_scale_moe`) beats the recipe's DeepGEMM grouped-masked composition ~10.6x GPU-time-weighted across the serve's full measured MoE workload census (all 55 classes, decode and prefill regimes), with MoE ≈ a third of the serve's measured GPU time. End-to-end (900 s agentic-replay serving benchmark, normalized per-user interactivity, tok/s/user), switching the runner nets **+8.1% median / +9.7% p90 at 20 concurrent sessions** over `deep_gemm`, attribution controlled by a FlashInfer-pin-bump-only arm that moved nothing outside the ±3.3% noise band. Dispatch was verified per-rank (API-call census on all 4 TP ranks) with a run contract that fails on any `deep_gemm` fallback.

## Modifications

Docs only: the GB300 / MXFP8 / balanced cell in `docs/src/snippets/configs/MiniMaxAI/minimax-m3.jsx` moves `--moe-runner-backend deep_gemm` → `flashinfer_trtllm`, and the M3 page gains a Note stating the gains, the FlashInfer floor, and the current-release caveat. Other platforms keep `deep_gemm`. Depends on the one-line MiniMax2 routing declaration fix (#47572) — without it the FlashInfer runner cannot start on M3 at all.

> **Dependency: conditional on the next FlashInfer release.** The unconditional form of this recipe needs the first FlashInfer release containing [flashinfer#8042](https://github.com/flashinfer-ai/flashinfer/pull/8042) (fused shared experts beyond DeepSeekV3 routing — the extension we requested in [flashinfer#7810](https://github.com/flashinfer-ai/flashinfer/issues/7810) and validated on GB300 against the PR head). With it, shared-expert fusion adds **+13.1% median interactivity at concurrency 8 and +7.5% median / +14.3% p90 at concurrency 20** on top of the runner switch, and single-stream flips ahead of `deep_gemm` (~+8% median) — no concurrency caveat left. On today's FlashInfer releases (without #8042), SGLang auto-disables shared-expert fusion on this runner (`srt/arg_groups/overrides.py`); the switch is then positive for concurrent serving (≥ ~8 sessions) but regresses single-stream ~9% median (the unfused shared expert runs as two extra dense MXFP8 GEMMs per layer per step). The page Note states both. We kept the PR open rather than Draft to match how #47187 carries its image dependency; happy to convert to Draft if preferred. A follow-up PR enabling shared-expert fusion on this runner (passing `num_fused_shared_experts` through the fp8 block-scale path; ~20 lines, already validated by us against the FlashInfer PR head) will come once the FlashInfer release ships.

## Numbers (GB300 sm103, TP4, MXFP8, 900 s agentic replays, zero request errors on every run)

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 53.7 | ~ -9% | 57.2 (**+8%** vs deep_gemm; +16.2% vs unfused) |
| 8 | 36.7 | 37.8 (+3.4%) | 41.0 (**+13.1%** vs unfused) |
| 20 | 23.6 | 25.6 (**+8.1%**, p90 +9.7%) | 28.1 (**+7.5%** more, p90 +14.3%) |
| 40 | 12.2 | 12.1 (-1.3%, queue-bound band) | 11.4 (-3.9% vs unfused, queue-bound band; most completed requests) |

Kernel-level numerics gated against fp32 references throughout (max 6.9%); no end-to-end accuracy benchmark was run — a standard accuracy check on the team's verification pipeline would be a welcome gate at merge.
