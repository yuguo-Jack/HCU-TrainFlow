## Motivation

Issue #39700. When using CP on deepseek v4.1, _forward_prepare_low_ratio_multi_stream will be disabled. This PR supports their compatibility.

## Modifications

**Developed based on #40548 and need #40548 to be merged first.** The implementaion is similar with #39588. 
1. **Do CP allgather in advance on the default stream**. And the multistream forward prepare accepts the pre-gathered kv score as input. No CP allgather will be performed inside the multistream forward prepare.
2. After cp materialization, store_swa_k, compressor and indexer(operations before mqa_logits computation) are overlapped in multiple streams.
3. In BCG for DSV4.1, enable the indexer and compressor to be captured into graph.
