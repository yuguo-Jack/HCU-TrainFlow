# [CI] quant: Lower the Mixtral AWQ MMLU threshold to 0.62 to clear its run-to-run spread

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47957) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

`TestAWQ.test_mmlu` in `test/registered/e2e/quantization/test_awq.py` (Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct AWQ-INT4, sgl-eval MMLU, 256 questions) is tracked as T16 in #42752. It failed in `base-b-test-1-gpu-large` on #46654 with 0.6367 and then 0.6328 on its retry, against `assertGreater(score, 0.64)`.

To see where the threshold sits, I collected every score this test logged (`[METRIC] mmlu_score=...`) in main's scheduled `pr-test` runs from 10-03 to 11-02: 48 attempts over 44 runs.

| score | 0.633 | 0.637 | 0.641 | 0.645 | 0.648 | 0.652 | 0.656 | 0.660 | 0.664 | 0.668 | 0.672 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| attempts | 2 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 7 | 11 | 5 | 4 | 1 | 2 |

- The mean is 0.652 and the sd is 0.0095, about 2.5 of the 256 questions. The mean was the same before and after 10-20 (0.6522 vs 0.6527), so the scores aren't drifting.
- 0.64 sits 1.3 sd below the mean. 5 of the 48 attempts scored below it.
- The built-in retry doesn't absorb this: main's 10-30 23:06 scheduled run failed both attempts (0.6328, 0.6367), the same signature as the #46654 failure.

The eval runs at temperature 0, but scores still vary between runs (two attempts in one job differ by up to 0.0078). So this is run-to-run spread, not a regression.

## Modifications

Lower the threshold to 0.62: 3.4 sd below the mean and 0.013 below the lowest score observed. A real AWQ breakage still fails it by a wide margin. The change is test-only.

This follows #36570, which lowered the AWQ Marlin MMLU threshold in the same file from 0.83 to 0.80 for the same reason.
