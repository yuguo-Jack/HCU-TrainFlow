# [AMD][CI] Partition stage-b-test-1-gpu-small-amd-mi35x to stop the 30-min timeout

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/41973) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

`stage-b-test-1-gpu-small-amd-mi35x` has failed on every scheduled PR Test AMD run since 2026-09-22 (and on the AITER Scout since 2026-09-19). Part of that is the suite outgrowing its step limit: it is the only Stage B job that still runs unpartitioned under a 30-minute `Run test` step, while the suite has grown steadily:

| Date | Files | est total |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-19 | 30 | 24.5 min |
| 2026-09-22 | 36 | 32.5 min |
| 2026-09-27 | 44 | 37.9 min |
| 2026-09-29 | 50 | 41.6 min |
| 2026-09-30 | 51 | 43.6 min |

The rocm10 leg hit `The action 'Run test' has timed out after 30 minutes` on 09-29 and 09-30. On 09-30 ([job](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/36714930323/job/109885486387)) it printed `Test Summary: 51/51 passed` one second before the limit and was still marked failed.

Two files account for most of the rocm10 time and are badly under-estimated, so the suite total understates the real cost:

| File | est_time | observed (36 runs) |
|---|---|---|
| `e2e/quantization/test_quark_mxfp4.py` | 106s | mean 446s, max 605s |
| `kernels/ops/attention/test_fp4_indexer_hip.py` | 60s | ~600s on rocm10, ~40s on rocm72 (addressed separately in #41793) |

## Modifications

- `pr-test-amd.yml`: split `stage-b-test-1-gpu-small-amd-mi35x` into two LPT partitions (`part: [0, 1]`, `--auto-partition-id/--auto-partition-size 2`), raise the step limit 30 → 45 min, and add the part index to the job name, matching the other partitioned Stage B jobs.
- `test_quark_mxfp4.py`: `est_time` 106 → 450 so the partitioner places it correctly.

Replaying the 09-30 rocm10 per-file timings through the same LPT split gives ~14 min and ~16 min per part (est 25.4 min each), leaving ample headroom under 45 min. Cost: 6 jobs per run on `linux-mi35x-gpu-1` instead of 3.

Not in scope: `test_quark_mxfp4.py` also flakes on GSM8K thresholds that sit inside run-to-run noise (`TestNVFP4ToMXFP4MOETP1` > 0.85, min seen 0.798; `TestFP8ToMXFP4MOETP1` > 0.92, min seen 0.900). That is an accuracy-bar decision for the test owners and will be a separate PR.
