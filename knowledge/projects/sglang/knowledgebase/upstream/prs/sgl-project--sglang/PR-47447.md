# [Perf] [AMD] kimi-k3: Fuse attention o_proj's GEMM with its attention-TP sum via mori

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47447) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

Kimi-K3 keeps attention in BF16; only the routed experts are MXFP4. Its attention `o_proj` (`[M, 1536] x [7168, 1536]` at TP8) is followed by an attention-TP sum that the stage boundary completes.

On ROCm gfx950, mori's `GemmAllReduceOp(quant="bf16")` (ROCm/mori#872) pushes each destination's row band to the SDMA engines from the GEMM epilogue. The reduce-scatter half of the sum therefore runs inside the GEMM.

The all-gather half cannot overlap the GEMM that produces its input. Under SP-MoE (EP a2a with attention TP) it is not needed at all: the FFN read only takes this rank's slice. That is where most of the gain is.

**Draft.** This depends on `GemmAllReduceOp(quant="bf16")` and `GemmAllReduceOp.reduce_scatter()` from ROCm/mori#872, which is not merged yet.

## Modifications

Opt in with `SGLANG_MORI_OPROJ_AR=1`; it needs `MORI_ENABLE_SDMA=1`. Default off, ROCm only, attention DP off.

Only prefill is fused: M >= 2048 (`SGLANG_MORI_OPROJ_AR_MIN_M`) with padding fill >= 0.85. Decode, CUDA-graph capture and small batches take the ordinary path.

- **New `srt/layers/communication/mori_gemm_ar_bf16.py`:**
  - **Load time.** One cco communicator over attention TP, and one op per o_proj shape. The window is built in `post_load_weights`, before the memory profiler runs. Every padded M is precompiled, then a self-test proves the SDMA puts move bytes. Every stage is agreed across ranks over the CPU group, so either all ranks fuse or none do.
  - **Hook point.** The hook is `o_proj.quant_method.apply`, not `forward`, because K3 MLA wraps `o_proj.forward` with its output gate (`x * sigmoid(gate)` before the GEMM).
  - **SP-MoE layers.** o_proj runs GEMM + reduce-scatter and returns a full-shaped placeholder. `reduce_scatter_add`, a scattering `ReadoutFusion`, hands the boundary this rank's reduced rows plus its residual. Each rank's `tensor_split` rows are laid at the start of its mori band, so ragged M matches the boundary's split.
  - **Other layers** (K3's dense layer 0). o_proj runs GEMM + all-reduce. The attention stage declares a `sum_impl` that passes exactly that output through and all-reduces anything else.
  - **Unconsumed output.** If a fused output was never taken by the boundary, the next o_proj raises instead of letting it be reduced twice.
- **`models/kimi_k3.py`.**
  - `_stage_facts` declares the `sum_impl` on non-SP-MoE layers.
  - `_ffn_input_fusions` offers the scattering fusion on SP-MoE layers.
  - `post_load_weights` prepares the layers.
- **`environ.py`.** Adds `SGLANG_MORI_OPROJ_AR`, `_MIN_M`, `_CLONE` (copy out of the window) and `_DEBUG` (per-call relL2 check against GEMM + collective).

Whether a call fuses depends only on M and static shapes, which every attention-TP rank shares, so the ranks never diverge into mori's device-side barriers.

The fused output aliases mori's window, and the next call on the op overwrites it. K3 consumes it first: the FFN read folds it into the residual, and the next attention read materialises a fresh prefix before the next o_proj. `SGLANG_MORI_OPROJ_AR_CLONE=1` gives the same accuracy.

## Accuracy Tests

The checks below use 8x MI355X and the MI35x nightly K3 recipe plus `--chunked-prefill-size 16384 --max-prefill-tokens 16384 --disable-radix-cache`:
- GSM8K: `few_shot_gsm8k`, 1319 questions.
- Long-context greedy: 32 prompts of 2.5-7K tokens, each asking for one item out of a numbered fact list, scored on whether the answer is right.

| arm | GSM8K | greedy lookup |
|---|---:|---:|
| TP8 | 0.960, 0.952, 0.955 | 30/32 |
| TP8 + GEMM+AR | 0.952, 0.955, 0.959 | 29/32 |
| EP8 SP-MoE | 0.951 | 29/32 |
| EP8 SP-MoE + GEMM+RS | 0.958 | 31/32 |

The extra TP8 miss is a first-token near-tie (`'The'` -1.94 vs `' Then'` -2.19 on baseline) that flips. With `SGLANG_MORI_OPROJ_AR_DEBUG=1`, KDA o_proj calls are at relL2 ~5e-5 against GEMM + all-reduce.

Op level (M=16384, N=7168, K=1536, 8 ranks): 300 ragged-M calls interleaving reduce-scatter and all-reduce on one op are all within relL2 1.7e-3 of GEMM + RCCL.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Prefill TTFT is measured with random ids and `max_new_tokens=1`, median of 2 rounds; all arms ran on one node (crsuse2-m2m-v3-054).

| | TP8 | EP8 SP-MoE | EP8 SP-MoE + GEMM+RS | vs EP8 | vs TP8 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TTFT 8K, conc 1 | 461.2 ms | 367.3 ms | **351.9 ms** | -4.2% | -23.7% |
| TTFT 16K, conc 1 | 875.2 ms | 676.0 ms | **654.2 ms** | -3.2% | -25.3% |
| throughput 8K, conc 8 | 19074 tok/s | 24887 | **25743** | +3.4% | +35.0% |
| throughput 16K, conc 8 | 18856 tok/s | 24454 | **25325** | +3.6% | +34.3% |

- **Per layer.** -21.8 ms per 16K step over 92 layers is ~237 us per layer. That matches the op-level gap: GEMM + RS 636 us vs `torch.mm` + RCCL reduce_scatter 885 us.
- **TP8 + GEMM+AR** (another node, against its own TP8): -1.0% at 16K, -1.9% at 8K. The profile shows why it is small: the server's QuickReduce all-reduce is ~1.0 ms at M=16K, and only the reduce-scatter half overlaps the GEMM. mori is 331 us GEMM + 298 us drain + 45 us reduce + 515 us gather, against 280 + 1000 us baseline.
- **EP8 itself** is most of the gap to TP8. The Kimi-K3 cookbook keeps TP8 as the 8-GPU default because EP a2a buffers compete with DCP's KV savings; DCP was off here.

**EP8 settings used:**
```
--ep-size 8 --moe-a2a-backend mori --deepep-mode normal
SGLANG_MORI_EP_V2=1 SGLANG_MORI_DISPATCH_DTYPE=mxfp8 SGLANG_MORI_RECV_BOUND=1
SGLANG_MORI_NUM_MAX_DISPATCH_TOKENS_PER_RANK=16384 MORI_SHMEM_HEAP_SIZE=34359738368
```
- **`SGLANG_MORI_EP_V2=1` is required.** With `MORI_ENABLE_SDMA=1` sglang falls back to mori EPv1, and K3 EP8 on EPv1 stalled in decode. Forcing EPv2 works with SDMA enabled.
- **EPv2 init deadlocks intermittently.** In 2 of 5 starts, with or without this change, ranks split between `Communicator.register_window` and `_cco_lsa_size`; a restart cleared it. Unrelated to this PR, noted for whoever runs it.
