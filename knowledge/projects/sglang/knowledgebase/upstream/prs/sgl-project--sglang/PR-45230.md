# [sglang-miles] [Diffusion] Stop a rollout at a requested step

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45230) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

DMD2 distillation in miles-diffusion (radixark/miles_diffusion#331) trains the few-step student at a random step `i`, on an input the student generated itself (backward simulation). One rollout has to return the student's input `x_{σ_i}` and its x0 prediction `x̂0_i`, and the steps after `i` are wasted work.

The rest of the sampler already exists here: with `rollout_sde_type="cps"` and `rollout_noise_level=1.0`, each step re-noises its x0 prediction with fresh noise, `x_next = (1-σ') x̂0 + σ' ε`, which is the DMD2 multi-step sampler. The only missing piece is stopping at step `i`.

## Modifications

- `RolloutRequest` / `SamplingParams`: new `rollout_stop_step_index`, default `None` (no behavior change).
- `truncate_rollout_sigmas` (`rollout_scheduler.py`): keeps steps `0..i` of a sigma grid and appends the terminal sigma, `sigmas[:i+1] + sigmas[-1:]`. Step `i` then goes straight to σ=0, where both `cps` and `ode` return `x̂0` exactly, and the engine runs `i+1` DiT forwards instead of `N`. An `i` outside the schedule is rejected. It takes a scheduler's tensor or a plain list.
- `TimestepPreparationStage`: for a rollout request with a stop, cuts the scheduler after `set_timesteps` (`truncate_rollout_schedule`: the sigmas through the helper, `timesteps[:i+1]`).
- `MiniMaxH3TimestepPreparationStage`: cuts the video and audio grids with the same helper, after the PDD check (it validates the full grid) and before the grids are published to `batch.sigmas` / `batch.timesteps`.
- Both stages add the field to their dedup fingerprint, so grouped requests with different stops don't share a schedule. Dynamic batching already keeps them apart: the field is a `SamplingParams` field without `batch_sig_exclude`, so it is in the batch signature.
- `prepare_rollout_if_enabled` rejects a stop when the loop's sigma grid was not cut to `i+2` points. Cosmos3 and LTX-2 run rollouts through this hook, but their own timestep stages don't implement the stop; without the check they would return a full rollout as if it had stopped.

The stop lives in the timestep stages, not in the rollout controller: the controller only replaces the update inside `scheduler.step`, after that step's DiT forward, and `_prepare_denoising_loop` derives the warmup, MoE boundary and cache-dit step counts from the schedule before the hook runs. diffusers stops early the same way (`denoising_end` in the SDXL pipeline cuts the timesteps before the loop). `num_inference_steps` keeps the requested `N`; only the progress bar and the cache-dit step count read it.

With the existing `rollout_return_step_indices=[i, i+1]`, the trajectory holds `x_{σ_i}` followed by `x̂0_i`, so no response field is added.

## Accuracy Tests

FLUX.2-klein-base-4B, 1×B200, 512², 4 steps, `cps` / noise level 1.0, guidance 1, debug tensors on, at the head commit 996dec8c3. Each stopped rollout is compared with a full 4-step rollout that uses the same seed:

| stop `i` | returned σ | `latent_step_indices` | `x_{σ_i}` vs full run | final latent vs `x_i - σ_i·v_i` (bf16) | DiT forwards |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1.0, 0 | [0, 1] | bitwise equal | bitwise equal | 1 |
| 1 | 1.0, 0.9581, 0 | [1, 2] | bitwise equal | bitwise equal | 2 |
| 2 | 1.0, 0.9581, 0.884, 0 | [2, 3] | bitwise equal | bitwise equal | 3 |
| 3 | 1.0, 0.9581, 0.884, 0.7175, 0 | [3, 4] | bitwise equal | bitwise equal | 4 |

Without the field, a full rollout at the head is bitwise equal to `sglang-miles` bff3f6ae7: latents, sigmas, model outputs and the decoded image.

MiniMax-H3 (t2va, 2×B200 TP2, 768p 16:9 4 s, 4 steps, flow shift 12 / audio shift 3, seed 1234; at 22f93b5c2): for `ode` and `cps` (noise level 1.0) and every stop `i` in 0..3, the returned σ is the full grid's `[:i+1] + [0]`, `x_{σ_i}` is bitwise equal to the full run, and the server runs `i+1` denoise steps. With `ode`, the full run's `x_{i+1}` replayed from the stopped x0 is bitwise equal. H3's `cps` floors the predicted-noise weight at `sqrt(1e-12)`, so its x0 differs from `ode`'s by up to 5.25e-6. Without the field, full `ode` / `cps` latents and sigmas are bitwise equal to 077b40c17. The last commit only moves H3's cut into the shared helper; the unit test below pins its grids and prefix at that commit.

## Tests

- `test_scheduler_rollout_unit.py`:
  - A stopped rollout replays the full run's prefix bit for bit and ends on the x0 prediction; it fails if the terminal sigma isn't re-appended. The same holds for H3, whose video and audio grids are cut alike.
  - A stop outside the schedule is rejected.
  - A stop that the timestep stage ignored is rejected by the rollout hook (the case fails without the check), and a cut schedule passes.
- `test_rollout_api.py`: the field is forwarded to the sampling params.
- 55 passed across both files, and 309 passed across the 8 unit files that touch the rollout hooks or the timestep stages (Cosmos3 rollout and H3 PDD included). `pre-commit run` on the changed files is clean. Validation was run locally.
