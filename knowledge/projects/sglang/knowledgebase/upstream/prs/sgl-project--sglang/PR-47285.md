# [Fix] cookbook: fix the new page's playground facts and reproduction notes

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47285) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

On the Mistral Large 4 cookbook page, the Playground can emit server commands that SGLang rejects, and the benchmark reproduction notes leave out parts of the measured setup.

- The `dcp` and `hisparse` flag-select options carry `disable` rules keyed on `hicacheOn` and `cpOn`. Neither fact existed in the flag-select apply scope or in the shared render constraint scope, so the rules never matched. As a result, the Playground emitted `--enable-hierarchical-cache --dcp-size 2`, `--enable-hierarchical-cache --enable-hisparse --disable-radix-cache`, and `--dcp-size` together with prefill CP.
- On the one-tray Vera Rubin recipe (TP4), the Playground offered DP-Attention 8 and EP 8.
- The speed cards come from a separate 16-request warm-up followed by the measured run, and at concurrency 64 the median of three runs. The reproduce command showed only one invocation.

## Modifications

- `_playground.jsx`: derive `hicacheOn` and `cpOn` from the composed flags in the flag-select `apply` and in the render `constraintBase`. Flag-selects apply after the HiCache and CP axes, so a disable rule now fires whichever option was picked first, and it drops a stale pick. The unused `hicacheOn` in the attention handler is removed.
- `mistral-large-4.jsx`:
  - disable DP-Attention 8 and EP 8 on `vera-rubin` + `single`;
  - in the speed template, run the warm-up and the measured invocation and state the c64 median-of-3 procedure.
- `Mistral-Large-4.mdx`: the §2 preamble states the warm-up and median procedure. Two sentences now name Vera Rubin next to Blackwell.

## Accuracy Tests

Docs and Playground only; no runtime change.

## Checks

- `node docs/scripts/check_cookbook_configs.mjs`: OK. `mint validate` and `mint broken-links` pass.
- A headless harness extracts the flag-select `apply` and `evaluateChip` from `_playground.jsx` and runs them on the B300 FP8 cell with HiCache on and with prefill CP 2 on.
  - Before: all three invalid combinations are emitted.
  - After: none is emitted, and DCP 2/4 and HiSparse ratio 2/3 render disabled with their reasons.
- The same harness ran the old and new flag-select `apply` on every cell, option and extra-flag combination of the other pages that use flag-selects:
  - DeepSeek-V4: 2,898 cases;
  - Kimi-K3: 5,760 cases;
  - Qwen3.8: 378 cases.

  The outputs are identical. Only the Mistral Large 4 config references `hicacheOn` or `cpOn`.
- Browser check on `mint dev`, B300 FP8:
  - Enabling HiCache disables DCP 2/4 and both HiSparse ratios, with their reasons. The command shows no `--dcp-size` and no `--enable-hisparse`.
  - Picking DCP 2 first and then enabling HiCache drops `--dcp-size`.
  - Vera Rubin single tray shows DP-Attention 8 and EP 8 as disabled.
  - No console errors.
