## Motivation

@humansand

Skip online NVFP4 conversion for MTP by default. Previously, `--quantization nvfp4_online` was inherited by the draft model, requiring `SGLANG_FP4_IGNORED_LAYERS` to exclude its experts. The draft now keeps its checkpoint precision unless online NVFP4 is explicitly requested.

Related: initial online NVFP4 support in https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/26083.

## Modifications

- **Default:** Do not inherit `nvfp4_online` into MTP or other speculative draft models. Leave the draft method unset so existing checkpoint detection preserves BF16/FP16 or checkpoint-declared quantization, including FP8.
- **Opt-in:** Retain `--speculative-draft-model-quantization nvfp4_online` for eligible draft MoE experts. `SGLANG_FP4_IGNORED_LAYERS` still applies when online conversion is selected.
- **Implementation:** Change the shared draft-default resolution and update CLI help, quantization docs, speculative defaults, and the environment-variable reference. Reuse checkpoint detection and explicit-option provenance without model-specific layer-name matching.
- **Scope:** Target quantization and inheritance for other methods, including `modelopt_fp4`, are unchanged. No kernel or weight-loader changes.
