## Summary
- Add DSv4 DSpark support for PP prefill and PD transfer.
- Handle context-only PP prefill ranks and empty draft model setup.
- Route DSpark draft KV injection through the final owning rank.
- Support PP-local context projection accumulation before final KV injection.
