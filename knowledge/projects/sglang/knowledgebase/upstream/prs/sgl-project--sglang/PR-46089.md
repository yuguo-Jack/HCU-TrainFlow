# [Perf] dsv41: Skip the Mega-mHC boundary for decode and verify on sm_107

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/46089) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

`SGLANG_OPT_DSV41_MEGA_MHC_PREFILL=1` (opt-in; off by default) runs the DeepGEMM Mega-mHC boundary for every forward with at least one row, including decode and target-verify graphs. On a CC 10.7 node with DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash DEP4, decode/verify is faster on the regular mHC path. There, the next sublayer's mix stats overlap the current sublayer on a side stream.

Mega-mHC is faster at every row count as a standalone kernel, so a row floor is the wrong gate. It would also push small prefix-hit extends to the slower path. The difference is the forward mode, not the size.

## Modifications

- On CC 10.7, Mega-mHC serves extends (and prefill graphs) of any size. Decode and target-verify take the regular path.
- Other architectures keep today's behavior.
- No new env var. The earlier `SGLANG_OPT_DSV41_MEGA_MHC_PREFILL_MIN_ROWS` revision is replaced.

Pairs with #46088: its fused decode post+combine sits behind the Mega-mHC branch in `_post_fusion`, so it only runs when Mega-mHC doesn't take the forward. That covers sm_107 decode with this PR.

## Accuracy and speed

On a CC 10.7 node with DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on the AgentX DEP4 recipe (DP attention + MegaMoE) with `SGLANG_OPT_DSV41_MEGA_MHC_PREFILL=1` opted in, arms ran in ABCCBA order within one job:
- steady-state decode throughput at bs128 and bs224 improves over main, and further with #46088 enabled;
- prefix-hit extends (1K/4K new tokens) and 128K prefill TTFT are unchanged;
- GSM8K unchanged.
