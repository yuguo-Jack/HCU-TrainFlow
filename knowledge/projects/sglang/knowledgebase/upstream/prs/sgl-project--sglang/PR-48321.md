# [Perf] [NVIDIA] convrot_int8: Use three stages and a 128x256 tile for large M on SM120

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/48321) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

On SM120, the convrot INT8 `mma.sync` path runs every GEMM with M > 256 on a 128x128x64 tile with five pipeline stages. That configuration needs 80 KB of shared memory, so only one CTA fits per SM. It can't hide the epilogue or the tail of the last wave. In LTX-2 at 960x544 and 121 frames, M is 8160 for every video projection and FFN GEMM.

## Modifications

The change is in `dispatch_convrot_int8_gemm` in `convrot_int8_entry.cuh`, in the SM120/SM121 branch. The SM90 and SM100 paths do not change.

- **Default for M > 256:** 128x128x64 with **3 stages** (48 KB). Two CTAs fit per SM.
- **Large M:** when M >= 6144 and (K >= 8192 or N >= 16384), the dispatcher picks a **128x256x64 tile with 3 stages**. This covers the long-K down projection and the wide up projection. Below M = 6144 the wide tile leaves too few CTAs per SM and is slower.

The sweep at M = 8160, in us. The before column is the current 128x128 tile with 5 stages.

| Tile (warp 64x64 unless noted) | q/k/v/out | a2v | ffn_in | ffn_out | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 128x128, 5 stages (before) | 393 | 210 | 1537 | 1688 | |
| **128x128, 3 stages** | **371** | **193** | 1494 | 1654 | chosen as default |
| 128x128, 4 stages | 390 | 205 | 1532 | 1655 | |
| **128x256, 3 stages** | 398 | 232 | **1488** | **1576** | chosen for large M with long K or wide N |
| 128x256, 4 stages | 398 | 238 | 1484 | 1579 | same as 3 stages, more shared memory |
| 256x128, 3 stages | 400 | 229 | 1464 | 1631 | 1% faster on ffn_in, 3% slower on ffn_out |
| 96x128, warp 48x64 | 423 | 216 | 1643 | 1690 | |
| 96x128, warp 32x64 | — | — | — | — | wrong results |
| 96x256 | — | — | — | — | wrong results |

## Accuracy Tests

The GEMM accumulates in INT32, so the tile shape does not change the result.

- **Output:** every LTX-2 GEMM shape matches the previous dispatch bit for bit, and so do end-to-end LTX-2.5 videos on 3 prompts.
- **Unit tests:** the convrot INT8 and FP8 attention unit tests pass on an RTX 5090 (148 passed, 1 skipped).

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Single GEMM, RTX 5090, M = 8160 (LTX-2 video tokens):

| GEMM (N x K) | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| q/k/v/out (4096 x 4096) | 393 us | 371 us |
| a2v / v2a (2048 x 4096) | 210 us | 193 us |
| ffn_in (16384 x 4096) | 1537 us | 1475 us |
| ffn_out (4096 x 16384) | 1688 us | 1588 us |

For M = 512, 1024 and 4096 (N/K = 4096/4096, 16384/4096, 4096/16384 and 3072/3072), every shape is the same speed or faster, except M = 4096, K = 16384, which is 1% slower and within run-to-run noise.

End to end: LTX-2.5 T2V, 960x544, 121 frames, 15 steps, one RTX 5090, 2nd and 3rd request. Both rows include the cross-attention change from the companion `fp8_fa_sm120` PR, #48320.

| | time per step | time per video |
|---|---|---|
| before | 0.587 / 0.592 s | 8.50 / 8.40 s |
| this PR | 0.568 / 0.573 s | 8.38 / 8.25 s |
