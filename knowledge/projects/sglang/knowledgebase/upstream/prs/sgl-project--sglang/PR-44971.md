## Motivation

Unified recurrent-state entries can have non-contiguous strides. Falling back to separate state scatters and translating replay indices outside the captured work adds launch and scheduler overhead.

## Modifications

Replayed onto the corrected prerequisites; its patch is unchanged.

- Support strided destination layouts in the fused state scatter, including destination strides in descriptor cache keys.
- Fuse replay-state index translation into captured work.
- Share checkpoint/index preparation between static and unified paths without changing their index domains.
- Add strided scatter, checkpoint, and replay-index regression coverage.
