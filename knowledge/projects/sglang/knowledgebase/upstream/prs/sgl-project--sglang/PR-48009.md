## Motivation

While any PD transfer was queued or running, every band of a unified pool was pinned, so under steady PD load compaction stayed off. This PR orders page moves after PD transfers per page, and keeps every move and reuse decision in one ledger per band.

## Modifications

- PD ordering (`mem_cache/kv/storage.py`, `kv/alloc/band.py`, `disaggregation/{prefill,decode,utils}.py`, `disaggregation/mooncake/conn.py`): a prefill send reads its pages, which may move at once, and the vacated row is reused only after the send ends; a decode receive writes its pages, which a move copies only after the receive ends; an opportunistic flush leaves receive pages in place. Each send or receive is its own network transfer. A move that cannot avoid one waits on the host, releasing the GIL, until every rank's engine is done with it (each rank judges its own; the judgements are MIN-reduced over the TP/CP group): on a prefill node until the transfer worker finished that send (Mooncake counts sends per room, so a chunked request never waits for its own later chunks), on a decode node until the receive succeeded or failed and drained as the deferred release requires. No stream ever waits on a value the host writes.
- One ledger per band (`kv/alloc/page_usage.py`): unfinished forwards and transfers, and pages a move vacated while readers could still read them; a free waits only the transfers recorded on its band. Only a transfer with a logical release, which every rank sees alike, stops an opportunistic walk.
- A queued HiCache load's window reservation ends when its rows are named by owner ids.
- Under overlap, a unified pool's decode node publishes its destinations inline, ordered after any page move.
- Band-wide pin sources are removed; a page that must stay is fixed by name and the band compacts around it.
- Tests: `test/registered/unit/disaggregation/test_unified_transfer_bookkeeping.py`, `test/registered/unit/mem_cache/test_unified_page_usage.py` (including a two-rank agreement check), plus updates to the move-gate, Mooncake batching, transfer-ordering, chain-realize and HiCache tests.

**Known limitation (intermediate heads):** from this PR until #48026, a unified pool's decode node under overlap publishes destinations inline, and a relief that must move a receive row recorded before its destinations reach the prefill can wait for a receive that has not started, which can hang the decode node. #48026 holds those rows in place until they are published and restores the deferred publish. The stack is meant to merge in order.
