## Summary

- fuse MiMo-V2.6 RoPE, V scaling, and K/V cache insertion on gfx950
- extend the original decode-only M<=16 path to DFLASH `TARGET_VERIFY` through M=128
- preserve fallback behavior outside the supported layout and shape guards
- expand parity and graph-replay coverage through M=128

## Validation

On the graph-fix + AITER #7027 baseline, matched profiles show:

- 60k/c16 verify wall: 22.009 -> 21.470 ms (-2.45%)
- 128k/c4 verify wall: 15.990 -> 15.361 ms (-3.94%)
- fused front cost: 0.290 / 0.281 ms across 70 layers

P4-only GPQA was 0.707, equal to the same-node unchanged DFLASH baseline.
