# [Perf] [AMD] fp8: Use the AITER CK blockscale GEMM by default on gfx942

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43815) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

#41071 added the AITER CK blockscale GEMM for dense block-FP8 linears on gfx942 behind `SGLANG_AITER_GFX942_BLOCKSCALE_USE_CK`, off by default, because Qwen3-32B-FP8 lost about 2 GSM8K points with it. #43526 traced most of that drop to the GSM8K setting: per call, CK matches Triton and correct rounding, and the drop comes from the 5-shot, 512-token setting, where many answers are cut off and small numerical changes flip them. With 8 shots, as in the AMD CI tests, Qwen3-32B is about a point lower on GSM8K and level on MMLU (below). CK gives up to 4x the decode throughput (#41071), so this PR makes it the default. `SGLANG_AITER_GFX942_BLOCKSCALE_USE_CK=0` keeps the Triton GEMM.

## Modifications

- `SGLANG_AITER_GFX942_BLOCKSCALE_USE_CK` now defaults to `true`.
- `environment_variables.mdx`: new default; replace the Qwen3-32B accuracy caveat with a short note that the difference depends on the evaluation setting, linking #43526.

## Accuracy Tests

8x MI325X (gfx942), ROCm 7.2.2, torch 2.10, AITER at the Docker pin (`e7d2453f`), main at `20c49f12` with this PR; `SGLANG_USE_AITER=1 --mem-fraction-static 0.8 --context-length 16384 --chunked-prefill-size 16384`, and Qwen3-235B and DeepSeek-V3 add the server args of their AMD CI tests. The Triton arm sets `SGLANG_AITER_GFX942_BLOCKSCALE_USE_CK=0`.

GSM8K is `sglang.test.few_shot_gsm8k` in the AMD CI setting (8-shot, 512 new tokens, 1319 questions), averaged over the default examples and four random sets of train-set examples, because a single prompt can move a model by several points. MMLU is `sgl-eval run mmlu` (14042 questions, thinking off, greedy).

| model | TP | GSM8K, Triton | GSM8K, CK | MMLU, Triton | MMLU, CK |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-8B-FP8 | 1 | 0.893 | 0.895 | 0.783 | 0.786 |
| Qwen3-14B-FP8 | 1 | 0.921 | 0.920 | 0.809 | 0.810 |
| Qwen3-32B-FP8 | 1 | 0.889 | 0.879 | 0.840 | 0.841 |
| Qwen3-30B-A3B-FP8 | 1 | – | – | 0.830 | 0.831 |
| Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Instruct-FP8 | 1 | 0.945 | 0.945 | 0.893 | 0.892 |
| Qwen3.5-122B-A10B-FP8 | 2 | 0.955 | 0.956 | 0.915 | 0.913 |
| Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507-FP8 | 4 | 0.955 | 0.956 | 0.896 | 0.896 |
| DeepSeek-V3-0324 | 8 | 0.955 | 0.953 | 0.893 | 0.894 |

- With the default examples alone, as in CI, Qwen3-235B scores 0.955 (Triton) and 0.967 (CK) against the 0.95 threshold of `test_qwen3_instruct_fp8.py`, and DeepSeek-V3 0.948 and 0.942 against the 0.935 of `test_deepseek_v3_basic.py`.
- Qwen3-32B: CK is lower on four of the five example sets (Triton 0.925, 0.857, 0.839, 0.911, 0.913; CK 0.916, 0.841, 0.816, 0.919, 0.904).
- Qwen3-30B-A3B: GSM8K is too unstable to compare. After the final answer the model often goes on in its thinking voice, and how often depends on the run: the same setting gave 0.819 (Triton) and 0.655 (CK) in one run and 0.752 and 0.889 in the next, and CK came out ahead in four of eight 8-shot runs.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

`sglang.benchmark.serving --dataset-name random-ids`, output tok/s, Triton → CK:

| model | 64 × (1024 in, 512 out), conc 4 | 384 × (8192 in, 1024 out), conc 128 |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507-FP8 (TP 4) | 133 → 289 (+118%) | 1033 → 1319 (+28%) |
| DeepSeek-V3-0324 (TP 8) | 257 → 293 (+14%) | 1209 → 1368 (+13%) |

Median TTFT also drops for both. #41071 has the Qwen3 models from 8B to 122B: +14% to +314% at conc 4 and +2% to +50% at conc 128.
