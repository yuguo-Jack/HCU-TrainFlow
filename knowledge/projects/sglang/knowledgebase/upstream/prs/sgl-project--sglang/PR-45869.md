# [CI] [AMD] diffusion: Set the retry deadline on the multimodal lanes

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45869) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## What

Set `SGLANG_DIFFUSION_RETRY_DEADLINE` on the two AMD diffusion server-test steps in `pr-test-amd.yml`, 1-GPU and 2-GPU. It is the step timeout minus five minutes, computed when the step starts and passed into the container with `docker exec -e`.

## Why

`pytest_runner.run_pytest` retries a retryable failure up to six times. A server `TimeoutError` counts as retryable. Before each retry it checks this deadline and stops if the next attempt would not fit. `pr-test-multimodal-gen.yml` sets the deadline; `pr-test-amd.yml` never did, so on AMD a case that fails every time keeps retrying until the runner kills the step.

That is what happened to partition 1 of `multimodal-gen-test-2-gpu-amd` while `ltx_2_5_diffusion_decoder_2gpus` failed startup on mi300 (#45861 skips that case). In [job 118624622125](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39421857090/job/118624622125), the initial pass finished in 82 minutes. The case was then retried at 20 minutes per attempt until `The action 'Run diffusion server tests (2-GPU)' has timed out after 180 minutes`. A step killed that way never prints the runner's attempt summary.

## Notes for the reviewer

- This does not make a failing case pass, and it saves little time on its own. A deadline at the step timeout still allows retries that fit. What changes is that the step ends on the runner's terms, with its failure summary and results, instead of a hard kill. It also guards against the next case that fails deterministically.
- The 2-GPU timeout is an expression that depends on `runner_arch`, so `STEP_TIMEOUT_MINUTES` repeats it with a "keep equal" comment. A step's `env` cannot be read by its own `timeout-minutes`.
- The unit-test step is unchanged.

## Test plan

- Dispatched the 2-GPU multimodal stage on rocm10 from this commit, without the #45861 skip: [run 39833426786](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39833426786). The deadline held. In [partition 0](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39833426786/job/120015564936), which drew the LTX-2.5 case this time, the runner made 5 attempts. It then logged `Retry budget exhausted: 986.8s remaining, next failed-item retry needs approximately 1380.4s`, printed its attempt table, and moved on. The step's standalone test files then ran and passed (6 tests), and the step ended at 175 min with the case's failure, not at the 180 min timeout. On `main` the retries consume the whole step, so those standalone files never run on that shard. [Partition 1](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39833426786/job/120015564871) passed. The job is red only because of the LTX-2.5 case.
