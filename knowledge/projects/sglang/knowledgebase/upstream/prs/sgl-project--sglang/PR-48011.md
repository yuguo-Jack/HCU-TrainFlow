## Motivation

A layout that rearranged anything compacted every end of the chain, even an end the demand never touched, and an urgent compaction waits the forwards writing the pages it copies, so a relief for one band could wait on another band's work. Relocated floats were also placed with the free room split evenly between gaps and moved to a computed start whatever their current place.

## Modifications

- `mem_cache/kv/alloc/pricing.py::plan` takes the ends to leave as they are; `first_plan` keeps, for a layout that only compacts ends, each end out of the compaction when the demand still fits that way. A layout that relocates floats still compacts every end. Whether a layout exists is unchanged.
- `mem_cache/kv/alloc/chain.py`: the chain keeps each band's growth (a fixed-point moving average of the pages its takes extend it by, read from the plans alone, so every rank keeps the same value) and hands it to the planner, which splits the free room between gaps in proportion to growth. A float with live pages stays as close to its place as it can and keeps its far edge, so a rearrangement copies only the pages its new start leaves behind.
- Tests: `test/registered/unit/mem_cache/kv/test_chain_plan.py` (placement never changes whether a layout exists; free room goes to the growing side; a float with room keeps its place; equal histories keep equal growth) and `test_unified_chain_realize.py`.

No change to whether a demand is admitted; only which layout is chosen.
