## Motivation

CUTLASS routing must define every permutation entry when routes are negative or nonlocal. Clearing maps, preparing activation-sized copies and materializing rounded scores separately adds work even when all routes are valid. The effective FP8 backend also needs to reach TopK and dispatch before they choose route layout and token-count ownership.

## Changes

- Publish the effective FP8 routing policy before TopK/dispatch, preserving raw signed IDs and the correct local token bound for native FP8/W4A8 consumers.
- Share one complete CUDA route producer between FP8 and W4A8. It writes invalid output-map entries as `-1`, unused input-map capacity as token zero, and the appropriate grouped-GEMM M/N layout. One CTA owns each expert cursor; no separate expert cursor allocation or clear is needed. Non-CUDA and DeepEP paths retain their existing producers.
- Reuse W4A8's existing maps, normalize exceptional ID layouts once, and mask dynamic scale loads before reading inactive rows. Valid inputs avoid the previous activation/ID copies.
- Use a native checked weighted reduction that excludes inactive payloads and NaN scores before access. The FP8 caller opts into the original score-to-output-dtype rounding before FP32 slot-order accumulation. Cached backend/type admission, strided-score support, the Triton fallback, current-stream execution and compile/fake behavior are retained.

- Complete the routing-policy interface for all four plain MXFP4 adapters without changing their inheritance or loading lifecycle. Derive native block-FP8 cardinality from materialized routes, including deferred finalize; widen FP16 scores to FP32 in one copy and preserve supported score storage. Keep Llama4's native per-tensor scaling convention after materialization.
