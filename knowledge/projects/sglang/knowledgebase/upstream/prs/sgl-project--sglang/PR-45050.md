# [Feat] dsv4.1: Return prompt logprobs under encoder SWA bounded replay

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45050) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

Encoder SWA bounded replay rejected `return_logprob` unless `logprob_start_len` was the prompt end, so the prefill-vs-decode KL replay in #44623 cannot score prompts on a replay-enabled server.

## Modifications

- Drop the prompt-logprob rejection in the tokenizer manager and the schedule batch. The prefix is already cut at `logprob_start_len`, so the scored rows are recomputed behind the replayed window.
- `drop_folded_rows` takes the backend's late-layer tail lengths: a prompt-logprob reader's decoder tail is its whole extend, not its last 128 rows.
- Unit test for the whole-extend tail under the fold.

## Validation

- Pre-commit and the CPU unit test pass (rerun-test 39180728875; 16 passed inside the GB300 container as well). The three GB300 DSv4.1-Flash recipes and the 8xB300 PD CP DSpark test pass on the pre-merge head.
- DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on 2x GB300, DEP2 (TP2/DP2/EP2, DP attention, MegaMoE, flashmla_mega, DSpark native acceptance), decoder replay on, eager prefill, decode graphs on, HiCache off. Eight 2,048-token prompts scored from position 1,280 (767 logprobs each), one request at a time: cold prefill, then the same request twice as a 1,280-cached hit, on a server with encoder replay on and on one with it off. A four-request folded hit with 512 replay rows and prompt logprobs (the shape this PR fixes) ran clean in an earlier job.

| Comparison, 767 positions, 8 prompts | KL k3 mean | mean abs | first 32 rows after the prefix | rows 256+ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| repeat floor: same request twice, same rank | 0.007-0.008 | 0.03-0.06 | 0.045-0.052 | 0.037 |
| cross-node floor: same cold prefill on two trays | 0.023-0.025 | 0.06-0.11 | 0.083 | 0.075 |
| paged-SWA hit vs its cold prefill (replay off) | 0.010 | 0.03-0.06 | 0.049 | 0.045 |
| encoder-replay hit vs its cold prefill (replay on) | 0.021 | 0.06-0.10 | 0.112 | 0.067 |
| replay on vs off, same hit shape, across trays | 0.031-0.040 | 0.07-0.12 | 0.127 | 0.076 |

The returned rows and tokens are aligned: the largest single-position delta is 4.2 nats where the floor already reaches 2.5, and 75-85% of positions agree within 0.1. What remains is the bounded-replay approximation itself: the replayed hit sits 3x above the engine's repeat noise and below the difference between two trays, strongest in the first window after the replayed rows and at the cross-node floor beyond 256 rows.
