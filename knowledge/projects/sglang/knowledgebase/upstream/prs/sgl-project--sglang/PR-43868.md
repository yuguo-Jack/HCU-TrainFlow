# [HiCache] Block-group pool hits, caller-owned load destinations, allocation-target FULL eviction

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43868) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

A HiCache storage backend can keep a second form of the FULL pages in a side pool, stored as whole key groups at block boundaries (for example a compact encoding of a block of pages). Serving the head of a prefetch span from such groups and the tail as raw KV needs three small pieces of shared plumbing that the hybrid cache controller and the unified radix cache do not have today:

- a way for a side pool to report which pages its leading complete groups cover without gating the KV hit itself;
- a way for the hit-time plan to record how many KV pages it skips in front of the KV transfer (the device-head trim, then the span head the groups serve), so the raw transfer and its acknowledgement are placed after them;
- a way to queue an H2D copy into device rows the caller already owns, with KV-derived side pools bound to the caller's logical anchor rows rather than to the destination rows.

Separately, an allocation-driven FULL eviction currently frees exactly the allocator's reported shortfall. An allocator can hide freed headroom behind a clamp (a reserve it refills before raw allocation sees a page again) or page rounding, so freeing exactly the shortfall can leave the availability target unmet and the allocation still fails after the walk.

## Modifications

- `PoolHitPolicy.BLOCK_GROUPS`, with `block_pages` / `group_pages` on `PoolTransfer` describing the group geometry (FULL pages per block, keys per group). The pool's verdict is the pages its leading complete groups cover; it never gates the KV hit.
- `PrefetchOperation.prefetch_kv_page_offset` and `group_skip_pages`: the KV pages the hit-time plan skips ahead of the KV transfer. `trim_prefetch_full_head` advances the first by the pages it trims.
- `HybridCacheController.load` takes keyword-only `device_indices` (caller-owned destination rows, never allocated or freed by the load) and `anchor_indices` (what KV-derived side pools bind against; default is the KV destination rows).
- `UnifiedRadixCache` FULL eviction driven by an allocation target walks to the target: the walk quota becomes the whole evictable FULL set, and the per-victim target check still stops it at the first sufficient victim. Explicit `evict()` calls and non-FULL components keep the requested count.

Tests:

- `test_unified_radix_allocation_eviction.py`: the shared fixture now gives `tree_core.full_evictable_size()` a value (a bare mock made the new `max()` raise), plus a regression case where the first victim's frees stay hidden and the walk must continue to the target.
- `test_unified_hicache_transfer_lifecycle.py`: a load into caller-owned rows allocates nothing, keeps those rows when a side pool fails and the load rolls back, and binds a KV-derived side pool to the anchor rows.
- `test_hybrid_pool_assembler.py`: the trim subtests also check the page offset the trim leaves behind.
