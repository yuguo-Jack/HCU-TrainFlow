# [Perf] [AMD] spec: Many-block softmax, top-p and chain rejection for ROCm DSpark verify

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47317) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

Stacked on #47316: its two commits show here until it merges.

## Motivation

At batch 1, a DSpark sampling step on ROCm runs its verify over a handful of 129280-wide rows, one block a row, so most CUs sit idle:
- the target probabilities: a temperature divide, a softmax, then the sort-and-cumsum top-p renorm, ~0.7 ms a call at 6-8 rows;
- the draft's temperature softmax;
- the classic chain rejection, one program a request.

With the AMD monokernels serving the layers, this is one of the largest pieces of the step left.

## Modifications

Both are opt-in children of `SGLANG_ENABLE_AMD_MONO_RUNTIME_OPTIMIZATION`. Off, every path is main's.

- **Temperature softmax and top-p over many blocks a row** (`softmax_top_p_probs`, `SGLANG_OPT_ROCM_SPEC_SOFTMAX_TOP_P`).
  - Each row splits over 4096-wide chunks.
  - Stats pass: each chunk's max and exp-sum of x / T, combined in chunk order.
  - Top-p pass: three histogram passes (11, 10 and 10 bits) select FlashInfer's pivot in 2^-48 fixed-point mass, so the sum is exact in any order. The kept mass comes from the same histograms.
  - Launches: one, with per-row barriers, when the grid fits the CUs; otherwise one a phase.
  - Callers: the speculative verify's target rows without top-k, and DSpark's draft rows.
- **Chain rejection over many blocks a request** (`chain_rejection_sampling`, `SGLANG_OPT_ROCM_DSPARK_CHAIN_REJECTION`).
  - The classic chain (no block verification) runs over (8192-entry chunk, request) blocks in one launch.
  - Every block redoes the accept chain and sums its chunk's residual into LDS.
  - The request's blocks meet at a barrier, and the chunk where the cumulative mass passes u * total finds the token.

A batch that takes main's sharded accept (sgl-project/sglang#44465: temperature only, no top-k or top-p) is unaffected; top-p batches still take the whole-vocab accept, and that is where these apply.

## Accuracy Tests

- `test/registered/kernels/ops/sampling/test_renorm.py`: the fused kernel against the temperature softmax and a float64 top-p, kept sets equal. Rows are strided, with 5 and 6 rows a request, and temperatures 0.3-2.5.
- `test/registered/kernels/ops/speculative/test_chain_rejection.py`: the same accepts, accept indices and tokens as the classic kernel from the same coins.
- GSM8K with the switch on (see #47316): 0.917.
