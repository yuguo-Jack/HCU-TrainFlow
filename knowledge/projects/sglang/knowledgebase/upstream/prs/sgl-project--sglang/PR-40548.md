## Motivation

Issue: #39700. The PR #40333 provides an initial support on BCG and CP. However, the indexer and compressor still remains eager. In this PR, we enables the indexer and compressor to be captured into BCG as well. 

This PR is fundamental to support cp and multistream forward prepare (#40179), because without BCG the cpu launch will erase the performance gains brought by multistream prepare.

## Modifications
Developed based on #40333
1. Enable DeepSeek V4.1 CP prefill BCG to capture the ratio 1/2 compressor and indexer, with capture-stable metadata and a bounded context width.
2. Add graph-compatible two-level candidate selection and remove the 16K seqlen restriction for BCG.
3. Provide an alternative dense indexer path for BCG (encoder and candidate source), since paged_mqa_logits has worse perf than mqa_logits on long seqs.
4. Switch the captured indexer to topk_v2 with replay-stable plans for either chunked rows or dense rows.
5. Optimize the candidate consumer to eliminate the performance gaps between bcg and eager, including 
- Only scan the real positions when doing topk rather than the full padded seq len.
- In the dense indexer path, use paged_sparse_mqa_logits for candidate consumer to avoid either costly full width indexer computation or dense k gather.
