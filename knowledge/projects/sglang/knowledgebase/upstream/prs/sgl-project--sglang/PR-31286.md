## Motivation

`CacheInitParams::cache_ttl_second` seems unused.  It was introduced by #18502 for unknown reason.  In fact, TTL-based eviction require extra synchronization over TP and PP ranks, which is not an easy task.   Let's remove it for clearance.

## Modifications

Removed unused field `CacheInitParams::cache_ttl_second`.
