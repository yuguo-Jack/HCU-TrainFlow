# [diffusion] perf: pipeline MiniMax-H3's Ulysses exchange against attention over the copy engine

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43164) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

With Ulysses, every MiniMax-H3 block exchanges q/k/v before dense attention and the output after it. Both exchanges run on SMs: an NCCL send/recv for q/k/v, and SM copies for the output. They sit between kernels that keep every SM busy (FlashAttention is ~63% of a denoise step on 2×H200), so their time is fully exposed.

## Modifications

Four commits. Each one is bit-identical to the sequential exchange.

1. **Copy-engine all-to-all with head-group pipelining** (`ipc_a2a_multi.py`, `_minimax_h3_pipelined_dense_attention`).
   - The transport is the two-rank `ipc_a2a` design widened to N same-host ranks: each rank writes its chunk straight into its peers' staging buffers with device-to-device copies on the copy engine, and a per-peer sequence flag orders data before flag.
   - Pipelining: group g's copies run while group g−1 attends; each group attends on its own stream; stream memory operations (`cuStreamWriteValue32` / `cuStreamWaitValue32`) order copies and kernels without holding an SM.
   - It falls back to the sequential exchange when any rank lacks peer access or stream memory operations (agreed across the group before any handle exchange), during graph capture, under `torch.compile`, and for other attention backends, VSA and ring.
2. **QK-norm + RoPE writes straight into the exchange.** The out-of-place QK-norm kernel writes each destination block's normalized q/k, plus v, in place of the in-place norm followed by a pack. Blocks for this rank go straight into its own receive slot, so they skip a device-to-device copy. Groups are produced in order, so the norm of later groups overlaps the attention of earlier ones.
3. **Each head group is merged into the output as it arrives.** Group streams write this rank's rows, and then each peer's, directly into the final tensor. This replaces the trailing merge kernel and the own-rank output copy.
4. **On by default.** `SGLANG_DIFFUSION_ULYSSES_PIPELINE_GROUPS` now defaults to `-1`:
   - it picks 7 groups for MiniMax-H3's 56 heads at Ulysses 2, 4 and 8;
   - when the buffers (11 × rows per rank × heads × head_dim; 3.9 GB for Ref2VA at Ulysses 2) would not fit on some rank, every rank keeps the sequential exchange;
   - `0` disables it, and `N ≥ 2` forces N groups.

   `SGLANG_DIFFUSION_IPC_A2A_MULTI` (the plain all-to-all over the copy engine) stays opt-in. The cookbook's configuration notes describe the default.

## Accuracy Tests

- The MP4 md5 matches main for T2VA (Ulysses 2 and 4) and Ref2VA (Ulysses 2) after every commit.
- `python/sglang/multimodal_gen/test/single_test_file/test_ipc_a2a_multi_gpu.py` (new; passes at 2 and 4 ranks on the final commit) checks, byte for byte:
  - the pipelined attention against the sequential exchange, over repeated calls (slot alternation);
  - the fill path;
  - the automatic group count;
  - a head count the groups cannot split, which must decline;
  - the N-rank exchange against NCCL.

Unrelated to this PR: `test_ipc_a2a_2_gpu.py` fails the same way on plain main ("AllToAll4D never took the IPC path").

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Client end-to-end latency, 4×H200, 50 steps, `--performance-mode speed`, eager, median of 2 requests, G=7. Arms are interleaved on one box: adjacent columns ran back to back. The box drifts ~0.3% across sessions, so compare within a row only.

| Case | main | 1: pipeline | 2: + fused QK-norm | 3: + per-group merge |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| T2VA 5 s, 2 GPUs, Ulysses 2 | 144.33 s | 142.24 s (−1.45%) | −0.62% vs 1 | 141.33 s (−0.42% vs 2, 141.92 s) |
| Ref2VA 5 s, 2 GPUs, Ulysses 2 (with #43117) | 223.19 s | 221.11 s (−0.93%) | −0.79% vs 1 | 219.97 s (one arm, not paired with 2) |
| T2VA 5 s, 4 GPUs, Ulysses 4 | 73.81 s | 72.55 s (−1.71%) | −0.78% vs 1 | 72.23 s (flat vs 2, 72.18 s) |

- **4: on by default.** With no environment variable set, the pipeline picks G=7 and runs as fast as the explicit G=7 arms: T2VA Ulysses 2 141.27 s (141.33 s with G=7), Ulysses 4 72.20 s (72.23 s). Outputs are md5-equal to main.
- **All four commits against main, one interleaved pass, no env:** T2VA Ulysses 2 144.80 → 141.32 s (−2.4%), Ulysses 4 74.19 → 72.23 s (−2.6%). For Ref2VA the chain of paired steps above gives about −1.7% on top of #43117.

- **Per-layer budget, Ref2VA at Ulysses 2 after commit 1 (ms):** wall 88.38, made up of the compute union 87.45, exposed exchange 0.58 and idle 0.36. The copy engine moves 2.53 ms/layer underneath the attention.
- **Why the gains are below the exposed time:** the H200s sit at their 700 W cap during denoise, so part of the freed SM time comes back as a lower clock.
