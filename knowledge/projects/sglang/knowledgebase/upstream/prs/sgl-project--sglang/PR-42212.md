# [MoE] Skip block-padding rows in the TMA down activation and quant

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/42212) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

With a `*_down.json` config that sets `USE_TMA: true`, the up-GEMM output is kept in the sorted, block-padded layout and sized for the worst case. `silu_and_mul` and the down-input `per_token_group_quant` process the whole buffer, but the down GEMM only reads the `M*topk` rows that `token_mask` keeps. In decode with E=256, topk=8 and BLOCK_SIZE_M=16, the buffer holds 16x the rows actually read. With the TMA down config, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8 runs 2.2% slower end to end than without it.

## Modifications

Skip the rows the down GEMM never reads: rows past `num_tokens_post_padded`, padding sentinels, and `-1` expert blocks. The kept rows are computed by the same kernels with the same math.

- `act_and_mul` (JIT): a separately compiled sorted-row variant next to the existing `expert_ids` filter. It is exposed as keyword-only args on `silu_and_mul`, `gelu_and_mul` and `gelu_tanh_and_mul`.
- `per_token_group_quant` (JIT): a new `sorted` layout next to `flat` and `masked`.
- `_fused_moe_kernel_sequence`: passes the row filter to the activation and pre-quantizes the block-FP8 down input in the sorted layout. `invoke_fused_moe_kernel` is unchanged.

The kernels already compiled for the existing paths are unchanged.

## Accuracy Tests

Bit-identical to the non-TMA path:
- New `test_moe_tma_down_padding_rows.py`: the MoE layer with the TMA down path is `torch.equal` to the non-TMA path, for FP8 and BF16.
- New cases in `test_activation.py` and `test_per_token_group_quant.py`: kept rows are bit-identical to the unfiltered kernels, and skipped rows stay untouched.
- Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8 produced identical output tokens before and after the change, both with TP=4/EP=4 and with EAGLE MTP. The MTP accept length was unchanged (3.1411 at batch 1, 3.1030 at batch 32).

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Setup:
- H100, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8, CUDA graph on (decode and piecewise prefill).
- The H200 `E=256,N=512` FP8 up/down configs, where down has `USE_TMA`.
- Input 256 / output 32 tokens.
- Before, after and non-TMA runs interleaved in one job.

**Routed-expert SiLU per MoE layer during decode (us, from profiler traces):**

| batch | before | after | non-TMA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 2.37 | 0.86 | 0.86 |
| 32 | 6.41 | 1.20 | 1.18 |
| 128 | 7.46 | 2.24 | 2.11 |

**Kernel level (us, L2-cold):**
- At M=32, SiLU goes from 10.58 to 2.03 us and the down-input quant from 4.07 to 1.77 us.
- At M=1024, SiLU goes from 53.06 to 20.05 us and the quant from 26.41 to 7.24 us.

**End to end (tok/s):**

| batch | TP=1 before | TP=1 after | TP=1 non-TMA | TP=4/EP=4 before | TP=4/EP=4 after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 857.5 | 871.0 (+1.6%) | 874.4 | 1476.2 | 1502.1 (+1.8%) |
| 32 | 1756.6 | 1800.7 (+2.5%) | 1797.0 | 3402.3 | 3469.8 (+2.0%) |
| 128 | 2354.7 | 2388.2 (+1.4%) | 2376.6 | 5587.0 | 5719.0 (+2.4%) |
