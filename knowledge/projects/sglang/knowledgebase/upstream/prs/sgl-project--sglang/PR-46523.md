## Motivation

Part 2/5 of the DeepSeek-V4.1 CP>1 support series, split from the original combined PR [#45954](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45954). This is a shared Hopper kernel fix, independent of the CP replay runtime changes.

The SM90 BF16x3 mHC projection has shown repeatability problems with the multi-stage launch under the pinned Triton toolchain. CP changes local row counts, so replay validation needs this projection to remain stable across repeated calls and interleaved row ownership.

For example, a frozen 257-row input gives CP8 ranks 0 and 1 33 rows each, and the other ranks 32 rows each. Reassembling these per-rank results should reproduce the full projection. Alternating a 32-row call with the same full input also checks that scratch reuse does not change its result. These are the scenarios encoded by the regression, not newly measured GPU results.

## Modifications

- Use `num_stages=1` for the existing BF16x3 projection on SM90, avoiding the affected multi-stage configuration.
- Keep `num_stages=3` on other architectures. Preserve the projection math, weight decomposition, and existing M-tile selection.
- Extend the existing mHC test suite with 257-row and 4097-row cases covering repeated calls, scratch reuse, interleaved shards, and input immutability.

This applies whenever SM90 selects this helper, including CP1. It is a scoped correctness workaround; restoring multiple stages requires validating the affected kernel/toolchain combination.

- QNan0513 · comment · 2026-11-01T11:18:47Z · [source](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/46523#issuecomment-6557226809)

We hit the same problem at CP1, and it is one of the two causes of #46798 on Hopper. On H20 (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, TP8/EP8), identical greedy prefills already differed at the first output token.

**Kernel level** (nightly 586355a8, Triton 3.8.0, same input, 20 runs per configuration):
- `num_stages` 2, 3 or 4 with `BLOCK_M` 64 or 128, at 4 or 8 warps: 20 of 20 outputs distinct at M = 511, 2048 and 8192. The shipped `num_stages=3` also gives 20 of 20 at each M we tried between 64 and 65536. The outputs differ by up to 5e-6.
- `num_stages=1` at `BLOCK_M` 64 or 128, and `BLOCK_M` 32 at any stage count: stable. These stable configurations also agree with each other bit for bit.

**End to end:**
- With the same SM90-only `num_stages=1`, 511-token prompts became identical across runs.
- Adding #46522 made every length we tried identical, up to 33000 tokens.

**Other architectures.** On B300 (SM103), `num_stages=3` is stable: 20 runs give one result at M = 4096, 8192 and 16384. `num_stages=1` would cost 40–60% there (50→70 µs, 92→140 µs, 165→265 µs at those M), so keeping 3 off SM90 is right.

**Cost on H20.** With `num_stages=1` the kernel itself goes from 21 to 34 µs at M = 511, 68 to 105 µs at 2048, and 259 to 293 µs at 8192. The whole `hc_mix_stats_sinkhorn_bf16x3` call goes from 65 to 66 µs, 78 to 115 µs and 283 to 318 µs. End-to-end TTFT at 2K and 8K prompts stayed within ±2%.
