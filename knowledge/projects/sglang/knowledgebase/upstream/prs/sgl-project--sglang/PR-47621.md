# [Fix] hicache: buffer_only cuts an oversized storage hit to the prefix that fits

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47621) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

Buffer-only host memory mode (`--hicache-host-memory-mode buffer_only`) stages a storage hit whole or not at all. The hit-time allocation declines any hit whose Full/sidecar bounce cannot fit an *empty* host arena (`can_fit_prefetch_host_buffers`, a logical-size check: base `python/sglang/srt/managers/cache_controller.py:1392-1397`, shared-arena override `python/sglang/srt/mem_cache/hybrid_cache/hybrid_cache_controller.py:754-776`) and allocates with `allow_partial=False` (`python/sglang/srt/mem_cache/buffer_mode/pipeline.py:1408-1418`). A request whose stored prefix is longer than the arena therefore recomputes everything, although the arena could have served most of it. Cache mode already shrinks such a hit (`allocate_storage_hit(allow_partial=True)`, `hybrid_cache_controller.py:814-847`) and accounts the shrink as a `host_capacity` shortfall. This is issue #45030. The PR replaces the parked #47621.

A second, smaller problem in the same function: when the sidecar (SWA window / Mamba state) staging came up short after the KV bounce had been allocated, the bounce was handed to the host release queue, so its rows stayed allocated until the next release drain while the operation was already parked on "no capacity".

## Modifications

1. A hit too large for one load is cut to the longest prefix one load can hold (`pipeline.py:1249-1297`, `_fit_oversized_prefetch` at `pipeline.py:1330-1381`).
   - "One load" is bounded two ways, both rank-identical constants (`_prefetch_span_fits`, `pipeline.py:1383-1392`): the empty arena (`can_fit_prefetch_host_buffers`, KV plus the sidecar pools' needs) and the load budget `prefetch_capacity_limit` (`cache_controller.py:644-646`, 90% of the arena in buffer mode), measured in the same anchor units `prefetch_tokens_occupied` is charged in (`_host_request_units`, `pipeline.py:420-434`). The budget is the most one load may ever occupy: a parked hit's retry is gated on it (`unified_radix_cache.py:3235-3239`), so a hit cut to the whole arena could never be allocated beside anything. A hit that fits the arena but exceeds the budget is cut the same way (it used to park until the arena was empty).
   - If the whole hit fits one load nothing changes. Otherwise the pipeline bisects on the page count (staging need is monotone in the prefix length) and keeps the longest fitting page-aligned prefix. A hit whose usable prefix is empty, or shorter than `prefetch_threshold` (a fetch the drain would have declined as below threshold), is still declined as `declined_host_oversize` with nothing allocated.
   - The cut is applied to the operation before the bounce allocation (`_trim_prefetch_tail`, `pipeline.py:1427-1450`), because the shared-arena allocation sizes and keys the trailing sidecars from the operation (`_shared_prefetch_requests`, `_sync_trailing_keys` at `hybrid_cache_controller.py:794-812`): `hash_value` and `storage_hit_count` shrink to the prefix; when a hit-time device rematch already dropped a FULL head (`trim_prefetch_full_head`, `hybrid_cache_controller.py:1706`), `sidecar_hash_values`/`sidecar_hit_pages` shrink by the same pages so `sidecar_hash_values` stays the chain ending where `hash_value` ends, the invariant the completion's belief healing uses (`unified_radix_cache.py:2769`) and the trailing-key sync reads (`_sync_trailing_keys`, `hybrid_cache_controller.py:1836`). The trailing sidecars' endpoint passed to the aux staging moves with the cut (`pipeline.py:1278`).
   - The dropped tokens resolve through the existing path, `_resolve_storage_prefetch_tokens(..., reason="host_capacity")` (`unified_radix_cache.py:2846`), exactly as cache mode resolves a partial allocation. Each cut also increments a new `cut_host_oversize` outcome counter next to `declined_host_oversize` (`unified_radix_cache.py:391`), exported with the other prefetch outcomes.
   - A cut hit the arena cannot hold *now* is declined, never parked (`_decline_cut_prefetch`, `pipeline.py:1299-1328`, reached from both the KV bounce shortfall `:1276-1283` and the sidecar staging shortfall `:1284-1295`). Parking it would be a liveness trap: its load needs the whole budget free, while the write gate stages writes up to `pool - occupied - 10%` (floored at 20%, `live_cap` at `pipeline.py:910-913`), i.e. up to 90% of the arena while no load is granted, and `live_cap` does not see parked demand (a parked hit holds nothing, `:1463-1470`); the cut hit would wait for the write queue to drain completely and, heading `pending_hit_allocs`, park every later hit behind it (`parked_ahead`, `unified_radix_cache.py:3381-3387`). Before this PR such a hit was declined outright, so declining it is never worse than `main`. The decline is the cache-mode host-capacity decline (`unified_radix_cache.py:3295-3306`): the remaining span resolves as a `host_capacity` shortfall (`_finish_storage_prefetch`), the operation is revoked holding nothing (`revoke_pending_prefetch`, which also drops the anchor lock and prefix context) and the deferred-token metric is logged. No paced re-issue is armed: a re-issued span that fits one load would come back as an ordinary un-cut hit and park under the same write backlog, which is the trap the decline avoids; `main` declined the oversized hit without a retry too, and the next request with that prefix queries storage again anyway. It increments a new `declined_host_capacity` outcome counter (`unified_radix_cache.py:392`) so a cut that could not land is visible next to `cut_host_oversize`. Beliefs are not touched: the hit is stored, the arena was busy.
   - Un-cut hits keep park-on-busy exactly as today: an arena without room for a hit that fits one load parks it whole through `_defer_prefetch_for_host_capacity`, holding nothing, and a parked hit keeps its FIFO turn.

2. Trailing sidecars keep the cut on a restorable prefix (`_restorable_fit_pages`, `pipeline.py:1394-1425`). An SWA window or Mamba state exists only at the boundaries where it was written, so a hit with a `TRAILING_PAGES` transfer may only end on a prefix every rank can restore. The hit-query agreement already MIN-reduces one restorable flag per prefix length (`_sync_prefetch_hit_query`, `hybrid_cache_controller.py:1493-1549`); it now keeps the agreed set on the operation as `PrefetchOperation.restorable_hit_pages` (declared `hybrid_cache_controller.py:189-195`, set at `:1541-1548`) instead of only returning its maximum. The cut picks the longest agreed-restorable prefix inside the fit; with none, the hit is declined as before. Two cases deliberately keep no set and therefore decline rather than cut: an assumed-stored hit (its flags are synthesized, not queried, so only the endpoint is evidence: `:1544-1548`), and a controller that agrees hits elsewhere and never sets the attribute (the documented contract in the `_restorable_fit_pages` docstring). The per-rank backend verdict in `pool_storage_result.restorable_prefix_pages` is left as it was (an existing test reads it after the sync).

3. With separate host pools (the non-shared layout) the fit now also checks each independent sidecar pool's window against that pool's own capacity, and counts a sidecar staged through the anchor's pool toward the anchor's need (`hybrid_cache_controller.py:758-769`, sizing shared with the arena path's `_shared_prefetch_requests`). Before, the fit read only the KV pool, so a window larger than its own pool was never caught and parked forever on the staging shortfall; it is now cut to the longest agreed prefix whose window fits, or declined.

4. A sidecar staging shortfall frees the KV bounce synchronously (`pipeline.py:1284-1295`): `cc.free_prefetch_host_buffers(operation, host_indices)` (base `cache_controller.py:1399-1403`, shared-arena `hybrid_cache_controller.py:849-861`) replaces the queued `append_host_mem_release`. The shared-arena variant also rolls back any independent pool the arena allocated together with the bounce and clears their `host_indices`, which the queued release did not do. Counters and `prefetch_tokens_occupied` are untouched on this path (the grant happens later in `_queue_prefetch`, `pipeline.py:1472`). An un-cut hit then parks as before; a cut hit is declined (point 1).

5. The base `can_fit_prefetch_host_buffers` docstring now states the contract the cut relies on (`cache_controller.py:1392-1401`): overrides must depend only on pool capacities and the operation, never on live occupancy, because the buffer pipeline cuts hits on this result without a collective.

Rank consistency (no new collective): the cut depends only on pool capacities and the load budget (`can_fit_prefetch_host_buffers` reads capacities and the operation's agreed chain and pool transfers, never occupancy: `cache_controller.py:1392-1401`, `hybrid_cache_controller.py:754-776` with `empty=True`; `prefetch_capacity_limit` derives from the PP-synced host size, `pool_host/base.py:153`) and on the MIN-reduced restorable set, so every rank computes the same prefix by construction. The park-or-decline choice after the cut reads the allocation result of the rank-synced drain point, exactly where the park decision was read before; a decline runs the same revoke every rank runs for a cache-mode host-capacity decline. The only mutations are operation-local, on the scheduler thread at the existing hit-drain point. The synchronous free changes when rows return, identically on every rank, not whether.

No new configuration knob. `hicache_storage.py` is not changed.

Known limits, stated for review:
- A cut hit declined under a write backlog is not re-issued (no `poll_miss`), exactly as `main` treated the oversized hit; the request proceeds with what the device holds and the next request with that prefix queries storage again. The request never holds a FIFO slot while waiting.
- The write gate (`live_cap`, `pipeline.py:910-913`) still does not see parked demand, so an un-cut hit parked behind a write backlog can head the FIFO as on `main`; making the gate yield to a parked head is a write-budget change left out of this PR.
- In a shared arena after a FULL head trim, the fit sizes the trailing window from the pre-cut endpoint (`_shared_prefetch_requests`, `hybrid_cache_controller.py:725-752` reads `sidecar_hit_pages`). This is conservative and only matters when a candidate endpoint is shorter than the window itself, i.e. tiny arenas; it can cut a page more than strictly necessary, never less.
- A controller whose prefetch operations are not `PrefetchOperation` instances must declare `restorable_hit_pages` (None keeps today's decline for oversized hits with trailing sidecars). Internal companion: the prod_inference mirror's Rust controller sets `RustMCSPrefetchOperation.restorable_hit_pages = None` in the PR that syncs this change, and keeps it None until the Rust side moves the trailing-window endpoint with the cut.
- The aux staging's own partial rollback (`_alloc_prefetch_aux_staging`, `unified_radix_cache.py:3121`) still goes through the release queue; it is shared with cache mode and is left as is.

## Accuracy Tests

New CPU unit file `test/registered/unit/mem_cache/test_buffer_mode_oversize_trim.py` (registered with `register_cpu_ci(est_time=20, suite="base-a-test-cpu")`), built on a real small CPU host pool group (9-page KV arena, 8-page load budget as the controller sizes it, 5-page SWA pool) so the allocation, accounting and staging paths run unmocked. The SWA cases drive the real `_sync_prefetch_hit_query` with a simulated peer, then the allocation, so the agreed-set coordinate contract is pinned end to end:
- `test_hit_larger_than_one_load_becomes_a_prefix_hit` (`:213`): a 12-page hit is queued as an 8-page hit (chain, count, bounce, occupancy), the cut tokens are logged once as `host_capacity`, `cut_host_oversize` is 1, nothing is declined, and the completed fetch stages exactly the prefix (staged span, hash values, KV beliefs stop at the prefix).
- `test_cut_is_bounded_by_the_load_budget` (`:255`): with the budget's margin busy, both an over-arena hit and an arena-sized hit are cut to the budget and staged at once instead of parking.
- `test_cut_hit_the_arena_cannot_hold_now_is_declined_not_parked` (`:276`): with three pages of write staging in the arena, a 12-page hit is cut to 8 pages and then declined (`cut_host_oversize` 1, `declined_host_capacity` 1, revoked, nothing allocated, the whole hit resolved as `host_capacity` in two steps, no paced re-issue armed), the call returns "handled" so the drain does not park it, and a 4-page hit from another request stages at once beside the backlog.
- `test_cut_hit_with_a_sidecar_staging_shortfall_is_declined` (`:329`): the same on the sidecar leg: the cut hit's window cannot be staged, the KV bounce is back in the pool, the release queue is empty, the hit is declined (not parked) and no re-issue is armed.
- `test_swa_hit_cuts_only_to_an_agreed_restorable_prefix` (`:355`): no agreed-restorable prefix inside the fit declines as `declined_host_oversize` holding nothing; when this rank can restore the fit itself but the peer cannot, the agreed set decides and the window is staged at the new endpoint; the rank's own backend verdict is left intact.
- `test_head_trimmed_swa_hit_keeps_sidecar_chain_consistent` (`:405`): after `trim_prefetch_full_head`, the cut keeps `sidecar_hash_values` ending where `hash_value` ends.
- `test_assumed_stored_hit_keeps_no_restorable_set` (`:433`): the sync keeps no set for an assumed-stored hit, and an oversized such hit declines.
- `test_hit_without_a_usable_prefix_declines_as_oversize` (`:452`): not one page fits; the only agreed prefix inside the fit is below `prefetch_threshold`.
- `test_fit_sees_a_sidecar_pool_of_its_own` (`:493`): separate pools, a window larger than the SWA pool: cut to the longest agreed prefix whose window fits, staged, not parked.
- `test_sidecar_staging_shortfall_frees_the_kv_bounce_at_once` (`:519`): an un-cut hit: the KV bounce is back in the pool before the call returns, the release queue stays empty, no occupancy or accounting moved, the hit parks as today and the retry launches.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

No benchmark. For a hit that fits one load the added work is one `_host_request_units` sizing next to the `can_fit_prefetch_host_buffers` call that already existed; the bisection runs only for hits that were previously declined or parked, costing O(log pages) capacity checks and no allocation.
