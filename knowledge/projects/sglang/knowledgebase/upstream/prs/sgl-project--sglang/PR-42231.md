## What

Add `@michaelzhang-ai` to the `.github` CODEOWNERS entry, next to `@bingxche`.

## Why

I maintain much of the AMD CI under `.github/workflows` (`pr-test-amd.yml`, the AMD nightly workflows, the ROCm release image/wheel workflows) and have 57 merged commits touching `.github`. AMD CI fixes currently wait on a `.github` owner even after an AMD reviewer has approved. For example, #41973 has an approval but is still `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. With this entry I can review and approve those changes alongside `@bingxche`.

This is a one-line CODEOWNERS change. I already have write access and the same `CI_PERMISSIONS.json` entry as the other AMD owners, so nothing else changes.
