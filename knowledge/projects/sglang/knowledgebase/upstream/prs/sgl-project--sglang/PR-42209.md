# [Spec] Exact snapshot-free GDN verify for DFLASH and NEXTN (SGLANG_ENABLE_GDN_REPLAYSSM_FOLD)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/42209) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

On GDN models (Qwen3.5, Qwen3-Next), the stock target verify stores the SSM state after every draft position, so that the commit can keep the accepted one ([memory_pool.py#L776-L787](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/67eab57057062e05eabd4227a943b1aa777e563f/python/sglang/srt/mem_cache/memory_pool.py#L776-L787)). For Qwen3.5-4B that is 48 MiB per draft position per running request: 768 MiB per request for DFLASH with block size 16, and 192 MiB for NEXTN with 4 draft tokens.

`--enable-linear-replayssm-spec` removes those snapshots, but on GDN models it has two limits:

1. GDN pools verify with the compact circular replay. It rebuilds the verify output in chunks instead of running the recurrent kernel, so its outputs are not bitwise equal to the stock verify's. In served greedy decoding with DFLASH, 79 of 80 requests diverged from stock in tokens.
2. DFLASH on a GDN model refuses the flag ([kv_cache_configurator.py#L1130-L1138](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/67eab57057062e05eabd4227a943b1aa777e563f/python/sglang/srt/mem_cache/kv_cache_configurator.py#L1130-L1138)). DFLASH commits through `HybridLinearAttnBackend.update_mamba_state_after_mtp_verify`, which only handles the KDA fold.

KDA pools avoid the first limit with fold-every-commit. The verify runs the same recurrent kernel, but instead of the per-position states it writes the draft window's raw inputs to a per-slot ring. The commit then replays the accepted prefix into the checkpoint. The GDN side of that protocol is still in the tree, but only KDA pools turn it on ([memory_pool.py#L568-L570](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/67eab57057062e05eabd4227a943b1aa777e563f/python/sglang/srt/mem_cache/memory_pool.py#L568-L570)). GDN moved to the compact replay in #35544.

<details>
<summary>More on the two limits</summary>

- Qwen3.5-4B has 24 GDN layers, 32 value heads and a 128 x 128 FP32 state.
- At the Qwen3.5-4B GDN shape, the compact replay's verify output differed from the stock verify's by up to 2.4e-4 in every case I tried.
- In the served DFLASH run, each first divergence was at an exact logprob tie (77 requests) or within one BF16 step (2). The 80th request matched in tokens but not in logprobs.
- The GDN pieces still in the tree are the ring allocation in `MambaPool`, the ring-writing verify in the GDN backend, and the GDN fold branch of `spec_utils.commit_mamba_states_after_verify`.

</details>

## Modifications

- `SGLANG_ENABLE_GDN_REPLAYSSM_FOLD=1`, together with `--enable-linear-replayssm-spec`, turns on fold-every-commit for GDN pools. It is off by default, and nothing changes without it.
- DFLASH on a GDN model accepts `--enable-linear-replayssm-spec`. Its commit hook takes the same GDN commit paths as NEXTN: the fold under the new variable, otherwise the circular ring.
- With the variable set, a server with `--pp-size` above 1 refuses to start, because I cannot test pipeline parallelism on one GPU.
- Two CPU tests, described below.

<details>
<summary>Details</summary>

- With the variable, GDN pools get per-slot raw rings sized to the draft window, the ring-writing recurrent verify, and the fold on commit. NEXTN/EAGLE then commit through the existing GDN fold branch of `spec_utils.commit_mamba_states_after_verify`.
- DFLASH's commit hook runs the GDN branches of `spec_utils.commit_mamba_states_after_verify` with the linear chain's accept lengths (last correct step + 1). Pools that only have the decode rings of plain `--enable-linear-replayssm` keep the stock scatter, because that verify still writes per-position states.
- DFLASH already refuses pipeline parallelism. The new check covers NEXTN/EAGLE under `SGLANG_ENABLE_PP_SPEC`.
- `TestDflashGdnReplaySSMCommit` (CPU, `test_ngram_mamba_verify_update.py`) calls the DFLASH commit hook on three pool modes. Decode-only rings must take the stock scatter, the circular ring the circular commit, and a fold pool the fold, each with accept lengths of last correct step + 1. On main the circular and fold cases fail, because the hook takes the stock scatter.
- `test_gdn_replayssm_fold_refuses_pipeline_parallelism` (CPU, `test_kda_helion_dispatcher.py`): with the variable set, argument checks pass at `--pp-size 1` and refuse `--pp-size 2`.

</details>

## Accuracy Tests

All on one GH200 with Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B. "Stock" is unmodified main at 67eab57057. "Fold" is this PR with `--enable-linear-replayssm-spec` and `SGLANG_ENABLE_GDN_REPLAYSSM_FOLD=1`.

- Unit tests on this branch: 87 passed, 1 skipped (it needs AMD gfx95).
- Kernel level, at the Qwen3.5-4B GDN shape: the fold's verify output is bitwise equal to the stock verify's. So is the state after folding a random accepted prefix, compared with stock's per-position state at the same step. This held in every case.
- Served greedy decoding, 80 prompts: fold and stock gave bitwise identical tokens and top-5 logprobs on all 80 sequences, for DFLASH (radix cache off and on) and for NEXTN. DFLASH with this PR's circular ring, without the variable, gave 0/80.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Output throughput, fold against stock, mean of two launches each:

- DFLASH block 16: 3.3% slower at concurrency 1 and 1.6% slower at 2. It breaks even at 4 and is 6-12% faster from 8.
- NEXTN: within the launch-to-launch spread at concurrency 1-2, and 2-7% faster from 4.

At low batch, the fold's ring-writing verify runs with value tiles of 32. The stock verify takes tiles of 4 for at most 64 sequences on SM90, so the fold launches 8x fewer blocks. On an older SGLang build, a session with narrow tiles for the ring-writing verify removed the concurrency 1 loss but gave up most of the gain at concurrency 8, compared with an earlier session without them. So this PR leaves the tile choice alone.

For NEXTN, though, `--enable-linear-replayssm-spec` already removes the snapshots on main, through the compact replay, and the variable replaces that with the fold. In an unpaired comparison across sessions, the fold is level with compact replay at concurrency 1 and 1.7% (concurrency 8) to 5.5% (concurrency 32) slower. For NEXTN the fold's value is exactness: its outputs are bitwise equal to stock verification, while compact replay's verify output is not.

The fold also leaves more room for the KV cache. With these flags, stock DFLASH block 16 resolved a KV pool of 307,937 and 307,991 tokens, while the fold reached the 1,000,000-token cap, because it allocates no per-position states.

## Not tested

- Pipeline parallelism (refused at startup), tensor parallelism and DP attention.
- GPUs other than the GH200 (SM90). On SM90 the stock verify takes value tiles of 4 for at most 64 sequences, while the fold's ring-writing verify keeps tiles of 32, as the comment in `_select_recurrent_launch_config` says. The two gave bitwise-equal outputs and states in these runs. On other GPUs both verifies select the same tiles.
- BF16 SSM state. With `--mamba-ssm-dtype bfloat16` and the FlashInfer verify, the fold's verify uses the CuTe DSL ring kernel instead. All runs here used the FP32 state that `--enable-linear-replayssm-spec` defaults to, and that Qwen3.5-4B's config sets for the stock runs.
- The timing of the circular ring (DFLASH on GDN without the variable). Only its outputs were compared above.
- A paired timing of the NEXTN fold against main's compact replay (`--enable-linear-replayssm-spec` without the variable). The comparison above is across sessions.
- KDA models: their paths are unchanged.
