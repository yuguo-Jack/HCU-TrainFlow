## Motivation

Adds KV-cache sharding for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next with shared context- and tensor-parallel prefill groups. Full-attention KV pages are distributed across prefill GPUs while preserving linear-attention state and cached-prefix reuse. Decode workers gather KV pages from all prefill ranks and receive auxiliary state from their matching rank, supporting both Mooncake and NIXL transfers.
