## Motivation

Fixes #37097.

When exact pretokenized input IDs are restored after HF preprocessing, the processor attention mask can have a different length. The single-sequence GLM-V mRoPE call then raises IndexError. Let the existing helper construct its all-ones mask from the final IDs.

## Modifications

- Pass attention_mask=None at the unpadded, single-sequence GLM-V call site.
- Refresh the processor fixture for current-main video configuration and verify exact mRoPE positions with shorter, longer, and absent processor masks.
