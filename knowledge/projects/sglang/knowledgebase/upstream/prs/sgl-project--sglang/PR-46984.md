## Motivation

Unified pools currently have separate facade classes for combinations of attention and state storage. Their callers can instead ask the allocator, storage plan and layer router for the facts they need.

## Changes

- Build a generic RoutedKVCache from the declared subpools and remove combination-specific unified facades.
- Migrate capacity, host-transfer, retraction and retained-window consumers to the owning allocator or routed views.
- Add coverage preventing new unified pool classes tied to combinations of KV kinds.

## Validation

Environment: 8× H200, PyTorch 2.14.1, FlashInfer 0.7.2, sglang-kernel 0.4.10, Mooncake 0.3.13. GPU/PD runs were not repeated on the rebased head.
