## Motivation

Kimi-K3 prefill on gfx950 leaves three MoE-side costs: the A8W4 experts quantize activations to MXFP8 even where
aiter's MXFP4 x MXFP4 kernels are faster, the merged MoE front GEMM is followed by a separate SiTU kernel for the
shared experts, and every rank runs the latent tail (norm, up-projection, adds) and the FFN-side AttnRes read on all
rows of 4K-8K-token chunks after the all-reduce.

## Modifications

Gated on gfx950.

- SiTU MXFP4 MoE calls of at least `SGLANG_ROCM_K3_PREFILL_MOE_A4W4_MIN_TOKENS` (1025) tokens quantize activations to
  MXFP4 and run aiter's a4w4 kernels on the gate/up-interleaved a8w4 weights (`gate_mode=INTERLEAVE`), only for padded
  sizes whose aiter config resolves to the mxmoe stage 1, the one that honors that layout (`mxfp4.py` records the
  interleaving on the quant info).
- `kimi_k3_front_situ`: at the prefill sizes where it beats the tuned GEMM + SiTU (129-160, 193-320, 1152-1280
  tokens), the merged front runs as one Triton GEMM whose gated tiles pair gate row j with up row 768 + j and write
  SiTU(gate) * up (`SGLANG_ROCM_K3_FRONT_SITU`); configs are compiled at weight-merge time.
- Row-sharded tails for batches of at least `SGLANG_ROCM_K3_ROW_SHARDED_TAIL_MIN_TOKENS` (4096): the MoE reduce-
  scatters its [latent | shared] partials by rows, runs norm + up-projection + shared + residual add on this rank's
  1/8 of the rows and all-gathers the stream; with `SGLANG_ROCM_K3_ROW_SHARDED_ATTN_READ` the attention output's sum,
  the residual add and the FFN read also run on the shard (`AttnBankState.ffn_row_shards`), leaving the residual
  there for the tail. `SGLANG_ROCM_K3_ROW_SHARDED_ATTN_MIN_TOKENS` raises the attention side's threshold separately.

## Accuracy Tests

(`AITER_SITUV2_A8W4=1 AITER_FLYDSL_FORCE=1 SGLANG_AITER_K3_OPT=1`), CUDA graphs for bs 1/2/4/8/16, and an aiter bf16 GEMM table with K3 rows re-tuned on this pod with HBM-resident weights

earlier `main`); the end-to-end numbers are from the integration of all seven PRs rebased on `main` b67785153a,

## Speed Tests and Profiling

- a4w4 vs a8w4 per MoE call (K3 TP8): 0.84-0.89x at 1025-2048 tokens, 0.77x at 4096, 0.85x at 8192; uncached 50K
  prefill 3.70 -> 3.60 s.
- Front SiTU vs tuned GEMM + SiTU (median of alternating runs): 144 tokens -31%, 160 -16%, 208-320 -6..-31%,
  1152-1280 -2..-7.5%; in-server extend TTFT -0.4..-2%.
- Row-sharded tails (8K rows): bank read 477 -> 56 us and up-projection 387 -> 55 us on 1K rows, RCCL RS + AG 652 vs
  INT8 quick all-reduce 498 us; uncached 50K prefill 3.47 -> 3.40 s with both sides, agentic turn TTFT neutral.
  Below ~4K tokens RCCL's fixed cost outweighs it (1.9K-token turn +2.5%), hence the default.
