The Qwen3.8-Flash-Next cookbook's B300 AgentX entry uses the RadixArk checkpoint. Add the NVIDIA NVFP4 configurations from [InferenceX #4683](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4683) to its existing interactive builder, with the digest-pinned SGLang image and checkpoint revisions.
