# [PP] Join and drop a slot's running rows at the ring commit

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43908) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

Stacks on #43907

- The PP + PD decode loop launches a copy of the running rows and takes it back at the ring commit.
- A PP slot's finished prefills join its running rows, and its finished rows leave, when the ring commits the slot's step; a slot's last step comes from the ring, so a slot that launched nothing has none; `last_mbs` is removed.
- The join happens at the ring commit, not at launch, because the PP output relay rebinds `input_ids`, `spec_info`, and `seq_lens` on the step there.

Behavior change (PP): load and running-count readers see a slot's rows after its ring commit.
