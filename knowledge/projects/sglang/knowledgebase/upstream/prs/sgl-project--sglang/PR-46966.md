## Motivation

Update the MI355X DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813 C1 deployment guidance with the ROCm 10 nightly image and AMD mono-kernel decode path.

## Modifications

- Use P-TP4 / D-TP8 for the C1 prefill/decode pair.
- Use `lmsysorg/sglang-rocm:v0.5.22-rocm10-mi35x-20261028` for the serving roles.

Changes are limited to the C1 instructions in cookbook §3.7. The DSpark, admission, decode graph, and offload settings remain unchanged.
