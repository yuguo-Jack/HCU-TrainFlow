# [CI] [AMD] docker: Start the ROCm 10 nightly at 10:00 UTC and ROCm 7.2 at 11:00 UTC

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45848) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## What

Stagger the two AMD nightly image builds instead of starting both at 12:00 UTC: ROCm 10 at 10:00 UTC, ROCm 7.2 at 11:00 UTC. The MILES nightly builds stay at 12:00 UTC.

## Why

All four AMD nightly image workflows (ROCm 10, ROCm 7.2, MILES rocm10, MILES rocm724) start at 12:00 UTC and share the `amd-docker-scale` runners. Scheduled runs fire in no fixed order, and ROCm 7.2 usually starts a few minutes before ROCm 10, so ROCm 10's jobs wait for a runner:

- [2026-10-21 ROCm 10 run](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39599341848): created 12:24, jobs started 13:03–13:35, gfx950 finished 15:59.
- [2026-10-22 ROCm 10 run](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39778193273): gfx950-rocm1000 waited 26 min and finished 16:01.

The ROCm 7.2 jobs take about 3 h and finished 15:05–15:26 UTC on the same days. With the stagger, ROCm 10 gets runners as soon as it starts. Its gfx1250 jobs (~1 h) free their runners before ROCm 7.2 starts, and the MILES jobs (~25 min) at 12:00 find runners free. Both images should then be published by about 14:15 UTC instead of about 16:00. That gives the nightly suites at 17:30 UTC more slack.

## Notes for the reviewer

- The image tag date is `date +%Y%m%d` on the runner, in UTC, so 10:00 and 11:00 UTC tag the same day as 12:00 did.
- The images now take main as of 10:00 / 11:00 UTC instead of 12:00 UTC. Commits merged in between land in the next day's image.

## Test plan

Schedule-only change; it can only be checked on the next scheduled runs. Not yet verified: the finish times above are expected from the last two weeks' job durations, not measured with the new schedule.
