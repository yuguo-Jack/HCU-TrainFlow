## Motivation

After #42898 every Kimi K3 configuration builds stage boundaries (`_uses_stage_boundaries()` returns `True`), so the communication the decoder runs itself is unreachable. This PR removes it. `kimi_k3.py` goes from 4286 to 3832 lines; `main` has 4014.

It also moves two decisions from an FFN to the stage that reads its output. Kimi K3's FFN declared the rows its consumer needs and the gather that brings them (`declare_ffn(exit_rows=..., attn_tp_gather=...)`), so the producer described its consumer. Now the consumer's read and declaration carry both.

## Modifications

Removing Kimi K3's own communication:
- Remove the decoder's own forward for the standard residual and the attention-residual bank:
  - its completion of o_proj's sum (`_finish_attn_reduce`);
  - the loop that kept rows sharded across layers.
- Remove the model's own pipeline handoff, bank and final aggregation.
- Remove the MLP's and the MoE's own DP gather around their body, and their `reduce_results` switches, since the boundary completes those sums.
- Remove the pending residual add in the dense MLP and the non-latent MoE. Only the latent MoE, which writes the next stream itself, still takes it.
- Remove the attention classes' `reduce_results` switch; they always leave the attention-TP sum to the boundary.
- Remove `_sp_all_gather_rows`, `_sp_local_rows`, `_dp_local_buffer_group` and `_uses_stage_boundaries`.
- Comments that described the removed communication now describe what the stage boundaries do.
- The bank tests compare the stage path against a reference written out in the test instead of the removed decoder.
- `test_kimi_attention_parallel_groups.py` and `test_kimi_mlp_parallel_groups.py` expect o_proj and the MLP's down_proj to leave their sums to the stage boundary (`reduce_results` off), and no longer build `KimiK3MLP` with a `reduce_results` argument.

An FFN's exit rows and gather come from its consumer:
- A read that needs every row of the attention's declares `reads_after_attn_tp_gather` (an optional `ResidualReadout` attribute). An FFN that would leave its output on this rank's attention-TP slice then returns it to the attention's rows. Kimi K3's bank read declares it unless the bank stays on the slices, and so does Qwen4-exp's PLE read. They replace `exit_rows` on the FFN before them (`_ffn_exit_rows` in Qwen4-exp).
- An exit runs its consumer's `attn_tp_gather`: the next stage's, or the final read's. `declare_ffn` no longer takes `attn_tp_gather`. Across a pipeline boundary the consumer's declaration comes from the neighbouring layer built on the meta device, so a declared gather may use only the communication state of the rank it runs on, not the declaring layer's weights or buffers.
- `make_layers` / `make_pp_layers` / `layer_stack` take the model's final read when it is more than a norm (`final_read=`). Kimi K3's `AttnBankOutputRead` is built before the layers and passed there. A final read with `reads_attn_tp_slices` keeps the last FFN on its slice, replacing Kimi K3's FFN declaring `ExitRows.SLICE`.
- The check that a declared gather runs moves from a single stage to the layer stack, since the gather may now run at the stage's entry or at its producer's exit.
- `declare_ffn(exit_rows=...)` stays as on `main`. Nemotron-H uses it to carry each FFN's sum to its consumer on the attention's rows, which a read's row requirement does not express.
- `layer_boundary.mdx` documents the read attribute, the final read, where a declared gather runs and what it may use across a pipeline boundary.

## Accuracy Tests

Removing the decoder's communication changes no reachable path. B200, synthetic Kimi K3 (`moonshotai/Kimi-K3` config at full width, 8 layers, 64 experts, dummy weights), with deterministic inference unless noted.

**Against the parent (#42898's head):**
- Per layer and in outputs: every pair of runs whose layer-0 outputs match is bit-identical. This covers TP 2 / TP 4 with the bank, SP-MoE (DeepEP) at TP 2 and TP 4 with and without the bank, SP-MoE with DP attention, `--disable-attn-tp-gather`, and the dense attention-TP MLP.
- DSpark draft captures, compared call by call: from the fifth forward on, every run of both trees is bit-identical.
- Kernel counts per rank are identical (TP 4, PP 2, SP-MoE TP 4, `--disable-attn-tp-gather`; with and without the bank). Under DP attention, decode is identical, and in extend only the KDA chunked-prefill kernels' launch counts vary, as they do between launches of one tree.

**At the removal commit against `main`:**
- Per layer: TP 4 with and without the bank, the bank with SP-MoE at TP 4 × EP 4, and PP 2 are equivalent.
- The fused all-reduce with the real kernel is equivalent. This run is not deterministic: custom all-reduce on, TP 4 with the bank, 4 launches each.
- Qwen3-30B-A3B GSM8K on H200 (fa3, deterministic): 200/200 identical answers in TP 4, DP attention, PP 2 and DeepEP configurations.

**At this PR's head against `main`, with K3's tuned SP collectives, shard-mode kernels and fused all-reduce running** (HGX B300, SM103; method in #42898): every configuration's largest group of bit-identical launches holds launches of both trees, at SP-MoE TP 4 / TP 8, shard mode TP 4 / TP 8 and the fused all-reduce at TP 8. This covers the tuned all-gather now reaching the MoE layers' exits through the stage that reads their output.

**Exit rows from the consumer, against the removal commit:**
- Stage plans, compared field by field: 732 constructions on CPU (Kimi K3 with and without the bank, bank kept on the slices, dense attention-TP MLP, PP 2; Qwen4-exp with PLE layers; five Nemotron-H layer patterns; across TP, DP attention, a2a, unpadded batches, two-batch overlap and both boundary reductions). Every plan a model can run is identical. Removing the new read rule changes 128 of them, so the comparison sees it.
- B200, synthetic Kimi K3, deterministic: both trees build and run the bank with SP-MoE at TP 4 × EP 4, TP 4, PP 2 with and without SP-MoE, and the dense-FFN configuration above. With the bank kept on the slices (`SGLANG_K3_SP_ATTN_RES=1`, its multicast requirement bypassed alike in both trees), outputs are bit-identical between the trees and between launches of each. In the other configurations two launches of one tree already differ (up to 0.32 in a logprob), and the trees differ by no more than that.
