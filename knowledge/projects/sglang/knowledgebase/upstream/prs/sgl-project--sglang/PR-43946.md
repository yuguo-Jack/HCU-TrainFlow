# [Observability] Report per-request storage prefetch and staging waits in meta_info

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43946) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

With a HiCache storage backend, a request in the waiting queue can be held back by two gates before the prefill adder sees it: the storage prefetch has to finish (`check_prefetch_progress`), and in buffer mode the prefetched rows have to be staged (`prepare_staged_prefetch`). Both are polled once per scheduler iteration. Nothing records how long a request spent blocked on either, so per-request TTFT cannot be split into "waiting for storage" and everything else, which is the evidence needed to choose a prefetch wait policy.

## Modifications

- `SchedulerReqTimeStats` gains `storage_prefetch_wait_s` and `storage_staging_wait_s`, fed by two new hooks, `observe_storage_prefetch_ready(ready)` and `observe_storage_staging_ready(ready)`, that the scheduler calls with every poll result. A wait episode runs from the first blocked poll to the next ready poll; episodes accumulate across a request's time in the queue, including a re-prefetch after retraction or a device hit loss.
- The accumulators are armed in `set_wait_queue_entry_time` only when metrics are enabled and the scheduler is not a PD decode role, where neither gate runs. `reset_prefill_retry_time` clears them with the rest of the queue timing.
- `convert_to_output_meta_info` exports them as `storage_prefetch_wait` and `storage_staging_wait`, in seconds. A measured zero is preserved so a request that never blocked is distinguishable from one that was not measured. A still-open episode is omitted rather than reported as a partial sum, and `__getstate__` does the same, so stats pickled mid-episode do not carry a half sum.
- Stats received from a scheduler that does not carry the fields deserialize to `None` ("not measured") and the keys are omitted, so a mixed-version pipeline does not raise.
- `Scheduler._get_new_batch_prefill_raw`: the two gate polls now report their result to the request's `time_stats`. Scheduling decisions and cache ownership are unchanged. The measurements are not additive and are not a causal estimate of storage overhead: the gates precede some admission checks, include polling gaps, and can overlap.

## Tests

`test/registered/unit/observability/test_req_time_stats.py`, new `TestStorageAdmissionWait`:

- `test_blocked_polls_accumulate_wait_episodes`: for each gate, a sequence of ready/blocked polls under a mocked clock sums to the expected total, and the key is absent while an episode is open.
- `test_zero_missing_and_open_intervals_survive_serialization`: a measured zero survives pickling as `0.0`; a stats object pickled mid-episode omits the key; a legacy state without the fields omits the key.
- `test_overlap_retraction_clock_rebase_and_retry`: overlapping gates, a retraction with a second queue entry, two clock rebases through `__setstate__`, and a prefill retry reset.
- `test_metrics_disabled_and_decode_do_not_record_local_prefetch`: with metrics off, or on a decode role, the hooks are no-ops and no key is exported.

All four are red on main (the hooks do not exist) and green here. `test_req_time_stats.py`, `disaggregation/test_prefill_result_polling.py`, `managers/test_scheduler_hicache_events.py`, `managers/test_priority_scheduling_disaggregation.py` and `managers/test_scheduler_chunked_req_gate.py` pass with the change (74 passed, 87 subtests).
