# [Refactor] multimodal: [MM refactor 7/9] Add a token-space strategy for Qwen VL models

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/46413) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

Qwen2/2.5/3-VL, Qwen3.5, Qwen3-Omni and Qwen4Exp are the first models moved to token space.

## Modifications

- Move the Qwen media helpers and the mRoPE index helpers into `multimodal/` (proven byte-for-byte) so the strategy imports nothing from serving or `layers/`.
- `QwenTokenSpaceProcessStrategy`: per-item image, video and audio processing, timestamps, expansion and M-RoPE.
- `QwenTokenSpaceServingSpec`: the legacy serving defaults and `mm_tokens`, so `/v1/decisions` accepts images with the flag on.
- Qwen3-VL family videos (Qwen3-VL, Qwen3.5, Qwen4Exp) are sampled and resized as qwen-vl-utils 0.0.14 does: factor 32 (patch 16 × merge 2), bicubic with antialias, no second HF resize. The legacy route still resizes them with the Qwen2-VL factor 28 and then again in HF; that legacy bug is fixed separately, so until then the two routes give different video features for these models. Their frames are decoded in parallel, as the legacy route decodes all Qwen videos.
- A video's own `preprocess_kwargs` reach both frame sampling and the HF video processor, on every model and for frame tensors and video readers alike.

## Tests

`test_qwen_tokenized_media.py` (outputs equal the HF processor's bytes), `test_qwen3_vl_video.py` (Qwen3-VL family videos equal qwen-vl-utils 0.0.14) and `test_processor_trainer_import.py`.
