# [Fix] hicache: Bound the storage prefetch timeout from the first blocked admission poll

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/44614) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

Under the `timeout` stop policy a storage prefetch is timed from the operation's own start. Every re-issued fetch for the same request therefore begins a fresh budget, sized by the current (possibly shrunken) hit chain, so a request can wait indefinitely across retries, and the per-request wait metric (`storage_prefetch_wait`) and the policy budget are timed by two unrelated mechanisms. Two further problems follow from how a cut read is handled: it is reported as `storage_transfer`, which heals the storage-existence beliefs for pages the store still holds, and a rank that has not yet reached its admission poll can re-issue a prefetch its peers already agreed to drop.

This PR also carries the deadline-cut attribution guards (the `deadline_expired` flag on `PrefetchOperation`, the `deadline` unfulfilled reason, the belief-healing skips, and the retracted-request admission close), which were previously proposed on their own in #44338 (closed); the timeout budget depends on them.

## Modifications

- `PollTimer` (new `python/sglang/srt/utils/poll_timer.py`) times an interval from its first blocked poll to its first ready poll. `SchedulerReqTimeStats` uses it for the storage prefetch and staging waits in place of two hand-rolled start timestamps; the serialized fields and the `meta_info` keys are unchanged.
- `UnifiedRadixCache` keeps a per-request `_StoragePrefetchWait` under the `timeout` policy. It starts at the first blocked admission poll, is sized from the span first requested (`prefetch_timeout_base + pages * prefetch_timeout_per_page`), survives re-issued fetches, and is closed by `finish_storage_prefetch_admission` or `release_aborted_request` (and by `StorageAttachment` on reset). A complete KV read that only waits on a sidecar is never cut.
- A rank-agreed expiry (the existing `_can_terminate_prefetch` consensus, now evaluating the shared budget on PP rank 0) marks the operation `deadline_expired`, cancels its pending retries, and suppresses further fetches for the request on every rank, including a rank that had not yet started its own budget.
- A deadline-cut operation is resolved under the new `deadline` unfulfilled reason instead of `storage_transfer`, its KV and auxiliary-pool storage beliefs are left untouched, `_invalidate_absent_from_hit_query` skips it (its agreed hit may be a MIN over skipped lookups), and the prefetch-outcome counters skip it since a deadline is not a storage verdict.
- `PrefillAdder` closes storage-prefetch admission for a retracted request after discarding its accounting, so the wait budget is dropped with the rest of its per-request state. `test_prefill_adder.py` asserts the call.
- Tests: `TestStoragePrefetchTimeout` in `test_storage_prefetch_lifecycle.py` covers budget start and survival across refetch, metric episodes not restarting the budget, the complete-KV-with-pending-sidecar guard, the stop-policy matrix, and a two-rank gloo case where only one rank has started waiting. Existing `SimpleNamespace` operation fixtures gain `deadline_expired=False`.
