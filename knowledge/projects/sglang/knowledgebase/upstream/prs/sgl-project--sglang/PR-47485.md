# [Perf] [AMD] lm_head: Opt-in aiter tgemm for the bf16 lm_head on gfx950

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/47485) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

#44455 routes the bf16 `lm_head` through aiter `tgemm` on gfx1250. On gfx950 the same path also beats `torch.matmul` (hipBLASLt heuristic) at decode/verify batch sizes, e.g. for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (vocab 248320, hidden 2560).

## Modifications

Add `SGLANG_OPT_AITER_LM_HEAD_GEMM` (default off). With `SGLANG_USE_AITER=1` it enables the existing aiter lm_head path on any HIP arch; gfx1250 keeps it on unconditionally.

## Accuracy

Unchanged: max relative difference vs `torch.matmul` is at bf16 rounding level (≤3e-3), and MTP accept length is unchanged in serving (3.03–3.05 vs 3.02–3.04).

## Benchmark (MI355X, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, NEXTN MTP 3 steps / 4 draft tokens, 8k/1k)

LM-head GEMM, [M, 2560] x [2560, 248320]:

| M | torch.matmul | aiter tgemm | Speedup |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 193.2 us | 187.1 us | +3% |
| 16 | 204.8 us | 192.8 us | +6% |
| 32 | 222.4 us | 194.3 us | +13% |
| 64 | 283.8 us | 202.1 us | +29% |
| 128 | 277.9 us | 278.7 us | 0% |

End to end (2 repeats, measured acceptance):

| | Output tok/s | Median TPOT | P90 interactivity |
|---|---|---|---|
| C16 | +4.6% | −4.3% | +4.5% |
| C12 | +1.3% | −2.8% | +4.5% |
