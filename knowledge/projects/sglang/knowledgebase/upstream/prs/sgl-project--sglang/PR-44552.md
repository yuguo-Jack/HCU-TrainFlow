## Motivation

A Kimi-K3 MoE layer at decode sizes runs aiter's biased top-k, two moe_sorting phases and the MXFP8 activation quant
before the experts, and every rank computes the whole replicated router and latent down-projection in the merged
front GEMM. The next layer's attention-residual reads re-score the same bank rows every layer.

## Modifications

Gated on gfx950 with aiter (`SGLANG_USE_AITER`).

- `kimi_k3_route_sort` (`route_sort.cuh`): the router top-k (sigmoid + bias selection as aiter's topk kernel,
  renormalized pre-bias weights) and aiter's block-padded moe_sorting layout in one launch for decode batches
  (`SGLANG_ROCM_K3_FUSED_ROUTE_SORT_MAX_TOKENS`, 16), with the A8W4 experts' MXFP8 activation quant + scale sort
  folded in up to 8 tokens (`SGLANG_ROCM_K3_FUSED_ROUTE_QUANT`). The routes reach aiter's `fused_moe` through
  `moe_sort_handoff`, which aiter's `moe_sorting` / `fused_dynamic_mxfp8_quant_moe_sort` hooks consume only for the
  exact call they were staged for (aiter's own sort runs otherwise). The aiter runner writes the routed output into
  K3's joint all-reduce buffer (`zero_copy_context`).
- Column-parallel MoE front (`SGLANG_ROCM_K3_SPLIT_FRONT`, <= 64 tokens): each rank's front GEMM reads its shared
  gate_up and 1/8 of the router and latent down-projection rows; one LL all-gather (`comm/front_gather_hip.cuh`)
  assembles logits and latent and also runs the MXFP8 latent quant, the router top-k (from 16 tokens; the route sort
  then only sorts) and the shared experts' SiTU.
- AttnRes bank partials (`attn_res/bank_partial_hip.cuh`, `SGLANG_ROCM_K3_ATTN_RES_BANK_PARTIALS`, <= 16 rows): the
  route sort's spare workgroups compute the next layer's softmax partials over its bank rows (max, sum, fp32
  weighted sum) for both of its reads; the fused reads of the LL change take them and fold in only the stream.
- The shared-expert down GEMM into the all-reduce buffer runs aiter's tuned FlyDSL kernel with `out=` (hipBLASLt
  otherwise).
- Opt-in direct route layout (`SGLANG_ROCM_K3_MOE_ROUTE_DIRECT_MAX_TOKENS`, 0): routes in selection order, one block
  each. Measured slower (C1 cycle 16.6 vs 15.7 ms; aiter's A8W4 kernels are tuned for expert-grouped blocks).

## Accuracy Tests

**Setup for every number below:** 8x MI350X (gfx950, 1000 W), Kimi-K3 (MXFP4 MoE, bf16 attention) at TP8 with EAGLE3
(topk 1, 3 steps, 4 draft tokens), FP8 KV cache, `SGLANG_USE_AITER=1`, aiter's K3 FlyDSL kernels
(`AITER_SITUV2_A8W4=1 AITER_FLYDSL_FORCE=1 SGLANG_AITER_K3_OPT=1`), CUDA graphs for bs 1/2/4/8/16, and an aiter bf16 GEMM table with K3 rows re-tuned on this pod with HBM-resident weights
(`AITER_CONFIG_GEMM_BF16`; not part of this PR, it belongs in aiter's model configs). EAGLE3 on K3 needs the
aux-capture commit of #43599, applied locally for these runs. Microbenchmarks rotate their weights through HBM.
Per-feature numbers were measured on the integration branch of this series as each piece landed (Oct 13-14,
earlier `main`); the end-to-end numbers are from the integration of all seven PRs rebased on `main` b67785153a,
together with #43599's aux capture, #41867 and #41848.

  End-to-end integration: see below.
- `test_kimi_k3_route_sort.py` (new): top-k ids, sorted rows, expert blocks and valid counts equal aiter's
  `biased_grouped_topk` + `moe_sorting`, the fused MXFP8 rows equal `fused_dynamic_mxfp8_quant_moe_sort`, NaN / saturated

- `test_k3_ll_collectives.py` (8 GPUs, extended): the front gather equals `dist.all_gather`, its quant / top-k / SiTU
  equal aiter's quant-sort, the route sort and `situ_and_mul`; reads that take the partials equal the reads over the bank.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

- Route sort at 4 tokens: 6.5 us vs 14.8 (aiter top-k + sort); with the quant 11.6 vs 16.9 us in-server.
- Split front: front GEMM 8.8 vs 16.7 us at M = 4, gather 4.6 us at 4 rows; C1 decode cycle 17.25 -> 16.86 ms. SiTU in
  the gather: 16.63 -> 16.24 ms. Top-k in the gather: route sort on precomputed routes 3.1 / 4.4 / 9.2 us at 4 / 16 /

- Shared-expert down GEMM with `out=`: 7.0 -> 4.5 us.
- Bank partials: C1 cycle -1.6%, C4 -0.6% (C16, 64 rows: +3%, hence <= 16 rows); isolated 8-GPU reads flat at
  8.3 us (gather) / 11.85 us (all-reduce) across nvb.

## Related

  sort to aiter's `fused_moe` (now ROCm/aiter#6484); on MI355X it measured 1-2.6% slower TPOT than separate kernels.

  pinned aiter); this PR uses the FlyDSL `out=` the pinned aiter already has.
