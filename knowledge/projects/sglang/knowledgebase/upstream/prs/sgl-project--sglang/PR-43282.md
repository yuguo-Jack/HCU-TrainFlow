# [MLX] Drain in-flight jobs before a retract-mode `pause_generation`

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/43282) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Motivation

`pause_generation(mode="retract")` does not account for the MLX overlap loop. That loop holds the job it has already launched in loop locals (`pending_curr` / `pending_next`), while the retract path drains only the CUDA overlap result. The launched job therefore survives the pause and is finalized after `continue_generation`, on requests that have since been retracted and requeued: their output receives a token after the retraction reset, and their state no longer matches the scheduler's.

## Modifications

- The MLX overlap loop keeps in-flight jobs in `self.result_queue` across iterations rather than in loop locals; at most one remains at the top of an iteration. The chain, finalize and launch steps are otherwise unchanged.
- `_drain_mlx_pending_jobs()` finalizes and processes every queued job, oldest first.
- A retract-mode `pause_generation` calls it under `enable_overlap_mlx` before retracting, where the CUDA overlap result is already processed.

Behavior outside the MLX overlap loop is unchanged.

## Verification

- `TestRetractPauseDrainsInflightJob`: a retract pause with a launched job queued processes the job before `retract_all` and leaves `result_queue` empty; it fails without the change.
- `test/registered/unit/hardware_backend/mlx/`, `test_scheduler_pause_generation.py` and `test_prefill_result_polling.py` pass on Apple Silicon.
- `test_qwen3_moe_mlx_correctness.py` (Qwen3-0.6B-4bit): same result as main.
