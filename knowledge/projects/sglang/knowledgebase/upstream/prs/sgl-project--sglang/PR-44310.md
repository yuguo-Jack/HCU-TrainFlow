# [Doc] cookbook: refresh MiniCPM-V 4.7 DSPARK benchmarks at the final model PR head

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/44310) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

Refreshes the MiniCPM-V 4.7 cookbook's DSPARK numbers against the final head of sgl-project/sglang#41993 (`3457dcf33d`), which includes the restored DSpark draft `rope_dim` fix.

## What changed

- All eight DSPARK benchmark cells re-measured on real hardware (GB300 and H200, 1B / 35B-A3B BF16 / FP8 / INT4), single GPU, `bench_serving` sharegpt with `--flush-cache`.
- The matched off → on TPOT table in the guidance is recomputed from the same runs.
- Accept lengths updated: GSM8K ≈4.5 on 1B at the final head (up from ~2.6 pre-fix); sharegpt accept ≈2.5 (1B) and ≈3.2-3.3 (35B-A3B variants).
- The DSPARK recommendations themselves are unchanged: on GB300 enable only for 35B-A3B BF16/FP8 at low concurrency; on H200 for 35B-A3B BF16 at both concurrencies and FP8/INT4 at low concurrency; off for 1B.

## Notable in the new data

- H200: DSPARK now wins c1 across all four variants (e.g. 35B-A3B INT4 TPOT 3.35 → 2.15 ms) and still wins c16 for 35B-A3B BF16.
- GB300: 35B-A3B BF16/FP8 c1 wins hold (2.97 → 2.56, 3.06 → 2.46 ms); c16 remains a loss as before.
- GB300 INT4 decode is ~2× slower than H200 regardless of DSPARK (kernel-level, reproduced on two independent boxes); the existing guidance to use INT4 on GB300 only for memory savings still applies.

Accuracy cells and non-DSPARK rows are unchanged.
