# [Feat] lora: MoE LoRA base-GEMM providers for quantized experts (Marlin MXFP4 W4A16; CuTeDSL block-scaled MXFP8, MXFP4 and NVFP4 W4A4) and tuned plan tables

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/48319) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

The v2 LoRA engine's MoE runner (`--lora-backend triton_v2` with the `lora_*` MoE runner backends) served BF16 and 128-block FP8 experts, and NVFP4 experts only through Marlin W4A16. FP8 checkpoints whose routed experts are MXFP4 (MiMo-V2.6, MXFP4 conversions of Qwen3.5), MXFP8 experts and native W4A4 execution of FP4 experts had no base-GEMM provider: those models could not serve LoRA on this path, or paid Marlin's dequantize-to-BF16 at every prefill. This PR adds the providers, lets the runner pick its MoE backend automatically, and tunes the LoRA plan tables from a five-model campaign on GB300 (SM100) and H200 (SM90).

## Modifications

One commit each, in order:

1. **Marlin MXFP4 W4A16 provider.** A new `mxfp4` weight family served by Marlin (`lora_marlin`), sharing the contiguous provider with the NVFP4 vendor; split-K reduced in FP32, padded gate/up slices cropped per slice. The LoRA runner's finalize owns route weighting for every quant method; plan rows gain an optional `vendor` selector.
2. **Block-scaled CuTeDSL grouped GEMM and row quantizers (SM100).** A tcgen05 block-scaled MMA kernel for E4M3 / packed E2M1 operands with E8M0 (MX) or E4M3 (NVFP4) scales, an FP32 epilogue with an optional per-expert alpha, 64- and 128-token tiles with decode-width subtiles; activation row quantizers into the same layouts, fused into the dispatch fill and the activation kernels, bit-exact against a standalone quantizer.
3. **Block-scaled providers (MXFP8, MXFP4 W4A4, NVFP4 W4A4) and runner auto-selection.** Providers on the masked and contiguous row domains; the FP8 MoE method keeps canonical MXFP8/MXFP4 weights under a `lora_*` backend (scales stored as E8M0 bytes); ModelOpt NVFP4 experts keep canonical weights for `lora_cutedsl`. With `--moe-runner-backend auto`, an MoE model and adapters that target the experts resolve to the family's default vendor: `lora_marlin` (W4A16) for NVFP4 and MXFP4 experts, `lora_cutedsl` for BF16, 128-block FP8 and MXFP8; an explicit runner always wins; adapters that leave the experts alone keep the vendor runner. `get_normalized_target_modules` moves next to `LoRAConfig` so resolution can read adapter targets without importing the model executor.
4. **Decline online base-weight updates the providers cannot refresh**, with a clear error instead of stale packed scales.
5. **MoE plan tables tuned** on SM90 and SM100 from a per-row kernel sweep at the campaign models' expert geometries, each change accepted only after a paired end-to-end A/B at rank 16 lost no cell, then checked at ranks 32-256, on Inkling-Small and at the GLM-4.7-Flash and Inkling geometries; tiles whose gain does not hold above rank 32 carry a tile-level `max_rank` band with the shipped tile above it (SM90 per-expert decode gate_up A; SM100 shared decode, FP8 512-expert decode and shared prefill rows). The commit message lists every row with its numbers.
6. **Dense plan tables tuned** from the dense geometry sweeps (two boxed SM100 prefill rows, the SM100 `prefill.n_le10240` block and B warps, 8 warps on one boxed SM90 decode row), each accepted after a paired A/B lost no cell and checked in serving at ranks 64-256.

Defaults are unchanged for users: Marlin W4A16 stays the default runner for NVFP4 and MXFP4 experts; W4A4 execution is an explicit `--moe-runner-backend lora_cutedsl`.

## Accuracy Tests

- Provider numerics tests check gate/up and down of each family on both row domains against references built by the row rule; the runner test drives the SM100 shared-outer decode and prefill rows end to end with two adapter slots and base tokens (0.2-0.3% relative L2 against a 1% tolerance while the adapters move the output by over 80%, decode under a captured CUDA graph replayed over changed adapter slots); CPU contract tests cover vendor resolution under the three LoRA backends, the E8M0 scale storage, the one-expert buffers and the compile-cache key; the runner-default test covers the family table, explicit choices, dense models, attention-only adapters and every `--lora-paths` form; the plan-table test validates the tuned tables.
- On a GB300 (SM100), the LoRA unit suites at the pre-table-tuning tree: 82 passed, 51 subtests, 0 failed (2026-11-02). After the rebase onto main 4c7c775cf4 (2026-11-03), the CPU-runnable LoRA unit tests (plan tables, provider contract, runner default) pass on a CPU stub environment: 27 passed; the GPU suites have not been rerun on the rebased tree yet (CI).
- Every serving run of the campaign includes a per-server generation sanity probe; end-to-end accuracy beyond the numerics tests above was not separately measured.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Campaign of 2026-11-01..03 on 4 x GB300 and 4 x H200: all-target rank-16 adapters (rank-8 second adapter), both routed-LoRA layouts, decode cells d1..d64 (128 in / 128 out) and prefill cells p512..p8k, three rounds; numbers are the LoRA server's throughput as a share of the fastest no-LoRA runner of the same job (decode share / prefill share).

- SM100, W4A4 (`lora_cutedsl`) against Marlin W4A16 on the same checkpoints: MiMo-V2.6-Flash 79 / 82 vs 56 / 33; Qwen3.5-122B NVFP4 74 / 78 vs 64 / 53; Qwen3.5-397B NVFP4 69 / 77 vs 60 / 55; DeepSeek-V3 FP4 77 / 73 vs 62 / 45; MXFP4 conversions of Qwen3.5-35B and -122B 102 / 130 and 97 / 109 vs 83 / 87 and 82 / 87. Inkling-Small NVFP4 (shared-outer): W4A4 101% decode and 150% prefill of the Marlin no-LoRA server (the shares above 100% measure the base runner's dequantize cost), Marlin LoRA 88 / 90.
- The block-scaled grouped GEMM is at parity with TRT-LLM's fused GEMMs on SM100 (per 2048-token forward: NVFP4 7.8 vs 8.2 ms, MXFP8 6.6 vs 7.5 ms) and 1.85x faster than TRT-LLM's MXFP4 W4A8 path on MiMo; the remaining LoRA gap is the LoRA kernels and glue themselves (profiles in the campaign report).
- Plan-table tuning: 2-5% end to end at the cells it touches on the campaign models at rank 16, the shared-outer rows +0.2..+2.3% prefill; with the rank-32 bands, Qwen3.5-35B and -397B at ranks 32-256 and Inkling-Small at rank 32 run within 1% of the pre-tuning tables where the campaign tiles do not apply (details in the two table commits).
- Not validated: B200 (no box), GLM-4.7-Flash and Inkling-952B end to end (kernel-level screening at their geometries only).
