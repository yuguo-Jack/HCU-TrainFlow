# [Perf] deepseek_v4: build the DeepGEMM prefill indexer geometry once per forward

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/44875) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Motivation

Torch-profiler trace of a DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash DEP2 prefill step on GB300 (MegaAttention, MegaMoE, Mega-mHC, decoder bounded replay, eager prefill, one 16K-token cold chunk per rank, rank 0, with the shared-expert fusion, the FlashInfer autotune and the wq_b permute applied): 1,516 kernel launches per step, about 500 of them small elementwise, index and copy kernels that take under 3 ms of GPU time together but cost 40 to 65 µs of dispatch each on the eager path.

Attributing the launches to their aten ops, one group comes from `get_deep_gemm_prefill_data`, which every ratio-1 and ratio-2 indexer layer calls:

| aten op in the step | launches | from |
|---|---:|---|
| `repeat_interleave` (cumsum, gather, compute) | 98 | `request`, `request_starts` |
| `index` | 52 | `starts_dev[request]`, `req_pool_indices[request]`, `req_to_token[...]` |
| `arange` | 25 | `request`, `position` |
| `floor_divide` | 22 | `k_slots // ratio` |
| `to` / `copy_` and host-to-device copies | 22 + part of 71 | `lens`, `starts`, dtype casts |
| `mul`, `sub` | part of 42 + 14 | `position * ratio`, `arange - starts` |

All of it depends only on the batch (sequence lengths, rows per request, request pool indices, positions, compress ratio) and is identical for every layer of a forward at the same ratio. Only the index queries, scales and head weights are per layer.

## Modifications

- `v41_indexer/scoring.py`: `DeepGEMMPrefillGeometry` holds the batch-only tensors (`k_slots`, `request_starts`, `compress_lens`, the length lists); `build_deep_gemm_prefill_geometry` is the former first half of `get_deep_gemm_prefill_data`. `get_deep_gemm_prefill_data` takes the geometry from `inputs.geometry_cache` when it matches (same compress ratio, same `positions` and `req_pool_indices` tensors, equal length lists) and builds and stores it otherwise, then adds the per-layer queries.
- `v41_indexer/types.py`: `PrefillInputs.geometry_cache`, default `None`, so other constructors are unchanged.
- `deepseek_v4_backend.py`: `DSV4Metadata.deep_gemm_prefill_geometry`, a per-forward dict passed into the prefill inputs; cleared in `copy_` and `refresh_for_breakable_cuda_graph_replay_` with the other per-forward caches. The late-layer tail metadata is its own object, so the tail rows keep their own geometry.

Numerics: the reused tensors are the ones the layer would have rebuilt from the same inputs.

## Expected effect

About 12 launches fewer per indexer layer that takes this path, roughly 250 per 16K prefill step on DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, and two host-to-device copies fewer per layer. GPU time is unchanged; the gain is dispatch on the eager path.

## Measured

GB300, DEP2 (TP2 + DP attention, EP2), MegaMoE, Mega-mHC, decoder bounded replay, eager prefill, 16K-token prefill budget per rank, same node, same base plus #44837, #44842 and #44838 (job 3483) against that plus this change (job 3487).

| Rank 0, cold 16K chunk, profiled step | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| Kernel launches | 1,516 | 1,407 |
| Host-to-device copies | 24 | 14 |
| `repeat_interleave` launches | 50 | 30 |

| Unprofiled, median of 3 (ms) | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 x 16K cold chunk per rank | 194 | 191 |
| 1 x 128K cold prompt, eight chunks | 1,525 | 1,511 |
| 8 x 2K fresh on 90K cached per rank | 559 | 548 |

The second commit, which publishes the early layers' request indices once per forward, removes 15 more launches on top of the other two PRs (job 3489 against 3488: 1,303 to 1,288) with no measurable wall change. GSM8K on GB300 (DEP2, DSpark with real acceptance, greedy, 1,314 examples): 0.9741 with all three of #44875, #44878 and #44879 applied (job 3495) and 0.9741 on the same stack without them (job 3496), the same 1,280 correct. GPU kernel time is unchanged. The geometry is now built 4 times per step (two compress ratios, the full-batch metadata and the late-layer tail metadata) instead of once per indexer layer: `floor_divide`, two per build, goes 26 to 16 with 8 of them elsewhere.

## Is the gain significant?

Modest. The step loses 109 of 1,516 launches (7%) and no GPU time, and the wall time moves 1 to 2% on the cold cells (194 to 191 ms, 1,525 to 1,511 ms), at the edge of run-to-run noise but in the right direction on every cell on the same node. The value is on the eager path only: inside a prefill CUDA graph these launches cost nothing, so this change is worth its 71 lines as a low-risk cleanup, not as a performance lever.
