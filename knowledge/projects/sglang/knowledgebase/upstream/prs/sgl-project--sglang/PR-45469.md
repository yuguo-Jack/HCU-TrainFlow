# [CI] [AMD] miles: Fix the ROCm 10 nightly suites that never finish

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/45469) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## What

The 2-GPU and 4-GPU jobs of the miles ROCm 10 MI35X nightly have failed every night since Oct 14 and Oct 6, without reporting most of their tests. This PR changes how the workflow schedules those suites. No test code changes.

## Why

**2-GPU suite: the runner goes down.** Every night since Oct 14 the `linux-mi35x-gpu-2` runner stops about 2 minutes into `test_glm5_2_744b_a40b_5layer_ci.py`, and the job ends with "The self-hosted runner lost communication with the server" ([Oct 20](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39467587161/job/118782916394), [Oct 19](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39295602704)). The test moved from the 4-GPU suite to the 2-GPU suite in radixark/miles#4053 (merged Oct 13). Before that, it ran on the 8-GPU pool and passed in 729 s ([Oct 12](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/38285672256)). The suite now runs on `linux-mi35x-gpu-8`, the same pool the 4-GPU suite already uses.

**4-GPU suite: the step timeout cuts it off.** The suite has 30 tests with ~22,000 s of estimated time, about 3.5 h of real time on MI355X, and the step timeout is 180 min. Every night since Oct 6 the step has timed out partway through ([Oct 20](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39467587161/job/118782916392): stopped at test 22 of 30). The suite is now split into two partitions with `run_suite.py --auto-partition-id/--auto-partition-size`, the same split radixark/miles uses for `stage-c-4-gpu-mi350` in its own ROCm PR CI. Each half is ~11,000 s estimated.

**MI30X.** The GLM-5.2 skip (its kernel needs more LDS than gfx942 has) moves along with the test from the 4-GPU entry to the 2-GPU entry. On MI300 the test now fails in the 2-GPU job ([Oct 19](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39325011628)).

## Notes for the reviewer

- On MI35X this takes four 8-GPU runners per night instead of two 8-GPU runners and one 2-GPU runner.
- Partitioning is deterministic (LPT on `est_time`, ties broken by filename), so the two jobs agree on the split without coordinating.
- `test_qwen3_4B_variable_gbs.py` fails with `HIPBLASLT Error: 6` in TransformerEngine. That is a real failure, and this PR leaves it alone.

## Test plan

Dispatched the MI35X workflow on this branch: https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/39542005262. All four jobs finished inside the step timeout and reported every test:

| Job | Runner | Job time | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2-GPU | `linux-mi35x-gpu-8` | 131 min | 33/33 passed, including GLM-5.2 5-layer (829 s) |
| 4-GPU part 1/2 | `linux-mi35x-gpu-8` | 100 min | 14/15 passed |
| 4-GPU part 2/2 | `linux-mi35x-gpu-8` | 148 min | 14/15 passed |
| 8-GPU | `linux-mi35x-gpu-8` | 160 min | 19/19 passed |

The two 4-GPU failures are test failures, not scheduling:
- `test_nospec_nor3_bf16_sgl_dpattn2x2_meg_tp2pp2.py`: the CI log-prob check failed on a difference of 3e-8 (`-0.4064931571` vs `-0.4064931273`). It passed on Oct 20.
- `test_inkling_small_6layer_lora_ci.py`: one SGLang engine failed to start because `cargo --version --verbose` returned non-zero while it was loading the Inkling Rust image processor. The rollout then got 503s until the 1800 s file timeout. It passed in 473 s on Oct 20, so this looks intermittent. The 30 min timeout is part of part 2/2's 148 min.

The MI30X change was not dispatched; it only moves an existing skip entry.
