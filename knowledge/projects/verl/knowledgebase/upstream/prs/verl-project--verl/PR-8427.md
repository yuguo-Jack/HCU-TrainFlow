<blockquote>
<h2>v0.8.5</h2>
<h2>Summary</h2>
<p>Liger-Kernel v0.8.5 adds partial RoPE, selected-token log probabilities in fused scaled cross-entropy, and DeepSeek V2 support. It improves RL-loss correctness, model compatibility, and  torch.compile  support, with fixes for CUDA 13/Blackwell MoE and CuTe DSL. Performance optimizations eliminate unnecessary distillation loss and frozen-parameter normalization gradient computations, alongside improvements to CI and release/nightly publishing.</p>
<p><strong>Compatibility update</strong>: The minimum supported Transformers version is now 5.0.0.</p>
