# [algo, trainer] fix: avoid cancellation in k3 KL estimates

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/verl-project/verl/pull/8386) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

### What does this PR do?

Use `torch.expm1(x) - x` for k3 KL estimates in the KL penalty, rollout rejection sampling, and off-policy diagnostics. The expression is mathematically equivalent to `exp(x) - 1 - x`, but avoids subtracting 1 from an exponential close to 1.

With float32 `x = -1e-4`, the current rejection statistic is approximately `-1.65965e-8`, although k3 is nonnegative and a float64 reference is approximately `4.99983e-9`. Summing over 32,768 tokens produces approximately `-5.43833e-4` instead of `1.63835e-4`. With a configured `seq_sum_k3` threshold of `1e-4`, the unpatched implementation retains a sequence that should be rejected. Conversely, `x = +1e-4` is incorrectly rejected by `token_k3` at a threshold of `1e-8`.

These examples demonstrate numerical and thresholding errors under small log-ratios and strict configured thresholds. They do not establish an end-to-end training regression or a model-quality improvement.
