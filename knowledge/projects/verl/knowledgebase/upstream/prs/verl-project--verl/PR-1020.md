# Integrate AdaRFT (Adaptive Curriculum Reinforcement Finetuning) into veRL

## Original PR description

### Integrate AdaRFT (Adaptive Curriculum Reinforcement Finetuning) into veRL

📄 Paper: [Efficient Reinforcement Finetuning via Adaptive Curriculum Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05520)  
🔗 Repo: https://github.com/uscnlp-lime/verl
🗂️ Data: [Datasets with Difficulty Scores for Curriculum Learning](https://huggingface.co/collections/lime-nlp/difficulty-estimation-math-datasets-67e85f875aeb74b76d763aef)

---

**Summary:**

This PR integrates **AdaRFT**, a lightweight curriculum learning extension designed for reinforcement finetuning (RFT). AdaRFT operates at the **data level**, dynamically sampling training examples based on difficulty without modifying:

- the reward function,
- model architecture, or
- the underlying RL algorithm.

As a result, AdaRFT is **fully compatible** with most RFT algorithms (e.g., PPO, GRPO, PRIME), and can be seamlessly integrated into existing RL pipelines.

---

**Key Features:**
- **Faster convergence and better performance:** Reduces training steps/time by up to 2x and achieves better performance
- **Data-level curriculum learning:** Adjusts training difficulty based on model performance on the fly.
- **Dynamic difficulty-based sampling:** Continuously updates the sampling distribution to keep training examples appropriately challenging.
- **No changes to RL internals:** Compatible with standard RFT algorithms out of the box.
- **Configurable scheduler:** Exposes hyperparameters (`η`, `α`, `β`) for controlling the curriculum dynamics.
- **Custom sampler**: introduces ```custom_sampler.py```, enabling users to design their own sampling strategies for training.

---

**Motivation:**

AdaRFT improves **sample and compute efficiency** by ensuring the model is neither overfitted on easy examples nor stuck on hard ones. The adaptive curriculum ensures smoother learning and faster convergence. Additionally, while there has been considerable research suggesting that curriculum learning can significantly boost RFT performance, no existing RFT codebase currently supports this feature. In addition to AdaRFT, this pull request also introduces ```custom_sampler.py```, enabling users to design their own sampling strategies for training. This addition expands verl’s utility, supporting more **dynamic and customizable** sampling techniques.

---
Let me know if you need any additional information about AdaRFT, or feel free to suggest a more appropriate way to integrate this feature into ```verl```.
