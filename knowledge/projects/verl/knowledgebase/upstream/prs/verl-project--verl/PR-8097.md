# [fsdp, model] fix: gather DTensor LM heads for fused kernels

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/verl-project/verl/pull/8097) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Summary

Alternative approach to #8091: explicitly materialize the FSDP2 LM-head weight before entering the Torch, Triton, or Liger fused autograd function. Only the weight is gathered; activations, logits, and labels are not gathered by this change.

This is a **draft for design comparison**, not a claim that explicit gathering is preferable to FSDP-managed execution.

## Related work and duplicate-work check

The issue discussion and open PRs were checked before submission. #8096 addresses the same underlying issue by registering the fused projection as an FSDP forward method on the independently sharded head. #8004 addresses an opt-in FP32-head path.

This draft explores a materially different mechanism: the fused-kernel wrappers accept the existing weight argument, conditionally gather a DTensor into a regular tensor, and rely on DTensor autograd for the head's gradient communication. It does not register a new head forward method or move the head into the root FSDP group. The alternative and its tradeoffs are documented on #8091. These approaches should be evaluated as alternatives, not landed as duplicate fixes.

## Implementation

- Add one shared weight-preparation helper outside `autograd.Function.apply`, preserving the gather/copy autograd graph even when a kernel receives raw tensor pointers.
- Move CPU-offloaded DTensor shards to the activation device before the NCCL gather, and align their compute dtype with the activations. Regular weights already on the correct device retain their identity.
- Use `Partial("avg")` gradient placements across the FSDP/HSDP mesh. A plain `full_tensor()` fixed forward execution but produced incorrect head gradients with rank-distinct inputs in the initial probe.
- Apply preparation to `FusedLinearForPPO` (Torch and Liger) and the Triton `linear_cross_entropy` wrapper.
- Remove premature Qwen3-VL/Qwen3.5 materialization so these adapters reach the shared preparation path with the original DTensor.
- Keep the directly gathered, independently wrapped head out of deferred FSDP gradient bookkeeping. DTensor autograd already reduces its gradients each microbatch; deferring the unused FSDP head group otherwise still triggers the reported missing `_unsharded_param` exception. Other layers retain deferred synchronization, and tied/root-owned heads retain their existing FSDP behavior.

## Validation

Tests were run on H200 and B300 pods with Liger-Kernel 0.8.4, Transformers 5.10.4, and CuTe DSL 4.6.0.

| Hardware | PyTorch | Triton | 2-rank FSDP | 4-rank HSDP (2 x 2 mesh) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H200 | 2.9.1.6+cu129 | 3.5.1 | 44 passed, 8 skipped | 44 passed, 8 skipped |
| B300 | 2.11.0.1+cu130 | 3.6.0 | 52 passed | 52 passed |

Counts are distributed cases per invocation, **not multiplied by the number of ranks**. All H200 skips are FP32 Liger cases: the SM90 fused scaled cross-entropy implementation supports BF16 only.

The matrix uses small randomly initialized **real Qwen3 models** and covers Torch/Triton/Liger, tied/untied heads, CPU offload on/off, FP32 master parameters with FP32/BF16 computation, and immediate/deferred synchronization. It compares outputs, every trainable parameter's accumulated gradient, and two momentum-SGD updates with two microbatches per update. Inputs and loss multipliers differ across ranks. Additional cases check regular and sharded CPU/GPU weights, identity preservation, and gradients returning to their original device.

The final reference invokes the **same fused kernel inside the head's normal FSDP forward lifecycle**, isolating materialization and distributed-gradient behavior from kernel arithmetic. Initial supported two-rank cases also passed against an unfused PyTorch reference. The four-rank unfused comparison exposed a Triton FP32 output difference of approximately 0.000893; the final matrix uses the same-kernel reference without relaxing its numerical tolerances.

## Tradeoffs and limits

- The fully materialized head weight is retained for kernel backward, rather than being freed and re-gathered through the head's ordinary FSDP lifecycle.
- The head synchronizes gradients **on every microbatch**, even when the rest of the model defers synchronization. This is a correctness tradeoff, not a communication-performance improvement.
- Callers bypassing VeRL's engine and calling `set_requires_gradient_sync(False)` directly must use the new VeRL helper or otherwise preserve the directly gathered head's synchronization.
- No throughput, peak-memory, pretrained-model training, full PPO/GRPO, or full FSDPEngine end-to-end result is claimed. Engine-context and adapter regressions passed; distributed numerical coverage uses Qwen3 rather than every VLM adapter.
- Ascend and composable tensor parallelism are not validated by this draft.
