## Background

Nightly Megatron-Core bump, main lane only (AUT-2560). MCore `main` moves from `db3a6cd8` to `ce852bfb` (selected once, 2026-10-19T06:05Z). This picks up NVIDIA/Megatron-LM #6981, #8180, #8318 and #8272. `.dev.commit` is unchanged.

## What changed

- `.main.commit` and the `3rdparty/Megatron-LM` gitlink now point to `ce852bfb93f364f64a556602fc71a5d9ed2c377e`.
- `uv.lock` was regenerated with `uv lock --upgrade` (revision 2, 347 packages):
  - Minor bumps: certifi, databricks-sdk, httpcore2, httpx2, platformdirs, python-discovery, starlette, virtualenv, wrapt (2.5.2rc1, allowed by the repo's prerelease policy) and xxhash.
  - torch, TransformerEngine and megatron-core entries are unchanged.
- `pyproject.toml` is unchanged: the MCore dependency metadata did not change.
- No Bridge source or test changes.

## Details

The MCore delta touches:
- loss-mask caching in `gpt_dataset`;
- `NCCL_ALGO` validation in `megatron/training/determinism.py`;
- runtime-service and tokenizer setup, which now happens inside `initialize_megatron`.

Bridge consumes these through training config determinism, data, pretrain and checkpoint conversion. Local test rows were selected for those paths.

  - L2 checkpoint conversion, both directions (mbridge↔mlm) for llama32_1b and qwen3_4b: all passed.

- `uv lock --check` passed.
