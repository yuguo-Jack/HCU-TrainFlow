## Background

Nightly Megatron-Core bump, main lane only. MCore `main` moves from `6e7e6ed4` to `4ab3af12d274b3a9c330b7a88db8f8e2768be971`. `.dev.commit` is unchanged.

## What changed

- `.main.commit` and the `3rdparty/Megatron-LM` gitlink now point to `4ab3af12`. `uv lock --upgrade` moves transformers 5.20.0→5.20.1, pillow→13.0, protobuf→7.37.0rc1 and wandb→0.31, among others.
- `use_megatron_fsdp` is now read and written only on the DDP config, because MCore #7778 removed `DistributedInitConfig.use_megatron_fsdp`. Updated: training config, setup and train loop; nemotronh recipes and perf recipes; example YAMLs; megatron-fsdp skill docs; tests.
- Transformers 5.20.1 is added to the tested releases for the Nemotron Super-VL processor adapter and the MiniMax-M3 vision RoPE contract test.
