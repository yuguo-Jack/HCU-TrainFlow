# fix(recipes): use GB200 finetuning defaults in text-only card

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Megatron-Bridge/pull/6690) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## What does this PR do?

Adds GB200 recipes for Nemotron 3 Super SFT and PEFT, including text-only Super VL sources, and switches the text-only verification card to them. Previously the GB200 commands selected H100 recipes and repeated hardware and execution settings as overrides. The H100 eight-GPU NVLink-domain defaults can fail on four-GPU GB200 nodes in `cudaIpcOpenMemHandle` before training starts.

- `nemotron_3_super_sft_64gpu_gb200_bf16_config`: TP8/EP64, HybridEP domain settings `64/72/1`.
- `nemotron_3_super_peft_16gpu_gb200_bf16_config`: TP8/EP16, HybridEP domain settings `16/72/1`.

The GB200 pretrain, SFT, and PEFT constructors define their configuration directly. They do not call H100 recipes or share a hardware-policy helper; only the standard task-common initializers are reused.

Both finetuning recipes require placement within one NVL72 domain. They own flex/HybridEP dispatch, natural routing, FP32 optimizer state and gradient reduction, synchronous checkpoint saves, and numerical checks. They retain the corresponding H100 objective, batch sizes, learning-rate policy, MTP and adapter settings, forward HF source/text-only/revision options, and preserve normal checkpoint resume defaults. H100 recipes are unchanged.

The card removes 20 SFT and 19 PEFT duplicated overrides. It retains run-specific source/checkpoint/output paths, Tulu dataset preparation and packing, duration, evaluation/logging and fresh-load choices. Generated documentation and the catalog are updated. Existing verified GB200 training and export results, dates, and metrics are preserved. The card explicitly attributes them to the historical revision and H100 recipes with explicit GB200 overrides. Displayed commands use the new GB200 recipes and require a revision containing them; the refactor does not claim a new 500-step measurement.

## Validation

- Final flat-source validation: 52 complete before/after config comparisons pass, covering all three GB200 constructors with native HF metadata, source/revision options, LoRA/DoRA, and custom adapters; independent nested configs are also checked.
- 44 recipe tests and 69 documentation/catalog tests pass on the final source.
- Card validation, generated-catalog consistency and all-files pre-commit pass.
- The earlier matched 16-GB200 probe reproduces the original IPC failure before step 1 and completes five finite steps with corrected topology.
- Direct PEFT recipe on 16 GB200 GPUs: five finite LoRA steps, checkpoint save, reload at step 5 with optimizer/RNG loading enabled, and two more finite steps; all ranks exit successfully.
- Direct SFT recipe on 64 GB200 GPUs: five finite steps, weights-only checkpoint save/reload at step 5, and two more finite steps; all ranks exit successfully. This continuation restarts optimizer/RNG/scheduler state.

The matched pretraining probe uses native Nemotron 3 Super random initialization and mock GPT data. Actual finetuning checks use an owned checkpoint from that initialization and synthetic local JSONL. Both use a sequence-length cap of 2048. GPU checks overlay the three recipe/export files onto the release-validation runtime; unit tests use the full PR source. Those completed GPU checks used the pre-flattening recipe implementation. The flat refactor is checked for complete resolved-config equality against that implementation. The GPU checks are bounded dispatch/training/checkpoint checks, not a new 500-step Super VL/Tulu card validation, export-parity check, or convergence claim. Functional checkpoint reload does not establish uninterrupted-run numerical parity.

The recipe and card updates were developed with AI assistance and independently reviewed.

### Resolve EP from the effective launcher configuration

**Failure:** Take the [published GB200 PEFT command](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Megatron-Bridge/blob/2e1f13bd589f2f245564d8bd8d83f12ee28a5afe/examples/model_verification_cards/nemotron-3.5-super-vl-120b-a12b-text-only/card.yaml#L419-L430) and replace `model.expert_model_parallel_size=16` with `-ep 16` or `--expert_model_parallel_size=16`. Both preserve EP16 in the [training parser](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Megatron-Bridge/blob/2e1f13bd589f2f245564d8bd8d83f12ee28a5afe/scripts/training/run_recipe.py#L237-L243), but this checker reads neither spelling, falls back to EP1, and rejects the correct ranks16 topology. Omitting the override also incorrectly produces EP1 here, although the [selected recipe defaults to EP16](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Megatron-Bridge/blob/2e1f13bd589f2f245564d8bd8d83f12ee28a5afe/src/megatron/bridge/recipes/nemotronh/h100/nemotron_3_super.py#L617-L640).

**Correction:** Change this function's EP resolution to recognize both convenience aliases and their `=value` forms, and use the selected recipe's default when no EP override is present. Preserve the [runner's precedence](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Megatron-Bridge/blob/2e1f13bd589f2f245564d8bd8d83f12ee28a5afe/scripts/training/run_recipe.py#L506-L513): trailing model overrides supersede convenience flags. Equivalent effective configurations must receive the same topology verdict. A complete replacement patch is uncertain because this torch-free module has no offline recipe-default resolver; that dependency needs settling rather than assuming a universal default of 1.

**Preservation and acceptance:** Proposed checks, not executed: the PEFT command must pass with `-ep 16`, `--expert_model_parallel_size 16`, `--expert_model_parallel_size=16`, and no EP override. With a trailing `model.expert_model_parallel_size=32`, ranks32 must pass and ranks16 must fail. Retain acceptance of the current explicit EP64/EP16 card commands, additive environment overrides, H100 leaves, and explicit DeepEP commands. Retain rejection of missing topology, ranks8 for explicit EP16, and assignments lacking the `env_vars.` prefix. Add these cases to `test_gb200_hybridep_topology_check`.
