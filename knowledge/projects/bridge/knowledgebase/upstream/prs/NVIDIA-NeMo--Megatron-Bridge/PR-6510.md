# perf(recipe): tune the Muse Spark Open GB200 recipes for HybridEP and re-verify the card

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Megatron-Bridge/pull/6510) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

# What does this PR do ?

Re-verifies the Muse-Spark-Open-816B-A42B model verification card on the public Hugging Face revision
(`0fb02478b02565b2ce7fc57e6051c91172774ac9`) on latest main, and retunes all nine Muse Spark Open GB200 recipes for
throughput. Every recipe now dispatches the routed experts with HybridEP, one expert-parallel group per NVL72
NVLink domain, and enables the Megatron-Core fusions that the builder-based model config leaves off. The 4K and 8K
SFT and LoRA recipes move to EP64 without activation recompute, and the image-text SFT recipe moves to EP64 with full
recompute because its first stage also trains the vision tower. The 32K long-context recipe moves to CP4 and
recomputes only the MoE layers. The pretraining and 64K CoderForge recipes keep their EP32 layouts and full
recompute, and the image-text LoRA recipe keeps EP16 and drops its `core_attn` recompute. The card records the tuned
recipes' runs; the five recipes the card does not cover were checked with short runs against main's versions.

Draft: GPU conversion, forward comparison, inference, pretraining, checkpoint resume, full SFT, long-context SFT,
and LoRA are verified on the pinned revision. CPU conversion and the SFT export reload stay unverified.

# Throughput

GB200, average of the last 10 optimizer steps of each card run (steps 76-85 for SFT and LoRA, 91-100 for
pretraining, 11-20 for long-context SFT) with the same data, batch, and schedule. "Previous" is the result recorded
in the card on main (earlier Bridge commits, local copy of the checkpoint); "this PR" is the pinned public revision
on latest main.

| Workload | GPUs | Previous recipe | Previous step | This PR recipe | This PR step | Speedup |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Full SFT, 4K packs, GBS 128 | 192 | TP4/PP6/EP32, full recompute | 6,688.57 ms (101.55 TFLOP/s/GPU) | TP4/PP3/EP64, no recompute | 4,195.30 ms (178.80 TFLOP/s/GPU) | **1.59x** |
| LoRA, 4K packs, GBS 128 | 64 | TP4/PP4/EP16, `core_attn` recompute | 15,306.70 ms (132.97 TFLOP/s/GPU) | TP4/PP1/EP64, no recompute | 11,099.81 ms (201.84 TFLOP/s/GPU) | **1.38x** |
| Pretraining, 4K, GBS 1024 | 192 | TP1/PP6/EP32, full recompute | 22,561.47 ms (267.29 TFLOP/s/GPU) | TP1/PP6/EP32, full recompute, fusions | 19,401.12 ms (310.87 TFLOP/s/GPU) | **1.16x** |
| Long-context SFT, 32K packs, GBS 8 | 192 | TP4/PP6/CP2/EP32, full recompute | 5,527.29 ms (70.03 TFLOP/s/GPU) | TP4/PP6/CP4/EP32, MoE recompute, fusions | 3,153.38 ms (120.32 TFLOP/s/GPU) | **1.75x** |

On latest main, a 10-step screen of the previous full-SFT layout averages about 7.1 s per step, so the same-code
SFT speedup is about 1.7x. Each EP64 HybridEP group is one pipeline stage in one NVL72 NVLink domain.

For pretraining, the previous recipe averages 23,978.62 ms over steps 91-100 on latest main, so the same-code
speedup is about 1.24x, all from the Megatron-Core fusions: in 15-step screens they cut the median step from about
23.2 s to 19.7 s at the same peak memory (105 GB). Recomputing only the MoE layers is about 3.5% faster still,
but it runs at 158 GB allocated (172 GB reserved) of 198 GB on the second stage and ran out of memory in NCCL when
resuming from a checkpoint, so the recipe keeps full recompute. TP4/PP3/EP64 without recompute (24,974.48 ms) is
slower: at TP4 each GPU processes a quarter of every microbatch, and on a 6-block profile the TP4 step is 31% idle,
with latency-bound HybridEP dispatch and tensor-parallel collectives, at 2.1x the per-token time of TP1. Virtual
pipeline stages, CUDA graphs, and EP overlap are not yet supported by the Muse Spark Open builder. Over the 100
card steps, the new recipe's loss is on average 0.34% from the previous recipe's (largest gap 1.8%), higher on 52
of 100 steps.

For 32K long-context SFT, the previous recipe averages 5,614.89 ms over steps 11-20 on latest main, so the
same-code speedup is about 1.78x. At GBS 8, CP2 leaves four data-parallel replicas with two microbatches each, so the
six-stage pipeline idles for most of each step; CP4 halves the replicas and doubles their microbatches. Median step
over steps 6-15 of 15- and 20-step screens on latest main: previous recipe 5.17 s; with the fusions 4.35 s; CP4
with the fusions 3.94 s; recomputing only the MoE layers instead of full layers 3.29 s; and HybridEP instead of the
all-to-all dispatcher 3.11 s (3.16 s with full recompute). The chosen recipe peaks at 123 GB allocated (136 GB
reserved) of 198 GB. Recomputing only `moe_act` and `layernorm` is faster still (2.68 s) but peaks at 169 GB
allocated (176 GB reserved), and without recompute the second stage runs out of memory. TP2/CP8 matches TP4/CP4,
and CP8 at TP4 is invalid for A2A context parallelism (four KV heads per tensor-parallel rank). EP64 with three
stages is slower with the all-to-all dispatcher (4.45 s) and hung in the first step with HybridEP. Fixed-width
packs, which removed the recompile spikes in the 4K recipes, hung in the first step of the 32K CP4 layout (the 8K CP2
recipe below runs fine with them), so the 32K packs keep their natural widths. The card run's window includes two
slower steps (3.9 s and 3.7 s at steps 11 and 14); its median step is 3.0 s. Virtual pipeline stages would shrink the
remaining pipeline bubble, but the Muse Spark Open builder does not support them yet. The 64K CoderForge recipe is
covered below.

## Recipes outside the card

15-step runs on latest main, average of steps 6-15, main's recipe against this PR's, with the same dataset and
global batch size (with a different layout, the sample order or the pack membership can differ). These are short
throughput runs, not card runs; every run used the committed recipe without overrides.

| Recipe | GPUs | Main's recipe | Main step | This PR | This PR step | Speedup |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Full SFT, 8K packs, GBS 8 | 256 | TP4/PP8/EP32, full recompute | 2,789.69 ms | TP4/PP4/CP2/EP64, no recompute, fixed-width packs | 1,688.12 ms | **1.65x** |
| Pretraining, 4K, GBS 1024 | 256 | TP1/PP8/EP32, full recompute | 20,488.93 ms | + fusions, HybridEP | 15,821.46 ms | **1.30x** |
| CoderForge SFT, 64K packs, GBS 8 | 192 | TP4/PP6/CP4/EP32, full recompute | 7,682.67 ms | + fusions, HybridEP | 6,071.13 ms | **1.27x** |
| Image-text SFT, CORD-v2, GBS 64 | 192 | TP1/PP6/EP32, full recompute | 6,954.35 ms | TP4/PP3/EP64, full recompute | 4,525.23 ms | **1.54x** |
| Image-text LoRA, CORD-v2, GBS 8 | 16 | TP4/EP16, `core_attn` recompute | 3,264.21 ms | TP4/EP16, no recompute | 2,175.03 ms | **1.50x** |

Main's recipes use the all-to-all dispatcher and no fusions; every PR recipe uses HybridEP and the fusions.

- The image-text SFT recipe as first changed in this PR (no recompute) ran out of memory at the end of its first
  step on the first stage, which also trains the vision tower. Recomputing only the MoE layers is a little faster
  (4,226 ms) but peaks at 167 GB reserved on that stage against 149 GB with full recompute, so the recipe recomputes
  full layers.
- For the 64K CoderForge recipe, recomputing only the MoE layers is faster still (5,569 ms) but reserves 182 GB of
  198 GB, so the recipe keeps full recompute.
- For the 8K SFT recipe, CP4 (two microbatches per replica) is no faster than CP2 at 8K tokens, and variable-width
  packs add recompile spikes, so the recipe keeps CP2 with fixed-width packs.
- The image-text LoRA recipe's 183 GB peak is identical in main's recipe, so it points to model setup rather than
  training; about 122 GB is allocated at the logged steps.
- Losses, with the same data and batches: the CoderForge losses stay within 0.4% of main's at every step, and the
  256-GPU pretraining losses within 0.9% except at step 11 (1.24%; main's run has a gradient-norm spike of 60.8 at
  step 10). In a 3-step image-text LoRA ablation, turning off the fusions or HybridEP moves the first-step loss by
  0.4% and 0.3%.

Two changes carry most of the EP64 gain:

- The Megatron-Core fusions that the builder-based model config leaves off (bias activation, gradient accumulation,
  RoPE, router, and permute into HybridEP) plus pipeline-output deallocation: about 10% on the EP64 pretraining
  layout, with lower peak memory.
- Fixed-width packs (`pad_to_max_length`). With variable widths (for example 4,080 or 4,096 tokens), each
  data-parallel replica recompiled nine `torch.compile`d router, dispatcher, output-gate, and cross-entropy fusions
  the first time its width changed, and every other rank waited for it at the next collective: SFT steps alternated
  between about 4.5 s and 21 s.
