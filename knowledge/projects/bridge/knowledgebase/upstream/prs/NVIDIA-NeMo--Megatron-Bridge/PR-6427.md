## Summary
- Add a `CUDNN_FRONTEND_GIT_REF` build arg to `docker/Dockerfile.fw_final`, pinned to the tip of `NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend` branch `feat/1.29.0-megatron`.
- The ref is installed over the PyPI wheel after the environment sync, before the base-runtime check. `fw_final` re-syncs with `fw_pyproject.toml` (pins `nvidia-cudnn-frontend==1.29.0`), so an install made earlier in `Dockerfile.ci` would be replaced.
- Skipped when the arg is empty or `PRESERVE_BASE_RUNTIME=True`.
- Docker-only: no change to `pyproject.toml`, `fw_pyproject.toml` or `uv.lock`. The Bridge CI image (`Dockerfile.ci`) is unchanged and keeps the locked wheel.
