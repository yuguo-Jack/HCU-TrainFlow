## Background

Nightly Megatron-Core bump, main lane only. This moves Bridge to MCore `main` at `441a987409b8fdd2b90b463f7999bac970109a43`. `.dev.commit` is unchanged (`4a4de865`).

## What changed

- `.main.commit` and the `3rdparty/Megatron-LM` gitlink now point to `441a9874`.
- Ran `uv lock --upgrade` with the uv version pinned in `docker/Dockerfile.ci`, then passed `uv lock --check`. The lock `revision` field goes from 3 to 2 because that is what the pinned uv writes.
- Merged current `main` (`4dbd9652`, #6355 → MCore `52fbcbc1`). Since `52fbcbc1` is an ancestor of the new pin, the net MCore change is two commits: #7854 (MFSDP v2 delayed wgrad sync) and #6871 (HybridDeviceOptimizer stale grads).
- The diff against `main` touches only `.main.commit`, `3rdparty/Megatron-LM` and `uv.lock`.

## Details

No Bridge source or test changes. The validation image was built from this lock and MCore pin, and its gitlink binding was checked against this head.

## Tested

Local H100 runs (`megatron-bridge` profile v15), with rows selected by impact on the MCore and lock changes:
- **Run on this exact head (442d7aae):**
  - Core unit shards 0/3, 1/3 and 2/3: 3930, 3907 and 3900 passed, 0 failed.
  - `L0_Launch_converter`: 7 passed.
- **Passed earlier, retained:** the Diffusion unit row and 15 functional rows (megatron_fsdp, converter_fsdp, converter_generate, models_deepseek, models_flux, peft_replicated_adapters, peft_router_checkpoint, training_pretrain/finetune/decentralized_pg, data, gtp_checkpoint_conversion, wan_ckpt_conversion, vlm_inference, recipes_llama_1b). They passed on the pre-merge candidate; a recorded input comparison shows the merged `main` changes do not affect them.
- **Not validated locally:** `L0_Launch_recipes_deepseek`. Its DSv4 toy test data is absent on the runner, so every case skips. The requester waived it; it is not a pass, and main CI is its gate.
- **Excluded by impact analysis, not run:** other model-family conversion rows (including GLM5) and miscellaneous training rows. Main CI is authoritative for them.

Main CI is pending.
