# feat(zaya1): add FP8 recipes that train cold on mock data

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/AMD-AGI/Primus/pull/1269) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Summary
- Add FP8 training for ZAYA1-8B (`fp8: hybrid`). CCA Q/K/V/O projections and expert SwiGLU GEMMs run as Transformer Engine linears (`parallel_mode="duplicated"`) under one FP8 autocast for the whole stack. The router, depthwise convolutions, RMSNorm, attention, embeddings, and the tied logits projection stay in BF16/FP32.
- Pad the token axis to a multiple of 8 before each TE GEMM. A top-1 route can hand one expert as few as 4 tokens, and TE FP8 needs the leading-dimension product divisible by 8. Reject FP8 GEMMs whose in or out features are not multiples of 16. The router stays BF16 because its last projection scores 17 classes.
- Add `zaya1_8B-FP8-pretrain.yaml` for MI300X and MI355X with `fp8_recipe: tensorwise`. Current scaling takes each scale from the tensor being cast, so FP8 is accurate from step 1 with no amax history. Delayed scaling starts with an empty history; on the released checkpoint a cold delayed-scaling forward scored SGLang rollouts at Pearson r 0.72.
- Switch the BF16 and FP8 ZAYA1 recipes to mock data so they run without a dataset mount. `vocab_size` stays 262272 from `zaya1.yaml`.
- Update `docs/04-technical-guides/zaya1/validation.md` and `docs/06-developer-guide/model-support-matrix.md`.

## Validation
Run on 1x MI300X node (tw042), `rocm/primus:v26.8`, TE 2.17.1, Megatron-LM `d3528a21`.

Log-prob parity against SGLang ([sgl-project/sglang#26347](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/26347)): the same 4 GSM8K greedy rollouts used for BF16, 1,024 tokens each, scored by Primus with the `Zyphra/ZAYA1-base` weights and no FP8 warm-up.

| Rollout tokens (n=4096) | Pearson r | MAE | RMSE |
|---|---|---|---|
| BF16 | 0.978 | 0.010 | 0.055 |
| FP8 tensorwise, cold | 0.935 | 0.026 | 0.101 |
| FP8 delayed, cold | 0.720 | 0.155 | — |
| FP8 delayed, after 5 backward calibration passes | 0.893 | 0.033 | 0.142 |

GSM8K pretraining from scratch with the FP8 recipe (seq 128, global batch 8, lr 1e-5). Early losses track the BF16 validation run:

| Step | BF16 | FP8 tensorwise |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 12.820 | 12.873 |
| 10 | 11.229 | 10.911 |
| 30 | 7.597 | 7.791 |

No skipped or NaN iterations. The 30k-step run was not completed: at 3.2 s/step on 4 GPUs it needs about 27 h, beyond the 8 h job limit.
