# feat(zaya1): add MI325X recipes and size 8B batches to GPU memory

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/AMD-AGI/Primus/pull/1270) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

Stacked on #1269 (base `dev/clairlee/zaya1`). Retarget to `main` once #1269 merges.

## Summary

- Add MI325X `zaya1_8B-BF16-pretrain.yaml` and `zaya1_8B-FP8-pretrain.yaml`. All six ZAYA1-8B recipes (MI300X, MI325X, MI355X × BF16, FP8) train on mock data.
- Size `micro_batch_size` on MI300X and MI325X to the largest multiple of 8 that completes on one 8-GPU node with `rocm/primus:v26.8`, end-of-training evaluation included. `global_batch_size` is micro batch × 8, so each GPU runs one micro batch per step.
- FP8 recipes set `NVTE_USE_CAST_TRANSPOSE_TRITON: "0"` in their `env:` block. The runner enables Transformer Engine's Triton cast-transpose, which autotunes for every new tensor shape, and ZAYA1's expert token counts change every step. With the Triton kernel, MI325X FP8 at micro batch 64 spent 307 s on step 1 and still took 2 to 10 s per step at steps 11 to 15. With the HIP cast kernel, steps take 1.8 s from step 3 on.
- Docs: MI325X added to the support matrix (config counts updated), and a throughput section in the ZAYA1 validation note.

## Results

Recipes run as checked in: 30 steps, mock data, sequence length 128, data parallel 8, `rocm/primus:v26.8` (PyTorch 2.14.0, TE 2.17.1). Step time is the median over steps 6 to 30, tokens/s/GPU the harmonic mean, and TFLOP/s/GPU the mean. Every run finished with 0 skipped and 0 NaN iterations.

| GPU | Precision | Micro / global batch | Step time | Tokens/s/GPU | TFLOP/s/GPU | Peak memory per GPU | Tokens/s/GPU before (micro 1, global 8) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MI300X | BF16 | 128 / 1024 | 1635 ms | 10016 | 118.6 | 181 of 192 GB | 171 |
| MI300X | FP8 | 120 / 960 | 1814 ms | 8478 | 100.4 | 187 of 192 GB | 115 |
| MI325X | BF16 | 216 / 1728 | 2049 ms | 13426 | 159.0 | 249 of 256 GB | 175 |
| MI325X | FP8 | 184 / 1472 | 2118 ms | 11154 | 132.1 | 245 of 256 GB | 119 |

Next size up: MI300X BF16 136 trains but stalls in the end-of-training evaluation at 98% memory. MI300X FP8 128, MI325X BF16 224, and MI325X FP8 192 run out of memory on the fp32 logits buffer.

TFLOP/s/GPU is Megatron's analytic GPT estimate. It models neither CCA nor the alternating attention and MoE stages, so compare it only between ZAYA1 runs.

FP8 is slower than BF16 at this size. With hidden size 2048 and top-1 routing over 16 experts, the expert GEMMs are small, and the FP8 casts cost more than the faster GEMMs save.

## Not covered

- MI355X was not run. Its batch sizes stay at micro 1, global 8. Its FP8 recipe gets the same `env:` override because the Triton autotuning comes from the TE build, not the GPU.
- `PYTORCH_HIP_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True` let MI325X FP8 fit micro batch 192 (+3% tokens/s), but MI325X BF16 224 still ran out of memory. Not adopted.
