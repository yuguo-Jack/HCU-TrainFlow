## Affected area

FE OSS kernels or CuTeDSL

## Summary

On cc 10.3, `fwd/placement`'s hand rule keeps dense d128 squares on the backend. That rule was measured on B200 ("parity to 1.07"), and the module docstring says SM103 "runs the same thresholds, not re-measured there". On a B300 the FROST forward is faster on every dense causal GQA square measured.

`placement_table.TABLE` gains two cc 10.3 entries for `sdpa_fwd_prefill_sm100`, bf16, (128, 128), prefill, dense, top-left causal, GQA: one on the 128-key tile and its off-tile twin. Both are `always`, emitted by `benchmark/placement_gen/gen.py --emit`. Mask-free graphs, MHA, f16 and cc 10.0 get no entry and keep the hand rules.

`test_cc103_d128_forward_dense_causal_gqa_entries_are_its_own` covers:

- on- and off-tile lookups;
- the same graph on cc 10.0;
- mask-free, MHA and f16 graphs, which have no entry.

## Why

On these graphs the default dispatch runs the backend. Across the 47 measured cases, the backend takes 1.00-2.0x the FROST time (1.07-2.0x on the holdout).

## The study

`sm103_d128_fwd_dense_20261031`. B300 SXM6 (1100 W), develop `f510fe8f`, backend leg on cuDNN 9.28.0.38, bf16, d 128/128, dense BSHD squares (s_q = s_kv).

Each case runs the three legs of `benchmark/attention_training/benchmark_single_sdpa.py --profile_pass fwd`: `cudnn` (backend only), `cudnn_oss` (FROST pinned) and `cudnn_auto` (default dispatch). Each leg times 20 iterations after 5 warmup, and the leg order rotates per case. The cases were split over two GPUs, each idle for 120 s before start. One shard was stopped and resumed on another GPU; no case was timed twice.

- Train (104 cases, sha256 `17b0df3e…`):
  - masks: top-left causal and mask-free;
  - heads: GQA 32/8, 16/2, 64/4 and MHA 16/16;
  - on the tile: S 512-16384 at 32k tokens, plus b 1 at S 512 / 1024;
  - off the tile: S 700-12000.
- Holdout (32 cases, sha256 `d887da73…`, seed 20261106):
  - four per class (mask × group × tile);
  - heads GQA 24/3, 40/5, 48/6, 12/2 and MHA 12/12, 20/20, 8/8;
  - S on the tile 768-10240, off it 600-14000; 4k-64k tokens.

Both lists were frozen before timing; the holdout was used once.

| class (causal GQA) | train: FROST / backend | holdout: FROST / backend | holdout: generated / default |
|---|---|---|---|
| on the tile (24 + 4 cases) | 0.750-1.000 | 0.896-0.934 | 0.894-0.934 |
| off the tile (15 + 4 cases) | 0.512-0.607 | 0.498-0.580 | 0.498-0.581 |

All eight causal GQA holdout cases pick the faster leg (regret 1.000).

**Supplementary qualification (review: shorter prefill and unequal Q / KV).** Each entry answers for its whole class: dense top-left causal GQA prefill, s_q >= 5, any s_kv, on or off the tile. That class includes shapes the study did not time. A second frozen set ran on the same part with the same three legs: 22 cases (sha256 `36cb85cb…`, seed 20261107), split as:

- 12 short squares, s_q 5-448 (on and off the tile), b 1-256;
- 10 unequal top-left cases, s_q < s_kv (64-2048 over 2048-8192) and s_q > s_kv (2048-8192 over 128-2048).

Results, by whether this PR changes the route:

- **Develop's default runs the backend (14 of the 22 cases): the entries move these to FROST.** FROST takes 0.26-1.00 of the backend's time on every one: short squares 0.51-0.89, s_q > s_kv 0.26-0.95, s_q < s_kv 0.55-1.00.
- **Develop's default already runs FROST (8 cases):** the decode-shaped s_q <= 16 squares, s_q 33-128 squares, and two top-left chunks with s_q << s_kv. The entries leave the route unchanged. The default leg was re-timed with this PR's table on 10 of the cases: these 8 ran the same plan at the same time (1.000), and the 2 that changed route ran 0.615 and 0.952.

One finding is outside this PR: top-left causal with s_q << s_kv (b 4, s_q 64, s_kv 2048, GQA 16/2) runs about 3x slower on FROST than on the backend. Develop's hand rules already route it to FROST.

**Classes left out.** They were scored on the same holdout:

- Mask-free GQA on the tile (fit: cost >= 31.5) lost on b 11, S 768, GQA 48/6: 1.34x the default.
- Mask-free MHA on the tile (fit: always) lost on b 43, S 768, MHA 20/20: 1.24x.
- Causal MHA: the fits rest on 5-8 cases, and one runs in the opposite direction (cost <= 31). They missed three holdout wins without regressing.

Over all eight classes the generated table scored 1.075 against the default's 1.189 (holdout geomean regret). Two cases came out slower than the default, so the full table is not proposed.

The two classes here were selected after the holdout was scored. `gen.py` fits each class on its own data, so the entries do not depend on the classes left out.

## Related issues
