## Summary

Speeds up SM120 (RTX PRO 6000) f16/bf16 SDPA decode on the FROST forward row `sdpa_fwd_prefill_sm120`:

- **Decode tile** (`kernels/sm120/decode_f16.py`, `tile_m` 16 / 32). A TMA producer warp streams 8 KB K/V stages (16 KB at head tile 256) through an mbarrier ring. Four consumer warps split the key axis (four key groups at 16 rows, two key groups x two row groups at 32) and merge their softmax state after the loop. Each key group owns its ring slots, so one waiter consumes each slot's phases in order. The tile serves dense graphs with head dims up to 256, the prefill row's masks, sinks, padding, Stats (natural or log2) and split-KV.
- **Whole-group PackGQA without divisibility** on the decode tile and the general / D=256 prefill templates. A Q tile holds `tile_m // G` whole tokens; the rows past `(tile_m // G) * G` are dead (zero Q, never stored). GQA 6 now packs.
- **Split-KV with an attention sink** on the half templates (decode, general, D=256, D=512). A split writes sink-free partials, and the existing split combine folds the sink once.
- **Heuristics.** A decode-shaped graph (`S_q * G <= 32`) leads with the smallest decode tile that holds the packed rows. Its split count comes from a stage-chain / bandwidth model fitted on RTX PRO 6000 ladders (72 ladders: mean regret 0.3 %, worst 5 %). Packed prefill sets take the smallest tile that holds a KV head's rows. Decode-tile sets always use `SCHED_NATURAL`, and the SM120 fallback plan is the smallest prefill pair in the kernel table.
- **Eligibility.** `mismatch()` now checks the (`tile_m`, `tile_n`) pair against `config_sm120.tile_domain`, not just each knob's own domain. A pair the template has no kernel for, such as `(64, 16)`, is declined before lowering.

Example of the behavior change (gpt-oss-20B decode, 64/8 heads, d64, sink, 128K keys, b=1):

| | plan | kernel time |
|---|---|---|
| before | prefill tile, 128 rows, unsplit (sink barred the split): 8 CTAs on 188 SMs | 1742 us |
| after | decode tile, 16 rows, packed, split-KV + sink-folding combine | 176 us |

These two times come from one screening pass. The same graph at 4K keys is in the gated A/B below: 67.5 us before, 11.5 us after.

## Why

A llama.cpp flash-attention comparison on RTX PRO 6000 showed three FROST decode gaps (screening-grade ratios, see below):

- gpt-oss with sinks ran at 0.15-0.26x of llama.cpp: the SM120 config barred split + sink.
- GQA 6 (Qwen3.6-27B, d256) ran at 0.47-0.65x: packing needed G to divide the tile, so each KV head was read 6 times.
- Single-token decode was at parity at best: a 128-row tile carried 4 live rows over single-buffered K/V.

Reproduced and measured on one RTX PRO 6000 Workstation (188 SMs), SM 2400 MHz / memory 14001 MHz locked, cuDNN 9.27.0.42, CuTe DSL 4.7.0. FROST time is the torch-profiler kernel time with a 256 MiB L2 scrub (median of 20 launches); llama.cpp time is the CUPTI busy time of its FA kernels under the same scrub.

**FROST after vs. FROST before (gated A/B).** 72 of the shapes, 3 alternating before/after process pairs, median of the per-pair ratios. Every shape's between-run CV is at most 1.2 % on both legs.

| f16 shapes | speedup over FROST before (geomean) |
|---|---|
| q = 1 | 1.77x |
| q = 2 | 1.80x |
| q = 3-8 | 1.83x |
| q = 9-31 | 1.50x |
| all 72 shapes | 1.65x |

The worst per-shape pair median is 0.996x, within noise: no shape regresses.

**FROST vs. llama.cpp (screening only).** llama.cpp ran in one separate pass, not in alternating pairs with FROST, so these ratios carry about ±10 % between-process noise. Each FROST column is one pass over all 744 shapes.

| f16 shapes (geomean speedup over llama.cpp) | FROST before | FROST after |
|---|---|---|
| q = 1 | 0.64x | 1.08x |
| q = 2 | 0.67x | 1.13x |
| q = 3-8 | 0.76x | 1.28x |
| q = 9-31 | 0.84x | 1.19x |
| all 744 shapes | 0.81x | 1.20x |

On the 72 A/B shapes, the gated FROST-after medians are 1.25x of that llama.cpp pass.

Known remaining gap (screening): GQA 6 / 8 at 17-24 query tokens over 64K-128K keys is 0.79-0.86x of llama.cpp. Those shapes need two Q tiles per KV head, so the KV is read twice; FROST before had the same gap.

## API and compatibility impact

- No public API change. `tile_m` 16 / 32 and `tile_n` 16 join the SM120 half row's knob domains (append-only).
- The SM120 FP16/BF16 row now splits sink graphs and packs GQA ratios that do not divide the tile. FP8 / MXFP8 rows are unchanged (sink graphs stay unsplit; packing keeps the divisor rule).
- A (`tile_m`, `tile_n`) pair outside `config_sm120.tile_domain` is now ineligible in `mismatch()` instead of raising in the adapter.
- `SUPPORT_MATRIX_TRACKER.md` is updated (Rule S2).
