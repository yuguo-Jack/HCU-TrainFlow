# gemm(frost sm100): mask K-padding scale bytes before tcgen05.cp (B02)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/2212) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Problem

This is the SM100 follow-up that #2010 (B02) left open ("The sm100 templates handle the K tail the same way and are left for a follow-up").

When `ceil(K / block_size)` is not a multiple of 4, the last F8_128x4 word of every scale row holds 1–3 padding bytes. That happens for MXFP8 / MXFP4 with `K % 128` in {32, 64, 96} and for NVFP4 with `K % 64 == 32`; gpt-oss K = 2880 is one example.
- The SF TMA box loads that word whole, and `tcgen05.cp` copies it to TMEM for the MMAs of the TMA-zero-filled K blocks.
- A padding byte that encodes NaN (UE8M0 0xFF, UE4M3 0x7F / 0xFF) gives 0 × NaN = NaN, so the whole output row (SFA) or column (SFB) becomes NaN.

On B300 (cc 10.3), develop d760fefe (the GEMM tree is the same as at 97b7bc31), every case below returns NaN-poisoned output when the K padding of both blobs is a NaN encoding. With finite padding it matches the dequantized reference.

## Change

- `tile_helpers.mask_block_scale_k_padding`: in one staged SF tile, ANDs the given K word with a live-byte mask in every row of every 128-row block, then issues `fence.proxy.async` and a warp sync.
  - Each lane masks one 16-byte row quad per block, with all loads issued before the stores.
  - With a peer rank it also masks the CTA pair's other CTA through `mapa` (`fence.proxy.async.shared::cluster`). Under `cta_group::2` the leader's `tcgen05.cp` reads both CTAs' SF: masking only the leader's copy left all 8 2-CTA cases wrong.
- MMA warp: once per kernel it computes the padded word `(K / block_size - 1) // 4` and its mask. In the K tile that holds that word (SM103: the SF group), right after the stage's SF barrier and before its `tcgen05.cp`, it masks SFA and SFB.
  - This covers `sm100_block_scale_matmul`, `sm103_block_scale_matmul` and the four MoE block-scale templates: normal, swap-AB, gather and gather swap-AB.
  - K values without padding skip the mask (a uniform branch). With finite padding bytes, the MMA inputs are unchanged.
- Tests, each filling the K padding of both blobs with a NaN encoding:
  - `test_sm100_block_scale_ignores_k_padding_scales`: MXFP8, MXFP4 and NVFP4; 1-CTA, 2-CTA and split-K 3.
  - `test_sm103_block_scale_ignores_k_padding_scales`: NVFP4 and MXFP4; 1-CTA, 2-CTA and split-K 3.
  - `test_e2e_k_padding_scales_are_ignored_sm100`: MoE, 3 combos × 1-CTA/2-CTA × normal/swap-AB.
  - `test_gather_k_padding_scales_are_ignored_sm100`: MoE GATHER, 3 combos × normal/swap-AB.
  - `_run_sm103_numeric` and `_run_gather_block_scale` gain a `k_pad_byte` parameter, like `_run_bs_numeric` and `_run_e2e` already have.

## Tests

On B300 (cc 10.3), develop d760fefe:
- New tests: all 27 fail on develop and all 27 pass with this change. The breakdown is 5 SM100 dense, 4 SM103 dense, 12 MoE and 6 GATHER; the 4 SM120 tests in the selection skip.
- `-m "L0 or L1"` over `test_block_scale_matmul` and `test_moe_grouped_block_scale_matmul_fwd`: 1095 passed, 350 skipped, 0 failed with this change. On develop the only failures in that selection are the new tests.
- Timing, median of 5×50 launches, 4096×4096 output; padded K = 2880 (MXFP8) / 2848 (NVFP4), unpadded K = 4096:
  - In a quiet round, every case was within about 3% of develop in both directions, including the 2-CTA case.
  - An earlier version did the peer writes one word at a time and took ~89 µs against ~50 µs for 2-CTA. The vectorized, loads-first form removed that.
  - The B300 was shared with other jobs, and some rounds swung by 2× in both trees, so these are indicative only.
- `pre-commit` on the touched files passes.
