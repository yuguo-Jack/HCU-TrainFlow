## Summary

**Stacked on #1181** (the Rule 8 core PR: `WorkspaceCarver` in `api_base`, the R9 detector). **For the HSTU owners to review**: plan-level classes only; the wrapper keeps its signature and allocates per call.

Rule 8 (#1167) for HSTU attention:
- **R2.** `HSTUFwdSm100` / `HSTUBwdSm100` gain `scratch_workspace_bytes()` and `execute(..., workspace=)`: the block-sparse metadata buffers and the backward's fp32 accumulator are carved from the caller's workspace instead of allocated per execute; the accumulator is zeroed on the launch stream every execute (R4).
- **R5.** Layouts the kernel cannot read natively decline in `check_support()` (`NotImplementedError` naming the tensor) instead of being copied per execute; contiguity is keyed on the D256 route.
- **R11.** `compile()` builds from fake tensors that match `mark_layout_dynamic`; 0 allocations at compile.
- **Detectors (R9).** Allocation-count + sync detectors for fwd and bwd, contract tests for the missing workspace; the CUDA-graph detector counts allocations inside the capture window (torch 2.13's `capture_begin` itself adds 2).

## Why

Rule 8: a plan owns no device memory and never blocks the host; per-execute metadata / accumulator allocations made `execute()` uncapturable and their GC-timed frees are the #1151 class of capture invalidation.

## Related issues

#1167, #1168 (merged), #1181; supersedes the HSTU part of #1173.

## API and compatibility impact

**Wrapper: signature and behaviour unchanged.** A wrapper-compatibility audit against develop found and fixed, in 4e8668931: `hstu_attention_forward` / `hstu_attention_backward` were surfacing the plans' layout declines (non-native `q/k/v/do`, caller-provided grads the plan cannot bind, and at D=256 every non-contiguous operand — e.g. packed `qkv.unbind(1)` inputs and `dqkv[:, i]` gradient outputs). The wrappers now stage in the eager layer on the launch stream: inputs cloned contiguous before the plan lookup, unbindable caller grads run into contiguous scratch and are copied back (identity kept), omitted grads allocated in an accepted layout. `_interface._normalize_window` / `_bwd_d256_route` are factored out of the dispatchers (no behaviour change). Wrapper-level success tests restored beside the kept class-level declines (`test_wrappers_normalise_layouts_the_plan_declines`). `api_index.txt` gains `HSTUFwdSm100.scratch_workspace_bytes` and `HSTUBwdSm100.scratch_workspace_bytes`; no removals. Downstream survey (TransformerEngine, Megatron-LM, flashinfer, vLLM, SGLang): no consumer of the HSTU classes found.

Plan-level classes: `execute(..., workspace=)` required when `scratch_workspace_bytes() > 0`; unsupported layouts raise `NotImplementedError` at `check_support()` instead of being copied.

- YangXu1990uiuc · comment · 2026-09-22T19:14:07Z · [source](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1185#issuecomment-5782456996)

Follow-up to my review on `604b9d6b6b2a`: one additional **P1 that blocks approval** is now independently reproduced in the HSTU wrapper change itself.

**Keep the original input alive until its staging copy finishes.** At [`api.py:177–179`](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/blob/604b9d6b6b2ae4ffb39bae34c10a9dc9cc5112eb/python/cudnn/hstu/hstu_attention/api.py#L177), `_stage_input` clones a non-native input on the explicit launch stream. The wrapper then replaces its reference with that clone. The class API records the clone's stream usage, but never sees or records the original allocation that the queued copy still reads. If the caller releases its original view after the wrapper returns, its allocation can be reused on the allocation stream before the copy runs.

I reproduced this through the unmodified `hstu_attention_forward` wrapper on SM100 with BF16 `(S,H,D)=(128,2,64)`, a Q view of `(128,2,65)` storage, and an explicit side stream. After warming both the wrapper and the probe's overwrite kernel, I delayed the side stream, called the wrapper, released the original Q, and filled a newly allocated buffer at the same address. The side stream was still pending at reuse, and `max|O-reference|` was **0.7025**. An otherwise identical control calling `q.record_stream(stream)` before releasing Q prevented address reuse and matched the reference exactly. Inputs were ready before the delayed launch; this is allocation lifetime, not a missing producer dependency.

Please record the original staged input on the resolved launch stream before dropping it, or retain it through completion. Cover staged Q/K/V and backward dO with a delayed-copy/allocator-reuse regression. The ordinary layout tests keep their inputs alive through synchronization, so they do not exercise this case. This is a high-risk P1 because an otherwise supported explicit-stream call can silently consume overwritten input. The separate inherited pending-workspace-cap finding remains unchanged.
