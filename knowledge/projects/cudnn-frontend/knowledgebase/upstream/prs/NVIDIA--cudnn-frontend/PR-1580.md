## Affected area

Test only: `test/python/block_sparse_attention/cutedsl/test_BSA_attention_forward.py`.

## Summary

`test_bsa_attention_forward_sm90_blk64_large_logits_mixed_warp` (added in #1213) fails both parametrizations on the three SM90 cutedsl lanes of develop schedule 73250492:
- `python/*/cutedsl:cutlass-rel:sm90`;
- `cutlass-4.7:sm90`;
- `cutlass-4.8:sm90`.

Failure:
- Line 566 checks the even-row LSE against `atol=1e3`.
- The max abs diff is **74624.0** for fp16 and **19648.0** for bf16, on an LSE of about 5.7e8.

**Cause: the reference, not the kernel.**
- The even rows have the same logit, about 6.4e9 × 0.088, on every key, so their LSE does not depend on the random data.
- The reference is an fp32 `einsum`. With TF32 matmul enabled, it is off by about 1.3e-4 relative.
- Measured on an H100 NVL against the fp64 value:

| dtype | kernel | fp32 reference | TF32 reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| fp16 | 6 | 58 | 74630 |
| bf16 | 13 | 51 | 19635 |

- `TORCH_ALLOW_TF32_CUBLAS_OVERRIDE=1` on develop reproduces the CI numbers exactly: 74624.0 and 19648.0.
- Without the override, the test passes locally, which is how it went green before merge.

**The fix.** The test pins `torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32 = False` through `monkeypatch`, as `test_BSA_sm90_backward_sync.py` already does. The kernel and the tolerances are unchanged.
