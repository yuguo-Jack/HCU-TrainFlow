# [SDPA] Use shared-memory alpha exchange for packed decode tiles

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1852) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

Packed half-precision attention in the shared SM100 decode tile exchanges the per-KV rescaling factor through TMEM, including a store wait and a subsequent load wait. Move that scalar exchange to a small shared-memory array under the existing `stat_full` / `stat_empty` release/acquire handshake. Final max/sum statistics stay in TMEM.

Specialize the exchange on the existing packing configuration. The measured packed paths benefit, while unpacked paths keep their original TMEM exchange and shared-memory footprint. This changes no public API, knob, plan admission, or heuristic ordering, and preserves both the normal and quadrant softmax paths.

Validation:
- The shared-alpha implementation passed 65 existing D64/D128/D192 functional regressions on B200, covering O/Stats, changed lengths, fresh bindings, and retained CUDA Graph replay. Two paged/nonpaged regressions also passed paired baseline/candidate memcheck, racecheck, and synccheck on SM100.
- The final packing specialization has seven paired CPU compiler checks: packed D64/D128 cubins are byte-identical to the validated shared-alpha implementation; unpacked D64/D128 and MHA cubins are byte-identical to the original implementation. This is representative binary-equivalence evidence, not a claim that all 65 GPU cases were rerun on the final branch.
- Completed B200 measurements compare the old and new legal FE plan pools in both Stats modes and traversal orders. Further matched-plan analysis retained unpacked slowdowns and motivated the compile-time specialization above. Raw results and limitations remain in the internal report; no default-dispatch or serving claim.
- Scoped pre-commit hooks pass. Normal SDPA GPU CI is requested for this final branch.
