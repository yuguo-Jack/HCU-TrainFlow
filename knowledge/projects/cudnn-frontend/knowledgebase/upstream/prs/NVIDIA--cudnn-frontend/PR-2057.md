## Summary

Three commits on `develop` (the cells + docs + tracker; the tracker's run record filled after the cc 10.0 run; the review follow-up: the EXTEND cells in L0 and the validation claim per form; squash at merge). A serving stack keeps ONE KV-cache tensor per layer, `[num_pages, H_kv, P, 2D]`, with K in columns `[0, D)` and V in `[D, 2D)` of every row (the HND cache of the 24 / 4 model family at `d_head` 256: `[pages, 4, 16 or 32, 512]` bf16). The gated attention block (`cudnn.gated_attention_block`) already admits that tensor as its two pools WITHOUT a copy: the two half views `kv[..., :D]` / `kv[..., D:]` are the "unified" layout of `SERVING_POOL_LAYOUTS`, both with strides `(H_kv*P*2D, P*2D, 2D, 1)` -- `D` contiguous, every other stride a whole number of 16-byte vectors, the V base `D` elements past the K base -- so `check_pool` admits them and the two samples share one layout kind, and the pool matrix of both serving suites already runs that layout at 24 / 2 and 8 / 2 on every page size. **No contract gap was found; this PR is cells, docs and the tracker.**

What no cell pinned before: the stack's own shape (`H_kv` 4, the 24 / 4 geometry) on the unified row, kv up to 2048, a page-32 speculative step on it, int64 slots, and an explicit assertion that the OTHER half of every row stays untouched. This PR adds one named cell per paged serving form, L0, `requires_dense_part` (cc 10.7 and cc 10.0), at 24 / 4, `d_head` 256 and a shrunk `d_model` 512, int64 slots, bf16 and f16:

| form | cell | what it pins |
|---|---|---|
| DECODE (`kv_source="paged"` on a dense `[B, S_q]` block) | `test_block_serving_decode.py::test_packed_kv_rows_of_a_serving_stack_bind_as_two_half_views_on_the_decode_form[p16-plain / p32-mtp4 x bf16 / f16]` | page 16 x `S_q` 1 and page 32 x `S_q` 4 over kv 2048 / 0 (padded) / 5 / 1000 / 33: `out` within the oracle's budget (the padded entry exactly 0); the ONE tensor behind the views (same base, the V data pointer `D` elements past the K one, the strides); the step's slots hold the slab's K band in the K half and the slab's V band in the V half, bitwise; the prefix rows unchanged in both halves; every row nobody addressed -- block 0, the stale tail slots, the spare pages -- still the sentinel in BOTH halves (a K store running past `D` would land in the V half of the same row, and vice versa); the written row count = the addressed slots x `H_kv`; two launches bitwise; `out` bitwise the HND-compact twin; the route pinned against the part's table (the gate in the combine on cc 10.7, its own launch on cc 10.0) |
| EXTEND (`kv_source="paged"` + `token_capacity`) | `test_block_serving_extend.py::test_extend_packed_kv_rows_of_a_serving_stack_bind_as_two_half_views[p16 / p32 x bf16 / f16]` | four sequences at `T_max` 512 -- a 148-token chunk completing a 2048-key context (128 pages at 16, 64 at 32), an empty-prefix sequence, a padded entry (the NULL block), a 100-token step over a 5-row prefix -- `T_live` 376; the suite's `check` (the per-sequence oracle, the rows read back through both halves bitwise the slab rows attention consumed, every unaddressed row still stale in both halves, the NULL page zero); two launches bitwise; bitwise the HND-compact twin on the same histories and pages; on cc 10.0 the stage pinned on the shared SM100 paged THD body |

Docs: one paragraph under the write-through pools table of `docs/operations/attention/gated_attention_block.md` naming the packed layout as served through the two views, with the stride rule. Tracker (Rule S2, same commit): the paragraph under the pool contract, one row each in the EXTEND and the DECODE pool-layout tables (pages 16 / 32 at the stack's shape), one row in the cc 10.0 table (the run record below).
