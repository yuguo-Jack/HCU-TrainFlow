# gated_attention_block: route the 32-column SM100 decode arm on cc 10.3

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/2152) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Problem

`DECODE_WIDE_TILE_CCS` is empty. On the SM100 line, every DECODE step that the SDPA adapter's rule puts on the 32-column decode tile at a wide launch is therefore a typed decline. That covers two kinds of step:

- `W`: 17 .. 32 packed rows;
- `U32`: an unpacked step of 17 .. 32 tokens.

The constant's own comment and the reasons give the lift: the tuple gains a part together with the arm's accept cells there. On cc 10.3 (B300), which runs the same SM100 kernels as cc 10.0, no cell of the arm had run.

## Change

- `DECODE_WIDE_TILE_CCS = ((10, 3),)`.
- On a part in the tuple, the unpacked off-rule reason (a small launch) says that a wide launch routes the step on this part, instead of naming the lift.
- Tests:
  - new `test_sm100_wide_decode_arm_matches_the_oracle` (the accept cells, below);
  - the on-part decline test routes the wide launch where the part is in the tuple;
  - the host row pins the tuple.
- `api.py` comments, `gated_attention_block.md` and `SUPPORT_MATRIX_TRACKER.md` say cc 10.3 since 2026-11-01. Dated history entries are left as they were.
- cc 10.0 is unchanged: no cell of the block has run the arm there, so it keeps the decline and the lift text.

## Accept cells (B300, cc 10.3)

These are the wide cells of `_SM100_WIDE` at the 24 / 4 geometry. Each is checked against the per-sequence fp32 oracle under the dense suite's budget, with the route pinned against the compiled kernel (`sm100/decode_d256_f16.py` at 32 columns) and block 0 untouched:

| arm | batch x max_kv_len, S_q | route (tile, n_q, units, packed, KV streams) | split | vs oracle |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| W | 2 x 65536, 3 | decode_d256_f16, 32, 1, packed, 1 | 16 | cos 0.999998, max abs 2.4e-4 |
| W | 32 x 4096, 3 | decode_d256_f16, 32, 1, packed, 1 | 1 | cos 0.999996, max abs 4.9e-4 |
| U32 | 1 x 22528, 17 | decode_d256_f16, 32, 1, unpacked, 6 | 8 | cos 0.999997, max abs 2.4e-4 |
| U32 | 4 x 5632, 17 | decode_d256_f16, 32, 1, unpacked, 6 | 2 | cos 0.999996, max abs 2.4e-4 |

`test_block_serving_decode.py` (`-m "L0 or L1"`) plus `test/python/api_index` on B300: 156 passed, 80 skipped. The new cells and both updated tests ran and passed rather than skipping.

This is functional only and unmeasured, like the other cc 10.3 rows of the tuple family. Before this change, these steps had no route to compare against on this part.
