# sdpa d192 (sm100): causal LPT schedule, exp2-split spelling by claim, dense backward lists the d256 row first

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/2219) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## What

- Forward heuristics: the SM100 d192x128 f16/bf16 engine without GQA proposes plain LPT for a causal graph of at most `_SM100_D192_LPT_MAX_WAVES` grid waves of 512-row pair units and LPT_L2 above that; on cc 10.3 a graph with more (batch, head) K/V streams than SMs keeps the natural walk; mask-free graphs keep NATURAL.
- `make_cfg_d192` derives `CfgD192.EXP2_TAIL_MIX` from the exp2 FMA split claim (and the L2-prefetching loader), so a half-precision d192x128 build without the claim traces the plain MUFU exp2 and the default BMM1/BMM2 issue order on the dense top-left causal arm. The kernel drops the causal-only tail emulation the split had replaced; the validator keeps the loader and the spelling apart.
- Backward heuristics: `recommend()` lists the `sdpa_bwd_sm100_d256` row ahead of the opt-in `sdpa_bwd_sm100_d192` row for a dense half-precision (192, 128) declaration; packed THD declarations keep the d192 row first. `SUPPORT_MATRIX_TRACKER.md` notes the order.

## Validation

- `test_sdpa_fwd_heuristics.py`, `test_sdpa_bwd_heuristics.py`, `test_sdpa_fwd_config_sm100.py`, `test_sm100_exp2_split_arch_gate.py`.
- `test_sdpa_fwd_dsl_sm100.py` (d192 subset, including the per-arch SASS pins) and `test_sdpa_bwd_d192_sm100.py` on cc 10.0.
