# [HOLD] benchmark: keep the nightly at its prior cost (serving-form axes off, training 10 iterations)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1869) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

**Hold: do not merge until the nightly owners decide on cost.** If they accept the larger nightly, close this PR. If not, merge it.

## What

#1867 added the serving form of the cuDNN call (paged K/V, THD prefill, mixed cache lengths) and turned it on in five inference configs. It also moved the training suite to 20 iterations. Together those grow the nightly:

| suite | before #1867 | after #1867 |
|---|---:|---:|
| inference `llama` / `qwen35` | 276 cases | 648 |
| inference `gpt_oss` | 552 | 1296 |
| inference `deepseek_v4` | 528 | 1248 |
| inference `kimi_k3` | 180 | 408 |
| training (all configs) | 10 timed iterations | 20 |

This PR restores those defaults: no `kv_layouts` / `kv_min_fills` in the configs, and 10 iterations for training. The TE context-parallel configs keep 20 iterations.

Unchanged from #1867:
- the harness capability itself (`--kv_layout`, `--kv_min_fill` and the config fields);
- the fp8 graph fixes (no `Amax_S` request, sinks wired in);
- the CSV, chart and report columns.

Turning the serving form back on later is a two-line edit per config.

## Tradeoff

- **Merging this** saves nightly time. In exchange, the nightly loses the paged/THD and mixed-length rows. Those rows are what showed the fp8 MTP (7–11×), gpt-oss fp8 sink (13×) and dsv4 paged (1.19×) default-dispatch misranks.
- **At 10 training iterations**, two runs of the same plan differed by up to 1.19× on large shapes (#1856). Regressions smaller than that cannot be told apart from noise.

## Tests

- `benchmark/attention_inference/tests`: 12 passed. The QWEN35 golden counts are back to 276/192. The axes test sets the fields explicitly.
- Dry runs: llama 276, gpt_oss 552.
