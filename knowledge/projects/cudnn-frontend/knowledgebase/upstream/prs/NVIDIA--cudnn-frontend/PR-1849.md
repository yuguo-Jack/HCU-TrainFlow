## Affected area

FE OSS kernels or CuTeDSL (SDPA forward placement).

## Summary

`_sm100_paged_full_square` is a new placement arm on cc 10.0. It covers paged bf16 d128 **full-prefill squares** (Q = KV) with:
- b 1–4, page 16, head-major in-page K, causal, no window or sink;
- **GQA 4 / 8 / 16 and 1024 ≤ S ≤ 8192**.

In that box the FROST block now leads. MHA and group 2 keep the backend.

## Why

Before this change, full paged prefill on SM100 defaulted to backend engine 10 outside the existing GQA16 / bounded-chunk box. I ran a legal-pool sweep (every FROST knob set plus the backend, timed per case) over mixed-length full paged prefills on an SM100 board. FROST's *first* plan beat engine 10 on 11 of 12 shapes, at 0.55–0.84× its time. The exception was a single 4096-token group-2 square.

A holdout was frozen before the rule was written, with 24 cases across groups 1–16 and S up to 8192:
- **GQA cases inside the box:** every one improved, at 0.49–0.94× engine 10.
- **MHA:** an MHA case at S = 2048 regressed (1.03×), and group 2 had no in-box case.
- **Decision:** the rule keeps only the GQA band. The MHA/group-2 band was removed rather than refit.

The same holdout was then run through FlashInfer's paged prefill wrapper on a B200, with bounded and exact page-table forms. Every changed default was faster in both traversal orders, and the full FP64 O / LSE, replay and capture checks passed.

## API and compatibility impact

None. Python-side default ordering only. Capabilities and EngineSpecs are unchanged (exempt from SDPA Rule S2).
