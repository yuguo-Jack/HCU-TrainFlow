# dsa(top-k): measured SM103 auto ordering for the deterministic decode top-k

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/2151) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Problem

`compile_topk_kernel` resolves `deterministic_ordering="auto"` with the measured SM100 bitmap limits (`_sm100_deterministic_bitmap_max_cols`) only when the device is exactly cc 10.0. On cc 10.3 (B300) it falls through to the generic rule: bitmap up to 256K bucketed columns, whatever top_k is. On a B300 neither rule fits.

I measured a grid of 120 cells: fp32 / bf16, rows 1 / 64 / 512, 4K-256K columns, top_k 64-2048. Each cell was timed with a captured CUDA graph and an L2 flush per replay. Both orderings returned identical indices in every cell.

- **Narrow rows:** bitmap wins at every top_k. The SM100 rule would pick bitonic below top_k 256 and be up to 1.245x slower here.
- **From 128K bucketed columns:** bitonic wins at top_k <= 128, and with more than 148 rows (the kernel's large-occupancy flag) at top_k <= 512. In these cells the generic rule is up to 1.109x slower than the better ordering.

## Change

- On cc 10.3, `"auto"` resolves through `_sm103_deterministic_bitmap(bucketed_cols, top_k, large_occupancy)`: bitonic if `cols >= 131072` and `top_k <= 128`, or with large occupancy `top_k <= 512`; bitmap otherwise.
- Past the 256K bitmap limit, bitonic stays the only choice.
- cc 10.0, other parts, and explicit `"bitmap"` / `"bitonic"` requests are unchanged.
- The dsa.md note on the ordering choice gains one clause for SM103.

## Measurements (B300)

I fixed the rule on the grid before drawing a holdout, together with the criterion: on every holdout cell the rule's ordering is at most 1.03x develop's.

The holdout had 32 distinct cells, with off-grid columns 8K-240K, rows 2-1024, top_k 32-1024, fp32 and bf16, drawn with a fixed seed. The rule changes the ordering in 7 of them, all faster:

| dtype | rows | columns | top_k | bitmap (develop) µs | bitonic (this PR) µs | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| fp32 | 32 | 90000 | 32 | 30.5 | 28.6 | 0.937 |
| fp32 | 100 | 120000 | 96 | 39.5 | 38.1 | 0.965 |
| fp32 | 8 | 120000 | 96 | 38.1 | 36.6 | 0.961 |
| bf16 | 200 | 120000 | 192 | 130.3 | 124.5 | 0.956 |
| bf16 | 32 | 200000 | 96 | 120.2 | 114.4 | 0.952 |
| fp32 | 1024 | 240000 | 384 | 555.8 | 490.5 | 0.882 |
| bf16 | 8 | 240000 | 96 | 125.5 | 118.5 | 0.944 |

The other 25 cells keep develop's ordering, at 1.000. Over the 32 cells the geometric mean is 0.987.

Route check through `DSA.IndexerTopK(deterministic=True)` (default ordering), develop vs this branch, alternating, twice:

- four changed shapes: 0.883-0.961;
- three unchanged shapes: 0.989-1.001;
- indices identical between the two trees in all seven.

## Tests

- New `test_DSA_indexer_top_k_sm103_auto_ordering`. It checks that `"auto"` resolves to the SM103 rule under cc 10.3, at both band edges and past the bitmap limit, and that cc 10.0 still follows the SM100 rule. Device capability is monkeypatched and the resolution is intercepted before compilation, so no GPU is needed.
- `test/python/deepseek_sparse_attention/cutedsl/test_DSA_indexer_top_k.py` on B300: 148 passed, 9 skipped.
