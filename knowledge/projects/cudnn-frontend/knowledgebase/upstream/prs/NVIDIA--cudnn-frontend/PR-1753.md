## Affected area

FROST SDPA tests.

## Summary

The prefolded-scale probe counts packed FMA instructions across the entire kernel, but uses a zero count to check that the score shift remains a subtraction. The independent row-sum update, `total_sum_vec * alpha_pair + iter_sum_pair`, can also lower to packed FMA, causing a false failure even when scale folding works.

Compare FMA counts between the scaled and folded builds instead of requiring zero. Also require 64 packed subtractions per traced softmax body so the score-shift check remains explicit. Existing multiply-removal and exponent checks remain in place.
