## Affected area

## Summary

`fwd/placement.py`'s two paged bf16 d128 prefill leads, `_sm100_paged_gqa_prefill` and `_sm100_paged_full_square`, were measured on cc 10.0 and gated to exactly `(10, 0)`. On cc 10.3 the same graphs keep the backend's engine 10, although FROST is faster there. Both rules now read `SM100_PAGED_D128_PREFILL_CCS = ((10, 0), (10, 3))`. The boxes, page size, K layout and declaration conditions are unchanged.

The module docstring records the cc 10.3 study. In `test_sdpa_fwd_placement.py`, the two cc 10.3 assertions flip from TRAIL to LEAD, and new assertions check that exact G8 and group 2 still trail on cc 10.3.

## Why

Inside these boxes on cc 10.3, develop's default runs 1.05-2.13x the time the FROST plan takes.

## The study

`sm103_paged_d128_prefill_transfer_20261031`.

- Setup: B300 SXM6 (1100 W), cuDNN 9.28.0.38, develop `f510fe8f`, one idle GPU per shard.
- Harness: `benchmark/attention_inference/benchmark_single_attention.py --phase context --kv_layout paged --page_size 16 --causal`. This is HND pages (head-major in-page K) with a shuffled block table and THD Q/O. Each leg runs 30 iterations after 5 warmup, and the leg order rotates per case.
- Legs: backend (FROST deselected), develop's default (`cudnn_auto`), and the default with cc 10.3 admitted.
- Cases: 44, seeded (20261105) and frozen before timing, sha256 `5cfd26de…`. All are inside the two boxes, b 1-4:
  - FS: full squares, Q = KV with S 1024-8192 (on and off the 128 tile), GQA 4 / 8 / 16; 14 with an exact Q total, 4 with none.
  - G16: chunks with 128 < Q <= 4096 and KV 2048-32768; 12 exact, 4 undeclared.
  - G48L: the same chunks for GQA 4 / 8 with the Q total undeclared (the rule's loose-declaration arm); 10.
- An undeclared Q total means `max_total_seq_len_q` is left unset, as in FlashInfer's exact declarations. A small wrapper drops it from the harness's graph.
- Criterion, fixed before timing: per arm, new default / backend geomean <= 0.97, and no case with new default / develop default > 1.03. Nothing is refit.

| arm | cases | new default / backend, geomean (range) | new / develop default, max |
|---|---|---|---|
| FS | 18 | 0.512 (0.470-0.598) | 1.000 (0.47-0.60 where develop ran the backend) |
| G16 | 16 | 0.823 (0.597-0.949) | 1.008 |
| G48L | 10 | 0.896 (0.791-0.951) | 1.003 |

Every arm qualifies. In 4 of the 44 cases develop already ran FROST through the split rules; those cases are unchanged (1.000-1.008).

The full squares' margin comes mostly from the backend. The study's squares all had lengths off the 128 tile, and there engine 10 runs at about half its on-tile rate. An earlier probe on four on-tile squares (S 1024-8192) put FROST at 0.855-0.916 of the backend.

**Numerics.** For 12 of the cases plus 2 on-tile squares, the new default's O and the backend's O were compared with an fp32 reference built per request from the gathered pages. The cases covered all three arms, exact and undeclared declarations, and on- and off-tile lengths. FROST's max abs error equaled the backend's on every case (at most 1e-3 against |O| <= 0.36).
