# gemm(frost sm100): divide the MoE token-axis AVG by the routed rows in use

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/2179) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Problem

#2013 (B14) fixed this for the SM120 MoE kernels. The SM100 copies still divide an ungrouped AVG over the token axis by the routed capacity: `Token.shape[1]`, or `len(token_index)` under GATHER. Rows past the final `first_token_offset` are routed but unused. No group covers them, and ADD, MAX and the grouped AVG already leave them out.

With offsets `[0, 32, 32, 96, 160]` over a capacity of 192, the scalar and per-feature AVG on a B300 come out at 160/192 of the mean. All five routings in the SM120 test fail the same way when pointed at the SM100 compiler: plain, plain swap-AB, plain GATHER, block-scale and block-scale swap-AB.

## Change

- The shared `_emit_reduction_avg_inverse` already takes the used-row count as `token_extent` (#2013). `sm100/epilogue_codegen.py` now passes it, under the name `moe_used_rows`. Dense chains ignore `token_extent`.
- The four SM100 MoE templates bind `moe_used_rows` in the epilogue warps, after the PDL wait. Those are `sm100_moe_grouped_matmul_fwd`, `sm100_moe_grouped_block_scale_matmul_fwd` and their swap-AB variants.
  - It is `first_token_offset[G]`, clamped to the capacity (`M`, or `N` under swap-AB), with a floor of 1. An empty routing then flushes 0 instead of NaN, as on SM120.
- `test_moe_avg_divides_by_used_rows_sm120` becomes a helper that both architectures run.
  - SM120 keeps its 16x16x32 config.
  - The new `test_moe_avg_divides_by_used_rows_sm100` uses `CONFIG_sm100_128x128x128_128x128x32_cluster1x1_1ctamma`.

## Tests

On a B300 (cc 10.3):
- The SM120 test pointed at the SM100 compiler, on develop: 5 failed. The scalar AVG is off by a relative 0.1667 (160/192).
- With this change, `-k used_rows`: 5 passed (SM100), 5 skipped (SM120).
- `test_moe_grouped_matmul_fwd.py`, `test_moe_grouped_block_scale_matmul_fwd.py` and both `_swiglu` variants, `-m "L0 or L1"`: 1174 passed, 707 skipped, 0 failed.
- `pre-commit` on the touched files passes.
