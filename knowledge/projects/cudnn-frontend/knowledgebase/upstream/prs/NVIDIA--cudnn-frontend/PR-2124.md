## Affected area

## Summary

On cc 10.3, `fwd/placement`'s chunked-prefill rule keeps a dense chunk (s_q < s_kv) on the backend in two cases: past `CHUNKED_MAX_Q_TILES` = 128 query tiles, or below a 4k cache (`CHUNKED_MIN_KV_TOKENS`). The B200 measurement behind those bounds was "256 tiles lose 1.02-1.07 for d64/d128". On a B300 the FROST forward is faster on every dense bottom-right causal GQA chunk measured, by up to 13x. For example, b 4, s_q 128, S_kv 2048, GQA 64/8 runs 0.26x the backend's time, and develop's default takes the backend there.

`placement_table.TABLE` gains two cc 10.3 entries for `sdpa_fwd_prefill_sm100`, bf16, (128, 128), prefill, dense, bottom-right causal, GQA: on the 128-key tile and its off-tile twin. Both are `always`, emitted by `benchmark/placement_gen/gen.py --emit`. MHA, f16, top-left graphs and cc 10.0 get no entry.

`test_cc103_d128_forward_dense_chunk_gqa_entries_are_its_own` covers:

- a chunk past both hand-rule bounds;
- the off-tile twin;
- cc 10.0, MHA, f16 and top-left chunks, which have no entry.

## Why

On the twelve GQA holdout cases, develop's default has a regret of 1.49 geomean (up to 4.6x). With the entries it is 1.000.

## The study

`sm103_d128_fwd_chunk_20261031`. B300 SXM6 (1100 W), develop `9226a665`, backend leg on cuDNN 9.28.0.38.

The harness was `benchmark/attention_inference/benchmark_single_attention.py --phase context --causal`: dense K/V and bottom-right chunks. Three legs ran per case, `cudnn` (backend), `cudnn_oss` (FROST pinned) and `cudnn_auto` (default), each timing 30 iterations after 5 warmup, with the leg order rotated per case. Each GPU sat idle for 120 s before the run.

- Train: 112 cases, sha256 `0ca8459d…`.
  - Heads: GQA 32/8, 16/2, 64/8 and MHA 32/32.
  - s_q 128 / 512 / 2048 over S_kv 2048 / 8192 / 32768 (on the tile) and 3000 / 10000 (off it).
  - b 1 / 4, only s_q < s_kv.
- Holdout: 24 fresh, seeded, off-lattice cases, sha256 `e6f3ddb1…`, seed 20261108. Six per class.
  - Heads: GQA 24/3, 40/5, 48/6 and MHA 16/16, 20/20.
  - s_q 64-3000; S_kv 4096-24576 on the tile, 2500-20000 off it; b 1-8.
  - Used once.

| class | train: FROST / backend | holdout: FROST / backend | holdout: generated / develop default |
|---|---|---|---|
| GQA, on the tile (48 + 6) | 0.076-0.994 | 0.217-0.936 | 0.217-0.937 |
| GQA, off the tile (36 + 6) | 0.105-0.986 | 0.220-0.942 | 0.220-1.004 |

Every GQA holdout case picks the faster leg.

**Classes left out.** On the tile, MHA fit `always` but lost one holdout case: b 3, s_q 768, S_kv 16384, MHA 16/16 ran 1.78x the default. Off the tile, MHA fit a cost threshold. Its on-tile twin has no entry, so neither MHA class gets one. The two GQA classes were selected after the holdout was scored. `gen.py` fits each class on its own data.

**Supplementary qualification of the whole class.** The entries answer for every dense bottom-right causal GQA prefill graph, not just the chunks timed above. A second frozen set ran on the same part (16 cases, sha256 in the study record, seed 20261111) with the three legs on develop. The default leg was then re-timed with this PR's table.

| cases | develop default | this PR's default / develop's |
|---|---|---|
| 5 short chunks, s_q 5-64 | FROST (the decode-shaped and chunk rules) | 1.000 (same route) |
| 2 short chunks, s_q 100 / 120 over S_kv 3000 / 16384 | backend | 0.21 |
| 5 bottom-right squares, S 256-9000 | backend | 0.55-0.95 |
| 4 cases with s_q > s_kv | not buildable (frontend validation rejects bottom-right with s_q > s_kv) | — |
