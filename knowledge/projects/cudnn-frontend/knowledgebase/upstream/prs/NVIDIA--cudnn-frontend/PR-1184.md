## Affected area

FE OSS kernels or CuTeDSL: `python/cudnn/flex_attention/{dispatch,execution}.py`, `test/python/fe_api/flex_attention/**`.

## Summary

**Stacked on #1181** (the Rule 8 core PR: the `compile_allocates_nothing` fixture). **For the flex_attention owners to review**: no API change; `compile()` no longer touches device memory.

Rule 8 (#1167), recipe R11: `FlexAttentionFwd/Bwd.compile()` builds its kernels from **fake (meta) tensors** carrying the declared dtype / shape / stride instead of allocating sample device tensors. Before: 8 allocations and a 97 MiB transient for the backward at compile; after: 0 allocations (`compile_allocates_nothing` detector on both classes). Execute is unchanged.

## Why

Rule 8: `compile()` must not own or allocate device memory; a compile inside a warm-up region on a memory-tight device failed for the backward's 97 MiB transient, and the allocation's GC-timed free is the #1151 class of capture invalidation when compile happens inside a captured region.

## Related issues
