## Affected area

FE OSS CuTeDSL KDA kernels: local H/M state generation used by Kimi context parallelism, including matching forward and backward recomputation.

## Summary / Why

Keep the BF16 KDA projection accumulator in FP32 through residual subtraction and beta scaling, then round only the resulting Y operand:

```
P_H = K_decay @ H;  Y_H = BF16(beta_fp32 * (V_fp32 - P_H_fp32))
P_M = K_decay @ M;  Y_M = BF16(beta_fp32 * (0      - P_M_fp32))
```

Previously the projection was first rounded to BF16, followed by packed BF16 subtraction/scaling. In cancellation-sensitive cases this can erase the entire residual. The analytic regression uses P = 1 + 1/256, V = 1, beta = 1/2: the old path returns zero instead of the exactly representable correction -1/512.

A register-only helper applies the rule in normal prefill, H/M summary, checkpoint recompute and backward Y regeneration. Backward dBeta retains the unrounded BF16-path residual. Prep-fed prefill only changes subtraction: beta is already folded into the prep records and is **not applied twice**. FP16 retains its existing projection/beta rounding and packed arithmetic.

## Relation to FLA precision staging

| Stage | FLA organization | cuDNN before this fix | cuDNN after this fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| H residual | FP32 accumulator from W @ H; subtract it from u in FP32 before narrowing the residual for the next MMA. | Round the projection to BF16 before residual subtraction and beta scaling. | Keep the projection, residual and beta scaling FP32 until Y packing. |
| M feedback | Build the complete chunk transition A = D - K_restore^T @ W in FP32, then apply A @ M using FP32-typed operands; no matching standalone projection-to-BF16 stage before beta scaling. | Round K_decay @ M to BF16, then form beta * (0 - projection) with packed BF16 arithmetic. | Form the negative-feedback residual/scaling in FP32 before the final Y cast. |

Source comparison: [FLA H projection/subtraction](https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention/blob/0d0a2f9a5bac74b34b5d2fb4e7192fe85374ea90/fla/ops/cp/chunk_delta_h.py#L124-L147), [residual narrowing](https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention/blob/0d0a2f9a5bac74b34b5d2fb4e7192fe85374ea90/fla/ops/cp/chunk_delta_h.py#L185-L215), [FLA M recurrence](https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention/blob/0d0a2f9a5bac74b34b5d2fb4e7192fe85374ea90/fla/ops/cp/chunk_delta_h.py#L269-L298).

These are different algebraic organizations: FLA folds beta and the chunk inverse into W/u, which themselves use low-precision operands/storage. FP32-typed dot operands do not alone establish IEEE FP32 or TF32x3 multiplication. This fix removes the identified early rounding points; it does not claim complete FLA precision parity or change cross-rank merge precision.
