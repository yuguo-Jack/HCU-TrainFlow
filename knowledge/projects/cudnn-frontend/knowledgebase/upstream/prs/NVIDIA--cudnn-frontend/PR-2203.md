# test(sdpa): qualify per-tensor FP8 attention sinks at S_q == 1 on SM100/SM103 and SM120

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/2203) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Problem

The SDPA support tracker kept the sink-at-decode (`S_q == 1`) status of the SM100/SM103 and SM120 per-tensor FP8 rows at ❔.

- `SDPA_FP8` graphs never went through the f16/bf16 rule that validated the half rows.
- No FP8 test drew a dense `S_q == 1` graph with a sink.
- The SM100 paged twin already runs: the `s_q == 1` row of `test_paged_fp8_sink_split` in `test_sdpa_fp8_sink_split.py`. On SM120 only the exact-D128 split was drawn (#2184).

## Change

- `test_sdpa_fp8_sink_split.py::test_dense_fp8_sink_at_decode` draws dense per-tensor FP8 graphs with an attention sink at `S_q == 1`.
  - Unsplit and split-4.
  - D64, D128, D192×128 and D256; E4M3/E5M2 inputs; bf16/E4M3 outputs.
  - It checks O, Stats and Amax_O against the file's existing reference, sink mass included, and asserts that the prepared plan carries the sink and the requested split.
- `test_sdpa_fp8_sink_split_sm120.py::test_sm120_fp8_sink_at_decode`: the SM120 equivalent, unsplit, at D64/D128/D256/D512 (the d ≤ 256 and d512 FP8 templates), E4M3/E5M2 inputs, bf16/E4M3 outputs, against the shared reference.
- `SUPPORT_MATRIX_TRACKER.md`: the SM100/SM103 per-tensor FP8 row is validated on cc 10.3 (dense and paged), and the SM120 per-tensor FP8 row on cc 12.0 (dense).
  - The SM107 FP8 and SM100/SM107/SM120 MXFP8 rows keep their ❔.
  - Neither a cc 10.0 nor a cc 12.1 run is part of this qualification.

No kernel or lowering change.

## Tests

On a B300 (cc 10.3), develop d760fefe:
- New test: 32 passed.
- `test_sdpa_fp8_sink_split.py`, `-m "L0 or L1"`: 114 passed.
On an RTX 5090 (cc 12.0), develop d760fefe:
- New SM120 test: 16 passed.
- `test_sdpa_fp8_sink_split_sm120.py`, `-m "L0 or L1"`: 41 passed.

`pre-commit` on the touched files passes.
