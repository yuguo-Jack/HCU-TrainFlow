# docs(sdpa): the measured per-form cost of the fused store's staged rounding convention

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/2077) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## What

Docs / tracker only, no code change: records the measured per-form cost of the fused gated e4m3 store's `staged` rounding convention (#2066, #2071) in the FROST SDPA support-matrix tracker, and points the sparse d256 adapter's `out_rounding` docstring at the note, so the convention is not switched on where it costs. No admission widens or narrows; the bytes of both conventions are unchanged. A per-form performance fact, not a numerics change.

## The two readings

Rubin 212-SM class (cc 10.7, the locked clock, an exclusive GPU); a decode-chain harness (a serving stack's prep -> the lean tile -> the fused combine / sigmoid gate / e4m3 store) with a `staged` twin arm declared beside the `single` arm in the same process, 3 cells x 2 captured twins x AB/BA = 12 pooled cold-L2 windows per arm, us per layer.

- **The SERVED combine form** of the lean decode route on cc 10.7 (form 0, the decode-grid combine): staged vs single is a tie inside the noise at 1x1 p16 for kv 256 / 1024 / 2048 (margins -0.16 / -0.21 / -0.06 us inside 0.18-0.31 us of noise) and costs 0.13-0.30 us per layer beyond the noise at 1x4 p32 (-0.9 .. -2.0 %) = under 0.15 % of a decode step.
- **Forcing the module's own combine forms** (`DECODE_GRID_OFF`, form 2; not the served form on cc 10.7): staged costs 1.18-1.47 us per layer at 1x1 (-6.7 .. -8.5 %) and 0.26-0.70 us at 1x4 (-1.0 .. -2.5 %), beyond the noise in every cell. The pre-merge review of #2066 predicted this from the staged rendering's resource usage on sm_100a (the plain `_combine_kernel`: REG 112 -> 128, a 16-byte stack frame, two spills, eight extra subroutine calls = the `div.rn.f32` slow paths of the sigmoid divisions). The bitwise contract with the serving stack's kernel pins `div.rn` + two `cvt.rn.bf16` per element, so only scheduling / live-range changes could remove the cost.
- **End to end** on the same class, under a serving stack's `staged` default no bucket was slower than under `single` (plain 1x1 +0.09 %, speculative verify 1x4 +0.06 %, ties), while the acceptance-length gap of the speculative verify bucket closed only by a third (2.857 -> 2.884 vs 2.930): the remainder is the attention core's accumulation order, not the store.

## Guidance (now in the tracker and the docstring)

Prefer `single` on declarations whose combine resolves to the module's own forms until the staged arithmetic is rescheduled; the served decode-grid form carries no measurable cost at 1x1 (the lean group walk was not in the timed cells; by its resource lines it adds no register, frame or spill under staged).
