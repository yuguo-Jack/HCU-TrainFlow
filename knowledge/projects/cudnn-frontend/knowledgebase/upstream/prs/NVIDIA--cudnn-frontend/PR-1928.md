[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1928) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

The D256 fused correction/P1 change in #1922 intentionally changed the two dense renderings of the shared SM100 template. Their recorded cubin hashes were left stale, so the rendering regression was the sole failure in the SM100 and SM103 runs for #1925.

Refresh those two hashes under CuTeDSL 4.8.0 and explain that the SM107 rendering here compiles the shared template directly. All eight THD/paged/gated hashes remain unchanged. No kernel or admission change.

Validation: reproduced the exact two-hash failure, then reran all ten SM100/SM107 trace compilations successfully after the update. Scoped pre-commit checks passed. The affected CI runs otherwise passed 18,250 tests on SM100 and 17,974 on SM103.
