## Affected area

CI or test infrastructure

## Summary

`test_stage2_2x2_sass_pins` expects the compute warpgroup to have `1` kv body for `dense` and `3` for any masked arm. The `causal` arm (parametrized only for `sm_107a`) has two. The pin now counts `dense: 1, causal: 2, causal_swa: 3`. The kernel is unchanged.

## Why

`bprop_d512_f16_2x2.py` traces the compute kv body once per range: low edge `[left, unmasked_lo)`, interior, high edge. `compute_kv_loop_bounds` sets `unmasked_lo = left` unless SWA moves it. Without SWA the low-edge loop is therefore `[left, left)`, and the compiler removes it. The SASS then has two compute kv bodies, so 4 `tcgen05.ld` (LDTM), where the pin expected 6.

`[sm_107a-causal]` fails the same way on every `cutlass-4.8` frost lane since the test landed (the 4.7 lanes skip `sm_107a`):
- develop schedule 72364083, job 475608986, `cutlass-4.8:sm100`;
- develop schedule 72424864, job 476055126, `cutlass-4.8:sm100`.

Both report `{'LDTM': 4, 'UTCHMMA': 64, 'UTMALDG': 32, ..., 'LINES': 2911}`.

None filed; this PR is the fix.

## Testing

B200, stable `nvidia-cutlass-dsl` 4.8.0 (cu13 libs), CUDA 13.4 `nvdisasm`, head = `gh/develop` 3b34edf3d.

| Build | LDTM | UTCHMMA | SASS lines |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sm_100a` causal | 4 | 64 | 2818 |
| `sm_107a` causal | 4 | 64 | 2911 |
| `sm_100a` causal_swa | 6 | 64 | 3288 |
| `sm_107a` causal_swa | 6 | 64 | 3389 |

The `sm_107a` causal row matches CI exactly, LINES included. `sm_100a` causal is not a parametrization of the test; it was compiled by a throwaway probe to confirm the count does not depend on the arch.

- After: 4 passed.

Not run: the DSL 4.8.0a0 nightly CI uses (the stable 4.8.0 reproduces the CI counts exactly), and the full frost suite.
