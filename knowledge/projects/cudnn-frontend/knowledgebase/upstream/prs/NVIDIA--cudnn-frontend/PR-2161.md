# norm: launch the remaining LN / RMS routes through tvm-ffi

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/2161) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Problem

#2141 moved the warp LayerNorm / RMSNorm forward, and #2146 the CGA GroupNorm / InstanceNorm routes, to a tvm-ffi launch. The remaining LN / RMS routes still wrap every operand with `dyn()` (`from_dlpack(...).mark_layout_dynamic()`) on each call:

- the backward routes: pipelined, tiled, plain (with and without the slot partials from #2139) and CGA;
- the plain forward and the CGA forward (long rows).

On a B300, that export is most of a warm `norm_bprop` call on these routes.

## Change

- Each of those routes compiles once with `--enable-tvm-ffi`. The fake operands are flat, with a `cute.sym_int()` row count (and partial-buffer length), built with `fake_rows()`.
- Each call passes the torch tensors directly through `ffi()`, which detaches them when they are on an autograd tape. This is the same pattern as the warp forward and the CGA GroupNorm routes.
- Compile keys and kernels are unchanged. The pipelined and tiled keys still contain the R-derived `nchunk` / `CHUNK`; that existing limitation is not changed here.
- The two ffi launch tests are renamed to `test_norm_bwd_ffi_launch.py` (new) and `test_norm_fwd_ffi_launch.py` (from #2141). The norm CI selection uses `-k "not frost"`, so the forward test merged with #2141 under its `test_frost_...` name has never been selected there.

## Measurements (B300, cc 10.3)

`cudnn.norm` torch API, bf16, 12 shapes: LN 4096x4096, 2048x1536, 4096x96, 600x2000, 17x1000, 3x2880, 64x32768, 32x131072, and RMS 8192x5120, 4096x1000, 256x65536, 100000x64. Develop and this branch ran in separate processes, alternating, twice. Warm enqueue per call:

| | develop rep 1 | develop rep 2 | this branch rep 1 | this branch rep 2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `norm_bprop`, all 12 shapes | 97-135 µs | 188-251 µs | 40-94 µs | 57-100 µs |
| `norm_fprop`, the 7 shapes on the plain / CGA forward | 69-91 µs | 134-172 µs | 39-55 µs | 39-55 µs |

The host was shared, which is why develop's numbers doubled between its two reps. The warp-forward shapes are unchanged by this PR.

## Correctness

- Over both reps, y, mean, rstd and dx are bitwise identical to develop on all 12 shapes.
- dgamma / dbeta differ (at most 1.1e-6 relative) only on shapes where develop itself gives a different dgamma / dbeta on two identical calls (the atomic reductions). On the three CGA shapes they are bitwise identical.
- **Tests:** the two ffi launch files give 8 passed. The full `test/python/norm` with this change gives 1368 passed, 1 skipped.
