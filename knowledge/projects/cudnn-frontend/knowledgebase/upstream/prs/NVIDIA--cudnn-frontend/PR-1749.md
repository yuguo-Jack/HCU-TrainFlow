# frost(gated_attention_block): the in-block QSA indexer -- qsa_compress + the scorer / top-k select stage behind QsaSpec(index_source="indexer"), and the e4m3 indexer cache (QsaSpec.index_cache_dtype)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1749) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Summary

The gated attention block runs the Qwen 3.8 Flash-Next **indexer inside the block**: a `QsaSpec` geometry declared with
`index_source="indexer"` (bf16 activations, `index_band=True`, dense `[B, S]` prompts) derives the per-query block selection
from the slab's own fifth band instead of taking it as `execute(block_ids=)`.  A new stage `(4i)` between the norm + RoPE and the
index-list sparse core -- five launches at `T <= 4096`, no kernel of its own beyond the compress:

1. the band's indexer **queries** -- RMSNormed with `w_iq_norm` and partially rotated at the token's position -- through the new
   block-compress kernel at pool 1 (bitwise the block's own norm + RoPE chain on those rows: one fp32 pass, one rounding);
2. the **compressed keys** -- the band's raw key head mean-pooled in fp32 per complete 4-token block, RMSNormed with `w_ik_norm`
   and rotated at the block START position -- the same kernel at pool 4 over the slab's raw-key slice (token stride `n_qkvg`, no
   copy), into the workspace and, on request, into the caller's `index_k_compressed` cache;
3. the **selection** -- `sum_h relu(q_h . kbar_j) / sqrt(index_head_dim)` over the blocks below `floor((pos + 1) / 4)` plus the
   fused radix top-k (ties to the smaller id) -- `cudnn.gated_attention_block.qsa_select`, the DSA compact-logits scorer: the dense
   `[T, n_blocks]` score tensor is never materialized;
4. the **canonical order** -- each row sorted (ids descending, `-1` last) in windows of 4096 rows into preallocated workspace, so
   two executes are bitwise and the list a caller reads back is canonical.

The list carries exactly the derived count `min(top_k, floor((pos + 1) / 4))` of valid ids then `-1`, so stage (4) takes it without
`block_lens`.  At `S <= QsaSpec.identity_bound` the block launches **no scorer**: the selection is a plan-time identity list (the key
compress runs only into a requested cache).  The RoPE tables are the attention's own `cos` / `sin` (block `j` reads row `4j`); no
appended table, no position ids.

* **`kernels/qsa_compress.py` (new)**: `frost_qsa_compress` -- mean-pool `POOL` (4) raw indexer keys, RMSNorm over 128, partial
  RoPE on 64 dims at the block start, ONE 16-bit rounding at the store (the single-rounding contract of `qk_norm_rope.py`, whose
  per-row body this is with a 4-row mean prepended; the norm weight as `(1 + w)` or as the checkpoint stores it).  Plain
  vectorized LDG / STG plus warp shuffles -- no mbarrier, no SMEM, no TMA, no tcgen05, so both pre-development tables are empty by
  construction and the kernel runs on any CuTe DSL device.  Its **decode form** `frost_qsa_compress_step` compresses the blocks a
  step's committed rows complete from a position-indexed raw-key ring (`RING >= S_q + POOL - 1`, enforced on the host): bitwise
  the prefill compress of the same tokens; a rejected draft leaves no trace in the ring or the cache; `compressed_slot` reports
  the block id written, `-1` (no block completed) or `-2` (a cache or table too short -- nothing written).
* **`qsa_select.py` (new)**: `qsa_select` (prefill, THD-packed queries; `block_ids` local to the token's sequence, `-1` padded,
  `deterministic=True` reproducible sets) and `qsa_select_decode` (one to a few rows per sequence: the dense scorer at the
  smallest MMA tile plus the radix top-k with one visible-block count per row; the shared step-0 list of the speculative-verify
  contract).  `indexer_scorer_head_groups()` reads the scorer's packed head groups (4, 8, 16, 32, 64) off the DSA tree.  Both
  gate the CuTe DSL floor BEFORE importing that tree (its package imports its kernel modules), so a sub-floor DSL reads as the
  typed version decline (Rule 7).
* **`frost/tile_dsl/pointwise.py`**: `rmsnorm_rstd` and `rope_rotate_half`, the two lane-group primitives the compress kernel shares
  with the block's norm kernels (spelled through the helpers rather than inline).
* **The fp8 indexer cache** -- `QsaSpec(index_source="indexer", index_cache_dtype=torch.float8_e4m3fn)` (appended): the compressed
  keys and the indexer queries as plain e4m3 of the RMSNormed + rotated values (no scales; the caller's `index_k_compressed` is then
  an e4m3 buffer), scored through `qsa_select`'s e4m3 arm (the DSA MXFP8 scorer with unit scale blobs).  No new kernel: the compress
  launches keep writing bf16 and the FP8 pipeline's per-tensor cast (`sat_e4m3(row x 1.0)`, round-to-nearest-even, never a NaN)
  writes the e4m3 operands into two workspace slots appended after the indexer's seven.  A **declaration attribute, never a
  knob**: the selection is a different function of the inputs (3 mantissa bits move the weakest selected blocks at the k-th boundary --
  on synthetic activations the e4m3 selection agrees with the bf16 one on 99.0 % of the blocks at S = 4096, 97.4 % at 32768 and
  96.5 % at 131072, every flip a rank-boundary flip; bf16 vs fp32 agrees at 99.8 %); bf16 stays the default and the reference.
* **API surface (append-only)**: `QsaSpec.index_cache_dtype` + the property `index_cache_e4m3` (`QsaSpec.validate()` names e5m2 and
  refuses the attribute -- any value -- under `index_source="caller"`, where nothing of the block reads or writes an indexer cache);
  `GatedAttentionBlockFwd.execute` gains `w_iq_norm` / `w_ik_norm` (REQUIRED under the indexer) and the optional `index_k_compressed` /
  `block_ids_out` / `block_lens_out`, appended after `v_scale` -- the END of the signature, so every positional caller of the earlier
  parameters is unchanged (the strict tail pin names the last ten) -- every one REFUSED on a block declared without the indexer,
  `block_ids` / `block_lens` REFUSED under it (form checks only, no value read, no sync); `qsa_select` gains `cu_seqlens_q_scale_padded`
  (appended; the e4m3 arm's padded scale prefix, refused on the bf16 arm); `_plan_workspace` gains `qsa_indexer_cand_floats` and
  `qsa_indexer_e4m3` (appended after `decode_q_lens`): the indexer's seven slots (`ix_q .. ix_sort_idx`) and the cache's two
  (`ix_q8` / `ix_kbar8`) at the END of the layout, every layout without them byte-identical to before (pinned).
* **Stream, allocation and gate contract of the stage** (`python/cudnn/AGENTS.md` Rules 1 / 5 / 7 / 8 and recipes R1 / R9): the row
  sort runs on the LAUNCH stream as a torch stream through `as_torch_stream` (a default-stream handle maps to torch's default stream,
  never to an `ExternalStream` of it); the plan constants (the query prefix, the scorer's scratch offsets, the e4m3 scale prefix, the
  counts, the identity list, the casts' scale word) are built on the host at `compile()`, which only allocates their device tensors,
  and the FIRST execute uploads their values on ITS launch stream behind the event `compile()` records (one async copy each from a
  pinned host copy), so no execute reads a constant written on another stream and `compile()` no longer synchronizes the device;
  `compile()` warms the scorer and the top-k at the real `(B, S, n_blocks)`, so `execute` compiles nothing, never reads back and --
  on both cache dtypes, the e4m3 scale prefix being a plan constant -- allocates nothing of its own; the ONE allocator transient on
  the path is torch's: `torch.sort` builds its 4 KB index template (`arange(top_k)` int64) per 4096-row window even into preallocated
  outputs (served from the caching allocator's pool; inside a capture, the graph's) -- pinned as the path's only allocation, with the
  in-kernel row sort that would remove it listed below.  A CUDA graph that captures a block's FIRST execute records the constant
  uploads with their values (a replay re-writes the same constants); every capture in the suite warms the block up on the capture
  stream first, the tree's convention.
* **Typed declines, each naming its feature**: f16 activations, an `index_head_dim` other than the scorer's 128, an `index_heads`
  outside the scorer's packed groups, `thd=True` (the scorer reads dense prompts), `kv_source="paged"` (the indexer's decode step
  over the pools is a follow-up; the decode mode consumes one caller list per sequence), the CuTe DSL floor at declaration (the
  head groups are read off the DSA tree, whose package imports its kernels) and again at `check_support`, a device outside the cc
  10.x family; at execute an `index_k_compressed` of the other dtype (the message names the declared one).
* **Docs and the tracker**: `docs/fe-oss-apis/gated_attention_block.md` gains "The in-block indexer" (the contract table, the identity
  below the bound, padding rows under `seq_lens`, the fp8 indexer cache, the costs, the stream and allocation contract, what the suite
  asserts); `overview.md` and `llms.txt` name the block's sparse and serving forms; `SUPPORT_MATRIX_TRACKER.md`'s index-list row

  pools and THD with the indexer as the open arms.  No `Capabilities` field and no `EngineSpec` moves.

No barrier, ring or SMEM change anywhere: the compress kernel has none, the scorer and the top-k are the DSA kernels with their own
suites, and the sparse core is untouched.

## Open items

* The indexer's **decode step over the pools** through the block (the step-0 selection over the compressed-key cache and the raw-key
  ring): `frost_qsa_compress_step` and `qsa_select_decode` are the standalone forms; the block's decode mode keeps consuming one
  caller list per sequence until it binds them.
* `thd=True` together with the in-block indexer (the scorer over a query prefix, one compressed-key row range per sequence).
* Below the identity bound the block knows the selection is the identity; routing that case to the dense SDPA would be a legal
  performance knob and stays a follow-up.
* The compress kernel's e4m3 store arm would fold the fp8 cache's casts away (a lever, not built); the band geometry's projection has no
  forced tile, so no capture-safe route until the band is padded to 256 columns.
* The canonical row order runs through `torch.sort` into preallocated workspace: five launches per 4096-row window (two DtoD copies, the
  index template's fill, its broadcast, the in-place radix sort) and torch's one 4 KB index-template transient per window.  A block-local
  in-kernel row sort (one launch per window, no index scratch -- the `ix_sort_idx` slot, `min(T, 4096) x top_k` int64, goes with it) is
  the lever; the row order itself (ids descending, `-1` last) is the contract either way.
