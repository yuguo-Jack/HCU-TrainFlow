## Affected area

FE OSS kernels or CuTeDSL (SM120 SDPA backward).

## Summary

Add native FP16/BF16 exact-D128 MHA THD backward to `sdpa_bwd_sm120` (engine 20600). Independent Q/K/V/O/dO/dQ/dK/dV and Stats origins remain device operands. Packed TH1 and padded-head HN Stats layouts retain their declared pitches.

## Why

SM120 has no FROST packed backward route, while the backend declines packed/ragged LSE on SM12X. Compile the existing row-predicated `cp.async` / `mma.sync` compute for SM120 with one Q/dO stage and direct FP32 dQ accumulation to fit its shared-memory budget. Reuse the registered native prepared ABI, including gradient-overlap guards and caller-owned metadata/scratch. Execution adds no normalization, tensor plumbing, allocation, host read or JIT.

## Related issues

Related to #381.

## API and compatibility impact

No graph preparation/execution API or placement change. Resource and capability fields are append-only and preserve SM80 defaults. Scope is cc12.0, exact-D128 MHA, fixed 64x64 tiles, positive finite FP32 scales, and unmasked/top-left causal THD. Declarations bound packed capacities and the flattened `B * max_SQ` Int32 launch count. Other head widths, GQA, deterministic, bottom-right/windowed, bias/sink, quantized THD and cc12.1 retain typed declines. Dense SM120 support is unchanged. No TMA optimization or performance claim.
