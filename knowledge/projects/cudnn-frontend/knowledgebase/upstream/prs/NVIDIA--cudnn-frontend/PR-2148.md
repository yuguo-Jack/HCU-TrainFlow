# sdpa fwd placement (sm80): refit the D192 / D256 dense prefill entries after the short-query tiles

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/2148) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## What

The SM80 short-query tiles (#2128, #2130, #2132) made FROST faster on dense prefill: D128 / D256 at S_q ≤ 128 and D192/V128 at S_q ≤ 64. The A100 placement entries from round 3 were fitted before those tiles, so they still trail FROST where it now wins. This PR refits them.

## How

- **Re-timing:** every round-3 training cell in the changed domain was re-timed at develop `5f42a0bc7`: 575 cells, both legs, on A100 80GB PCIe (8 nodes, no co-tenants) with cuDNN 9.29.0.18. Consumed holdout 3 joins training outside that domain.
- **Fit:** `benchmark/placement_gen/gen.py`, unchanged.
- **Acceptance rule:** a refit entry replaces its parent only if no training cell runs more than 5 % slower than under the parent (a sacrificed win is no change; a led loss is a regression).
  - **Adopted (10):**
    - D192/V128 MHA, causal and mask-free, on and off the tile: `always`.
    - D256 GQA, causal and mask-free: `ge` on tiles.
    - D256 mask-free MHA (2): dropped after holdout 3; its failing cell sat in the re-timed domain.
  - **Kept (6):**
    - The four D192/V128 GQA classes. FROST wins everywhere except two cells at S_q 192 (b 1, 64/8: 1.19x and 1.25x). The single-threshold refit would give up the wins above S_q 256 to take the new ones below.
    - Two D64 thresholds that moved without changing any training decision.

## Evidence

- **Training, vs develop:** the adopted entries win 4-50 cells per class (up to 2.38x), with no cell more than 5 % slower.
- **Holdout 4:** 108 fresh cells frozen (sha256) before timing, crossing q stratum × KV stratum × on/off tile × d × mask once each. 13 decisions change; all 13 are faster than develop (geomean 0.63x over the changed cells, best 1.9x) and none is slower.
- **Tests:** host placement/heuristics suites pass (1811 passed).
