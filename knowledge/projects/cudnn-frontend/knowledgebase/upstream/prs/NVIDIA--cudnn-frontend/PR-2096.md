## Affected area

FE OSS kernels or CuTeDSL

## Summary

cc 10.3 (B300 / GB300) had no placement-table entries, so every SM100-row graph there fell back to the hand rules, and the gb300 nightly showed the same default-dispatch losses as gb200 before #1957 / #2094. This PR lets cc 10.3 borrow the cc 10.0 entries, **study by study, only where a transfer check on a B300 qualified them**:

- `placement_table.PART_TRANSFERS[(10, 3)] = ((10, 0), {six studies})`. `lookup` falls back to the cc 10.0 entry of the same class only when that entry's study is listed.
- A later cc 10.0 study does **not** answer for cc 10.3 until a transfer check names it.
- An unmeasured part (cc 10.1 in the tests) keeps the hand rules.

## Why

### Method (study `sm103_transfer_20261031`)

B300 SXM6, 1100 W, cuDNN 9.29.0.17 (the nightly build). Cases were frozen (sha256) before timing.
- **Transfer sample (201 cases):** a cost-stratified sample of each listed study's frozen cc 10.0 grid, on and off the 128-key tile. It covers:
  - d256 decode, dense and paged;
  - paged D576 q1 / MTP;
  - paged d512 decode;
  - dense D576 decode;
  - paged d512 / d256 context, including the short-KV cells.
- **Holdout (56 cases):** off the training lattice (odd head counts and batches, off-tile lengths), scored once.
- **Scoring:** the full runtime placement (table plus hand rules) at cc 10.3, against develop's placement on the same graphs. Regret = chosen ÷ faster provider.
