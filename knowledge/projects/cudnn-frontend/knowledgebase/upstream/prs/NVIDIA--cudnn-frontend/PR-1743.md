# test(sdpa): cover 16k backward with bounded draws and stable references

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1743) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Problem and change

The five randomized backward suites stop at 8192 tokens although their references already walk KV blocks. Raising the limits alone introduces 20 OOMs in the 512-case f16 dense suite on GH200: deterministic backward workspace and dBias still scale with `b*h_q*s_q*s_kv`.

Raise both sequence caps to 16384 in the f16 dense/THD, fp8 dense/THD and mxfp8 dense suites. Before drawing per-batch lengths, reduce the batch of an oversized draw to keep score cells within the previous suite maximum (`16*8*8192^2` for f16, `4*8*8192^2` for fp8/mxfp8). Sequence lengths, seeds and comparison tolerances remain unchanged; model-specific suite limits remain unchanged.

The wider sweep also exposed a backward-reference rounding problem that occurs under the old 8k limits. Combining a large row maximum with `log(normalizer)` in FP32 loses low bits; reconstructing `P=exp(S-LSE)` can then move dS across a half-precision rounding boundary and amplify the dK difference. Keep the row maximum and shifted normalizer separate, computing `P=exp(S-max)/normalizer`, including the sink probability. Twelve regression cases check equal logits beside half rounding boundaries and exact sink probabilities.

Related to #965. The Blackwell validation requested there remains outstanding; this contribution records Hopper coverage without treating architecture skips as passes.
