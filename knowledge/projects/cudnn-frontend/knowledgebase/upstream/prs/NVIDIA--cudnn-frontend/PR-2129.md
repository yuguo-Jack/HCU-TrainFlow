## Affected area

FE OSS kernels or CuTeDSL

## Summary

`fwd/placement._place_sm100_fp8` keeps a per-tensor FP8 d192x128 chunk (s_q < s_kv) on the backend past `FP8_D192_MAX_Q_TILES` = 64 query tiles. The B200 measurement behind that bound was "128 tiles lose 1.19-1.72". On a B300, causal chunks of at least 512 query tokens run 1-16 % faster on FROST at every launch size measured. These are the long prefill chunks of a d192 FP8 MLA-style model.

On cc 10.3, causal d192 chunks with `s_q >= SM103_FP8_D192_LONG_CHUNK_MIN_S_Q` (512) now lead at any tile count. Shorter chunks, mask-free chunks and cc 10.0 keep the tile bound unchanged.

`test_sm103_fp8_d192_long_causal_chunks_lead` covers:

- a long chunk and one at the bound;
- a shorter chunk past 64 tiles, a mask-free chunk and cc 10.0, which still trail;
- a 16-tile chunk, which still leads under the B200 bound.

## Why

Develop's default runs the backend on these graphs. The default leg with this change ran 0.846-0.988x of develop's time.

## Measurements

B300 SXM6 (1100 W), cuDNN 9.28.0.38, develop `9226a665`, per-tensor E4M3 Q/K/V/O. The harness was `benchmark/attention_inference/benchmark_single_attention.py --phase context --kv_cache_dtype fp8_e4m3 --head_dim_qk 192 --head_dim_vo 128` with h_q = h_kv. Three legs (backend / FROST pinned / default) timed 30 iterations after 5 warmup each, with the leg order rotated per case.

1. **Tile sweep, causal, 12 cases.** FROST / backend by query tile count:
   - 32 tiles: 0.640 (the existing lead);
   - s_q 128-256 at 128-1024 tiles: 1.41-1.56;
   - s_q 512-4096 at 1024-8192 tiles: 0.867-0.891.

   The split follows s_q, not the tile count.
2. **Frozen qualification, 24 cases (seed 20261112), causal and mask-free.** The set has s_q 384-6000, S_kv 2048-32768, h 16-128 and b 1-8.
   - Causal with s_q >= 512 (9 cases): 0.839-0.989, every case faster.
   - Causal with s_q 400-448 (3 cases, outside the new box): 0.834-0.872.
   - Mask-free with s_q >= 512 (9 cases): 0.93-1.03, and one 1.335 (b 1, s_q 768, h 16, 96 tiles).

   Mask-free chunks were therefore left out. That restriction was chosen after this set was scored, and it only narrows the qualified box.
3. **Route check.** The default leg was re-timed with this change on four causal cases in the box: 0.846-0.988x develop's default, now on the FROST plan. A mask-free control stayed on the backend at 1.001.

The 512 bound is conservative. Causal s_q 400-448 also won, but no shorter causal case past 64 tiles was qualified.
