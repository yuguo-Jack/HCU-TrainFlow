## Why

**dGEGLU.** On torch 2.14.1 the kernel's `d_row` matches the analytic closed-interval derivative to 1.9e-7 relative, and `dprob` matches autograd to 1.8e-7. All 1078 of 131072 cells where kernel and autograd disagree hold an input of exactly ±7.0. Central differences on 12 interior cells agree three ways (FD, autograd, kernel). Boundary gradient of `x.clamp(-7, 7)` at `x = [-7, 0, 7]`:

| torch | gradient |
|---|---|
| 2.11.0, 2.13.0 | `[1, 1, 1]` |
| 2.14.1 | `[0, 1, 0]` |

The green job (473962714) and the first red one (475494701) use the same image tag `cudnn_13.4.1` and the same DSL wheel. The red image was rebuilt: cuda-pathfinder 1.6.1 → 1.8.1, and quack-kernels 0.6.2 is present. A local bisect over the six commits in the break window, starting with b220335e4, which predates the green schedule, fails on every commit under torch 2.14.1, so no FE commit caused it. `import quack` is ruled out: with and without it, 4 fail before the fix.

**GAB dense tail.** The dense-tail cells now run instead of pinning the decline:
- inference FP8 (unfused and fused) and MXFP8: S=1000 against the fake-quant oracle at the matrix's cosine floor; MXFP8 also checks every output cell was written;
- the quantized training forward's `s992_dense_b2` cells: bitwise against the inference block;
- the bf16 backward's dense S=1000 cells: against fp64 autograd at the module's bounds;
- a new dense `s992` quantized backward cell (FP8 and MXFP8): finite gradients plus the existing bitwise layer (quantizers, scalars, `delta`), the same standard as the launch-only cells.

The backward pads K/V with zeros, so pad columns add nothing to dQ, and real dK / dV columns do not read them. The forward's Stats now comes from the masked tail.
