# Overlap SM100 D64 softmax after alpha publication

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1973) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

SM100 BF16 D64 causal prefill delays its second softmax warpgroup until the first finishes publishing both probability chunks. Release that handoff immediately after alpha publication so the two softmax stages can overlap earlier. Scope it to the measured dense 128x128/two-subtile configuration; other shapes and architectures retain their existing schedule. Math, producer/consumer barriers and publication fences are unchanged.

Both warpgroups own separate score/probability/Stats slots. Each still reaches named barrier 8 once per iteration, including transitions through masked and skipped tiles. Extend the existing tail/sink/packing/replay tests with growing key-tile magnitudes to force repeated online rescaling. No public knob, eligibility or heuristics change.

Validation:

- `cd test/python && pytest -q -m L0 sdpa/frost/test_sdpa_frost_d64_causal_softmax.py`: 24 passed on SM100, covering both CTA widths, packing, causal alignment, sinks, fresh bindings and changed-input graph replay against FP64 O/Stats.
- The 12 `-k rescale` cases pass both Compute Sanitizer memcheck and synccheck with zero errors on a second SM100 device.
- Five unaffected compile controls produce the expected cubins: SM103 BF16 D64 and SM100 FP16 D64 at both CTA widths, plus SM100 D128 CGA2. Whole-module AST comparison after folding the new gate preserves the previous disabled specialization.
- Six existing GPT-OSS configurations, two build/execution orders, independent unchanged-source control: all 48 benchmark arms pass O/Stats and poisoned changed-input replay. Warm CUDA-Graph latency improves 0.51–1.28%, with geometric-mean reductions of 0.98% and 0.93%. This was measured on a local SM100 engineering device (148 SMs, 600 W), not production B200 or serving throughput. The larger backend performance gap remains.
- Scoped pre-commit passes.
