## Problem

On develop (8cf664347), `sdpa/frost/test_sdpa_prepared_stats_int64.py::test_prepared_thd_stats_wide_head_stride[wide-stride-512-512-half]` fails on B200:

```
ValueError: Out of bound value on argument #14 when calling: `_host(... lse_ext: int32, ...)`, expected to be in int32 range
```

The two d512 2x2 forward kernels are `sm100/prefill_d512_f16_2x2.py` and `sm107/prefill_d512_f16_2x2.py`.

- **Where they came from:** #1323 added them as twins of `prefill_d512_f16.py`, branched before #1377.
- **What #1377 did:** widened packed Stats addressing in every prepared forward kernel. It made the `lse_ext` host argument `Int64` and stored packed Stats by direct wide indexing instead of through an Int32 row view.
- **What the twins kept:** the old `Int32 lse_ext` and the row-view stores.

A packed THD Stats buffer with a head stride above 2^31 elements is rejected at launch on the 2x2 route. The same buffer works on every other prepared kernel.

## Change

The same three edits #1377 made to `prefill_d512_f16.py`, applied to both 2x2 kernels:

- `lse_ext: cutlass.Int64` in `_host`, and the matching `cutlass.Int64(0)` placeholder in `compile`;
- token-major and head-major packed Stats are stored with `lse_arr[..., index] = lse` rather than through a row view.
