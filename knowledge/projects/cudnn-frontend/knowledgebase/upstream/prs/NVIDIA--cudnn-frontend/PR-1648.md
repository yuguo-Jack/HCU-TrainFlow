# frost(sdpa): walk the split combine's tail in groups of 16 loads and price every partial in the decode split model

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/1648) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Summary

Two changes behind one measurement: the shared split combine of the FROST SDPA forward (`sm100/split_combine`) walked its split axis one memory round trip at a time, and the d256 decode tile's split model priced that combine as a flat cost -- together they made the batch-1 decode route split 64 ways and pay a 40 us combine behind a 12 us kernel.

- **Kernel (`split_combine.py`, the lean fp32-partial path):** splits past the 4-split head group are now loaded in groups of `G_T = 16` (LSE and partial-O rows of the whole group first, then the group's math) in both the max pass and the accumulate pass. The arithmetic order is unchanged -- a slot past `n_splits` re-reads the last split and is selected out of the sums -- so the output is bitwise what it was at every split count (checked output for output against the previous kernel at splits 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 / 64 on four decode cells).
- **Model (`heuristics.py`):** `choose_decode_tile_split_kv` adds `s * _DECODE_TILE_COMBINE_PER_PARTIAL` (0.17 KV tiles per partial, fitted on the grouped-load combine) to the split path's cost. The batch-1 optima measured at kv 4K / 32K / 128K (splits 16 / 32 / 64) and the batch-4 x 128K one (16) fall out; every existing B200 pin holds.

## Measurement (Rubin cc 10.7, 212 SMs @ 2376 MHz lock, one exclusive GPU, Qwen3.5-397B 24/2 geometry = 2 KV heads x 12:1 packing, d 256, page 16; device microseconds of the decode tile + combine per step from the profiler over 5 replays; the TensorRT-LLM column is the decode kernel FlashInfer 0.7.0.post1 launches for `trtllm_batch_decode_with_kv_cache` on the same cells)

## Regression guard on the SM100 line (GB200, cc 10.0, 152 SMs @ 1830 MHz lock, one exclusive GPU; a guard on one node class, not a performance claim)

The combine kernel and the decode split model are shared by the SM100 and Rubin bodies, so the same FlashInfer-shaped paged decode graphs (S_q 1, bf16, page 16, per-batch lengths, no Stats) were timed on both trees on a GB200: the engine's own leading plan per tree, a CUDA-graph replay under CUDA events with an L2 flush before every launch, 3 rounds x 100 and a control twin per cell, the SM clock sampled at the lock and the power-cap counter read around every cell, alternating processes of the two trees (3 before / 3 after). Speed-ups are `before / after - 1`:

| cell | d256, 24/2 (12:1) | d128, 24/2 (12:1) | d128, 32/8 (4:1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| B 1 x kv 32K | 72.2 -> 35.6 us (+103.0 %; split 64 -> 32) | 37.4 -> 32.5 us (+14.8 %; split 16 -> 16) | 48.9 -> 43.1 us (+13.5 %; split 16 -> 16) |

No cell loses beyond the run-to-run noise of its unchanged kernel (the B 32 cells run split 1 or 2, inside the head group the grouped walk never touches; their outputs are bitwise identical between the trees). On this part the per-partial term is not refitted here (it was fitted on the Rubin tile; every existing B200 pin holds): the combine at 64 partials reads ~50 us before and ~16 us after the grouped walk, and the batch-1 kv 32K route moves from 64 to 32 splits.
