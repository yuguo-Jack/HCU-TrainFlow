## Affected area

## Summary

This PR speeds up decode and verify steps on the SM120/SM121 f16/bf16 forward engine (`sdpa_fwd_prefill_sm120`). It builds on #1627, which gave the SM100 half flavors sink-free split partials and a combine that folds the sink once, and adds only what develop does not have yet. Three commits:

**1. SM120 sink split, whole-group PackGQA, SM120 combine price.**
- **Attention sink with split-KV.** The general, d256 and d512 f16/bf16 templates compile their sink fold out at `SPLIT_KV > 1` and write sink-free partials. #1627's sink combine entry then folds the sink once, as it does for the SM100 half flavors.
  - `Capabilities.split_sink` names the rows whose half kernels write such partials. It is set on the SM100 and SM120 f16/bf16 rows, and `mismatch()` and the split heuristics read it in place of develop's SM100 arch test. SM100 eligibility is unchanged.
  - The unsplit fold now keeps a row with no visible key and a `-inf` sink at O := 0, LSE := -inf instead of NaN.
- **Whole-group PackGQA.** A GQA group that does not divide the Q tile packs whole on the general and d256 templates: `q_tile // G` tokens per tile, with the remaining rows dead (no Q load, no O or Stats store).
  - `pack_gqa_group_size` / `pack_gqa_supported` gain a `tail=` contract, and `Capabilities.pack_gqa_tail_d_shapes` lists the flavors that implement it.
  - The kernels launch `ceil(S_q / (q_tile // G))` Q tiles, and `heuristics._split_launch` counts the same.
  - The causal mask covers the extra KV tile a tail-packed frontier can straddle.
  - Groups 5, 6, 7 and 12, which used to run unpacked and re-read the KV once per query head, now pack.
- **SM120 per-wave combine price.** The SM120 half row prices the split combine per wave at its own coefficient, `_SM120_SPLIT_KV_COMBINE_COST = 0.01` KV tiles, passed through a new trailing `combine_cost=` keyword of `choose_split_kv`. The B200 price, 0.1, charged a verify step's multi-wave combine up to a whole SM120 tile per partial and stopped it at split 2–4.
- **Base-2 Stats.** The d256 and d512 templates keep split partial LSEs natural-log when a direct caller enables base-2 Stats (Rule S4).

**2. SM120 packed decode tile.** A packed set used to ride the largest tile that admits the GQA ratio, 128 rows. For SM120 decode and verify, the 64-row tile is faster on most shapes: it carries fewer dead rows and, past 64 live rows, twice the units.
- `_sm120_tiles(..., pack_g)` picks 128 rows only when `S_q · G ≥ 128` and the 128-row units fill the machine with splits of two or more KV tiles; otherwise it picks 64 rows.
- Packing is still decided on the 128-row tile, so `64 ≤ S_q < 128` keeps its plan.
- Tile runners cover every tile except the lead's own, and the half row's runners are rescored to their own split.
- Measured on 80 b = 1 shapes per GPU, with every packed tile × split timed, the geomean regret against the ladder best falls from 1.28 to 1.04 on an RTX PRO 6000 and from 1.30 to 1.02 on an RTX 5080.

**3. Half-partial combine form.** #1627 tuned the combine's lean, serial and decode forms on fp32 partials. SM120 splits write half partials, which those forms read one element at a time.
- Half partials now take a half form at every S_q and split count:
  - one warp per row;
  - LSE passes that stride the split axis across the lanes, 32 splits a round;
  - an O pass that loads each split's four columns as one 8-byte vector, eight splits in flight.
- The sink and Q-length entries fold exactly as before, and fp32 partials keep develop's forms unchanged.
- Half partials are written by every SM120 split, the SM100 MXFP8 d512 split and the cc 10.7 flavors without an fp32 partial slot.
- The SM120 half row no longer charges #1627's B200-fitted serial-walk split price, which models a form its partials never take.

## Why

On an RTX PRO 6000, FROST's default plan ran at a geomean 0.75× of llama.cpp's FlashAttention kernels over 744 f16 decode and verify shapes from real models (kernel time, cold L2, one process per case). Two gaps were structural:
- **Sinks never split on SM120.** A gpt-oss-20b decode step (64/8 heads, d64, sink) at 64K KV ran on 8 CTAs: 1173 µs, against 133 µs for llama.cpp.
- **GQA 6 ran unpacked.** A Qwen3.6-27B decode step (24/4 heads, d256) at 64K KV re-read the KV once per query head: 524 µs, against 245 µs for llama.cpp.

After those, the split combine and the split depth were the next costs at decode.

## API and compatibility impact

- No public API signature or dependency changes. The engine stays opt-in (`CUDNN_FRONTEND_ENABLE_FROST_ENGINES=1`). No native binding source changes: SM120 sink splits ride #1627's binder.
- New eligibility on SM 12.x f16/bf16:
  - dense unpadded sink graphs may split their KV;
  - GQA groups that do not divide the Q tile may pack, on the general and d256 flavors.
- `sm100/split_combine.py` is shared, so the SM100 MXFP8 d512 split and the cc 10.7 half-partial splits also take the half form.
  - B200 combine microbenchmark: 1.36× warm, 1.41× after an L2 scrub (below).
  - Those rows keep the B200 serial split price; they are unmeasured with the half form, see follow-ups.
- SM121 is admitted by the cc 12.x row but not validated.

## Follow-ups

- The other rows with half partials (SM120 FP8 / MXFP8 splits, the SM100 MXFP8 d512 split, the cc 10.7 flavors without an fp32 slot) also take the half form but keep the B200 serial-walk split price, which is unmeasured against that form. They may split shallower than their best.
- The split model's wave term still uses the B200 per-CTA cost (`_SPLIT_KV_CTA_COST = 21` KV tiles). On the RTX 5080 it under-splits batched S_q = 32 (b = 4/16: 6–21% regret) and gpt-oss S_q = 32 at 64K KV (10%). On the RTX PRO 6000 the 64-row tile at 4K–64K KV takes 16 splits where 32–64 run 9–17% faster warm (qwen S_q 1 at 4K: 8.1 against 7.1 µs). An SM120 per-CTA cost is a separate fit.
- The SM120 FP8 / MXFP8 / d512-FP8 sink folds have the same keyless-row `-inf`-sink NaN that this PR fixes in the half templates. It is pre-existing and not touched here.
