## Summary

**Stacked on #1181** (the Rule 8 core PR). Rule 8 (#1167) for the gated attention block:

- **R4.** The FP8 / MXFP8 quant words (`alpha_*`, `scale_*`, `descale_*`) live in a workspace slot filled with `fill_word_async` on the launch stream at execute, instead of small device tensors allocated per execute.
- **R7.** The graph route owns ONE process-lifetime cuDNN handle per device (`proj_gemm.graph_handle`, created under `torch.cuda.device(idx)` at `compile()`, never at a possibly captured execute) and re-streams it with `cudnn.set_stream` before every `graph.execute`. Re-stream + execute are one critical section per device (`_graph_handle_lock`), also when a caller hands the shared handle back in as `handle=` (recognised by identity, `_shared_handle_device`); a caller-owned handle bypasses the lock.
- **Detectors (R9).** `test_graph_route_handle_is_per_device_and_restreamed` (monkeypatches `cudnn.create_handle` to fail, executes on two streams, records whether the lock is held per execute), quant-slot layout tests, allocation-count detectors.

## Why

Rule 8: no per-execute allocation, no resource creation inside a captured execute (`cudnnCreate` inside capture = #1151-class invalidation), and a cuDNN handle must not be driven by two threads at once.
