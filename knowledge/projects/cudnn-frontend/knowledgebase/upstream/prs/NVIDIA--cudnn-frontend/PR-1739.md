## Affected area

- FE OSS kernels or CuTeDSL

## Summary

- Moves the QSA backward from `cudnn.sdpa.bwd` to the DSA namespace: `cudnn.DSA.GqaSparseAttentionBackward` and `cudnn.DSA.gqa_sparse_attention_backward_wrapper` (follow-up of #1616).
- Admits SM103 (cc 10.3) next to SM100.
- Replaces the single-pass sparse kernel with two passes: a query-major kernel writes dQ and per-(query, block) P / dS records, a key-major kernel accumulates dK / dV from them through a counting-sort inverted index, so the fp32 dK / dV atomics of the sparse path are gone.
- Dense key-tile kernel: the work unit follows the grid's causal work per CTA-pair slot instead of a size table, and sequences up to 2051 tokens skip the selection bitmap kernel (every list is the whole causal prefix).
- Host: the key-major kernel runs on every SM and the dense grid backfills its tail (SM split table removed), one dense / sparse crossover constant.

## Why

- The DSA namespace is where the index-list sparse attention family lives.
- The sparse path was bound by fp32 dK / dV atomics at long sequences; records plus a key-major pass turn that into coalesced operand gathers.
- Short sequences left most SMs idle in the dense kernel; sizing the unit from the causal work fixes that without a per-length table.

## Related issues

Follow-up of #1616.

## API and compatibility impact

- `cudnn.sdpa.bwd.SparseGqaBwdDslSm100` / `sparse_gqa_bwd_wrapper_sm100` are removed; the same signatures live under `cudnn.DSA` (the API was experimental and unreleased).
- SM100 and SM103, `nvidia-cutlass-dsl >= 4.7.0`.
- Workspace grows with the records: about 384 bytes per (sparse query, selected block) pair, roughly 10 GiB at 64K tokens and 52 GiB at 256K.
- Results stay non-deterministic (fp32 reductions), as before.
- Performance (B200-class GPU, locked clocks, relative to the merged kernel): 2.3x to 3.8x at 1K to 2051 tokens, 1.2x at 32K, 1.3x at 64K, 1.4x at 128K and 256K; 4K to 16K unchanged within noise.
