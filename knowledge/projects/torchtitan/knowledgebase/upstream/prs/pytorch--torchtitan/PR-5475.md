# [rl] Drop only the empty rollout, not its group

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan/pull/5475) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

**TLDR:** When one rollout in a group has no completion tokens, drop only that rollout, not its whole group. On a GB300 Terminal-Bench run, 56 empty rollouts threw away 25 groups (~400 good rollouts).

**Problem**
1. `TrainingSampleBuilder.build_from_group` drops the whole group if any sibling has no completion tokens.
2. A rollout that fails before its first turn has no turns, so it has no completion tokens.
3. On a GB300 Terminal-Bench run, a 7-minute sandbox image-pull burst made 56 rollouts fail before their first turn.
4. Each one threw away its group of 16: 25 groups, ~400 good rollouts, were lost.

**Solution**
1. Drop only the rollouts with no turns or an empty completion, and build the group from the rest.
2. The builder already dropped a rollout with an empty later turn, inside its loop. That filter moves up front and also catches rollouts with no turns or an empty first turn. Same metric: `training_sample_builder/num_rollouts_dropped_empty_completion`.
3. A group with fewer than 2 rollouts left (out of 2 or more) is dropped and counted in `training_sample_builder/num_groups_dropped_untrainable`, whether or not zero-std groups are dropped.
4. The zero-std check runs on the trainable rollouts.
5. Unchanged: groups of 1 by config, and errored rollouts that have turns (they still train at their error reward).

```
group of 16, 1 rollout failed before its first turn
before: 0 samples,  num_groups_dropped_untrainable += 1
after:  15 samples, num_rollouts_dropped_empty_completion += 1
```

Side effect: a rollout whose first turn is empty but a later turn is not used to raise `non-final turn 0 has no completion` in `rollout_to_training_samples` and stop the run. It is now dropped like the others.

You may ask:
- Why drop a lone trainable rollout? Its advantage was centered on the dropped siblings' rewards: with 15 of 16 failing at reward 0, a passing survivor would train at advantage 1 - 1/16 = 0.94 for beating siblings that never ran.
- Why not center the advantages on the trainable rollouts only? `AdvantageEstimator` runs in the rollouter, before the builder, and would need the same empty check. Until then, 1 of 16 dropped at reward 0, with about half the rest passing, shifts each kept advantage by about +0.03. A TODO marks it.

NOTE: CPU unit tests only, no end-to-end run.

**Test plan**
`pytest tests/rl/unit_tests/cpu/test_rollout_utils.py`
- `test_build_from_group_drops_only_the_rollout_with_an_empty_completion`, 4 cases: no turns, empty first turn, empty first of two turns, empty later turn
- `test_build_from_group_drops_a_group_with_fewer_than_two_trainable_rollouts`, 0 or 1 left, with zero-std groups kept
