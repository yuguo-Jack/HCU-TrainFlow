# [RL] Keep generator actors admitting new requests during vLLM engine steps

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan/pull/4917) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Summary

`VLLMGenerator._engine_loop()` currently calls the blocking `LLMEngine.step()` on the generator actor's asyncio thread. While a long prefill or decode step is running, the actor cannot service concurrent `generate()` calls, so those requests cannot enter the generator queue until the step returns.

This PR runs `LLMEngine.step()` on one dedicated worker thread by default. It intentionally does not add a config switch.

- Keep vLLM engine access serialized with a private `max_workers=1` executor and await every step before the next engine operation.
- Initialize the worker's thread-local CUDA device and TorchTitan DeviceMesh/SPMD state.
- Enter `torch.no_grad()` inside the worker because grad mode is thread-local.
- Stop the worker after the engine loop finishes and before renderer teardown.

This is a focused change: it contains no disaggregated P/D routing, KV connector, or weight-sync changes.

## Performance

### Five-step DAPO Math A/B on GB300

I ran an apples-to-apples current-main A/B with Qwen3-4B-Base and DAPO-Math-17k: six independent TP4 generators plus one TP4 trainer, five real RL steps, 8 prompts x 16 samples/step, 8K maximum response, `max_num_seqs=107`, FULL CUDA graphs, prefix caching, BF16 KV cache, no KV offload, and `direct_rdma=False`. Both jobs used the same FairTitan launcher and runtime and ran sequentially.

| Metric | Inline control ([`1608867`](https://meta-fair.wandb.io/jianiw/torchtitan-dapo-thread-offload/runs/v4hd3nrp)) | Threaded ([`1608921`](https://meta-fair.wandb.io/jianiw/torchtitan-dapo-thread-offload/runs/u6vj2zid)) | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Completed generation requests | 1,616 | 1,776 | +9.9% |
| vLLM aggregate output throughput | 15,153 tok/s | 16,930 tok/s | +11.7% |
| Mean active requests per engine | 14.64 | 17.68 | +20.7% |
| Mean of per-step max inflight | 97.6 | 105.4 | +8.0% |
| Warm ITL mean (steps 2-5) | 6.112 ms | 6.012 ms | -1.6% |
| Warm TTFT mean (steps 2-5) | 61.860 ms | 62.073 ms | +0.3% |
| Warm decode-time mean (steps 2-5) | 5,212.3 ms | 5,148.4 ms | -1.2% |
| Warm selected output length | 895.1 tokens | 889.1 tokens | -0.7% |
| Five-step trainer total | 76.826 s | 80.603 s | +4.9% |
| Full job elapsed | 411 s | 410 s | -0.2% |

The PR improved its intended target: requests entered the engine sooner, admitted concurrency increased, and the same-length run completed 9.9% more requests at 11.7% higher periodic output throughput. It did **not** demonstrate an end-to-end training speedup in this short run. The six TP4 generators were overprovisioned for one TP4 trainer: vLLM reported zero waiting requests and at most 0.6% KV-cache use, so the extra rollouts were mostly background work rather than trainer-critical-path work. The five stochastic step totals are not sufficient to call either an end-to-end regression or improvement.

The higher internal vLLM queue time in the threaded arm is expected from the measurement boundary: requests that previously waited invisibly in the actor mailbox are now admitted earlier and counted in vLLM queue time. The controller-level training window and job wall time remain the end-to-end measures.

The periodic throughput is the aggregate across six engines, averaged over seven 10-second vLLM logger windows with TP-rank duplicates averaged out. Both runs completed 5/5 steps with no NaN, OOM, or runtime error; selected output length differed by only 0.7%, and prefix-cache hit rates were 67.37% versus 66.88%.

### Prior focused admission stress tests

On the TorchTitan native-vLLM diagnostic path, Qwen3.5-4B with two unified TP4 generators, 512 synthetic requests per warm wave, five real RL steps, `max_num_seqs=128`, prefix caching and CUDA graphs enabled, BF16 KV cache, no KV offload, and `direct_rdma=False` showed:

| Metric | Inline control (`1606865`) | Threaded (`1606910`) | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Warm 512-request span | 16.053 s | 13.245 s | -17.5% |
| Warm output throughput | 1,459.865 tok/s | 1,701.022 tok/s | +16.5% |
| Median warm request mean latency | 3.262 s | 2.771 s | -15.1% |
| Five-step trainer total | 218.280 s | 211.317 s | -3.2% |
| Wait for rollout batch | 44.838 s | 38.907 s | -13.2% |
| Forward/backward | 140.204 s | 140.241 s | tied |
| Blocking weight sync | 9.023 s | 9.007 s | tied |

The same isolated change was repeated twice on a P/D decoder: raw output throughput improved by 5.7% and 8.6%, pause-adjusted throughput by 4.1% and 6.8%, and decode ITL p95 by 34% and 43%.
