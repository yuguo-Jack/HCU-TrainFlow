# [rl] Optional native rollout timeout (14/14)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan/pull/5512) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

**TLDR:** `RolloutWorker.Config.rollout_timeout_s` ends a native rollout that runs past it as `ERROR_TIMEOUT`, keeps its completed turns, and cancels its in-flight generate call. A liveness guard against a hung env or generator; off by default.

**Problem**
1. A native rollout runs `env.init()`, then alternates a generate call and `env.step` until the env is terminal.
2. Only a single env step has a deadline (`TokenEnv` `step_timeout_s`, 1800 s).
3. A generate call or an env that never returns holds its rollout, and its group's buffer slot, forever.

**Solution**
1. `RolloutWorker.Config.rollout_timeout_s` (default None) puts `env.init()` and every turn, not `score_group`, under one `asyncio.timeout`.
2. Past it, the rollout ends as `ERROR_TIMEOUT` with its completed turns. Its in-flight generate call is cancelled (with PR 11, the generator also stops the request), and the session is still released.
3. A `TimeoutError` raised inside a turn (e.g. aiohttp's connect timeout) is not a rollout timeout: it ends as `ERROR_SYSTEM` (PR 7).
4. Unchanged: Verifiers rollouts, which use Verifiers' `timeout.rollout` and keep the trace.

**Results**
Numerics: bitwise vs main: `rollout_timeout_s` defaults to None, and no recipe sets it.
```text
GPU, with PR 11: alphabet sort, Qwen3-0.6B, trainer TP2 + one TP4 generator,
rollout_timeout_s=0.5 (generate p50/p99 0.25/0.50 s), 5 steps
training steps                     5 of 5, no errors
rollout timeouts (worker log)      568
decisions with aborts              14
per TP rank: ids asked / aborted   490 / 300 (the rest had already finished)
TP ranks agreeing                  4 of 4
rollout_timeout_s=3                no rollout timed out (inert when it doesn't fire)
```

You may ask: why off by default? A cut biases training toward short rollouts; set it well above the p99 rollout time.

NOTE:
- Most of the diff re-indents the rollout loop under `async with`; hide whitespace (`?w=1`) to read it.
- `asyncio.to_thread` work inside `env.step` keeps running past the timeout, as with `step_timeout_s`.
