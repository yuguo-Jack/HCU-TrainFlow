# [kimi] Make KDA L2Norm numerics deterministic

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan/pull/5358) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Summary

- Bump Attention Gym from 0.0.16 to 0.0.17.
- Disable KDA L2Norm backward autotuning when PyTorch deterministic algorithms are enabled.
- Keep the existing Kimi K3 B200 golden: two fresh exports with the pinned schedule reproduce it byte-for-byte.

## Why

TorchTitan already pins the chunk KDA schedule in deterministic mode, but L2Norm backward had an independent Triton autotuner. Attention Gym 0.0.17 adds an autotune flag to L2Norm, allowing the complete KDA training path to avoid benchmark- and cache-dependent schedule selection during numerical validation.

Upstream fix: https://github.com/meta-pytorch/attention-gym/pull/632

## Numerical validation

### B200

- Back-to-back B200 export: https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan/actions/runs/38472842069
- Config: kimi_k3_debugmodel_mm_muon
- Topology: FSDP2/EP2 on two B200 GPUs
- Steps: 10
- Metrics: full-precision TensorBoard loss and grad_norm
- Both exports and the checked-in golden are byte-for-byte identical.

### Local GB300 repeatability

- Ran the same FSDP2/EP2 10-step export three independent times on physical GPUs 0 and 1.
- All three full-precision loss/grad-norm traces are byte-for-byte identical.
- The GB300 trace is repeatability evidence only; it does not replace the B200 golden.

## Validation

- Focused K3 kernel parity and integration-definition tests: 20 passed.
- Final B200 exact comparison on the rebased head: https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan/actions/runs/38477944618
  - kimi_k3_mm_muon completed with rc=0.
  - loss_compare.py --assert-equal matched all 10 losses and all 10 gradient norms exactly.
