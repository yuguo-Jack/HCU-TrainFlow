**Bitwise vs main?** Yes, unless a rollout fails for a non-policy reason. Training samples are byte-identical on CPU (shas below).

**TLDR:** a rollout that fails for a reason that isn't the policy's (its code raised, or Verifiers recorded an infra error such as an unreachable generator or a sandbox failure) now stays in its group as `ERROR_SYSTEM`, recorded with its error, but gets no reward or advantage and isn't trained. Its siblings train, centered on their own mean. Before, it dropped the whole group of 16 if it raised in Verifiers or failed on the first call, and trained as an error otherwise.

**Problem**
1. A rollout fails either because of the policy (bad format, a prompt past the context, a timeout) or because of the code around it (an unreachable generator or env server, a sandbox failure, an env bug).
2. **Native rollouts** turned any exception into `ERROR`:
   - raised before the first turn (e.g. the generator unreachable): no tokens, so the sample builder dropped the whole group as untrainable. In a single-turn env, that's every infra failure;
   - raised later: the rubric scored it (`error_reward` or 0), its reward entered the group mean, so every sibling's advantage moved, and its partial turns trained.
3. **Verifiers rollouts:**
   - a rollout that raised (e.g. an env-server request failed) made its group raise, and the controller dropped the group;
   - Verifiers records most infra failures on the trace instead of raising (a generator 5xx or failed connection, a sandbox error). Those became `ERROR`, with the same two outcomes as native.
4. How often: not measured. `rollout/system_error_rate` will show it.

**Solution**
1. A new status, `RolloutStatus.ERROR_SYSTEM` (`is_error()` is true).
2. Native `_run_single_rollout`: an exception ends the rollout as `ERROR_SYSTEM` (the rollout timeout stays `ERROR_TIMEOUT`), with the error in `Rollout.logs["error"]`.
3. `rollouts_from_gather`, used by both `run_group`s: a rollout that raised becomes an `ERROR_SYSTEM` rollout with no turns. A group raises only on a cancel.
4. Verifiers' `rollout_status` reads the first recorded error, the one that stopped the rollout. Only a generator 4xx (a prompt past the context) or the agent's own exit (`HarnessError`) is the policy's and stays `ERROR`. Any other (a sandbox, env, tool or task-code failure, a generator 5xx or failed connection, an untyped error) is `ERROR_SYSTEM`.
5. Both `run_group`s score and center only the other rollouts: the rubric and the advantage estimator never see an `ERROR_SYSTEM` rollout, whose reward and advantage stay None.
6. `TrainingSampleBuilder` skips `ERROR_SYSTEM` rollouts before its group filters, so one no longer drops its group. A group left with one rollout is dropped as zero-std (its advantage is 0).
7. The rollout recorder always keeps `ERROR_SYSTEM` rollouts (they're unscored, so the reward ranking never picks them). A new metric: `rollout/system_error_rate` (and `validation/system_error_rate`).
8. Unchanged: the statuses an env returns (`ERROR`, `ERROR_PARSE`, `ERROR_TIMEOUT`, `ERROR_ABORT`, truncation) are scored and trained as before.

```text
1 of 16 rollouts fails, not the policy's doing   before                                        after
native, raises before its first turn             the group of 16 dropped                       ERROR_SYSTEM: recorded, not trained; the 15 train
native, raises later                             ERROR: scored, in the mean, partial turns     same as above
Verifiers, raises                                the group of 16 dropped                       same as above
Verifiers, infra error recorded on the trace     like native (first call: group dropped)       same as above
```

You may ask:
- Why not retry the failed rollout? A retry needs a fresh env and re-runs every tool side effect; leaving it out keeps the siblings' work and re-runs nothing.
- Why a status, not a flag? The recorder and the metrics already read the status, and one value says why the rollout wasn't trained.

NOTE:
- An env that raises on odd model output now gets no penalty for it (the rollout is `ERROR_SYSTEM`). An env that wants a penalty returns an error status instead.
- Native, with `TokenEnv.Config.max_rollout_tokens` unset: a prompt past the generator's context fails the generate call (a 400), so the rollout is `ERROR_SYSTEM`, not penalized. Set it to the context length to keep that a trained `TRUNCATED_PROMPT_TOO_LONG`. The in-repo recipes set it or stay under the context.
- When infra fails, `rollout_reward/group_zero_std_frac` also counts groups left with one rollout, and a native `ERROR_SYSTEM` rollout's partial turns still count in `rollout/num_turns` and `rollout/output_tokens`.
