## Agent details

Prepared with AI assistance.

Indexed frozen samples need stable global ordering independent of the data-parallel degree. Share the existing indexed sharding helper and add opt-in strided ownership after global ordering, repeating before slicing so uneven epoch boundaries remain correct. Keep contiguous ownership as the default and storage clients outside core.
