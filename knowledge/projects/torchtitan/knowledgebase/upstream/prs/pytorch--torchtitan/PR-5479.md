# [rl] test_engine_loop: wait up to 30 s for a reply, not 5

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan/pull/5479) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

**TLDR:** `test_engine_loop.py` waits at most 5 s (`_TIMEOUT_S`) for each engine-loop reply. On a busy host, two weight-load tests went past 5 s once in ~31 full CPU-suite runs. This PR raises the bound to 30 s. Passing tests take as long as before: it is an upper bound, not a sleep.

- The slow step is the load's `gc.collect()` (`EngineLoop._load_model_state_dict`), ~0.7 s in the full suite's heap.
- The ~31 runs were on the CPU KV offload branch. Main runs the same `gc.collect()` (`engine_loop.py:497`).

**Test plan**
- `pytest tests/rl/unit_tests/cpu/test_engine_loop.py`
