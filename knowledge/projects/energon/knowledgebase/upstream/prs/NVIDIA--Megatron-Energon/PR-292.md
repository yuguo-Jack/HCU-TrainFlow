## Summary

Allows resuming training after the recipe changed (datasets added, removed, reordered or
reweighted) while preserving the progress of datasets that are still present.

## Changes

- New `megatron.energon.checkpoint` package:
  - `collect_resume_metadata(loader)` — save next to the loader state;
  - `migrate_dataloader_state(new_loader, saved_state, saved_metadata)` — maps saved leaf
    progress onto the new topology by stable identity (path, `split_part`, subset, filter);
    unmatched or ambiguous leaves start fresh;
  - `RankStateIterable` & co. extracted from `tools/checkpoint.py`.
- Docs: *Migrating a Checkpoint After Recipe Changes* in Save and Restore, internals.
