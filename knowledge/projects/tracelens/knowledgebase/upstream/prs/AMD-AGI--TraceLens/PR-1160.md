# Count E8M0 scale bytes in the `gemm_afp4wfp4` perf model 

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/AMD-AGI/TraceLens/pull/1160) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description


## Summary

On MI355X (gfx950, CDNA4), AITER's Triton MXFP4 GEMM (`aiter::gemm_afp4wfp4_`) streams three things from HBM: the packed FP4 operands, one E8M0 scale byte per 32 K-elements for each operand, and the BF16 output. The kernel feeds the scales to the scaled MFMA instructions (`V_MFMA_SCALE_F32_16X16X128_F8F6F4` / `32X32X64`), so they are real HBM traffic. The TraceLens perf model counted only the operands and the output, and its docstring called the scales negligible. This was confirmed via AITER runs. Note `hipblaslt_gemm_fp4` and `aiter_gemm_a4w4` already count scale bytes. At small decode sizes these GEMMs are HBM-bound on MI355X. 

`bytes()` now adds `x_scales` and `w_scales`, using the shapes and dtypes the trace records at `Input Dims[2:4]` and `Input type[2:4]`. FLOPs stay at 2·M·N·K. The scaled MFMA applies the scales inside the instruction, and the MXFP4 peak is quoted on 2·M·N·K.

## Examples

Bytes per call, before and after the fix (BF16 output):

| M | N | K | Before (MB) | After (MB) | Scale bytes (MB) | Increase |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 7168 | 7168 | 25.71 | 27.31 | 1.61 | +6.2% |
| 16 | 7168 | 7168 | 25.98 | 27.59 | 1.61 | +6.2% |
| 64 | 2112 | 7168 | 8.07 | 8.56 | 0.49 | +6.0% |
| 256 | 7168 | 2048 | 11.27 | 11.75 | 0.48 | +4.2% |
| 4096 | 7168 | 7168 | 99.09 | 101.61 | 2.52 | +2.5% |

The scales add (M + N)·K/32 bytes against (M + N)·K/2 for the packed operands, so the increase approaches 6.25% when operand reads dominate. It shrinks at large M, where the output write takes a bigger share. T

## What changed

| `TraceLens/PerfModel/extensions/perf_model_extensions.py` | `gemm_afp4wfp4`: read the two scale shapes and dtypes in `get_param_details`, add their bytes in `bytes()`, and update the FLOPs and Bytes sections of the docstring |
