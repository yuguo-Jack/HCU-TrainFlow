# Added patches for v32 and v33 (rocm10)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/AMD-AGI/TraceLens/pull/1195) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Summary
- Add vLLM v0.32.0 and v0.33.0 graph-capture patches (V2 decoder, speculator, and V1 encoder), including one `sample_*` span around the speculative-drafter serving step.
- Point the v0.32 and v0.33 image builds at `vllm/vllm-openai-rocm:v0.32.0` and `vllm/vllm-openai-rocm:v0.33.0-rocm100`.

## Tests
V2 runner, `--max-cudagraph-capture-size 32`. Qwen3-8B has no MTP head, so MTP used `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B` with `--spec-method mtp --spec-tokens 3`. Capture time is the profiled pass. Serving traces have `execute_*` annotations; MTP traces also have `sample_*`.

| vLLM | Workload | Shards (decoder + subsystem) | Graph capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.32.0 | Qwen3-8B FP8, no MTP | 14 | 13 s |
| 0.33.0 | Qwen3-8B FP8, no MTP | 14 | 14 s |
| 0.32.0 | Qwen3.5-4B MTP, 3 spec tokens | 12 + 19 speculator | 13 s |
| 0.33.0 | Qwen3.5-4B MTP, 3 spec tokens | 12 + 19 speculator | 12 s |
