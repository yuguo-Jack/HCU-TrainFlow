# PR information

Refs #10405, specifically the remaining BNPO chunk normalization.

When dynamic rollout requires chunking, BNPO averages per-chunk token means by sample count, changing the loss and gradient for unequal completion lengths. Aggregate the policy contribution by the effective token count after overlong/off-policy filtering, keeping empty-token chunks out of the denominator. Keep auxiliary losses sample-weighted, including CHORD's policy coefficient, and retain dummy backward graphs.

AI assistance was used for implementation and CPU validation. The human owner explicitly approved publication; human review and responsibility remain required.

Tested base: `1152aa7947238e11e7555b08195da4f93e290bf2`; candidate: `aef44c5511cb02cfb0b0eb491f2a67d44b344e79`. No new runtime dependency.
