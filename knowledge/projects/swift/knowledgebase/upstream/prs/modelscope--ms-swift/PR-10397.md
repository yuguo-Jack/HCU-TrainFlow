# PR information

Related: #10116 (gradient-accumulation normalization).

When dynamic rollout produces more samples than the per-device batch size, `_compute_loss_chunked()` takes a sample-weighted mean of chunk losses. DAPO/CISPO/FIPO policy losses are already normalized by the global completion-token count, so this applies an extra chunk sample weight. With gradient accumulation set to 1, splitting four samples into two or four chunks shrinks every policy gradient to 1/2 or 1/4. A 3+2 split weights the first three samples by 0.6 and the remaining two by 0.4.

Pass the inverse chunk sample weight into `_compute_loss_and_metrics()` and apply it only to the window-normalized policy loss, before SDAR/CHORD are added. The existing outer weighted mean then sums the policy contributions correctly while retaining the auxiliary-loss weighting. Dummy chunks use a scale of 1 and retain their zero-weight backward graph.

This contains only the production change, as requested by the submitter; additional regression tests remain local. Searches for GRPO/chunked loss and references to #10116 found no duplicate fix. #10116 addresses accumulation-step scaling; #10256 concerns the separate Liger path. This bug also occurs with accumulation set to 1.

AI assistance: OpenAI Codex implemented the patch and ran the validation below. Human review/attestation checklist items remain unchecked.

## Experiment results

CPU; Python 3.12.13, PyTorch 2.13.0+cu130, Transformers 5.14.1, TRL 0.29.1. Reproductions use a small trainable output head and the actual trainer dispatch, slicing, loss reduction, and backward functions.

| DAPO execution, accumulation=1 | Before | After | Gradient ratio after |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Unchunked | -0.333333 | -0.333333 | 1 |
| Two equal chunks | -0.166667 | -0.333333 | 1 |
| Four equal chunks | -0.083333 | -0.333333 | 1 |

For 3+2 samples, per-sample gradient ratios change from `[0.6, 0.6, 0.6, 0.4, 0.4]` to all ones. CISPO and FIPO behave equivalently. GRPO and Dr. GRPO controls retain matching results.
