#  [MiniMax-M3 Performance] Prefer CuTe for MiniMax M3 FP8 indexer prefill

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60930) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

For MiniMax M3 FP8 indexer prefill, `auto` now uses MSA's persistent CuTe scorer instead of FMHA `OnlyScore`. Depends on [vllm-project/MSA#36](https://github.com/vllm-project/MSA/pull/36) (score-only API) and a follow-up pin bump.

**Not a duplicate of #59979:** that PR splits work inside FMHA; this one switches the prefill scorer to CuTe. They are complementary; the tables below measure both.

## Claims

- On FP8 indexer prefill, CuTe beats the FMHA kernel it replaces on all 78 prefill shapes, and never drops below 0.95× of it.
  - Kernel: 6.9× on GB300 and 7.6× on Rubin.
  - E2E prefill: 0–3% faster up to 32K tokens and 6–10% faster at 150K.
- No significant GSM8K difference on either platform.
- BF16 indexing, the default (`indexer_kv_dtype` resolves to BF16), keeps FMHA. Default settings are unchanged.
- `fmha` and `cute` force a backend without changing precision.
- Native Top16 and decode routing are unchanged.

## Validation

**Setup:** TP2, with the companion MSA API. Measured at `0421c58`, before squashing and rebasing. Since then only comments, docs and tests changed, and a redundant Q/K dtype guard was removed; scorer selection and dispatch are identical.

**Speed ratios:**
- Speedup rows give CuTe's speedup over the column's arm (baseline/CuTe, so values above 1 mean CuTe is faster).
- E2E figures are medians over 3 groups.
- Kernel figures are `score` µs per rank/layer with a cold L2, as a geomean over 78 prefill shapes.

**Accuracy:** GSM8K (out of 1,319) uses EAGLE3 k=3, over two independent boots.

**Findings:**
- **Rubin E2E outlier:** one group of 512/256/C8 measured 0.71 TTFT; the other two measured 1.08 and 1.09.
- **vs FMHA:** CuTe never loses at kernel level.
- **vs #59979:** CuTe loses on short-Q / 32K-prefix shapes (Q·H < 2048 on GB300, < 4096 on Rubin), by 1–6 µs, plus Rubin's fr NCU attributes the short-Q losses to the static persistent schedule's tail.

## Details

**Prerequisite:** the MSA `plan(..., score_out=)` / `run_scores()` API must land, and the pin (currently `968061a`) must be b
- Until then, `auto` keeps FMHA and `cute` errors.
- The B200 CI job runs `test_minimax_m3.py`, but its CuTe cases skip until the pin is bumped.

**Host sync:** CuTe plans on device (two small kernels, no D2H) once per batch, outside CUDA Graph capture, and `run_scores()h no allocation or sync. It replaces the FMHA plan's `seq_lens.cpu()` and boolean-mask gather, removing two host syncs per prefill step. Kernels compile during the existing MiniMax M3 warmup.

```bash
--attention-config '{"indexer_kv_dtype":"fp8","minimax_m3_indexer_prefill_backend":"auto"}'
```

CuTe is admitted for:
- SM100/103/107
- FP8 E4M3
- D128, page 128
- H ∈ {1, 2, 4} per rank
- `sparse_local_block` ≥ 1

Otherwise, `auto` keeps FMHA.
