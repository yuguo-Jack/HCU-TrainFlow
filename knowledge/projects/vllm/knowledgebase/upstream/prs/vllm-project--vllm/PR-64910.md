## Original PR description

## Overview

Update the GB300 AgentX presets of MiniMax-M3.1-Flash (`gb300_agentx_agg`, `gb300_agentx_pd`) to the Dynamo + vLLM lanes of [SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX#4631](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4631) (head `5d3aa21b`). Only touches `recipes/models/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3.1-Flash.yaml`.

- **Frontend:** `DYN_TOKENIZER=fastokens` (Dynamo's fastokens tokenizer backend). Frontend tokenization of 100K+ token prompts drops from about 150 ms to about 1 ms per request, which comes straight off TTFT.
- **Mooncake pool:** 160 GB to 180 GB per rank. Run etcd, NATS, the Mooncake master and the frontend on a host without engine workers, so the trays' host DRAM goes to the pool.
- **Measured points:** 6 DEP2 prefill + DEP8 now runs up to 400 sessions, and DEP16 up to 360. The table, tuning rules and source revision are updated.

## Validation

- Measured in the lane's sweep ([run 41103476412](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/actions/runs/41103476412)): 6 prefill + DEP8 at 280/320/360/400 sessions: 93.1k/100.5k/107.9k/114.5k tok/s/GPU at 240/229/224/219 tok/s/user, TTFT p90 2.7-5.9 s; 6 prefill + DEP16 at 300/360: 75.0k/85.8k at 296/285, TTFT p90 1.8/2.4 s.
