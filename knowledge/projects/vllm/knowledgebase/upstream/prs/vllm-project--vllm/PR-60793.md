# [ROCm][Kimi-K3] Mono MoE as a vLLM kernel (vllm/models/kimi_k3/amd/mono)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60793) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

**Draft.** One kernel launch for a Kimi-K3 MoE layer at decode, routed experts and shared expert together, for steps
of up to 32 tokens on MI355X (gfx950). Opt-in with `VLLM_ROCM_MONO_DECODE=1`. The layout under
`vllm/models/kimi_k3/amd/mono/` follows [#60397](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60397) (DeepSeek-V4.1).

**Related:** [#60683](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60683) runs Kimi-K3's decoder layers as two
persistent launches, K1 (the KDA attention block) and K2 (o_proj through the latent MoE and its all-reduces), on
speculative-verify steps of at most 8 rows, and is faster there. At its current head it needs speculative decoding:
plain decode, and any step above 8 rows, takes the stock path. This PR is one launch for the MoE's experts, for plain
and speculative steps of up to 32 rows. The two are meant to stack: #60683 for speculative steps up to 8 rows, this
launch for everything else up to 32.

  DSpark k = 3 at 4–32 rows.

## What it does

- **The kernel:** one persistent launch per MoE layer: top-k, expert sort, a4w4 gemm1 and gemm2 for the routed
  experts, and the bf16 shared expert, which runs in CUs that routing leaves idle. It replaces seven kernels on
  two streams. The shared expert loops over 16-row blocks (`M_MAX = 32`, one compile per 16-token bucket).
  `mono/README.md` describes the stages, tickets, hand-offs and numerics.
- **vLLM side:** `mono_decode.py` (`MonoLatentMoE`) decides which layers and steps take the launch;
  `ROCmLatentMoERunner` only dispatches to it. A step takes it on gfx950, at M ≤ 32, with AITER's a4w4 SiTUv2 MoE
  (the default) and vLLM's bf16 KimiMLP shared expert. Anything else runs the stock path unchanged.
- **AITER:** nothing vendored. gemm1 is AITER's a4w4 stage-1 body, traced with device-scope output stores; gemm2 comes
  from AITER's `compile_gemm2_a4w4_port` hook. The JIT cache key includes a digest of the package and of the AITER
  sources it traces.
- **Tests:** `tests/models/kimi_k3/test_amd_mono_gate.py` (CPU, which calls take the launch) and
  `test_amd_mono_moe.py` (gfx950, against `biased_grouped_topk` + `fused_moe` + KimiMLP at TP8 shapes, eager and graph
  replay, M 1–32, M 33 refused). They run in the existing MI355 Kimi-K3 step and pass on MI355X at this
  head (27 passed).

Debug tooling (layer recording, replay benchmark, fault probe) is on a separate branch,
[amd-mghanimi/vllm#13](https://github.com/amd-mghanimi/vllm/pull/13). This PR carries no debug code.
