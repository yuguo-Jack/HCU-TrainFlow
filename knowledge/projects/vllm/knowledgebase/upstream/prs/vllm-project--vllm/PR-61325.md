## Overview

**Draft, stacked on #60833, which is stacked on #60683.** Until those merge, the compare view also shows their commits. This PR is the four commits after #60833's head `1301a7cfcf`, from `1e6de71f3a` on: 2,931 added and 6 removed lines in 12 files ([this PR's commits only](https://github.com/mjkvaak-amd/vllm-project/compare/rocm/qwen38-mono-decode...rocm/flashnext-mono-decode); Files changed shows 13.8k, #60683's and #60833's included). The code both Qwens share (`vllm/models/qwen3_5/amd/mono/{moe,gdn}.py`, `mono_base.py`, including the MTP verify-step path) is in #60833.

This PR ports the Qwen3.8 mono decode (#60833) to Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (`Qwen4ExpForConditionalGeneration`, `amd/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Quark-MXFP4`) at TP2 on MI355X (gfx950). It is opt-in behind the existing `VLLM_ROCM_MONO_DECODE=1`. On pure decode steps, and on MTP verify steps, of up to 8 rows, each decoder layer runs as two persistent launches instead of the stock chain of hyper-connection (HC) GEMVs, norms, GDN kernels, all-reduces and MoE kernels. It reuses #60683's mono framework (mailboxes, the peer buffer, the GEMV stage). The GDN stages, the fp4 GEMV and the whole MoE are #60833's, shared rather than copied: Flash-Next passes its dimensions (`GdnDims`, `MoeDims`) and two hooks, the b / a source and the norm's gate. Both models' host code shares one base, `QwenMonoDecode`, holding the refusals, the step gate, the buffers and the MTP verify steps. Flash-Next's own code is 2,233 lines.

Flash-Next differs from Qwen3.8 in ways the 2.4T kernels did not cover: a 4-stream hyper-connection around each attention and MLP block (low-rank 320 mix, gated inject), sigmoid output gating in the GDN norm, QSA attention with an indexer on every fourth layer, a PLE layer, and TP2 shapes (8 / 24 GDN key / value heads per rank, a 320-wide MoE intermediate padded to 384).

Not a duplicate: I searched open PRs for "mono decode", "Flash-Next" and "qwen4_exp". #60833 (Qwen3.8 2.4T), #60683 (Kimi-K3) and #60397 (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash) are the same technique for other models; none covers the hyper-connection or QSA layers.

## Claims

Measured on `nightly-2eb491e1` plus this branch, MI355X, TP2, stock vs mono in the same image:

- Mean TPOT drops 45% at 8k/1k concurrency 1 (6.34 → 3.46 ms), 29% at concurrency 8 (10.39 → 7.38 ms), and 17–25% at 60k/600; output throughput rises 8–73%. TTFT is unchanged (prefill runs stock).
- A decode step at 8k/64, concurrency 4, takes 4.86 ms instead of 8.59 ms of GPU time, with 270 kernels instead of 1,398 (torch-profiler trace); the host gap between steps is unchanged (0.22 ms).
- GSM8K (model card recipe, two paired runs) 0.966 / 0.966 → 0.963 / 0.967, inside the stock spread on this nightly (0.962–0.966 over four stock runs). NIAH 64k 0.722 → 0.722, 128k 0.248 → 0.286 (paired p = 0.18).
- With MTP-3, the verify steps on the kernels take mean TPOT from 3.04 to 2.16 ms at 8k/1k concurrency 1 (−29%), from 4.39 to 3.47 ms at concurrency 2 (−21%) and from 2.56 to 2.02 ms at 60k/600 (−21%). At concurrency 4 a verify step has 16 rows, past the 8-row cap, so it stays on the stock path. GSM8K with every verify step on the kernels (2 threads) 0.962 stock, 0.970 and 0.960 in two mono runs; at 8 threads (mostly stock steps) 0.965 → 0.967.
- The kernels match vLLM's ops on random weights in the checkpoint's formats, and a mixed-width FULL graph of both layer kinds replays bit-identically 300 times (see Unit tests).

The Qwen3.8 tests (`tests/models/qwen3_5/test_amd_mono.py`) run in the same CI step and pass on this branch, since the MoE and GDN stages are shared.

- `test_amd_mono_decode.py` (host only):
  - every refusal reason at once, and each parallelism refusal;
  - MTP: accepted with `num_speculative_tokens` + 1 ≤ 8, refused past that or for another method;
  - decode steps of 1–8 rows take the kernels, and so do MTP verify steps;
  - the same steps in a piecewise cudagraph, mixed or prefill steps, wider steps and steps without attention metadata do not.
- `test_amd_mono_layout.py` (host only): every fp4 byte and e8m0 scale of a routed expert (w13 and w2, with the intermediate padded from 320 to 384 and AITER's scale columns padded to a multiple of 8), and of the online-MXFP4 linears, is at the address K1 / K2 read.
- `test_amd_mono.py::test_hc_front_matches_vllm_ops` (1 GPU): the HC front (grouped Gemma norm, the down + inject GEMM, `hc_silu`, the up GEMM, the gate-mix) against `GatedResidual.mix`, at widths 1, 3 and 8, from K2's sums of squares or its own.
- `test_amd_mono.py::test_k1_matches_vllm_ops` (1 GPU): K1 against the HC mix → online-MXFP4 `in_proj_qkvz` / `in_proj_ba` → AITER's conv update and fused gated delta rule → `RMSNormGated` (sigmoid gate), with pad rows. It checks the conv state, SSM state and core output, that pad rows output zero, and that the null block is left untouched.
- `test_amd_mono.py::test_k2_tp2_matches_vllm_ops` (2 GPUs): K2 against `out_proj` (MXFP4) / `o_proj` with the attention output gate + all-reduce + `hc_combine`, the MLP HC mix, softmax top-10 renormalized routing, the MXFP4 experts with the fused shared expert, the MLP HC combine and the next layer's sums of squares, at widths 1, 3, 4 and 8 (both epoch parities). The MoE matches a torch emulation of the stock decode kernel's numerics (cosine > 0.9999), the same emulation as #60833's test. Against AITER's `fused_moe` itself the two differ by MXFP4 rounding (cosine about 0.999, `fused_moe` and the emulation alike), so the test requires K2's error against an unquantized fp32 MoE to be within 2% of `fused_moe`'s. Both ranks must agree bitwise.
- `test_amd_mono.py::test_mixed_width_graph_replay` (2 GPUs): the race stress, as in #60833. A GDN layer (K1, K2 with the MXFP4 `out_proj`) and a QSA layer (the HC front, K2 with the gated `o_proj`) run at six consecutive decode steps of widths 8, 1, 3, 8, 2 and 5 with pad rows. They are captured in one HIP graph, as a FULL decode graph holds them, and replayed 300 times. Every replay must be bit-identical to the eager run on both ranks. The steps share the peer buffer and the epoch, so a scratch race between widths shows up as a mismatch.

Both kernels compile for widths 1–8 at gfx950 with no VGPR spills. K1 uses up to 55 KB of LDS and K2 up to 68 KB.

**Serving smoke test.** `vllm serve amd/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Quark-MXFP4 -tp 2` with the flags below and `VLLM_ROCM_MONO_DECODE=1`: the first decode steps of 8, 4, 2 and 1 rows took the mono path, and requests returned. The gate now leaves piecewise steps to vLLM; with greedy decoding, short prompts give the same completions as stock again. ## Details

**Path.** The step is chosen as a whole in the uncompiled outer forward (`Qwen4ExpForConditionalGeneration.forward`, and `Qwen4ExpForCausalLM.forward` for the text-only class). Eligible steps have no prefill, 1–8 rows, no intermediate tensors, and are not a piecewise cudagraph step (those graphs also serve short prefills, so they must be captured from the stock model). With MTP, the eligible steps are verify steps of up to 8 rows, i.e. up to 2 requests at `num_speculative_tokens` 3. They run the mono forward on the materialized 4-stream state; everything else runs the stock compiled model unchanged. The FULL decode graph captures the mono forward once per width.

Per decoder layer (48: 36 GDN, 12 QSA):

- **K1, GDN layers** (`gdn_pre`): the attention HC's grouped norm and mix (down + inject GEMV, `hc_silu`, up GEMV, gate-mix) → online-MXFP4 `in_proj_qkvz` and bf16 `in_proj_ba` → causal conv1d update → gated delta rule → sigmoid-gated `RMSNormGated`, writing `out_proj`'s input. No cross-rank traffic.
- **K1, QSA layers** (`hc_front`): the attention HC's norm and mix alone; vLLM's QSA attention (fused prepare, indexer, attention) then runs up to `o_proj`'s input and the output gate.
- **K2, every layer** (`layer_post`): `out_proj` (MXFP4) / `o_proj` (bf16, with the sigmoid output gate) + its TP all-reduce + the attention HC combine → the MLP HC's norm and mix → softmax top-10 renormalized router → MXFP4 routed experts with the shared expert fused as expert 512 → MoE all-reduce → the MLP HC combine, writing the next state and the next front's sums of squares.
- The PLE layer (decoder layer 1) and the final HC mixer run vLLM's modules on the materialized state.
- **MTP verify steps:** a request's rows are consecutive tokens of one sequence, so the GDN conv and recurrence need vLLM's spec-decode state handling. GDN layers run K1's HC front (`hc_front`), then vLLM's GDN core (`qwen_gdn_attention_core` with the step's spec metadata) and gated norm; QSA layers run as above. K2 runs unchanged, so the HC GEMVs, the all-reduces and the MoE stay on the kernels. The drafter's input, the final hidden states before the HC mixer, is copied to the buffer the stock model fills.

**Design decisions.**
- **Materialized HC combine:** stock defers each HC combine into the next mix; K2 materializes it with the same bf16 rounding, so the PLE layer, the QSA path and the final mixer read a plain state.
- **Numerics contract** as in #60833: K1 reproduces AITER's decode conv and gated delta rule; the routed experts run x and silu(g)·u through dynamic MXFP4 like AITER's decode MoE. The MoE intermediate is padded from 320 to 384 as vLLM loads it (zero codes); K2 reads only the real 320.
- **Buffers:** per-width scratch for K1 and K2; a width-independent peer buffer; mailbox tags carry a device epoch bumped once per step.
- **Refusals, as in #60397:** an explicit `VLLM_ROCM_MONO_DECODE=1` that cannot run raises at init, listing every reason (CDNA4, TP2, no PP, no expert / data / context parallelism or EPLB, no LoRA, spec decode only as MTP with `num_speculative_tokens` + 1 ≤ 8 and the AITER GDN path, no KV connector, the fused shared expert on, at least 256 CUs). The checks are `QwenMonoDecode.refusals`, shared with Qwen3.8. A weight layout the kernels cannot read raises at the first decode step. A model variant outside the kernels (other shapes, GDN or HC variants, attention without the QSA indexer or the output gate) stays on the stock path, logged once.

**Shared code.** `qwen4_exp/amd/model.py` gains 23 lines: the import, the constructor hook and the forward dispatch in both model classes. With the env var unset, or off ROCm, `_mono` is `None` and nothing else changes.

Flash-Next uses #60833's shared Qwen mono code instead of copies of it:
- the MoE (route, gate | up, down, the fp4 GEMV, x through MXFP4) from `mono/moe.py`, sized by `MoeDims`;
- the GDN stages from `mono/gdn.py`, parameterized by `GdnDims`, the b / a source and the norm's gate;
- the K-split GEMV reduction (for the HC down GEMV) and the `in_proj_ba` tasks;
- the host side from `mono_base.py` (`QwenMonoDecode`: refusals, the step gate, buffers, the MTP verify steps).

A CPU-only ISA dump of all 160 kernel variants of both models (widths 1–8, every flag) checked each move. Every Flash-Next variant is byte-identical to before, except K2 at 4–8 rows (the same instructions, reordered). Qwen3.8's K2 is identical. Its K1 with the split `in_proj_ba` gains 10–24 instructions (of 3.4k–9.4k) for the b / a rows' addresses, with the same registers and spills.

**Limitations and TODOs.**
- **Scope:** TP2 only (`Layout.TPS`). The dims records carry the per-rank shapes, so another TP size is a matter of the layout's divisibility checks, but only TP2 has been run. TODO: TP1 (stock TP1 has open issues), TP4 and TP8.
- **MTP:** only verify steps of up to 8 rows run on the kernels: 2 requests at `num_speculative_tokens` 3. Larger batches verify on the stock path. The GDN core of a verify step is vLLM's. Serve with `max_num_batched_tokens` 8192, as stock MTP needs on Qwen3.8-2.4T. Other spec-decode methods are refused.
- **TODO:** K1's GDN variants spill 6–63 SGPRs, to VGPR lanes, at 5–8 rows (`s_movk` / `v_writelane` pairs, no scratch memory). No variant of either kernel spills VGPRs.
- **TODO: fail-stop.** A mailbox wait on a peer that never posts spins until the engine's timeout. #60683 has since gained RFC #60904's MonoKernel framework, at `vllm/models/common/mono` (`a717180301`). It brings bounded in-kernel waits with per-CTA error words, a runner-side check that fails the step before its tokens are returned, `--monokernel` in place of `VLLM_ROCM_MONO_DECODE`, and `MonoSpec` declarations. This stack is on #60683's earlier head. Porting both Qwens onto it means two changes: the kernels take its health / alarm words through the shared `Mailbox`, and the host side becomes a `MonoSpec`. The per-layer hook returns what vLLM's layer does, so Flash-Next's materialized HC combine has to move to a layer-boundary contract.
- **CI:** Flash-Next's tests run in the Qwen3.8 mono step (`models_basic.yaml`: 8x MI355X, 40 minutes), next to the shared code's other user. The numerics tests need gfx950 and 1–2 GPUs. The gate and layout tests are host only.
- **Size:** 2,233 lines under `vllm/models/qwen4_exp/amd/`. That is 1,704 of kernels (K1, K2, the HC stages), 390 of host code and 139 of layout and sources. Tests add 671 lines. Before the sharing, Flash-Next's own code was 3,186 lines.
