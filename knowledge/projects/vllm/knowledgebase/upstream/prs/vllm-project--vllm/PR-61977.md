## Overview
Document the aggregation semantics of NIXL KV connector metrics across TP ranks.
Related to #41230.


## Claims
- Clarify that NIXL metrics are computed from pooled rank-level transfer observations.
- Explain that average payload size, means, and P90s use the combined observation pool.
- Document throughput as total transfer volume divided by summed transfer durations.
- Describe the logging pipeline and clarify that `observe()` receives statistics already aggregated across workers.
