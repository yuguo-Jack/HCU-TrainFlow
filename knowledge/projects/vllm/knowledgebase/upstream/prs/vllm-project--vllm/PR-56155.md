## Purpose

This is the first change in the layer-wise mixed KV-cache stack. It preserves a
layer's logical KV-cache encoding independently of its physical storage dtype
and allocation layout.

This PR:

- adds `AttentionSpec.cache_dtype`;
- populates it from `Attention.get_kv_cache_spec()`;
- adds the generic `QuantizationConfig.get_kv_cache_dtype(prefix)` hook;
- preserves `cache_dtype` and `kv_quant_mode` through full-attention, MLA,
  sliding-window MLA, sink, and recurrent-sliding-window merges; and
- makes FlashInfer resolve KV dtype as explicit global override, then per-spec
  dtype, and reject an ambiguous quantized spec.

This PR does not parse ModelOpt layer-wise metadata or enable heterogeneous KV
allocation. It preserves metadata during merges and adds validation for ambiguous
quantized specs; existing explicitly configured global BF16, FP8, and NVFP4
paths retain their dtype selection.

This change is the first prerequisite for #56116.

This contribution was developed with AI assistance. The human submitter
reviewed the complete diff and remains responsible for the implementation and
validation.
