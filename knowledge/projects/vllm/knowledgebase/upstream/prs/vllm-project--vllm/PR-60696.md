# [Frontend] structured decisions: noul and score question types

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60696) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

Lands on top of #59299, adding two more question types.

Note: this is landing in parallel with diffusiongemma support (#60466) and a rough merge branch is available here: https://github.com/mmastrac/vllm/tree/systemone-question-types-dgemma

## Overview

Adds the two question types the /v1/systemone contract defines beyond choice, following the Jev API shapes:

- `noul`: yes or no. `criteria` is null, or an object with optional `true` and `false` descriptions. Any other key is a 400. The labels are `yes` and `no`. The answer carries the probability of yes.
- `score`: rate the state on an ordered list of 2 to 10 levels. The labels are `0` to `9`, the same index the answer uses. The answer is the expected level, a legend of level names, and probabilities by level index.

Each question type declares its labels with `label_set`. The read strategy finds the token for every registered type's labels at startup, and fails there if one is not a single distinct token after the chat prompt.

## Claims

JevBench public items (231: 139 choice, 74 noul, 18 score), one read per item, thinking off. "Mapped" sends noul and score items as `choice` questions over the task's labels, which is all main supports. "Native" sends them as `noul` and `score`. Choice items read the same in both runs.

Smaller Qwen3 models are included solely for comparison purposes - these models would not be recommended for general use. Their solve rates do appear worse, but the models are noisy and no sampling was performed. 

| model | run | Intelligence | noul | score | label_mass (noul / score) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Qwen3-0.6B | mapped | 26.1 | 43/74 | 7/18 | 0.98 / 0.89 |
| Qwen3-0.6B | native | 21.4 | 36/74 | 6/18 | 0.98 / 1.00 |
| Qwen3-4B | mapped | 52.7 | 48/74 | 11/18 | 0.93 / 0.84 |
| Qwen3-4B | native | 47.3 | 43/74 | 8/18 | 0.99 / 0.99 |
| DiffusionGemma 26B | mapped | 79.0 | 58/74 | 16/18 | 0.97 / 0.91 |
| DiffusionGemma 26B | native | 80.6 | 62/74 | 13/18 | 0.96 / 0.92 |

- **Noul**: option order explains the Qwen gap. JevBench lists "no" first, and the noul prompt lists "yes" first. On Qwen3-4B the same items as a `choice` score 48/74 with "no" first and 42/74 with "yes" first, against 43/74 native. On DiffusionGemma, native noul beats both orders (60/74 and 58/74).
- **Score**: native trails by 1 to 3 of 18 items on every model. The digit-labeled reads favor level 2 (11 of 18 items on Qwen3-4B, 6 expected). 18 items are too few to settle it, so the labels stay `0` to `9` to match the answer and #60676.
- DiffusionGemma reads are not exactly repeatable: choice items scored 121 and 122 on two runs of unchanged requests.

## Validation

On a GB10 (DGX Spark), `vllm/vllm-openai:nightly` at 92044241a with this branch's files mounted over it:

- **Tests**: `pytest tests/entrypoints/generate/structured_decisions tests/entrypoints/generate/generative_scoring`, 51 passed (Qwen3-0.6B end to end, plus the unit tests). The unit tests also pass on the DiffusionGemma merge branch (23 passed).
- **JevBench**: the numbers above. The client is `jevbench_systemone.py` from #59299 with a `--native` flag that sends noul and score items as their own types. DiffusionGemma is NVFP4 with canvas 64, on the merge branch.

## Details

- A noul side with no description is left out of the prompt.
- A score has at most 10 levels, so that every label is one digit.
- Not covered in CI: DiffusionGemma, to keep a 26B checkpoint out of CI.
