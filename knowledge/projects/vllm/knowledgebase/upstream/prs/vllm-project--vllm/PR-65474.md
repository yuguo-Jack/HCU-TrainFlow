# [ROCm][Perf][Qwen4Exp] Tune QSA sparse GQA and indexer on gfx942 and gfx950

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65474) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

Stacked on #62590 and #62589 . It tunes the two Triton QSA kernels on gfx942 and gfx950:

1. Sparse GQA: pad the head group to 16 rows and use one BF16 launch profile for both archs.
2. Indexer: score only up to the batch's longest sequence, not the whole padded page table.
3. Indexer: use #62589's one-row decode kernel on gfx950 too, with its own tile.

Not a duplicate: searched open PRs for "qsa_sparse_paged_attention", "qsa_select_paged_tokens", "QSA indexer"; #57969 pads `BLOCK_M` for gfx11 only (same line, will conflict textually).

## Claims

- Sparse GQA, against #62590: geomean 1.48x prefill / 1.57x decode on gfx942, 1.24x / 1.45x on gfx950, never slower by more than 1% (104 cells, TP1-TP8).
- Indexer prefill, against #62589: 1.3-6.6x at 8K-60K context, about 10x for 1K-8K prompts.
- Indexer decode on gfx950: 2.2x at 32 rows, 1.2x at 128 rows; gfx942 unchanged.
- Flash-Next FP8 TP2 on MI300X, 3 repeats: against main from 8K input up, TTFT 0.51-0.57x and output throughput 1.13-1.76x. Against #62590 + #62589, TTFT 2.5-6% lower and output throughput 1.6-3.1% higher from 8K/c16 up. 8K/c4 is flat. At 1K/c64, TTFT is 1.27x and throughput 0.98x (TODO).
- GSM8K unchanged against both. TODO: MTP acceptance length.

## Details

- **Sparse GQA:** with BF16 K/V, `tl.dot` with fewer than 16 rows is slow on gfx942 and gfx950, so the TP4/TP8 head groups are padded to 16. From 32 programs up, both archs use `BLOCK_N=16` with enough splits for about 2,048 programs and one warp, or 2 warps at 32-128 programs. On gfx950, 32-128 programs use about 1,024 programs with 4 warps. FP8 K/V is unchanged.
- **Indexer bound:** the logits are `cdiv(max_seq_len, compress_ratio)` columns wide instead of the full table width (65,856 at a 262K `max_model_len`). This is the same bound the NVIDIA indexer uses. Graph capture passes `max_seq_len = max_model_len`, so captured decode is unchanged. Exact ties at the top-k boundary can break differently.
- **One-row decode:** gfx950 uses (128 rows, `BLOCK_N=128`, 2 warps), and gfx942 keeps (32, 32, 4).

Limitations and CI gaps: TODO: MI355X e2e and tests. TP2 e2e only; the kernel sweeps cover TP1-TP8. FP8 K/V profiles were not swept.
