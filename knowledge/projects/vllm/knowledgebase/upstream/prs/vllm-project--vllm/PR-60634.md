## Overview

While profiling, I ran into an instance of freakishly long `ncclDevKernel_AllGather_RING_LL(ncclDevKernelArgsStorage<4096ul>)` and `ncclDevKernel_ReduceScatter_Sum_bf16_RING_LL(ncclDevKernelArgsStorage<4096ul>)` seemingly caused by or causing cudagraph breakage.

Reproduction steps:

