# [ROCm][Perf] Muse Spark: column-shard the MXFP4 MoE down projection at TP prefill

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63263) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description



## Overview

Muse Spark MXFP4 on ROCm at TP > 1: for eager prefill batches of >= 1024 tokens, compute the MoE down projection column-sharded from a second, row-resharded copy of `w2`, so the per-route `[T, top_k, H]` reduce-scatter before the post-expert norm is replaced by much smaller gathers.

## Claims

- **Prefill latency**, TP8 + DSpark on 8×MI355X, single request: TTFT −12% at 2k tokens, −25% at 8k, −26% at 16k, and −26% for a burst of 4 × 8k.
- **Serving**, random 8k in / 1k out: output throughput +13% / +15% at concurrency 32 and +17% / +14% at 16, mean TTFT −24% to −25% at 16 and 32, mean TPOT −12% / −13% at 32 (two independent A/B sessions).
- **Memory cost: +15.9 GiB of weights per GPU (TP8), so the KV cache shrinks by 10%** (6.35M -> 5.72M tokens with `--gpu-memory-utilization 0.90`). The startup log prints the cost. **`VLLM_ROCM_MUSE_MOE_COLUMN_SHARDED_DOWN=0` turns it off** and gives the memory back to the KV cache. It is also off automatically under EP, at TP1, and for the BF16 layers.
- **Accuracy**: GSM8K unchanged (paired exact McNemar p = 0.34). Batches below 1024 tokens are bit-identical; larger prefills change at BF16 rounding level (see Details).
- No change to core files: everything is in `vllm/models/muse_spark/amd/`, plus one env var in `vllm/envs.py`.

## Details

**Why.** At TP > 1 without EP, each rank holds `w2[E, H, I/TP]`, so its down projection gives a partial sum of every route over all `H` columns. The per-route post-expert RMSNorm needs the full sums, so `MuseSparkTopKWeightAndReduce` reduce-scatters `[T, top_k, H]` before normalizing. At T = 8192 that is 512 MiB in BF16 per layer, the largest collective in a prefill step.

**How.**

- *Weights, at load.* Before the AITER shuffle, one all-to-all turns this rank's `w2[E, H, I/TP]` and its scales into `w2[E, H/TP, I]` (its output rows over the full intermediate dim). The result is shuffled the same way AITER shuffles the regular `w2`, which is kept for decode and small batches.
- *Experts, T >= 1024, eager.* AITER's `_fused_moe_impl` runs as usual, with its `_stage2_override` hook. Stage 1 is unchanged. In the hook, the MXFP4 stage-1 output and its E8M0 scales are all-gathered along the intermediate dim. Each rank's slice is whole 32-element quant groups, so the gathered tensors equal the full-width quantization exactly. The same stage-2 kernel then runs on the resharded `w2` and gives the exact routed outputs `[T * top_k, H/TP]` for this rank's columns.
- *Finalize.* An all-reduce of the per-route sum of squares (`[T, top_k]` fp32, 256 KiB at T = 8192), two Triton kernels for the norm and the top-k weighted sum on the local columns, and an all-gather of `[T, H]`, the same size as the existing all-gather. Per layer at T = 8192, a 512 MiB reduce-scatter becomes about 136 MiB of all-gathers plus 256 KiB of all-reduce. Stage-2 FLOPs and weight bytes are unchanged.
- *Fallback.* Only the FlyDSL stage-2 kernels, which AITER selects for these shapes, are covered. If AITER picks another stage 2 or a one-stage config, the experts run unchanged and the batch takes the existing reduce-scatter. The choice depends only on shapes and AITER's tuning tables, so all ranks agree.
- *Scope.* The path is off at TP1, under EP, for non-SiLU, bias or padded experts, for `H` or `I/TP` not 256-aligned, for AITER without `_stage2_override`, for the BF16 (Triton) layers, and during graph capture. The recipe captures <= 512 tokens, so graphs never reach the threshold. The DSpark draft has no MoE.

**Memory.** The cost is the second `w2`: per MXFP4 layer and rank, 256 MiB of FP4 plus 16 MiB of scales, 15.9 GiB over 60 layers at TP8. There is no extra workspace: the routed outputs reuse 1/TP of the modular kernel's output buffer, and the gathered stage-2 inputs are transient and smaller than the reduce-scatter buffers they replace. A deployment that values KV capacity over prefill latency can set `VLLM_ROCM_MUSE_MOE_COLUMN_SHARDED_DOWN=0`. Keeping only the resharded copy would avoid the cost, but decode runs in CUDA graphs with AITER kernels the hook does not cover, and at small T one all-reduce is cheaper than three collectives. That is left for a follow-up.

**Numerics.** Both paths compute the same products from the same quantized inputs. The baseline rounds each rank's partial sum to BF16 and then reduces the partials in BF16 (`VLLM_ROCM_MUSE_MOE_REDUCE_DTYPE=bf16` in the recipe). This path accumulates the full intermediate dim in fp32 and rounds once. The unit test bounds the difference elementwise by `2^-8 · (Σ|partial| + |sum|)` with a 2× margin. On TP8 the worst element reaches 0.78–0.86 of the bound, which explains the first-token logprob differences above.

**Complexity.** The PR adds 656 non-test lines (High complexity per the checklist). The breakdown:

- `column_sharded_moe.py` (305): the load-time reshard, the stage-2 hook with the activation and scale gathers, the support check, and `all_gather_columns`.
- `ops/column_sharded_finalize.py` (199): the two Triton kernels.
- `muse_moe_runner.py` (+142): the wiring.
- `envs.py` (+10): the switch.

Most of the work reuses existing code: AITER's stage-1 and stage-2 kernels and tuning tables through the existing `_stage2_override` hook (no new GEMM), AITER's custom all-gather, the experts-to-finalize handoff, and the fused norm parameters. Two pieces looked replaceable but were measured and kept:

- `all_gather_columns` (AITER custom all-gather in row chunks) takes 0.49 ms per layer for the three column gathers at T = 8192 on TP8. `tensor_model_parallel_all_gather(dim=-1)` takes 0.74 ms, so switching would cost 15 ms per 8k prefill.
- The two Triton kernels fuse the norm, the weighting and the top-k sum on the local columns. A PyTorch version would make several extra passes over `[T, top_k, H/TP]` per layer, an estimated ~0.4 ms per layer.

The return is −25% TTFT on 8k–16k prompts and +13–15% throughput at concurrency 32, from the largest prefill collective in the model.
