# [Doc] Add 2x RTX 3090 (Ampere, sm86) AWQ INT4 recipe for Qwen3.8-27B

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65612) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

Adds a 2× RTX 3090 (Ampere, sm86) deployment to the Qwen3.8-27B recipe: a new
`int4_awq` variant (AWQ INT4, `cyankiwi/Qwen3.8-27B-AWQ-INT4`) served at TP2, plus a
`verified` `rtx_3090_2x` hardware entry and the Ampere section of the guide.

Depends on vllm-project/recipes#971, which adds the `rtx_3090_2x` taxonomy profile.
Until that merges, the recipe check cannot resolve the new hardware key (draft until then).

## Claims

- `int4_awq` is distinct from the existing `int4` variant: Red Hat's W4A16
  compressed-tensors build fits one Blackwell/Hopper card, while the AWQ build is the one
  validated end to end on two 24 GB sm86 cards. The recipe keeps both.
- Verified on vLLM 0.29.0 (`vllm/vllm-openai:latest`), 2× RTX 3090, TP2, 262144 context,
  FP8 KV pinned at 6.0 GiB, GDN state in fp16, MTP off: text decode **67.0 / 138.6 /
  236.8 / 329.6 tok/s** at C1 / C2 / C4 / C6. Single-video prompts (60 s sub-chunks,
  ~48k vision tokens) served stably.
- `--mm-encoder-tp-mode weights` is added as an `rtx_3090_2x` override on the
  `encoder_parallel` feature: the default `data` mode dumps one video's activations on a
  single card (~6.6 GiB) and OOMs a 24 GB GPU; weight sharding balances ~2.9 GiB per card.

## Validation

Hardware prerequisites are described in the guide rather than encoded as flags, because
they are host-level and cannot be set by `vllm serve`:

1. **PCIe BAR1 P2P** — 3090 has no NVLink, and consumer cards lack MAILBOXP2P, so P2P
   needs the BAR1 driver patch + ReBAR VBIOS; `nvidia-smi topo -p2p r` returns `OK`.
   Without it TP all-reduce falls back to host SHM and TP is slower than PP.
2. **GPU0 power lock at 350 W** — the factory 390 W OC cap trips
   `hw_power_brake_slowdown` and costs ~20% throughput on a TP2 run.
3. **Explicit `--kv-cache-memory-bytes 6442450944`** — leaving KV to
   `--gpu-memory-utilization` auto-alloc leaves ~200 MiB on GPU0 and OOMs on large-video
   prefill; the cap also raised the KV pool from 351,637 to 385,367 tokens with MTP off.

`int4_awq` is intentionally absent from `spec_decoding.modes.mtp.variants`: MTP was never
qualified on this build, so the builder should not offer it.

## Details

- Variant sizing follows the recipe's real-bytes rule: 21.0 GB of weights × 1.2 → 26 GB,
  which is why one 24 GB card is not enough and TP2 is the target.
- `supported_hardware: [rtx_3090_2x]` — eligibility, not a claim about other Ampere cards.
- `meta.date_updated` bumped; guide gains a "2x RTX 3090" section and the checkpoint link.
- No change to any existing variant, argument, or hardware entry.
