# [Model][Perf] Fuse Beam's TP all-reduce with its sandwich norms and MoE finalize

## Overview

Fuse Beam's TP all-reduce with its sandwich norms and, for TRTLLM-Gen NvFP4 MoE layers, the top-k finalize. Stacked on #62074 (base branch `woosuk/beam-model`), and rebased onto its released-schema cleanup; this was split out of that PR so the model can land without a runtime-JIT CUDA source.

## Claims

- `vllm/models/beam/nvidia/allreduce.{py,cu}`: MNNVL one-shot/two-shot all-reduce built on FlashInfer's Lamport protocol and workspace, with Beam's FP32 norm-residual-norm epilogue and an optional MoE finalize before the reduction (keeping the local BF16 rounding). Large batches fall back to FlashInfer's plain all-reduce plus the Triton boundary kernel.
- `FusedMoEConfig.defer_moe_finalize(allow_ep=True)` lets a consumer that reduces across a replicated-token EP group take the TRTLLM-Gen top-k finalize under TP=1, EP>1. Without it, behavior is unchanged.

## Details

- **Packaging (open question):** `allreduce.cu` is JIT-compiled at runtime with FlashInfer's JIT and shipped through `package_data`. The module name hashes the installed FlashInfer header, because the kernel speaks FlashInfer's Lamport protocol on a workspace allocated by `flashinfer-python`. Building it ahead of time against fetched headers could silently mismatch a different installed FlashInfer. The alternative is to vendor the FlashInfer device helpers into `csrc/` and allocate the multicast workspace in vLLM (as `custom_all_gather_reduce_scatter.cu` already does), keeping the same device code.
- **Not a duplicate:** no other open PR touches Beam's all-reduce.
