# [Perf][DSv4.1] Gather the compressed KV once per KV-source group in prefill

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/59131) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Purpose

DeepSeek-V4.1 keeps compressed KV caches only on its kv-source layers (2, 8, 14 and 20 on V4.1-Flash); the other 34 compressed layers read their source's cache. The mega-attention prefill path still dequantized and gathered the whole compressed history in every compressed layer: 38 gathers per step for 4 distinct caches.

This PR gathers each source group's compressed history once per chunk:

- The gathered chunk sits in a retained span at the tail of the shared workspace (new `WorkspaceManager.get_retained`). Scratch requests come from the head, so they leave it alone.
- The span is keyed by source and chunk. A consumer reuses it when `get_retained` reports it intact under that key, and gathers again otherwise. Source layers always gather.
- The sparse indexer now requests its batch's K gather rows instead of the profiled bound. Before, it claimed the whole workspace and broke the span.
- No extra memory: the KV cache is unchanged (66.3 GiB on TP4).
