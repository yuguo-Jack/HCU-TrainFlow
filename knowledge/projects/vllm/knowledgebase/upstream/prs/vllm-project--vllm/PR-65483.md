# [Perf][MiniMax-M3.1-Flash] Specialized decode on B200 and B300

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65483) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description



## Overview

Specialized decode for MiniMax-M3.1-Flash on B200 and B300 (RFC #60904). It's off by default; `--additional-config '{"decode_backend": "latency_specialized"}'` turns it on.

I'm building the whole decode step in this PR. What's here now is the FFN half at 1-8 rows: persistent CuTe DSL kernels chained with PDL, instead of the stock chain of 11 launches. The attention, the all-reduce and the `plan()` executor come next, on this branch. Prefill and larger batches stay stock. All numbers below are for the FFN half.

## Claims

- FFN half per layer, one GPU, CUDA graph replay, real weights and real router inputs: 1.62x geomean on B200 and 1.63x on B300 over 1-8 rows (B300: 30.4 -> 17.4 us at 1 row, 39.4 -> 26.1 us at 8 rows).
- Real model, `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3.1-Flash`, 4x B200, TP=4: TPOT -21% at C1 (4.97 -> 3.91 ms), -21% at C2, -23% at C4, -19% at C8.
- No measurable accuracy change on GSM8K, GPQA-Diamond or AIME 2024 at concurrency 8.
- With the setting off, none of the new kernels are built or run.

## Validation

Component, one GPU. 12 layers in a CUDA graph with layer 3's real weights cut into the four TP4 ranks' slices, 48 windows of real consecutive MoE input rows, 7 trials of 200 replays. The stock side is the same layer on `forward_unfinalized`, autotuned, sharing the weights.

| rows | B200 stock -> FFN us | B200 speedup | B300 stock -> FFN us | B300 speedup | byte bound us (B300) |
|---:|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 31.2 -> 18.1 | 1.73x | 30.4 -> 17.4 | 1.75x | 6.5 |
| 2 | 33.3 -> 19.3 | 1.73x | 31.9 -> 18.6 | 1.72x | |
| 4 | 36.2 -> 22.4 | 1.61x | 35.4 -> 21.8 | 1.63x | |
| 8 | 40.5 -> 26.7 | 1.52x | 39.4 -> 26.1 | 1.51x | 15.3 |

The bound is weight bytes over about 7.65 TB/s of HBM. Noise between trials is 1.5%. Run on this head.

Numerics: each case also runs real, scattered, hot, distinct-expert, production-like and extreme inputs (zero rows, 1e-30, x64, spikes), replayed from the CUDA graph with new values and checked against a float64 reference of the stock chain, including its rounding, with per-element, projection and per-tile bounds calibrated on stock. The FFN stays inside them, and its max error is within 0.03% of stock's in all 192 checks. Outputs aren't bitwise equal to stock, because the shared FC1 sums over K in a different order.

Full model A/B. 4x B200, TP=4, official checkpoint, one container, server starts on / off / on / off, the two sides differ only in the setting. It ran on this branch one commit back (the last commit only adds a comment and CI paths), on the nightly it is merged with:

## Details

Why launches: at 1 row stock runs 11 kernels per layer, half of them near the 4 us floor of a separate launch, and DRAM stays under 25% of peak (ncu, B300). At 8 consecutive rows only 12.5 experts are touched, so bytes aren't the limit either.

What runs now, 9 kernels:
- Main stream: the stock gate; a front (top-4, routing weights, per-token NVFP4 of the input, routing record); routed FC1 with SwiGLU-OAI in the epilogue; FC2's per-token input quantization; routed FC2.
- Aux stream, as stock: MXFP8 input; shared FC1 split over K into 144 work items (stock has 12 CTAs at 9% warps active); activation + MXFP8; shared FC2.
- The `MoEOutput` goes to the existing fused boundary kernel unchanged.

Under ncu, which runs kernels one at a time with a cold L2, the serial sum only moves 69.6 -> 59.4 us at 1 row, and the GEMMs read at the same 25% of peak as stock (65% at 8 uniform rows). So most of the gain is overlap and launch count, which only shows in graph replay. Routed FC2 on 4 pipeline stages instead of 8 gives 6% at 1 row with bitwise equal output, and I don't have the mechanism for that one.

Where it runs: FULL CUDA graph captures of pure decode steps of 1-8 rows, on the MoE layers that defer their finalize (56 of 57; the last one stays stock). Eager forwards, PIECEWISE captures, prefill and larger batches run the stock code. At startup, `decode_backend=latency_specialized` raises with every missing requirement: SM100 family, MRV2, M3.1-Flash's shapes at TP=4, FULL decode graphs, TP only (no PP, DP, EP or context parallelism), no torch.compile, sleep mode, LoRA or batch invariance, and the fused boundary with NVLink multicast. `specialized_decode/AGENTS.md` has the scope and paths, like #61868, and that's the only doc for now. I'd add the M3.1 recipe entry once the setting has been reviewed, tell me if you'd rather have it in this PR.

Reuse: the routing record and persistent GEMM skeleton are imported from `deepseek_v41/nvidia/specialized_decode/moe/`, and the CI step's dependencies now list that directory. Size: 1,297 lines of kernels, 588 of host code, 125 of config, 44 in `model.py`, 290 of tests.

Not measured: C16 and up (stock path), DSpark, the full model on B300, and off against main (off adds one attribute check per `forward_unfinalized`).

#65433 and #65434 extend the boundary kernel past 32 tokens and don't touch the FFN half; whichever lands second has a small merge in `_init_fused_moe_finalize`. No open PR adds a specialized decode for M3.1 on NVIDIA. #62712 and #63398 are the MI355X ones.
