# [Perf][glm5next] Add SM121 TP=2 low-latency decode GEMM plans for GLM-5.3-Flash

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/62949) · state: closed · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

GLM-5.3-Flash decode TPOT **-12% at concurrency 1 on 2x DGX Spark (SM121, TP=2)**. On `nvidia/GLM-5.3-Flash-NVFP4` the attention projections (KDA and MLA), shared experts and LM head stay BF16 and take roughly 46 of a 68 ms decode step on cuBLAS gemv. `glm5next` has no low-latency GEMM module yet, so this adds one modelled on `qwen4_exp/nvidia/low_latency_gemm.py` (#59753, #59632, #61437) with measured SM121 plans for the TP=2 local shapes, installed from `Glm5NextForCausalLM` and `Glm5NextMTP`.

## Claims

- 7 local `(N, K)` shapes at M = 1/2/4 (20 entries), routed only on capability `(12, 1)`; other GPUs, dtypes, token counts and shapes keep the standard linear path.
- Kernel: 1.35x to 1.74x over cuBLAS gemv at M=1 (238-283 GB/s vs 162-176 GB/s), 1.04x to 1.36x at M=2/4.
- End to end, no speculation, concurrency 1: TPOT 64.9 -> 57.0 ms (-12%), 15.0 -> 17.0 tok/s. With MTP k=3 the target forward runs at M=4, where cuBLAS already uses a GEMM kernel: ITL -2.5%, TPOT and tok/s unchanged.
- GSM8K (n=100): 93 base, 94 plans.

## Validation

Probe through the module path on GB10 (CUDA graph replay, weight rotated out of L2, nightly 8e2beaa3bf), cosine 1.000000 vs `F.linear` for every entry:

| local (N, K) | layer | calls/step | M=1 cuBLAS us | M=1 plan us | M=1 | M=2 | M=4 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| (12576, 4096) | KDA in_proj | 34 | 608 | 429 | 1.42x | 1.07x | 1.05x |
| (4096, 4096) | KDA o_proj | 34 | 204 | 137 | 1.48x | 1.08x | 1.06x |
| (2048, 4096) | shared gate_up, MLA qkv_a | 53 | 103 | 64 | 1.61x | 1.22x | 1.24x |
| (4096, 1024) | shared down | 42 | 51 | 30 | 1.74x | 1.34x | 1.36x |
| (8192, 1536) | MLA q_b | 11 | 147 | 102 | 1.45x | 1.13x | 1.20x |
| (4096, 8192) | MLA o_proj | 11 | 291 | cuBLAS | 1.01x (not listed) | 1.04x | 1.07x |
| (77440, 4096) | lm_head | 1 | 3606 | 2663 | 1.35x | 1.05x | 1.06x |

These are cold-L2 numbers, which is the serving case: decode reads each weight once per step and 45 layers do not fit the 24 MB L2. The M=1 entries add up to 12.8 ms per step; the server gains 8 ms because the GEMMs interleave with MoE and attention. M=8/16 are within 2% of cuBLAS and are not listed.

End to end, `nvidia/GLM-5.3-Flash-NVFP4` TP=2 on two Sparks, FP8 KV, SPEED-Bench 2K in / 1K out, 4 requests at concurrency 1, two interleaved runs per arm, nightly 8e2beaa3bf:

| Arm | TPOT p50 (ms) | ITL p50 (ms) | Output tok/s | GSM8K (n=100) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| base | 64.83 / 64.98 | 64.53 / 64.64 | 15.06 / 14.87 | 93 |
| plans | 57.85 / 56.16 (**-12%**) | 58.88 / 55.20 | 16.77 / 17.30 | 94 |

With MTP k=3 (`--speculative-config '{"method":"mtp","num_speculative_tokens":3}'`, 2026-10-17 nightly):

| Arm | ITL p50 (ms) | TPOT p50 (ms) | Output tok/s | Acceptance length |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| base | 102.18 / 102.73 | 40.93 / 41.52 | 24.21 / 23.48 | 2.60 / 2.52 |
| plans | 99.56 / 100.27 (-2.5%) | 41.76 / 42.01 | 23.85 / 23.71 | 2.48 / 2.48 |

An nsys census of the plans arm shows every listed shape on `CuteSkinnyGemm` in the server (45% of GPU time without speculation, 39.5% with MTP); what is left on cuBLAS is KDA `f_b`/`g_b` (4096, 128) (#57617), the fp32-output router gate (288, 4096) and MLA o_proj at M=1.

Tests: `pytest tests/kernels/test_bf16_skinny_gemm.py -k glm5next`, 26 passed on GB10 (table validity, capability routing, hook installation, 20 selected shapes with cosine > 0.999). The selected-shape test skips off capability (12, 1), which CI does not have.

## Details

- Plans come from a CUDA-graph sweep over block_size, outputs_per_block, k_unroll, vector_width and static_k with the weight rotated out of L2, keeping entries that beat cuBLAS by more than 5%. `(2048, 4096)` serves both the shared-expert gate/up and the MLA fused q_a/kv_a projection.
- The replacement method follows the quant method the layer carries: a quant config that excludes `lm_head` gives `ParallelLMHead` an `UnquantizedLinearMethod` rather than an `UnquantizedEmbeddingMethod`, and the head stayed on cuBLAS until this was handled.
- Not covered: TP=1 (the model does not fit one Spark), KDA `f_b`/`g_b` `(4096, 128)` (#57617), MTP `eh_proj` (a plain `nn.Linear`), and M=3, which uses cuBLAS.
- AI assistance (Claude Code) was used for the sweep scripts, the module and the tests; all code and measurements were reviewed and run by the author.
