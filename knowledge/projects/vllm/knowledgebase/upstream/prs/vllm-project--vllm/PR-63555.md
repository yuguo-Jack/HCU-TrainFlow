# [ROCm] GLM-5.3-Flash MonoKernel: MTP verify step

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63555) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

Runs GLM-5.3-Flash's MTP verify step inside the `--monokernel` decode of #61662, so `--speculative-config '{"method": "mtp", ...}'` and `--monokernel` work together on MI355X. Stacked on #61662: only the last four commits (the MTP verify step, its README, the row-walk fix and the KDA head split) are new; I will rebase once #61662 merges.

## Claims

- With MTP, verify steps of whole requests (1 + k rows each, at most 8 rows; 4 at TP2) run in the MonoKernel instead of vLLM's layers.
- One request, 8k in / 1k out, time per output token against MTP on vLLM's layers, same image:
  - TP8: −13% to −32% for every k = 1–7.
  - TP4: −3% to −28% for every k = 1–7.
  - TP2: −6% to −17% for k = 1–3.
- Same draft acceptance (within 0.15 tokens a step) and GSM8K (0.91–0.94) as MTP on vLLM's layers.
- The gain shrinks as concurrency grows: at 8 requests every point is within ±4%.
- Speculative methods other than MTP, and MTP with adaptive verification, dynamic speculation or KDA RecoverSSM, stay refused with a reason.

## Details

- **KDA:** a verify step walks each request's 1 + k rows in order, as vLLM's spec-decode kernels do: the conv window starts at the last accepted token's column and keeps 3 + k columns, and every row's recurrent state goes to its own spec slot.
- **Sparse attention:** a request's rows enter the kpool cache in position order, with the tail ring sized as vLLM sizes it for k drafts. The indexer reads a block-table row a token when vLLM flattens the verify step.
- **Config:** `specialized_decode_config` sets the widths to whole verify requests and lifts the speculative-decoding refusal for MTP only.
- **Routing** accepts uniform verify steps. The kpool tail's metadata is no longer read for the decision: its builder splits decode from prefill at one token, so every verify request read as a prefill and every verify graph was captured on vLLM's path.
- **Row walk (`f11bc45dfa`):** the first sweep had the MonoKernel 7–20% slower at k ≥ 5. Per-row clock stamps showed four per-row costs that added up with k:
  - the 64 KB state store went out as scattered 16-byte pieces (2.7 µs a row);
  - the gate decay was recomputed on all 128 lanes (1.6 µs a row);
  - the mailbox inputs were polled one row at a time;
  - the sparse-attention cache stage fenced after every row (2.6 µs a row).

  The fix: lanes own runs of 4 adjacent k, so the stores are 64-byte runs; the decay is computed once per k; all rows' inputs are polled in one wait; and there is one fence a request.
- **KDA head split (`b0f7d5a194`):** in a verify step one workgroup walked all 128 value rows of a (request, head), so at TP4 with one request 16 of 256 workgroups did the KDA walk. Up to 4 tasks now share a head, each owning 32 value rows of the recurrent state (rows are independent), as many as fit one pass of the grid. The head's first task gathers every row's outputs once for the gated RMSNorm, through the kernel's bounded poll, and then writes the conv state, which every task reads before its walk. Decode without speculation keeps one task a head. One KDA layer at 8 rows: TP4 1 × 8 140 → 128 µs, 2 × 4 134 → 124 µs; TP8 1 × 8 105 → 97 µs; 8 × 1 (decode) unchanged.
- **Limitation:** a launch takes at most 8 rows, so the number of requests a verify step can hold falls as k grows. Larger verify steps run vLLM's layers, so the gain is largest at low concurrency and small k:

| k | requests a kernel step, TP4 / TP8 | TP2 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1, 2, 4 | 1, 2 |
| 2 | 1, 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 1, 2 | 1 |
| 4–7 | 1 | refused |
