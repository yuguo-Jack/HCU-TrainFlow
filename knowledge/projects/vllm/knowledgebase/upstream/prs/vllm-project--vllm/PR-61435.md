## Purpose

Kimi-K3 specialized decode on MI355X, structured like #60683 and enabled the same way: `--additional-config '{"decode_backend": "latency_specialized"}'`. A spec-verify decode step (TP8, DCP1 or DCP8, fp8 KV) runs each MoE decoder layer on persistent FlyDSL kernels instead of vLLM's per-op launches. Everything lives in `vllm/models/kimi_k3/amd/specialized_decode/`; nothing under `vllm/v1/` or `vllm/config/` changes.

**Kernels**
- **K1:** a KDA layer's attention prologue and epilogue.
- **K2:** AttnRes, the o_proj all-reduce, the router and the MoE (shared and routed experts).
- **M1a / M1b:** an MLA layer's prologue (norms, `qkv_a` + `g_proj`, fp8 KV write, `q_b`, W_UK, DCP query gather) and epilogue (DCP LSE combine, W_UV, gate). M1b runs inside K2's launch.
- **M1c:** FlyDSL MLA decode attention, run inside K2's launch at DCP1 with k = 3 / 7. An MLA layer is then two launches, M1a → K2, instead of M1a → AITER asm decode → K2. Elsewhere (DCP8, other k, or a width M1c can't split evenly, e.g. 12 rows at k = 3) the asm decode stays.

**Commit.** Rebased onto `main` after #60683 merged: one commit, the delta over #60683 (16-row K1 / K2, M1a / M1b, M1c, DCP8), plus #60683's last docstring fix. Then: the startup refusal of unserved (max-num-seqs, k) pairs, and M1c's long-context rework (below). After the rebase onto `main` with #61628 and #60110: `_cprr_heads` drops the removed `VLLM_ROCM_AITER_MLA_DCP_VERIFY`; M1c's split geometry imports without FlyDSL (the CUDA CI job). #60110 also broke every DCP1 serve (its folded q_b_proj / W_UK weight was never built); that fix, first carried here as 789727275e, landed separately as #64158, so after merging `main` (05a7546a26) this PR no longer touches `model.py`. 05a7546a26 also quotes three docstrings' tensor layouts for the docs build. Review follow-up (265d786de7): M1 requires the `ROCM_AITER_MLA` backend (checked at config time and on the loaded layers; DCP1 without it is refused instead of failing at the first verify step), and startup builds only the widths max-num-seqs reaches (`max_num_seqs × (k + 1)` rows at most). Second follow-up (481df757ca): the fp8 KV checks accept only E4M3 (`fp8` / `fp8_e4m3`; `fp8_e5m2` passed before, though M1a writes E4M3); `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=1` (and `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_MOE`) is checked at config time instead of failing after the weight load; the unused, broken `runtime/mla.py` `pre` / `post` helpers are removed; README and docstrings no longer describe an asm fallback at DCP1.

## Supported configurations

Startup accepts these concurrency (`--max-num-seqs`), k (speculative tokens) and DCP combinations and refuses every other one:

| Concurrency | DCP8 (MLA: M1a → AITER asm decode → K2) | DCP1 (MLA: M1a → K2 with M1c) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | k = 1 – 15 | k = 3, 7 |
| 2 | k = 1 – 7 | k = 3, 7 |
| 4 | k = 1 – 3 | refused |
| 8 | k = 1 | refused |

Rule: max-num-seqs ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8} and max-num-seqs × (k + 1) ≤ 16. DCP1 serves only what M1c runs, since AITER's asm MLA decode is not served there: k = 3 / 7, and not c4 / k3, whose 3-request (12-row) steps M1c can't split. Prefill and mixed-length steps run vLLM's decode. The same single `ValueError` also lists TP ≠ 8, DCP other than 1 / 8, no speculative decoding, AITER off (`VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER` / `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_MOE` unset), at DCP1 a KV cache other than fp8 E4M3, `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_FP4BMM` unset or an MLA attention backend other than `ROCM_AITER_MLA` (e.g. `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_MLA=0` or `--attention-backend TRITON_MLA`; at DCP8 the MLA layers then run vLLM's attention ops, then K2), LoRA, KV connectors, and other features #60683 also refuses.
