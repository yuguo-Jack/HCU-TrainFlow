# [Perf][MiniMax-M3] Tune SM100 sparse decode

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/62098) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

Tune MiniMax-M3 Triton sparse decode on SM100 with an SM-count-based split policy and optional FP16 dot operands. BF16 remains the default.

**Draft blocker:** default BF16 regresses on GSM8K by **0.9856 percentage points**, exceeding the predeclared local 0.5-point limit. This needs investigation before merge.

## Claims

- Support non-power-of-two splits using the existing decode and merge kernels, targeting two CTAs per SM.
- Allow FP16 dot operands through `AttentionConfig`, with FP32 accumulation.

## Validation

Measurements compare `a450316` against `9731f30`. The subsequent commit only renames the constexpr to `USE_FP16_DOT`; normalized AST and pre-commit checks pass. [Scripts, complete results, runtime details and reproduction instructions](https://github.com/wolfcomos/vllm/tree/2c5dfae05cb32d8e8329baa8d2be43aa2d4d4045) are published separately. **Kernel performance:** synthetic full decode + merge on GB200, warm CUDA graphs, seven rotated paired rounds across 24 shapes.

| Mode | Geometric mean speedup | Across-shape range |
|---|---:|---:|
| BF16 | 1.120× | 0.989–1.608× |
| FP16 opt-in | 1.136× | 0.928–1.631× |

Each shape uses the median of paired speedup ratios. The sweep includes 21 scalar-FP8 cases and three controls; two controls fall back to BF16. Small-batch regressions remain, and FP16 adds little beyond split tuning in most scalar-scale cases. These timings exclude host planning and compilation and do not establish serving throughput. Per-round variability is in the supplement.

All 24 timed shapes and 38 edge cases pass FP32-reference and graph checks, including batch-invariant, layout, dtype-fallback and active-row padding coverage. Separate range tests cover finite FP16 saturation.

## Details

The old policy targets 256 CTAs and rounds splits down to a power of two. The new SM100 policy uses the GPU's SM count and masks padded merge lanes. Other architectures and batch-invariant mode retain the original split policy; prefill and CUTLASS decode are unchanged.

Enable FP16 with `--attention-config '{"minimax_m3_msa_decode_fp16_dot": true}'`. It applies to SM100 BF16-query/FP8-KV Triton decode with absent or scalar scales and batch-invariant mode disabled. Q and scaled K/V saturate to the finite FP16 range. Split changes can alter reduction rounding; FP16 additionally changes operand precision.

Scalar-scale folding is excluded because #52060 covers it. Related #56932 targets SM120 page splitting and SM103/SM120 single-page decode; #55372 explores query-tiled speculative verification.
