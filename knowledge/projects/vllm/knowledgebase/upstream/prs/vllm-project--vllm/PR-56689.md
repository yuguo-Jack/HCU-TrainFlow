## Overview

Preserve tied parameter and repeated submodule names in `ModuleSource`, so native weight synchronization updates a pipeline stage's output head even when the trainer shares its embedding storage. Related to #31848.

## Claims

- Keep tied aliases in cached `metadata`, `iter_full()` and `iter_local()`.
- Preserve DTensor ownership, placements, rank membership and metadata caching.
- Cover both alias forms and iteration modes across successive updates, and document the repeated transfer/gather cost.

## Details

PyTorch's default parameter iterator removes names that share a Parameter or registered submodule. A trainer can tie its embedding and output head while inference needs those names on different pipeline stages. Omitting the head name leaves that stage on the old weights even though synchronization reports success.

All three source enumerations now use `named_parameters(remove_duplicate=False)`, retaining the existing ownership and DTensor materialization logic. The regression stays in the existing dense `TestModuleSource` suite and uses real Torch modules and shared Parameters.

Each alias adds a transfer, and `iter_full()` may gather a tied DTensor again. Checkpoint-aware treatment of explicit output heads remains the loader's responsibility.
