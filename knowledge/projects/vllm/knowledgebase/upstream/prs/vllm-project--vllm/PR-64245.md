# [ROCm][Perf][GLM-5.3-Flash] MonoKernel: quantize routed expert mid to MXFP8 once, in up/gate (on #61662)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/64245) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

The MonoKernel's expert-down stage spent 5.6 µs of every width-4 launch re-quantizing the expert intermediate (`mid`) to MXFP8, and every one of the 256 CTAs did the same work on the same 36,864 values. This PR quantizes the routed experts' `mid` once, in the up/gate CTAs that produce it, and publishes MXFP8 words and E8M0 scales instead of f32. The down stage then polls a quarter of the bytes and copies them into LDS. The LDS words and scales are unchanged, so launch outputs are bit-identical.

**Stacked on #64225**, which is stacked on #61662. Neither has landed, so the diff against main includes both. This PR's own change is the last commit, `aff9dc4a91` (`mono/ffn/kernel.py` and `mono/ffn/layout.py`): [compare against #64225](https://github.com/amd-dlimpus/vllm/compare/269796ffc2...aff9dc4a91). `aff9dc4a91` has the same tree as `0fb606374d`, which the numbers below were measured on; only a missing Signed-off-by was added. It touches different code from #64207 and stacks cleanly with it.

**Independent of the KV cache dtype**: the change is in the MoE stages, after attention.

## Where the time went

From a traced width-4 launch (TP2, FP8 KV, 2,048-token context), with all waves synchronized before each mark. Polling `mid` finishes about 0.8 µs after the last up/gate CTA, so waiting is not the cost:

| Down-stage span, per CTA | p10 | p50 | max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MXFP8 quant of `mid` into LDS | 5.5 µs | 5.6 µs | 5.7 µs |
| Down MMA | 8.5 µs | 8.8 µs | 10.1 µs |

The quant span barely varies from CTA to CTA, so it is compute rather than waiting. It is the redundancy: each CTA owns a slice of the hidden rows but needs every row's whole `mid` in MXFP8.

## The change

- **Up/gate task mapping.** When the per-rank intermediate size is a multiple of 4 × 256 (TP2: 1024; TP1: 2048), up/gate runs 4 rounds of 8 columns per CTA. A CTA now keeps one routed slot across 4 consecutive rounds and walks one 32-column scale group, instead of keeping one 8-column chunk and walking slots. Weight bytes per CTA are the same. The shared expert stays on the same CTAs and rounds as before.
- **Producer quant.** Each routed row's 8 activated columns per round are staged in a free slice of LDS. After the 4th round the CTA quantizes the 32-column group with the same `quant_mxfp8` and publishes one word per 4 values plus one scale (`mid8`, `mid8s` mailboxes).
- **Down stage.** The down stage polls the routed words and scales straight into LDS. The shared expert's `mid` stays f32 (its columns are spread across 128 CTAs) and is quantized in the down stage as before: 1/9 of the former work.
- At TP4 and TP8 (intermediate 512 and 256) up/gate has fewer than 4 rounds, so those widths keep the f32 `mid` path. The shared up/gate unit code was refactored for both paths (`ug8_units` derives `w_rg` and `w_ln` from the column, and `ug8_emit` takes it), so the TP4 and TP8 op tests still need a GPU run; see Tests.

## Accuracy

Bit-identical. The f32 values, the per-32 amax, the scale and the E4M3 conversion are the same operations on the same inputs, just in another CTA. Checked at the kernel level: seeded weights and inputs, an FFN launch and a KDA launch, TP2, widths 2 and 4, both ranks. `x`, residual, `post`, `comb` and the KDA conv and recurrent states all match pad-chain's head bit for bit.

## Performance

Launch time from the MonoKernel bench (TP2, FP8 KV, MI355X), mean of two alternating rounds on the same pair of cards:

| Launch | #64225 (µs) | This PR (µs) | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FFN layer, width 2 | 48.2 | 45.2 | −3.0 |
| FFN layer, width 4 | 71.8 | 66.8 | −5.0 |
| Sparse-attention layer, width 4, 2K context | 164.5 | 161.2 | −3.3 |
| Sparse-attention layer, width 4, 8K context | 183.5 | 179.5 | −4.0 |
| Sparse-attention layer, width 2, 8K context | 125.8 | 121.9 | −4.0 |

The width-2, 2K-context sparse-attention launch was too noisy in this run to call (117.4 and 107.9 µs on this PR, 113.2 and 112.9 µs on the base).

End to end: TP2, FP8 KV, `--monokernel`, real-text prompts (about 1K-token document excerpts), 2,048 output tokens, vLLM `bench serve`, MI355X, image `vllm/vllm-openai-rocm:nightly-rocm100-5d35ce3934a7804b298559d2d99eec33792e1eb9`, both arms on the same pair of cards. The base arm is #61662 + #64207 + #64225, and the other arm adds this PR. The PR arm gave the same 5.28 / 6.57 ms on three separate boots, and the base arm reproduced #64225's own result (5.37 / 6.75 ms) within 0.02 ms:

| Concurrency | Median ITL, base (ms) | + this PR (ms) | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5.38 | 5.28 | −1.9% |
| 4 | 6.77 | 6.57 | −3.0% |

Against #61662's head (6.09 and 7.15 ms), the three stacked PRs give −13.3% median ITL at concurrency 1 and −8.1% at concurrency 4.

The runs above sent one wave of requests per concurrency, had vLLM's torch profiler attached for 3 s inside the measured window, and had #64207 under both arms.

Repeated A/B on this PR's own stack: #64225 at `269796ffc2` against this PR's `aff9dc4a91`. TP2, FP8 KV, `--monokernel`, image `vllm/vllm-openai-rocm:nightly-rocm100-5d35ce3934a7804b298559d2d99eec33792e1eb9`, MI355X, the same pair of cards for every arm. Two server boots per arm, interleaved; 10 × concurrency prompts per point; 1,024 output tokens; no profiler. ISL 1024 is real-text prompts, ISL 8192 random. ## Tests

- Kernel bit-identity against pad-chain's head as above.
- `test_ops.py`, `test_flash_spec.py` and `test_runtime.py` at TP2: 52 passed. The 13 TP4/TP8 cases take the f32 `mid` path through the refactored unit code. They were skipped on two cards.
- MonoKernel GPU tests at TP4 on aff9dc4a91 (`pytest tests/kernels/models/amd/glm5next_mono`): 67 passed, 5 skipped. The 5 skips are TP8 cases. The TP4 cases cover the f32 `mid` path; TP8 is still pending.
- Kernel outputs at TP4, widths 2, 4 and 8 (FFN and KDA launches, seeded weights and inputs): bit-identical to #64225's head on all four ranks (`test_gate_dump.py` + `cmp_gate_dump.py`).
