# [ROCm][Step5] Use one warp for CSA prefill sparse attention on CDNA3/CDNA4

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65599) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

Launch the Step-5 CSA prefill sparse attention kernel (`sparse_attention_paged`) with one warp on CDNA4 (gfx950, MI350X/MI355X) for all KV dtypes, and on CDNA3 (gfx942, MI300X/MI325X) when the KV cache is FP8. Follow-up to #61837.

## Claims

- FP8-KV prefill sparse attention is 1.6x faster on MI325X, with bit-identical output. On MI350X it is 1.21-1.22x faster for FP8 KV and 1.04-1.42x for BF16 KV.
- Step-5-Preview serving throughput improves by 2-7% on 8x MI325X and by 0.5-1.4% on 8x MI350X.
- BF16 KV on CDNA3 and every other target keep `num_warps=4` (BF16 KV is not faster with one warp on CDNA3).

## Validation

**Setup:** Step-5-Preview, 8x MI325X, TP=8, `vllm/vllm-openai-rocm` nightly. Before is the same container without this change.

```bash
VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=1 vllm serve stepfun-ai/Step-5-Preview --tensor-parallel-size 8 --max-model-len 262144 --kv-cache-dtype fp8 --block-size 32 --gpu-memory-utilization 0.90 --moe-backend triton --attention-backend ROCM_AITER_UNIFIED_ATTN --safetensors-load-strategy prefetch --no-enable-prefix-caching --reasoning-parser step5 --tool-call-parser step5 --enable-auto-tool-choice --mm-encoder-attn-backend ROCM_AITER_FA --mm-encoder-tp-mode data --mm-processor-cache-type shm --limit-mm-per-prompt '{"image":1,"video":0}'
```

### Kernel

`sparse_attention_paged` at the per-rank Step-5 shape (8 query heads, 1 KV group, head dim 256, top-512 regions of 8 tokens). Outputs of `num_warps=4` and `num_warps=1` are bit-identical in every case.

| KV dtype | Tokens / context | Before | After | Speedup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FP8 | 4096 / 64k | 2550 us | 1601 us | 1.59x |
| FP8 | 16384 / 128k | 10169 us | 6240 us | 1.63x |
| BF16 | 4096 / 64k | 2922 us | 2911 us | unchanged (4 warps) |
| BF16 | 16384 / 128k | 12513 us | 12549 us | unchanged (4 warps) |

With one warp, BF16 KV ran 0.79-0.82x on MI325X, which is why CDNA3 is gated on FP8.

MI350X (gfx950), same shape:

| KV dtype | Tokens / context | 4 warps | 1 warp | Speedup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FP8 | 4096 / 64k | 1796 us | 1476 us | 1.22x |
| FP8 | 16384 / 128k | 7415 us | 6135 us | 1.21x |
| BF16 | 4096 / 64k | 2099 us | 1474 us | 1.42x |
| BF16 | 16384 / 128k | 9021 us | 8691 us | 1.04x |

On MI350X, 2 warps was 0.92-1.21x and 8 warps 0.54-0.71x, so one warp is the best setting there for both dtypes.

### Tests

`tests/models/step5/test_amd_csa_paged.py::test_sparse_attention_paged` covers BF16, FP8 e4m3fn and FP8 e4m3fnuz K/V, so it runs both launch configurations. On MI325X, re-running its 6 launches with both `num_warps=4` and `num_warps=1` gave bit-identical outputs, and on MI350X `test_amd_csa_paged.py` passes 11/11 with every `num_warps` setting. `pytest tests/models/step5` passes except 3 cases in `test_csa_tail_ring_block_size.py`, which test #65153 (newer than the nightly image) and fail the same way without this change.

## Details

Each program handles one query token and one KV group; Step-5's 8 heads per group are padded to `BLOCK_H=16` rows. With four warps the QK^T and PV tiles are split across waves, so every softmax row max and sum is reduced across waves through LDS. With one wave those reductions stay in registers. For FP8 KV this is a clear win on both architectures; for BF16 KV it wins on CDNA4, while on CDNA3 the larger per-wave load work outweighs it, so BF16 keeps four warps there.

The gate is `cdna == 4 or (fp8 and cdna == 3)` with `cdna = get_cdna_version()`. CDNA5 (gfx1250) keeps the default until it is measured. The helper is imported inside the function, like the other ROCm-only checks in model ops, so importing the module on CUDA is unaffected.
