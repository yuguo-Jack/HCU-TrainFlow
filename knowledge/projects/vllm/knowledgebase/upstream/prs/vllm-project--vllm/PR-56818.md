# [Bugfix][Core] Cache final multimodal boundaries for Mamba siblings

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/56818) · state: open · merged: False

## Overview

Fix appended-image prefix-cache misses on Mamba-attention hybrids with APC and Mamba `align`, including producers with a long text tail. Addresses the reproduced case in #43587, not every reported configuration.

## Claims

- Materialize and retain the shared multimodal checkpoint, including the lower checkpoint required by EAGLE/MTP.
- Preserve shared multimodal prefill splits when an image is appended, resolving the observed cached-versus-cold numerical mismatch in the tested configurations.

## Details

Attention hashes can match while no Mamba state remains at the shared image prefix: prefill may skip that boundary, or sparse retention may discard it. The fix makes the final multimodal grid boundary both a prefill stop and a retention point. EAGLE/MTP's fallback is one joint scheduler block lower, keeping generation and retention consistent under DCP's larger attention blocks.

Stopping only at the final image also changes earlier prefill partitions after another image is appended. In the 448px reproducer, identical inputs and weights produce different BF16 gate projections at `M=528` versus `M=2640`; changing only those gates reproduces the GDN difference. All 24 SSM restores are byte-exact. Preserving each multimodal grid boundary resolves this case without changing arithmetic or decode.

Limits: extra prefill stops have measurable cold-prefill cost (13% for five images, 23% for 32 images in the 4B benchmark above), and retained states consume cache capacity. No latency or general batch-invariance guarantee is claimed. The vision-enabled text-only zero-hit report was not reproduced on the tested 4B/27B prompts, including over 51k input tokens. FP8 KV + MTP + FlashInfer 0.7.2.post1 fails the sync checker on an unpinned host copy on both base and head; it is not counted as passing. Triton's native-FP8 alternative rejects SM86 hardware. The existing rejection of `align` plus `--disable-chunked-mm-input` is unchanged.

Unlike application-directed checkpoints (#55873/#55875/#55876) or prompt-boundary lifetime protection (#61282), this fix adds automatic multimodal grid checkpoints. Precision defaults, hash format and kernels are unchanged. AI assistance was used.
