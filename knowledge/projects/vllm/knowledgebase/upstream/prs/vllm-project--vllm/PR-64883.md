# [ROCm][Perf][DSv4.1] Mono: tuned K2 FFN seam at TP4 / TP8

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/64883) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description


## Overview

**Stacked on #64152, which is stacked on #63893 and #63027. Only the last commit, `3e7bf14c49`, belongs to this PR.**

On gfx950 (MI355X) at TP4 and TP8, K2's FFN seam and the FFN launch's seam now use the tuned seam that #63027 wrote for its TP2 compress-ratio-2 layers. The MoE route also loads its bias before it polls the logits. Outputs stay bit for bit the same as the generic seam's. On DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash with TP4 `--monokernel`, the decode step is 144 us shorter at c1 (-2.8%) and 449 us shorter at c4 (-5.3%).

## Claims

- **Decode step at TP4 (measured):**
  - Setup: 4x MI355X, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash `--monokernel`, DSpark k=6, synthetic AL 3.87. 4-round ABBA against the stack without this commit.
  - Results: c1 **-144 us (-2.83%)**, c2 **-80 us (-1.26%)**, c4 **-449 us (-5.35%)**.
  - Every round is faster at every concurrency. Each mean is larger than t x SE and larger than the base A/A spread, the rule #63893 states.
- **Outputs bit for bit unchanged (measured, at the tested shapes):**
  - The numerics test compares the whole layer (K1 + K2) bit for bit against a `generic=True` runner at TP4 and TP8, at 6-48 rows. It now compares the FFN launch the same way.
  - The test passes on MI355X. It is not vacuous: a mutant that scales half of the tuned seam's fp32 TP sums by 1 + 2^-7 (1-2 bf16 ulps) fails it. With the mutant in K2, the whole-layer compare fails; with it in the FFN launch, the new compare fails.
- **Accuracy within noise (measured):** GSM8K with every decode-only step on mono goes from 0.9199 to 0.9165 (McNemar exact p = 0.20, 6 paired passes). AL goes from 3.5785 to 3.5810.
- **Scope:**
  - The gate is fixed at build time, with no env var. `MonoBuild.tuned_seam` equals `tuned_front`: gfx950, not `generic`, TP4 or TP8, plus the TP2 ratio-2 builds, which already had this seam.
  - It selects the tuned seam for the target's K2, the DSpark draft's K2 and the FFN launch, and it turns on the route's early bias load.
  - K2's attention back half, the router and the rest of the MoE stay generic.
  - TP2, gfx942 and `generic=True` builds compile the same code as before. This is inferred from the gate; those builds were not recompiled. The scratch layout does not change.
- **TP8:** checked only by the bit compare and the CI cases. There is no TP8 end-to-end A/B.

## Details

**What the tuned seam does differently.** All of this is #63027's existing code. `tuned_seam` now selects it in `run_ffn`, `build_mono_k2` and `build_mono_ffn`.
1. **Reduce fused into the slice** (`stage_reduce_slice`, `reduce_slice_quad`, `slice_rest`):
   - Each thread takes 4 columns of a token through the TP sum (in rank order), R_new of the 4 streams and the layer input, all in registers.
   - The residual, the comb/post/pre mixes and the `hc_fn` weights are loaded before the poll on the TP partials.
   - Before, the generic reduce polled, stored to `pend_lds` and hit a barrier, and only then did the slice load its operands.
2. **`slice_mix_all`:** it runs every token tile's mix MFMAs, then one barrier, then every tile's wave sums.
   - That is 2 barriers per slice task instead of 2 per token tile.
   - The seam's LDS `red` is sized for all tiles (`_seam_lds(s, key.tuned_seam)`). Up to 16 rows the size is the same.
3. **Tuned `stage_norm`:**
   - `norm_w` is loaded before the row's wait.
   - The 3 chunks are polled in one batch of 12 pairs instead of 3 serial polls.
   - Each thread writes its 8 normed values with one 16-byte store (`store_normed(wide=True)`) instead of 8 bf16 stores.
   - There is no tail barrier; the publish's drain provides one.
4. **Norms before gates:** the MoE waits on the norms, and nothing in the launch waits on the gates. Past 16 rows (more than one router group), the gates run on the route CTAs after the route (`after_route`).
5. **Early bias** (`stage_route`): the routing bias is loaded before the logits poll. #63027 does this under `MoeBuild.tuned`. Here `cb["early_bias"]`, a build-time bool, turns it on while the MoE stays generic.

The arithmetic and its order do not change. Only loads, polls, stores, barriers and the stage order move, and the bit compares check that.

**Gate.**
- **Separate name:** `tuned_seam` returns `tuned_front`. It has its own name so that K2's seam and K1's front each have their own gate.
- **What stays off:**
  - `BackBuild.tuned` and `MoeBuild.tuned` still come from `MonoBuild.tuned`, which is TP2 ratio 2 only. So at TP4 and TP8, K2's tuned back half and the tuned MoE stay off.
  - In particular, the router's gate weights still load after the wait on x (`router_task_weights` is used only under `MoeBuild.tuned`).
- **CUDA graphs:**
  - #63893 notes that a TP4 tuned K2 hung during FULL capture in an earlier experiment.
  - This commit turns on only the seam and the bias.
  - The 300 graph replays at TP4 and TP8, and all 8 PR servers (CUDA graphs on), finished without a hang.
- **`ffn_kernel` now passes `generic=self.generic`:**
  - Before this commit, the FFN launch never took a tuned path: it builds at ratio 0, where `tuned` is false, so ignoring `generic` did not matter.
  - With `tuned_seam`, ignoring it would have made the generic runner's FFN launch tuned too, and the new compare could not fail.
  - Only test runners set `generic`.
- **TP8 poll:** this commit builds the tuned seam's TP poll at TP8, which #64152 noted was not built. It is 8 specs of 2 pairs, one batch under `POLL_MAX = 12`. So the TP8 poll-batch fix that #64152 left out is not needed.

**Motivation:** keep operands in flight before each wait, as in Kog AI's [MI300X single-kernel engine write-up](https://blog.kog.ai/building-a-single-kernel-latency-optimized-llm-inference-engine-on-amd-mi300x-gpus/). At TP4 / TP8, K2's FFN seam and the route's bias still issued their loads after their polls.

**Limitations and coverage gaps**
- **The FFN launch never ran end to end:**
  - With this config every layer takes the whole-layer path; the server logs list no FFN-launch layers.
  - The FFN launch serves only layers whose attention the kernels do not take: another KV cache dtype or indexer, or a compressor without an indexer.
  - The unit bit compare is its only check.
- **Not bit-compared on GPU:**
  - the DSpark draft's K2: at TP4 its only check is the GSM8K AL at `--parallel 6`, and at TP8 nothing checks it;
  - the index-source K2: only the layers test checks it, within tolerance;
  - 14 and 28 rows, the c2 and c4 widths: layers `[8-28]` checks 28 rows at TP8, within tolerance.

  The >16-row path (gates after the route) is bit-compared at 36 and 48 rows.
- **TP8 performance is not measured.** TP8 takes the same code as TP4. Its builds compile with no spill, and they pass the bit compares and the 300 replays.
- **Per-width gains not explained (not profiled):**
  - c4's -449 us is larger than the 7-row microbenchmark suggests. At 28 rows, `slice_mix_all` saves two barriers and the gates run after the route.
  - c2 gains less than c1 (-80 vs -144 us) with twice the rows. 14 rows is the TP4 K2 width that spills, in both arms.
- **Scaling estimate (inferred):** 18 ratio-2 layers x 2.58 us plus 20 ratio-1 layers x 2.40-3.00 us gives 94-106 us, against the 144 us measured at c1. The 2 ratio-0 layers and the K2 of the 3 draft layers were not benchmarked.
- **Repeated prompts:** each perf run reuses its prompts (seed 100 + c), so runs 2 and 3 hit the prefix cache during prefill. The metric is the decode step, which this does not affect.
