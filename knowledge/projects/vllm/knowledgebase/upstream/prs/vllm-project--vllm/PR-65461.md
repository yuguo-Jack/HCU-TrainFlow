# [ROCm][Perf][DSv4.1] Mono: merge K2(l) and K1(l + 1) into one launch at TP8

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65461) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

At TP8 on MI355X (gfx950), DeepSeek-V4.1 mono decode (`--monokernel`) now launches K2 of layer l and K1 of layer l + 1 as one persistent kernel. This covers 34 of the 39 target layer boundaries and both DSpark draft boundaries. Each merged boundary saves the kernel gap plus K1's start-up.

Stacked on #64883 → #64152 → #63893 → #63027; **review only the last commit**.

## Claims

- **C1-shaped decode (1 × TP8, conc 1, DSpark k = 6): −96.8 µs per step (−2.21%).** That is 4 ABBA pairs, SE 4.3 µs, measured on the earlier stack image. <!-- TODO: replace or confirm with the A/B on this branch's base -->
- **Layer bench, TP8, 7 rows:** −1.71 µs/layer at compress ratio 2 and −2.08 µs/layer on the checkpoint's layer pattern (4 processes).
- **Bit-exact.** Merged and unmerged runs give identical outputs, KV writes and top-k on every rank, eager and after graph replay. Accuracy and acceptance length are unchanged.
- **TP8 only.** TP2, TP4, other widths and gfx942 keep two launches, and their K1, K2 and FFN-launch builds are byte-identical. There is no new env var.

## Validation

**Correctness (MI355X, TP8)**
- `tests/models/test_dsv41_mono_merge.py` compares the merged chain with the separate K1/K2 chain using `torch.equal` after the eager run and after 50 graph replays. It covers:
  - every layer's output, residual and mixes, its SWA cache and the top-k buffers;
  - ratios 0/1/2, an index-source layer, fold off, the checkpoint's layer pattern and the DSpark draft dims.
- A whole-decode-step test (`tests/models/test_dsv41_mono_decode.py`) runs the full 40-layer target plus the draft with the merge on and off. Outputs are bit-identical on all ranks, eager and in FULL graphs. This includes steps where a layer declines mono, which forces a flush.
- Mutant control: a 1-ulp change on the merged path only fails the compare.

**Accuracy:** GSM8K-1319 (greedy, real block acceptance, `--parallel 6` so mono engages) gave 0.9161 merged vs 0.9177 unmerged, with AL 3.5835 vs 3.5825. Pooled McNemar p = 0.54 over 18 passes per arm, on the earlier stack image.

**Stability:** 28 servers with no HSA errors or hangs. In serving there were 34 merged launches per target graph and 2 per draft graph, and no deferred K2 ever had to be flushed on its own.

**CI:** the merge tests need 8 GPUs and are added to the MI355 mono step in `.buildkite/test_areas/models_basic.yaml`.

## Details

**Why no cross-CTA hand-off is needed.** K2's MoE reduce task j, its FFN-seam slice j and K1(l + 1)'s seam slice j all run on CTA j, over the same 32 columns. In the merged kernel, K1's slice takes the MoE all-reduce itself in place of K2's reduce stage, and it reads back the residual its own thread stored. The only cross-CTA inputs are K2's FFN-gate mixes. They are handed to the slices as tagged pairs and are ready ~30–60 µs early. The boundary is an LDS-reuse barrier, with K1's argument block loaded ahead of it and no drain of K2's stores.

**When a boundary merges.** It merges only into a whole mono layer of the same runner, with no torch op in between and when that layer takes the kernels on this step.
- Engram (0→1, 13→14) and the aux hidden-state capture (36→37, 37→38, 38→39) keep their boundaries.
- The decision comes from step metadata that every rank holds, so all ranks make it alike.
- A module hook on the layers turns merging off.
- Anything else that reads a layer's outputs, including the final norm, a layer that declines mono, and the end of the draft runner, flushes the deferred K2 first.

**Scope and limits.**
- Widths: 7 target rows and 6 draft rows at TP8 (conc 1 with k = 6). Other TP8 widths, such as 14, keep two launches until benched.
- Registers: merged builds have 0 VGPR spills and the same LDS as K2. Some pairs carry 3–7 more SGPR spills than K2, into VGPR lanes, with no scratch.
- Workload: the CI agentic workload runs longer contexts than the 32k bench, so the percentage gain there is likely somewhat smaller. The absolute saving per step should carry over.
- TP4: the same merge was measured there (−81 µs at conc 1, −60 µs at conc 2 per server) but is left out of this PR.
