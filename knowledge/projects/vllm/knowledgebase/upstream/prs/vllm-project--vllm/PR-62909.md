# [ROCm][Kernel] Optimize GDN Prefill with 16×16 MFMA on AMD GPUs for gfx942

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/62909) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

On gfx942 (MI300X/MI325X), give the two slowest Gated DeltaNet (GDN) prefill kernels, `chunk_fwd_kernel_o` and `chunk_gated_delta_rule_fwd_kernel_h_blockdim64`, gfx942-specific Triton autotune configurations that use the 16×16 MFMA form. The GDN algorithm and kernel arithmetic do not change, and outputs are bit-identical. On MI300X, one GDN prefill layer is 1.34–1.48x faster at 8,192 tokens and 1.24–1.58x faster at 32,768 tokens.

## Claims

- On MI300X, one GDN prefill layer is 1.34–1.48x faster at 8,192 tokens and 1.24–1.58x faster at 32,768 tokens, at the head counts of Qwen3.5/3.8-27B and Qwen3-Next-80B at TP1, and of Qwen3.5-397B and Qwen3-Next-80B at TP8.
- On MI300X at 8,192 tokens with 16 key / 48 value heads, `chunk_fwd_kernel_o` goes from 473.9 to 168.8 µs and `chunk_gated_delta_rule_fwd_kernel_h_blockdim64` from 572.6 to 477.0 µs.
- On MI300X, Qwen3.8-27B-FP8 at TP1 had 1.0–2.8% lower median TTFT at every tested input length and concurrency with the first version of this PR, whose layer speedup was smaller (see Validation).
- Output and final recurrent state are bit-identical to the stock configurations.
- Only gfx942 changes. CUDA, other AMD GPUs, and the AITER FlyDSL path selected by `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=1` keep their configurations.

## Details

On gfx942 the two kernels get their own configuration lists:

| Kernel | gfx942 configurations |
|---|---|
| `chunk_fwd_kernel_o` | `BK=64, BV=128, matrix_instr_nonkdim=16, num_stages=1`, `num_warps` 2 and 4 |
| `chunk_gated_delta_rule_fwd_kernel_h_blockdim64` | `BV=32, matrix_instr_nonkdim=16, waves_per_eu=2, num_warps=8, num_stages=2` |

- **Use the 16×16 MFMA form.** Without `matrix_instr_nonkdim=16`, Triton emits `v_mfma_f32_32x32x8_bf16`. With these kernels' `warpsPerCTA = [2, 2]` layout each warp owns a 32×16 slice of a 64×32 output, so warp pairs compute duplicate 32×32 tiles; the 16×16 form removes that duplicated work in `chunk_delta_h`. In `chunk_o`, the 16×16 accumulator layout reaches the second `tl.dot` through 16-byte `ds_write2st64_b64`/`ds_read2st64_b64` instead of 2-byte `ds_write_b16`/`ds_read_u16`.
- **Wide tile and one stage in `chunk_o`.** `chunk_fwd_kernel_o` recomputes q·kᵀ and re-reads q and k for every BV slice, so the stock pick's `BV=32` does both four times per chunk and head. `check_shared_mem()` uses an NVIDIA-oriented threshold, so gfx942's 64 KB LDS hides 128-wide tiles from the stock list. At `num_stages=2`, Triton keeps the pipelined operands in unswizzled LDS buffers, which cause bank conflicts at the wide tile; with one stage every buffer is swizzled. A sweep of 219 compiling `chunk_o` configurations (BK, BV, `num_warps`, `num_stages`, `waves_per_eu`, MFMA form) found nothing more than 1% faster than `BK=64, BV=128, num_stages=1` at 8,192 and 32,768 tokens. `BK=128` is not used: BK changes the FP32 reduction grouping and the last bits.
- **`waves_per_eu=2` in `chunk_delta_h`.** It caps occupancy so the compiler can give each wave more registers; with `num_warps=8` and the 16×16 form it is 2–20% faster than without it and bit-identical.
- **Why fixed lists rather than appended candidates.** Serving autotunes once at 64 tokens (one chunk), and T is not in the autotune key. With the stock list kept, the 64-token warm-up picked `chunk_o BV=64` in 6 of 6 runs, 17% slower than `BV=128` at 8,192 tokens; lists that leave out `BV=64` fell back to the stock `BK=32, BV=32` pick (2.5x slower) in 5–6 of 6 runs. For `chunk_delta_h`, appending the `waves_per_eu=2` configuration let the warm-up pick the older one at 16/48 heads. Fixed gfx942 lists remove that dependence; the stock lists stay as they are on every other platform. The gfx942 lists have 2 and 1 configurations instead of 36 and 8, so startup autotuning does less work (not re-measured).
- **Compatibility and CI.** `matrix_instr_nonkdim` and `waves_per_eu` are AMD Triton options; vLLM's ROCm MoE path already uses `matrix_instr_nonkdim`. CI runs `tests/kernels/fla` only on B200 and MI355 (gfx950), where the gfx942 tests skip. An optional `:amd: (MI300) FLA gfx942 Autotune Configs` step (`mi300_1`) runs them; like the MI355 FLA job it runs on the scheduled nightly or when unblocked.
- **Related PRs.** #41446 (stale, needs rebase) found the same `chunk_o` tile and uses fixed configurations on all ROCm, bundled with a fused kernel and other changes. With the same kernel source on MI300X, this PR's configurations are 1.5–6.7% faster than #41446's at 16 key heads and 15–29% faster at 2 key heads (8,192 and 32,768 tokens), mostly from `chunk_delta_h` `num_warps=8`; #41446's `kpack=2` also changes the output bits. #43047 prunes configurations that exceed shared memory and adds none.
- **Limits.** KDA models (Kimi Linear, Kimi-K3, GLM-5-Next) also reach the gfx942 `chunk_delta_h` configuration through its `gk` path, which the test does not cover. End-to-end serving was measured with the first version only. Not measured: MI325X performance, serving at TP>1, variable-length batches, and the larger models end to end.
