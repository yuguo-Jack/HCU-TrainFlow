## Disclosure

## Overview

**JUST FOR DEMO, DO NOT MERGE.** This branch is `main` plus the five build-speedup PRs, and exists only to measure them together: #62716 (paged attention units), #62717 (topk softplus-sqrt), #62718 (skinny GEMM families), #63364 (int4 skinny GEMM), #63365 (fused MUSE-Spark). Each is reviewed on its own; nothing here is new.

The two builds are bound by different things, which is why the series has five parts: at 1 arch three kernel groups take turns being the longest unit, at 9 archs two files that none of the first three touch are.

| build | `main` (`f4edf9f0f0`) | All 5 PRs (#62921) (`8ef419ad13`) |
|---|---|---|
| `MAX_JOBS=16`, `gfx950` only | 107 s | 87 s (−19 %) |
| `MAX_JOBS=64`, `gfx950` only | 85 s | 49 s (−42 %) |
| `MAX_JOBS=64`, 9 archs | 1263 s | 472 s (−63 %) |

## Claims

- 1 arch: 85 s -> 49 s, by cutting the topk unit (74.5 s -> 7.4 s) and the paged attention units (71.6 s -> 31.1 s) and keeping the skinny GEMMs from becoming the next tail.
- 9 archs: 1263 s -> 472 s, by splitting the two tails the 1-arch PRs cannot see - `skinny_gemms_int4.cu` (1232 s -> 448 s) and `fused_muse_spark_qknorm_rope_kv_insert_kernel.cu` (478 s -> 171 s).
- The 9-arch wheel is about the same size as `main`'s (266 -> 270 MB).

## Validation

Single arch (`gfx950`, cold, `pip install -e .`):

| `MAX_JOBS=16` | `MAX_JOBS=64` |
|---|---|
| 87 s | 49 s |

9 archs (cold `setup.py bdist_wheel`, the 9 release archs of `docker/Dockerfile.rocm_base`, mold):

| | wall | wheel |
|---|---|---|
| `main` | 1263 s | 266 MB |
| this branch | 472 s | 270 MB |

Environment, one host: AMD EPYC 9575F (64 cores / 256 threads), 8x AMD Instinct MI350X (251 GiB each), ROCm 10.0, `torch 2.12.0+rocm10.0.0`, clang 23, mold.

Kernel tests on this host, on the per-PR revisions: `test_attention.py` 393 passed, `test_topk_softplus_sqrt.py` 1673 passed / 7 skipped, `test_rocm_skinny_gemms.py` + `test_bf16_skinny_gemm.py` 2519 passed / 636 skipped (same as `main`); the int4 op's test is gfx11/gfx12-only and skips here.
