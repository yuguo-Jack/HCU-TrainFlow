## Overview

Follow-up to #64821. Removes the DEP4 `c96` and `c128` points from the `gb300_agentx_dep4` preset of `recipes/models/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3.1-Flash.yaml`, to match [SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX#4662](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4662), where those two points exceed the 10 s TTFT limit and are no longer run. The preset keeps DEP4 `c64`, and the TP4 and TP2 presets are unchanged.

## Changes

- Drop the `c96` and `c128` points from `gb300_agentx_dep4`.
- Word the DEP4 runbook for a single point, and pin all three presets to the current InferenceX head.
