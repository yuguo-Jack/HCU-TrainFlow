# [Recipe] Kimi-K3: add B200 AgentX aggregated Mooncake preset

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/65529) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Purpose

Adds the `b200_agentx_agg` preset to `recipes/models/moonshotai/Kimi-K3.yaml`, documenting the InferenceX AgentX lane `kimik3-fp4-b200-vllm-agentic-dspark` from [SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX#4759](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/4759) (pinned to `9fe1038`). It serves one aggregated engine over two 8-GPU B200 nodes with TP8 x PP2 + DCP8, the Inferact DSpark draft, and an embedded RDMA Mooncake DRAM tier at selected concurrencies.

- **Scope:** two-node aggregated, one hardware (`b200`), strategy `multi_node_tp_pp`. Opt-in (not `default`), because the lane carries benchmark-only settings.
- **Points:** concurrency 1, 4, 10, 16, 24, 32, 48, 96. DSpark K is 7 at c1-16 and 4 at c24-96. Each point sets `--max-num-seqs` and its CUDA graph capture sizes. The Mooncake store is on at c1, 4, 10, 48 and 96 and off at c16, 24 and 32.
- **Image:** `vllm/vllm-openai@sha256:d1723262293b4d3dc7d0125b4beac8b4a193054a40b776d91d507d98c2762cd1`.
- **Left out, with reasons (in the runbook):** `--enable-prompt-tokens-details`, `VLLM_SERVER_DEV_MODE`, `VLLM_LOG_STATS_INTERVAL`, `TILELANG_CLEANUP_TEMP_FILES` and the Hugging Face cache paths (usage reporting, logging or cache location only); the lane's fabric device and GID selection and `WITH_NVIDIA_PEERMEM=0` (specific to its cluster); `VLLM_PREFIX_CACHE_RETENTION_INTERVAL` and `VLLM_RPC_TIMEOUT` (not read by the pinned image's `vllm/envs.py`).
- **Notes:** the recipe has no DCP selector, so the DCP8 flags (a2a, `TRTLLM_RAGGED` prefill) are written in the preset. `--disable-flashinfer-autotune` from the Blackwell baseline is removed in favour of the lane's `--no-enable-flashinfer-autotune`. Concurrency 4 sets `VLLM_PP_LAYER_PARTITION=50,43`, and concurrency 96 lowers `--gpu-memory-utilization` to 0.915.
