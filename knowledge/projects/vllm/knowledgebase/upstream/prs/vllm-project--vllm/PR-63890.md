# [ROCm][Perf] Use AITER's all-gather for small TP all-gathers

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63890) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

On ROCm gfx950 and gfx942, TP all-gathers now use AITER's IPC all-gather instead of RCCL when the TP group already runs AITER custom all-reduce. "Small" means fp32/fp16/bf16 at TP 2, 4 or 8, up to AITER's own per-rank limit (`should_custom_ag`: vLLM's 128 MiB AITER buffer / (2 x TP) = 32 / 16 / 8 MiB). vLLM adds no cap of its own: in two AITER vs RCCL sweeps, no timed size (TP2: up to 20 of its 32 MiB) was faster on RCCL in both runs. gfx1250 stays on RCCL until it is measured; gfx942 was verified by @frida-andersson on MI325X ([comment](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63890#issuecomment-6590588082), Claims). This extends #48247 (DP only) to TP. #48446's RCCL path stays as the fallback. Below, `base` is a main-equivalent image, `pr` adds the previous head's files (b8fd6897a0, with a 2 MiB cap) and `r1` adds 1eb0aa0805's: the round-1 code with a gfx950-only gate, called "this head" below. The current head, 8ca2504b97, is that code rebased onto main with the gate widened to gfx942; it routes gfx950 the same way. See the image table under GPU. cN means N requests in flight.

## Claims

- **Decode step 1.2-1.9% faster at TP4 c1-c4 (4 swapped rounds), 1.4% at TP4 c8 (one swapped pair) and 0.8-1.2% at TP8 c1-c4** (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, MI355X; `base` matches main on the touched files).
  - TP4 `--monokernel`, previous head, 4 position-swapped rounds (P1-P4): -106 / -120 / -102 us at c1 / c2 / c4 (-1.94 / -1.79 / -1.19%). pr was faster in all 12 round and concurrency pairs, and each drop exceeds the largest same-arm, same-half spread (15 / 27 / 77 us). This head routes c1-c4 decode the same way (every decode gather there is at most 1.81 MB per rank, under the old cap). A c1 re-run on the rebased head gives -113 us against base (previous head: -106 us) and -12 us against pr.
  - TP4 c8 / c16 / c32, this head, where the logits gathers (3.1-14.5 MB per rank) now move to AITER. Against base (one swapped pair, R1/R2): -185 / -350 / -133 us (-1.41 / -2.11 / -0.65%). Against pr (R3/R4, the cap removal alone): -83 / -52 / -307 us. Together they imply pr - base of -102 us at c8, in line with P1-P4 (-106 us at c1), but -298 / +174 us at c16 / c32, where pr differs from base only by Engram gathers. So about 200 us of between-round drift sits in c16 and c32, and neither is established. c8 holds in all four orientations but is marginal: against base, R1's -121 us is just above r1's 116 us same-half spread and inside the cell's largest rep range (145 us); against pr, both orientations (-77 / -89 us) are inside the spread.
  - TP8 without `--monokernel`, previous head (T1-T4: base, pr, pr, base): -0.81 / -1.11 / -1.24% at c1 / c2 / c4, each above the same-arm spread across server starts (21 / 6 / 54 us). The routing there is unchanged; not re-run.
  - Always copying in, instead of AITER's registered capture path (scratch mutant `mut_reg`, M1/M2), costs about 25 us per step at c1, about a quarter of the c1 gain.
  - Under the torch profiler, pr's TP4 step was slower at c1 and c2; without it, pr and r1 were faster than base in every run (see Tradeoffs).
- **gfx942 (MI325X), verified by @frida-andersson** ([comment](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63890#issuecomment-6590588082)), with the gate widened to gfx942. The cell is TP4 `--monokernel`, DSpark k=6, synthetic AL 3.87, random 32768 -> 1024, 32 prompts, c1. ITL p50 went 7.15 -> 7.01 ms (-140 us) on the same 4 GPUs and 7.04 -> 6.99 ms (-50 us) on different GPU halves: two passes, not position-swapped. TTFT and TPOT were flat, and `niah_single_2` at 16384 scored 1.0. Not measured on gfx942: c2 and up (including the c8+ gathers above the old 2 MiB cap) and the AITER vs RCCL sweep.
- **AITER never lost to RCCL in both of two sweeps** (TP 2/4/8, 768 timed points each; table below). The sweeps cover 1 KiB up to AITER's limit (TP2: up to 20 MiB of its 32), dims 0 / middle / last, bf16 and fp32, eager and graph replay, all bit-exact against RCCL. The better run is at least 1.07x at every point. Run 1 alone lost at 13 points (down to 0.32x, TP2 graph, 2.004 MiB), each at least 1.21x in run 2. Dim 0 (a copy-in plus AITER's kernel against one RCCL kernel) has graph-replay medians of 1.18 / 1.41 / 1.43x at TP 2/4/8 and lost only in run 1 (5 graph points of 128 KiB or less), so it stays on AITER.
- **Bit-exact gathers; GSM8K accuracy unchanged; acceptance length (AL) has no consistent direction.** The PR's GPU test checks TP2 with `torch.equal`, eager and under graph replay; scratch matrices extend this to TP4 and TP8 and up to AITER's limit. On GSM8K-1319 (6 base-vs-PR pairs of the same pass), mean accuracy is 0.916 on both arms, and the per-question flips between arms (68-82) are about the same as within one arm (63-81). AL changed by +0.005, -0.010, -0.021 and -0.055 with pr on half B, and by -0.005, +0.013, +0.027 and -0.014 with r1 on half A. Only the -0.055 (GSM8K-300 with expandable segments, E1, one unswapped run) exceeds every same-arm AL span (at most 0.030). The +0.027 (r1, 16 in flight, no same-condition repeat) exceeds every same-condition span (at most 0.024), but not base's 0.030 across 4/16/32 in flight. The perf tables use synthetic AL 3.87.
- **No change elsewhere.** CUDA, ROCm archs other than gfx950 and gfx942, ROCm without `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=1`, non-TP groups, TP6 and gate rejects keep their current path. No new flag. GPU-checked on gfx950: gate rejects, the DP group and `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_CUSTOM_AR=0`. The arch gate rests on a CPU probe.
- **Not claimed:** gfx1250 (gated off, not tested); gfx942 beyond the reviewer's two c1 passes (no sweep, no c2+, no swapped rounds); other models; timing at TP2 between 20 and 32 MiB per rank (bit-exact in the matrix, not timed). The earlier stack-image result (-2.2% to -2.6% at c1, with 5 other PRs) is context only (see Runs and noise).

## Details

**Motivation.** #48446 kept ROCm TP all-gathers on RCCL (the pynccl + `movedim` path was slower), and #48247 enabled AITER's all-gather for DP only. Latency-bound decode gathers, such as the `LogitsProcessor` logits gather and DeepSeek-V4.1's Engram gathers, still pay an RCCL launch plus a `movedim` copy when dim != 0. Models have added their own AITER gather helpers (#57832, #63263, #63322, and #60606 for the DCP group). This PR moves TP gathers in the communicator instead; it does not replace those helpers, whose int64, above-limit and DCP-group gathers stay outside its gate.

**Design** (`CudaCommunicator._aiter_all_gather`, inside the existing ROCm branch):
- **Gate:** `use_aiter_tp_all_gather`, set once in `__init__`, is `use_aiter_allreduce` (TP only) and `get_cdna_version() in (3, 4)` (gfx942, gfx950). Per call: TP 2, 4 or 8 (AITER all-reduce allows 6, but its all-gather launches nothing there); bf16/fp16/fp32; non-empty and contiguous; and `should_custom_ag` accepts it (a multiple of 16 bytes, at most 32 / 16 / 8 MiB per rank at TP 2/4/8). Anything else goes to RCCL. Only arch, dtype, shape and contiguity decide, so all ranks agree.
- **Any dim.** A later dim runs as a last-dim gather of the 2-D view `(-1, prod(shape[dim:]))`, with no `movedim` copy. The row must be a whole number of 16-byte packs.
- **Always copy-in (`all_gather_unreg`), also under capture.** Persistent TP inputs such as Engram `staged_rows` may not be IPC-exportable (for example, with expandable segments on), and AITER's registered capture path raises on those instead of falling back (`mut_reg`). Copy-in adds nothing to the capture-time registration #57481 relies on (pr registered exactly base's graph-address counts) and costs one D2D memcpy node per captured gather.
- **Off switch:** `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_CUSTOM_AR=0` or `--disable-custom-all-reduce`. Both also turn off AITER all-reduce; there is no gather-only switch. `VLLM_BATCH_INVARIANT=1` also keeps gathers on RCCL.

**Tradeoffs and limitations.**
- **Scope.** The path runs on gfx950 and gfx942. End to end, only DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash was measured: here on MI355X at TP4 `--monokernel` (c1-c32) and TP8 (c1-c4), and by the reviewer on MI325X (gfx942) at TP4 c1. Other models, MI350X (also gfx950) and MI300X (also gfx942) are unmeasured.
- **Copy-in leaves about 25 us per step on the table at c1** (M1/M2: -25 and -26 us; c2 within its rep range; c4 noise), matching the ~23 us the 5 captured copy-ins take per profiled step. Using the registered path for IPC-exportable inputs is a possible follow-up (see More detail).
- **Under the profiler, pr's TP4 step was slower** at c1 and c2 (8024 vs 6930 and 7913 vs 7180 us): its longer target-graph `hipGraphLaunch` call lands on the critical path once the profiler slows the host. Without the profiler, pr and r1 were faster in every run.
- **The margin narrows with size.** At the largest timed sizes the sweep gives 1.07-1.29x at TP2 and TP4 (16-20 MiB) and 1.31-2.45x at TP8 (8 MiB). At TP4 the target logits gather now stays on AITER up to 37 requests in flight and the draft logits gather up to 43 (4 and 5 with the old cap).
- **Dim 0 is measured only in isolation.** It becomes a copy plus a kernel instead of one RCCL kernel.
