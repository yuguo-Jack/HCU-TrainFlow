# [Quantization] Block Hadamard input rotation for linear layers: online `mxfp4_hadamard32` and Quark online-rotation checkpoints

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/62750) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Overview

Adds `InputHadamardLinearMethod`, which wraps any linear method and rotates the layer input by an orthonormal block Hadamard matrix in PyTorch before the wrapped `apply`, in the manner of `CompressedTensorsLinearTransformMethod`. Rotating each block of the input dimension of both operands leaves the GEMM unchanged, \((xH)(WH)^T = xW^T\), while spreading outliers across the block, which shrinks block-scaled quantization error. Two producers use it:

- Online quantization: `QuantSpec.hadamard_size` and the shorthand `mxfp4_hadamard32` (also per layer through `--quantization-config.targets`). The wrapper rotates the weight once before the online method quantizes it.
- Quark MXFP4 checkpoints exported with an online rotation: the weights are stored rotated and each layer carries an int8 `input_rotation` (the +-1 Sylvester matrix), which is checked and dropped.

The wrapper doesn't depend on the weight format, the backend or the platform. Paths that haven't been validated raise `NotImplementedError` with a TODO: online methods other than MXFP4 linear, MoE, online rotation on top of a checkpoint that is already quantized, Quark formats other than MXFP4, and non-Sylvester rotations.

Follow-up: #63272 lets kernels rotate inside their activation quant instead of in PyTorch (AITER's HIP MXFP4 quant, ROCm/aiter#6734). It removes the serving cost of the PyTorch rotation measured under Limitations below.

Not a duplicate: searched open PRs for "hadamard", "online rotation", "input_rotation" and "rotation mxfp4".
- #32272 also loads Quark online-rotation checkpoints, covering OCP MX, FP8, INT8 and trained rotations. It has needed a rebase since February, and its last code change was in April. It has no online path. If it lands first, the Quark part here can move onto its loader.
- #54006 adds MXFP4 Hadamard rotation for AutoRound checkpoints on XPU.

## Why not compressed-tensors' `HadamardTransform`

`CompressedTensorsLinearTransformMethod` is the closest existing logic, and the wrapper follows its pattern: wrap the scheme's method, transform the input before `apply`, and hand the v1 weight loader to wrapped methods that aren't in `WEIGHT_LOADER_V2_SUPPORTED`. Its `HadamardTransform` can't be reused as it is:
- It raises `NotImplementedError` for tensor parallelism above 1, and the target model runs at TP8.
- It loads the transform matrices from the checkpoint as `SharedWeightParameter`s. Online quantization has no matrices to load, and Quark stores a +-1 matrix with a different name and scale.
- It applies the transform with `ops.hadacore_transform`, a CUDA sm80+ kernel with no ROCm or CPU build.
- It is configured with compressed-tensors types (`TransformScheme`, `TransformArgs`).

Generalizing `HadamardTransform` to cover these would be a larger change to compressed-tensors. This PR keeps a small, format-agnostic wrapper that compressed-tensors could later route through.

## Claims

- On Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B Quark MXFP4 (TP8, MI355X), `mxfp4_hadamard32` on the GDN projections, compared with round-to-nearest (RTN) online MXFP4 on the same layers:
  - GSM8K: reasoning chains 4.7% shorter, and 140 answers fixed against 98 broken (p = 0.0077).
  - Against BF16 projections: 2.6% longer, which is within the ±5% equivalence margin, and -0.2 accuracy points (p = 0.057).
  - GPQA Diamond: reasoning chains 9.6% shorter (mean ratio 0.904 [0.867, 0.942]), with accuracy unchanged within noise (93.1% vs 92.4% mean over 5 reps; +19 / -12 answers, p = 0.28).
- Quark MXFP4 checkpoints with an online rotation load and run.
  - Qwen3-0.6B exported with a size-32 rotation matches Quark's simulated quantization to a mean absolute per-token logprob difference of 0.69 to 0.81 in eager and compiled mode, against 12.4 with the rotation skipped.
  - Quark's public size-64 test checkpoint gives a wikitext word perplexity of 45.82, within the test's 45.9 ± 0.1. Without this PR it fails to load.

## Details

**Online path.**
- `QuantSpec.hadamard_size` (a power of two) makes `OnlineQuantizationConfig` return `InputHadamardLinearMethod(<online method>(), size, rotate_weight=True)`.
- Online methods may process weights twice (once after layerwise loading, once from the model loader), so the wrapper rotates the weight only while `_already_called_process_weights_after_loading` is unset.
- `_ONLINE_HADAMARD_LINEAR_METHODS` lists the validated online methods; only `Mxfp4OnlineLinearMethod` so far.

**Quark path.**
- `QuarkConfig.maybe_update_config` finds `*.input_rotation` tensors in the safetensors metadata when the config has a `rotation` algorithm. It rejects anything that isn't a square int8 matrix (trained rotations).
- `get_quant_method` wraps `QuarkLinearMethod` with `rotate_weight=False` for layers whose shards all share one rotation size.
- The wrapper registers `input_rotation`, checks on load that it is the Sylvester matrix of that size, and drops it after loading.

**Interaction with fusions.** The rotation is a separate op in front of the wrapped method's `apply`, so compile passes see it and can't drop it. A pass that fuses RMSNorm with the following linear's activation quant would have to keep the rotation in between.

**Limitations and CI gaps.**
- Validated with MXFP4 linear layers only, at sizes 32 (online) and 64 (Quark's test checkpoint). Other paths raise `NotImplementedError`.
- The rotation is an fp32 matmul on `(tokens, K / size, size)` per rotated layer, so it costs throughput here. TP8 serving, Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B Quark MXFP4, dummy weights, ISL/OSL 1024/1024, mean of 2 reps, rotation on the GDN projections (measured in #63272, which also has the fused arm):

  | GDN projections | Output tok/s c=4 | c=64 | c=256 | Mean TPOT ms c=4 | c=64 | c=256 |
  | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
  | RTN `mxfp4` | 373.0 | 1807.1 | 4028.3 | 10.45 | 34.09 | 60.91 |
  | `mxfp4_hadamard32`, PyTorch rotation (this PR) | 326.9 (-12.4%) | 1694.4 (-6.2%) | 3787.7 (-6.0%) | 11.94 | 36.23 | 64.86 |

  #63272 moves the rotation into the activation quant on ROCm and brings it to within 1% of RTN (371.3, 1796.6 and 4024.2 tok/s).
- CI: AMD's quantization job runs on MI300 (gfx942, no MX), where the MX tests skip. The CPU tests run there and on NVIDIA. The MX paths, wikitext and the TP8 runs above were validated on MI355X outside CI.
- CUDA: nothing changes unless a rotation is requested. The rotation path is untested on NVIDIA hardware beyond the CPU tests.
