# [vLLM IR] Deprecate ir_op_priority ahead of vLLM IR removal

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63371) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description



## Overview

Deprecate `ir_op_priority` (`--ir-op-priority`) as the first step toward removing vLLM IR. Nothing changes functionally: the option still works and now logs a one-time warning that it will be removed in v0.34.0.

## Claims

- Setting `ir_op_priority` through `--ir-op-priority`, `EngineArgs(ir_op_priority=...)`, or `EngineArgs(kernel_config=KernelConfig(ir_op_priority=...))` logs a one-time deprecation warning.
- Users who don't set it see no warning. Platform defaults and internal `replace()` copies of `KernelConfig` (e.g. the spec-decode draft config) don't trigger it.
- The `--ir-op-priority` help and the config reference, both generated from the field docstring, and `docs/design/vllm_ir.md` now say it is deprecated.

## Validation

- `pytest tests/engine/test_arg_utils.py -k ir_op_priority`: 2 passed. The new test fails without this change.
- `pytest tests/test_config.py tests/config/test_config_utils.py -k "ir_op or kernel"`: 7 passed.
- End to end on GB200: `vllm bench throughput --model Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B --input-len 128 --output-len 32 --num-prompts 16 --enforce-eager`
  - Without the flag: no warning (5.78 req/s).
  - With `--ir-op-priority.rms_norm vllm_c`: one warning from `arg_utils.py`, and the priority is still applied (5.87 req/s).
- `vllm serve --help=ir-op-priority` shows the deprecation note.
- `pre-commit` passes.

## Details

- The warning is raised in `EngineArgs.create_engine_config()` after the top-level `ir_op_priority` has been merged into `kernel_config` and before platform defaults are applied. At that point every non-empty entry was set by the user. A pydantic validator on `KernelConfig` would also fire on `dataclasses.replace(kernel_config, ...)` once the platform defaults are filled in, which would warn users who never set the option. The test covers that case.
- Users who construct `VllmConfig`/`KernelConfig` directly, without going through `EngineArgs`, won't see the warning.
- Duplicate check: no open PR or issue proposes deprecating `ir_op_priority` or vLLM IR (searched for "vLLM IR" and "ir_op_priority deprecate").
- Follow-ups: move the IR ops back to `CustomOp` dispatch, retarget the fusion-pass patterns, then delete `vllm/ir` and the option.
