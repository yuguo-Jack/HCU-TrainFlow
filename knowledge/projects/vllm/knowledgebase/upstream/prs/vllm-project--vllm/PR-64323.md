# [ROCm][Perf][GLM-5.3-Flash] Native gfx950 sparse decode for mxfp4_mla rows (on #64121)

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/64323) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview

At TP2 with more than 4 decode rows, the MonoKernel steps aside and GLM-5.3-Flash runs the regular sparse MLA decode. With the `mxfp4_mla` KV cache (#64121), that decode is the bf16 split-K kernel, which unpacks every selected row to bf16 before the dot products. In a C64 trace it takes 140.1 µs per call against 23.5 µs for the FP8 kernel. Over 11 calls per step that is 1541 µs against 259 µs, and the step goes from 23.50 ms (FP8 KV) to 24.98 ms. This PR adds a gfx950 decode that reads the packed rows on the scaled MFMA instead, with no bf16 or FP8 copy of the cache.

**Stacked on #64121** (and through it on #61662 and #60321), which has not landed, so the diff against main includes them. This PR's own change is the last two commits, `adebc3a278` (the kernel) and `03240b0753` (gather each tile once and prefetch): [compare against #64121](https://github.com/amd-dlimpus/vllm/compare/839e7d12a1...03240b0753).

**Status:** the unit tests pass on gfx950 (MI355X) and the kernel is timed per call (below). The KV accuracy gate shows no significant difference from the bf16 unpack (see Accuracy). The end-to-end numbers are pending.

## The change

- `vllm/v1/attention/ops/mxfp4_mla/decode.py` (new): `mxfp4_mla_decode_partial_kernel`, same grid and per-split partials as the bf16 split-K kernel, so the reduce kernel is shared.
  - QK: `tl.dot_scaled(codes, row scales, "e2m1", q, q scales, "e4m3")`. The row's E8M0 scales lie on QK's reduction axis, so they are used as stored. The query is MXFP8-quantized in registers. Tokens are on M, so the warps split the tile's tokens.
  - PxV reduces over tokens, but the instruction scales V per (dim, 32 tokens). Each token's scale is folded into its V codes relative to the largest scale in its 32-token block while the codes are widened to e4m3 (`v_cvt_scalef32_pk_f16_fp4`, `v_cvt_scalef32_pk_fp8_f16`), and the block maximum becomes V's scale. P is e4m3 at P·256 with a 2^-8 scale.
  - Scale codes are capped at 254 on load: E8M0 255 is NaN and would poison a whole 32-token block maximum. Because the cap is always applied, the kernel does not rely on the cache coming only from #60321's writer, so it needs no KV-connector gate (unlike the DeepSeek tile's trust flag, which skips its scrub).
  - Each tile's rows are gathered once, as V's words plus the scale words, and QK takes its codes from the same words. The next tile is gathered while the current one runs. The gather is unmasked: invalid tokens read row 0, because a masked load puts vmcnt waits behind exec branches that drain the prefetch. **The KV pool must therefore hold at least one row.** Invalid tokens' scores are masked, so their P is 0 and their V cancels.
- `rocm_aiter_mla_sparse.py` (ops): `native_mxfp4` flag on `rocm_sparse_attn_decode_bf16`, and `rocm_sparse_decode_mxfp4_native_num_splits` (128-token tiles, so no split gets less than one tile).
- Backend: native for decode-only batches when the cache is `mxfp4_mla`, the device is gfx950 and the latent is 512; everything else keeps the bf16 unpack.

The tile follows the DeepSeek V4.1 MXFP4 decode tile, whose 272-byte record is byte-identical to this row at a 512-wide latent. Its split counts and warp choice were tuned for 16 heads and top-512, so they are not copied; GLM's are to be swept at 32 heads per rank and top-2048.

## Accuracy

This adds three lossy steps over the bf16 unpack: Q as MXFP8, P as e4m3, and V tokens more than 2^8 below their block maximum flushing toward zero. The KV accuracy gate compares native (`03240b0753`) against the bf16 unpack (`mxfp4d`, #64121 at `892a9593f0`), both with `mxfp4_mla` KV at the default `--max-num-seqs 32` and client concurrency 32, so decode steps exceed the MonoKernel's 4 rows and run this kernel. Setup: Quark-MXFP4 weights, TP2, `--monokernel`, 196,608-token context. The full gate, including #64121's MonoKernel arm against e4m3 KV, is in #64121.

- **GSM8K** (1319 items, greedy, paired exact McNemar): native 1282 vs bf16 unpack 1274. 13 items only native got right and 5 only the bf16 unpack got right, p=0.096.
- **RULER NIAH multikey-2, 32K:** 50/50 on both arms.
- **GPQA-Diamond** (official sampling: temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, 163,840-token cap), seeds 42-44:

| Seed | Native | bf16 unpack | Only native | Only bf16 unpack | McNemar p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 42 | 182 | 178 | 10 | 6 | 0.454 |
| 43 | 177 | 171 | 14 | 8 | 0.286 |
| 44 | 183 | 183 | 7 | 7 | 1.000 |

  - **Means:** native 91.25% (SD 1.62) and bf16 unpack 89.56% (SD 3.04); the difference is 1.68 ± 1.54 points; paired t over seeds p=0.20.
  - **Pooled McNemar:** 31 vs 21, p=0.21.
  - **Question-clustered** (198 questions): mean difference 1.68 points (cluster SE 1.54); t-test p=0.28, sign-flip permutation p=0.33, Wilcoxon p=0.36, bootstrap 95% CI −1.35 to +4.71.

No statistically significant difference, and native is nominally ahead on every measure.

## Tests

`tests/v1/attention/mxfp4_mla/test_mxfp4_mla_native_decode.py` (gfx950 only):
- `test_widen_v_matches_dequant`: every e2m1 code, scales up to 2^8 below the block maximum and masked tokens. The widened V times its block scale must equal the exact dequant bit for bit on the valid tokens (masked tokens' V is cancelled through P instead). This guards the convert `op_sel` halves and byte order, which still compile when wrong.
- `test_native_decode_matches_bf16_unpack`: ragged rows (empty, 1, 127, 128, 129, 600 tokens) with padded and out-of-range slots, 1/3/8 splits, 16 and 8 heads. Bounds: max error at most 5% of the max reference, mean error at most 2% of the mean reference.

All 7 pass on gfx950 (image `dlimpus/vllm-rocm-nightly:mono-mxfp4`, built on `vllm/vllm-openai-rocm:nightly-rocm100-5d35ce3934a7804b298559d2d99eec33792e1eb9`).

## Registers (gfx950, 4 warps, 1 stage)

GEAK's on-device builds of `03240b0753` report 244 arch VGPRs and no VGPR spills. The 347 SGPR spills of the offline compile did not occur in the on-device build of `adebc3a278` (69 SGPRs, no AGPRs). 256 VGPRs is a cliff: a 256-token tile on top of the prefetch reached 348 and regressed 64-256 rows by 25-30%. Two pipeline stages spill (768 VGPRs offline), hence `NUM_STAGES = 1`.

## Performance

Per call, CUDA graph, 32 heads (TP2), top-2048 random indices into a 65,536-row pool, MI355X. The `adebc3a278` column is from an earlier session; the other two are from the same run.

| Decode rows | bf16 unpack (µs) | Native, `adebc3a278` (µs) | Native, `03240b0753` (µs) | vs unpack |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 26.2 | 33.6 | 21.3 | -19% |
| 4 | 27.7 | 23.0 | 22.2 | -20% |
| 16 | 49.0 | 29.6 | 27.9 | -43% |
| 64 | 142.9 | 50.2 | 46.0 | -68% |
| 128 | 264.6 | 79.2 | 69.9 | -74% |
| 256 | 506.1 | 138.8 | 118.8 | -77% |

`03240b0753` is bit-identical to `adebc3a278`. At 64 rows the native call is 46.0 µs against the 23.5 µs FP8 kernel, so the traced C64 step should drop by about 1.1 ms of its 1.48 ms gap to FP8 KV. The end-to-end A/B is pending.
