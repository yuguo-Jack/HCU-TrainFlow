Preserve an exact direct P/D KV endpoint when every transfer-enabled replay group is loaded, while still replaying a missing Decode-only/local group. **Draft, stacked on and dependent on #59197.**

## Claims

- Keep `512 + 86 = 598` as the external-load endpoint instead of rounding the external hit to 64 and ending at 576. A missing local replay group still rebuilds from `598 - 128 = 470`.
- Avoid querying connector loaded-group state on a zero external hit.
- Retain the replay-start block at 32/64-token boundary-minus-one endpoints, with matching admission-memory accounting. The Mooncake regression preserves source pairing `8 -> 829`, `9 -> 499`.

## Details

The old replay decision required every replay group to appear in connector loaded-group IDs before preserving an unaligned external endpoint. A Decode-only group with `enable_kv_transfer=False` therefore forced a valid 86-token direct hit down to 64 tokens. Prefill ended at 598, while Decode allocated for 576, shifting Mooncake's tail-aligned source pairing.

Separate the conditions: loaded transfer-enabled replay groups determine whether to preserve the exact endpoint; all replay groups determine whether replay can be skipped. A missing local group keeps replay enabled without shrinking the peer endpoint. Bounded-replay sliding windows retain one additional token, and the admission bound includes that retention.
