# [ROCm][Kimi-K3] Mono MoE for decode steps the specialized decode does not take

## Overview

**Two commits on current main (`aa31fa038f`).** Specialized decode from
[#60683](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60683) has landed, so this PR is no longer stacked on it.
**This PR's own change:
[574f5b4b7b...kimik3/stack-plain-k2](https://github.com/amd-mghanimi/vllm/compare/574f5b4b7b...kimik3/stack-plain-k2)**
(1 commit, 17 files).

1. `[ROCm][Kimi-K3] Support regular specialized decode` (`574f5b4b7b`), James E T Smith. Cherry-pick of
   [#61498](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/61498) `145a0fb`, unchanged, including the author and the
   sign-off. It enables ordinary decode on the specialized decode (K1 at `qlen=1`, then K2, widths 1 through 8).
   That contribution stays in this commit and is reviewed on #61498. This PR does not claim it. If #61498 merges
   first, drop the commit.
2. `[ROCm][Kimi-K3] Mono MoE: one launch for a decode MoE layer of up to 32 rows` (`29deba706f`). This PR. The mono
   MoE launch from [#60793](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60793), on current main.

The previous head (`0f3f1484e7`) carried a different plain-decode design: KDA layers ran vLLM's AttnRes and KDA up to
`o_proj`'s input (`forward_core`), then K2, and K1 was skipped. That commit is removed. Plain decode on this head is
#61498's path.

**Mono MoE.** One persistent launch for a Kimi-K3 latent MoE layer at decode on gfx950: biased sigmoid top-k, the
expert sort, a4w4 gemm1/gemm2 and the bf16 shared expert, for steps of up to 32 rows. Opt-in with
`VLLM_ROCM_MONO_MOE=1`. It checks each layer on its first forward and falls back to the multi-kernel path where the
launch does not fit. The specialized decode (`additional_config={"decode_backend": "latency_specialized"}`, the
landed form of `--monokernel`) still takes its own steps, at most 8 rows. With both on, those steps run the
specialized decode and every other decode step's MoE, up to 32 rows, takes this launch. Either switch works alone.

The kernels are under `vllm/models/kimi_k3/amd/mono_moe/`. `ROCmLatentMoERunner._forward_impl` dispatches to them.
Both the stock forward and the sharded latent tail enter `_forward_impl`. The device helpers use a local `traced`
(`ASTRewriter.transform`) and their own `!llvm.ptr` casts, so the launch also runs on AITER v0.1.25 and later, which
removed `communication_ops_utils.traced`. `g2_kstatic=True` is passed only where AITER still takes it
(v0.1.24.post1, which vLLM's ROCm base image pins).

**Not re-measured at this head.** Every number below is from a previous head, on AITER v0.1.24, and the plain-decode
rows used the removed `forward_core` design rather than #61498. They are not claims about this head.

## Claims

Previous heads only:

- Speculative decoding (DSpark), 9 to 32 rows per step: the mono MoE was as fast as #60683 or faster, never slower
  beyond noise. At `4929a0ada8`, k = 3: 12 rows -5.7%, 16 rows -4.9%, 20 to 32 rows within 1%. Repeated on the
  earlier base with k = 3 and k = 7: 12 rows -5.7%, 16 rows -2.5% (k = 3) and -5.0% (k = 7), 32 rows 0% (k = 3) and
  -3.0% (k = 7).
- Speculative decoding, up to 8 rows per step: the same as #60683 (both ran #60683's K2; the mono MoE did not).
- Plain decode on the removed design, 1 to 8 rows: median TPOT 1.19x to 1.38x better than stock. 12 and 16 rows: 2%
  to 3% better; 24 and 32 rows: 0.3% to 0.9% slower than stock. That path is not in this head. #61498 is the
  plain-decode path here, and it has its own measurements.
- Every decode step of at most 8 rows ran K2 (60,698 of 60,698 counted at the previous head), so the mono MoE launch
  only served steps above 8 rows.

carries `concurrency` rows in plain decode and `concurrency x (k + 1)` rows with DSpark (TRITON_MLA drafter). Every
request succeeded and no server logged a memory access fault. Negative is faster. Each table compares builds on one
node only. All of it is at a previous head.

### Which path steps of at most 8 rows take

Counted at the previous head with vLLM's `monokernel_steps` counter, labelled per step with the graph mode and the
row count, on that server: 8k / 1k at c1 to c8 plus short requests (256 in / 128 out, 64 requests per concurrency,
frequent arrivals).

| Decode | Steps of at most 8 rows | of which K2 | Steps above 8 rows (K2 declines: width) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| DSpark k=3 | 9,537 | 9,537 | 4,509 full graph, 244 piecewise, 137 eager |
| plain | 51,161 | 51,161 | 250 piecewise, 137 eager |

Every step of at most 8 rows ran K2; the only declined steps had more than 8 rows. The earlier base gave the same
counts.

### Tests and accuracy

- At the previous head on MI355X: the mono MoE numerics and gate tests, 27 passed; #60683's mono tests, 49 passed
  (44 on #60683's own tree; the 5 more were the removed plain-decode cases in `test_spec.py`); #60683's 8-GPU
  numerics passed on both trees.
- GPQA Diamond (198 questions, no thinking, 4k tokens, greedy) at the previous head: 0.803 with DSpark k = 3, 0.773
  in plain decode (before that rebase: 0.798 and 0.778; one question is 0.005).

## Details

- The mono MoE commit is #60793's kernels, squashed and placed at `vllm/models/kimi_k3/amd/mono_moe/` beside the
  landed specialized decode. #60793 itself stays based on main. `VLLM_ROCM_MONO_MOE` is its own switch: the
  specialized decode refuses at startup the deployments it does not serve (TP other than 8, more than 7 speculative
  tokens, the V1 model runner, piecewise-only graphs), while the mono MoE falls back per layer and per step.
- The regular-decode commit is not modified here. Its files are the seven #61498 touches under
  `specialized_decode/` and its tests.
