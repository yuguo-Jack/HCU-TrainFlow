# [ROCm][Kimi-K3] Mono MoE on #60683's mono decode, with K2 in plain decode

## Overview

**Draft, stacked on [#60683](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60683) at its head `df3693c8f8`.** Only the top 3
commits belong to this PR; everything below them is #60683. **This PR's changes alone:
[df3693c8f8...kimik3/stack-plain-k2](https://github.com/amd-mghanimi/vllm/compare/df3693c8f8...kimik3/stack-plain-k2)**
(3 commits, 21 files). It puts the mono MoE launch of
[#60793](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/60793) on top of #60683's mono decode for Kimi-K3 on MI355X (gfx950),
and adds K2 in plain decode steps so the two work together. The 3 commits:

1. Mono MoE: one launch for a decode MoE layer of up to 32 rows (from #60793).
2. Mono MoE: numerics and path-selection tests (from #60793).
3. Mono decode: K2 in plain decode steps.

**K2 in plain decode steps.** #60683 runs K2 (o_proj, its all-reduce, the MLP AttnRes and the latent MoE) only on
speculative-verify steps. K2 does not depend on how o_proj's input was made, so KDA layers now run vLLM's AttnRes and
KDA up to o_proj's input (`KimiK3DeltaAttention.forward_core`), then K2, as MLA layers already do. A plain decode step
of at most 8 rows takes this path.

**Rebased on #60683 `df3693c8f8` (Oct 16).** The previous head (`4929a0ada8`, on #60683 `a717180301`) had a 4th commit
that merged the mono widths into the resolved CUDA graph sizes. `df3693c8f8` fixes the same capture-size issue in
#60683, so that commit is dropped. The mono MoE now also runs on AITER v0.1.25 and later, which removed
`communication_ops_utils.traced` (ROCm/aiter#6377) and `compile_gemm2_a4w4_port(g2_kstatic=)`. The device helpers use
a local `traced` (`ASTRewriter.transform`, as #60683's own helpers do) and their own `!llvm.ptr` casts, and
`g2_kstatic=True` is passed only where AITER still takes it (v0.1.24.post1, which vLLM's ROCm base image pins). **All
results below were measured at the previous head, on AITER v0.1.24; the current head has not been re-measured yet.**

Steps above 8 rows keep vLLM's layers, with the mono MoE launch (up to 32 rows) for the MoE. Switches: `--monokernel`
(#60683's mono decode, now with plain decode) and `VLLM_ROCM_MONO_MOE=1` (mono MoE), each usable alone.

## Claims

- Speculative decoding (DSpark), 9 to 32 rows per step: as fast as #60683 or faster, never slower beyond noise.
  Measured at the previous head, k = 3: 12 rows -5.7%, 16 rows -4.9%, 20 to 32 rows within 1%. Repeated on the earlier
  base with k = 3 and k = 7: 12 rows -5.7%, 16 rows -2.5% (k = 3) and -5.0% (k = 7), 32 rows 0% (k = 3) and -3.0% (k = 7).
- Speculative decoding, up to 8 rows per step: the same as #60683 (both run #60683's K2).
- Plain decode, 1 to 8 rows per step: median TPOT 1.19x to 1.38x better than stock, measured at the previous head. 12 and 16
  rows: 2% to 3% better; 24 and 32 rows: 0.3% to 0.9% slower than stock, in both runs. #60683 alone refuses plain
  decode.
- Every decode step of at most 8 rows runs #60683's K2 (60,698 of 60,698 counted at the previous head, below), so the
  mono MoE launch only serves steps above 8 rows.

## Validation

`moonshotai/Kimi-K3`, TP8, MI355X, MI355X OOB serving settings, 8k input / 1k output, median TPOT in ms. A step
carries `concurrency` rows in plain decode and `concurrency x (k + 1)` rows with DSpark (TRITON_MLA drafter). Every
request succeeded and no server logged a memory access fault. Negative is faster. Each table compares builds on one
node only.

### Which path steps of at most 8 rows take

Steps of at most 8 rows that K2 declines would fall to the mono MoE instead of #60683's MoE. Counted at the previous head with
vLLM's `monokernel_steps` counter, labelled per step with the graph mode and the row count (a measurement-only copy of
`routing.py`), on this PR's server: 8k / 1k at c1 to c8 plus short requests (256 in / 128 out, 64 requests per
concurrency, frequent arrivals).

| Decode | Steps of at most 8 rows | of which K2 | Steps above 8 rows (K2 declines: width) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| DSpark k=3 | 9,537 | 9,537 | 4,509 full graph, 244 piecewise, 137 eager |
| plain | 51,161 | 51,161 | 250 piecewise, 137 eager |

Every step of at most 8 rows ran K2; the only declined steps had more than 8 rows (prefill and mixed steps, and
speculative steps above K2's widths, which take the mono MoE). The earlier base gave the same counts.

### Tests and accuracy

- At the previous head on MI355X: this PR's mono MoE numerics and gate tests 27 passed; #60683's mono tests 49 passed (44 on
  #60683's own tree; the 5 more are this PR's plain-decode cases in `test_spec.py`); #60683's 8-GPU numerics passed on
  both trees.
- GPQA Diamond (198 questions, no thinking, 4k tokens, greedy) at the previous head: 0.803 with DSpark k = 3, 0.773 in plain
  decode (before the rebase: 0.798 and 0.778; one question is 0.005).

## Details

- The mono MoE commits are #60793's eleven, squashed into the kernel and its tests and moved beside #60683's
  `vllm/models/kimi_k3/amd/mono/` (as `mono_moe/`); #60793 itself stays based on main. Its switch is
  `VLLM_ROCM_MONO_MOE` because `--monokernel` turns on the mono decode, which refuses at startup the configs it does not
  serve (TP other than 8, more than 7 speculative tokens, Model Runner V1, piecewise-only graphs); the mono MoE checks
  each layer on its first forward instead and falls back to the multi-kernel path where the launch does not fit.
- On the rebase, `VLLM_ROCM_MONO_DECODE` and the fault-injection variables, which #60683 removed, were dropped from
  `vllm/envs.py`.
