# [Model][Doc] Add RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell support for DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/64867) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

## Overview
Adds NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (sm_120, 96 GB GDDR7) deployment support to the DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp recipe, including 8-GPU and 4-GPU placement presets, validated launch commands, and benchmark numbers.
Closes vllm-project/recipes#1204.

## Claims
- Adds `rtx_pro_6000_8x` and `rtx_pro_6000_4x` supported hardware SKUs and presets for DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp.
- Configures sm_120 architecture-specific flags (`--attention_config.indexer_kv_dtype fp8` and `--moe-backend auto`).
- Clarifies that 1× and 2× RTX PRO 6000 configurations are unsupported due to the 202 GB minimum VRAM budget.
- Documents OCRBench accuracy (83.4%) and throughput benchmarks for 8× RTX PRO 6000 workstations.

## Validation
- Validated `recipes/models/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp.yaml` against the official schema (`https://recipes.vllm.ai/recipe.schema.json`) with `jsonschema`.
- All pre-commit hooks passed.

## Details
DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp requires ~168 GB weights and 202 GB minimum VRAM. Single-GPU (96 GB) and dual-GPU (192 GB) do not meet the minimum memory requirement and are marked unsupported. 8-GPU (768 GB) and 4-GPU (384 GB) configurations provide sufficient capacity and are documented with tested TP8+EP and TP4+EP commands.
