# [MRV2] Keep warmup out of the EPLB load stats

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/56716) · state: closed · merged: True

## Original PR description

## Purpose

MRV2 warmup forwards record expert load into `expert_load_pass` like real ones. The router kernel records during the forward, so skipping or suppressing the EPLB step afterwards does not keep that load out of the stats. It lands in the next real step. This happens at startup and on every elastic EP re-warm right after the rebalance.

This adds `EPLBController.suppress()` which turns off both the EPLB step and `should_record_tensor` and puts both back when done. It is used for `skip_eplb=True` in `step_eplb_after`, around MRV2 `compile_or_warm_up_model` and around `profile_cudagraph_memory`. `EplbState.step` now also zeroes the dummy load before the profile early return.

MRV1 has the same recording issue on a serving rank. #57151 fixes it there.

Split out of #53934

LGTM. This bug also affects ordinary MRV2 startup with EPLB enabled, even without Elastic EP: initial warmup samples can enter the load window and bias the first EPLB reshuffle. This can degrade serving performance until the second reshuffle. In my experience, reshuffling based on incorrect statistics can produce very poor expert mappings and significant performance degradations.
