## Overview

Add opt-in FlashInfer ordinary GDN decode for Qwen hybrid models; auto remains Triton and prefill/MTP selection is unchanged. Invited takeover of #41966 via [#53463](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/53463#issuecomment-6006969716); #53645 targets SM120. [Duplicate/takeover evidence](https://github.com/positive666/vllm/tree/69e9d682a5390e616d9d56ab009449d227cad772).

## Claims

- Lazy pinned FI API, separate errors, explicit packed-decode override log, aligned gate staging where needed, and mixed-batch prefill-tail-only copy.
- Adapter scope: BF16 input, FP32 recurrent state, K=V=128, SM80+. L20/SM89 hardware tested; SM80 remains untested.
- Integration and bounded kernel gains; no general serving-speedup or accuracy-equivalence claim.
