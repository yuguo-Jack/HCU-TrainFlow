# [Model] Load q/k/v scales for the quantized Kimi-K2.6

[Upstream PR](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63097) · state: open · merged: False

## Original PR description

Register MLAAttention with the compressed-tensors KV-cache quantization method and map Kimi-K2.5/K2.6 checkpoint q/k/v scales into the model's nested MLA attention module.

Keep the generic cache-scale mapper unchanged; all scale-name translation is scoped to Kimi.

Fixes intel/auto-round#2370.

## Validation

Validated on vLLM 0.32.0 using 4x B200 GPUs (`tensor_parallel_size=4`) with [`intel-ai/Kimi-K2.6-MXFP4-FP8ATTN-CT-AutoRound`](https://huggingface.co/intel-ai/Kimi-K2.6-MXFP4-FP8ATTN-CT-AutoRound) (106 shards, 524.76 GiB).

- All 106/106 shards loaded with zero `*_scale is not loaded` warnings.
- All 183 checkpoint q/k/v scales (61 layers x q/k/v) exactly matched the post-load scale buffers on every TP rank:

```text
CHECKPOINT_SCALES=183
RANK=0 LOADED=183 MISSING=0 EXTRA=0 MISMATCH=0
RANK=1 LOADED=183 MISSING=0 EXTRA=0 MISMATCH=0
RANK=2 LOADED=183 MISSING=0 EXTRA=0 MISMATCH=0
RANK=3 LOADED=183 MISSING=0 EXTRA=0 MISMATCH=0
SCALE_VERIFICATION_PASS 183/183 exact matches on every rank
```

- [`examples/basic/offline_inference/generate.py`](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/examples/basic/offline_inference/generate.py) exited with status 0 and generated coherent text for all four prompts:

https://huggingface.co/intel-ai/Kimi-K2.6-MXFP4-FP8ATTN-CT-AutoRound

```text
Prompt: 'Hello, my name is'
Generated text: ' David and I am a professional photographer...'

Prompt: 'The president of the United States is'
Generated text: ' the head of state and head of government of the United States...'

Prompt: 'The capital of France is'
Generated text: ' Paris. The capital of the United States is Washington, D.C....'

Prompt: 'The future of AI is'
Generated text: " here, and it's not just about robots taking over the world..."
```

Generation command:

